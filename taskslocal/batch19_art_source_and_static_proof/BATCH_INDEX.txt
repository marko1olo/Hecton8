COMMON PROOF LANGUAGE
- STATIC VERIFIED: file exists, text inspected, image source inspected, or non-Unity static QA artifact exists.
- STATIC REJECTED: inspected source failed a documented source-art or channel-contract gate.
- PENDING SOURCE: required source candidate not found or not generated.
- PENDING UNITY OWNER: requires Unity import, material assignment, scene placement, runtime/editor screenshot, profiler, build, or player capture.
- BLOCKED BY AUTHORITY: requested action conflicts with AGENTS.md, project bibles, mandates, or no-Unity gates.

CONTINUOUS QUALITY CONSEQUENCES
- GlobalQualityWeight 0.0 to 0.25: preserve silhouette, readability, tile cleanliness, non-muddy color, and material identity with compact source sizes and minimal channel set.
