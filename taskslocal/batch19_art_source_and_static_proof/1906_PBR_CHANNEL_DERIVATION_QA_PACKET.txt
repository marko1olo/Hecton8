PHASE 2 - DERIVATION LOGIC QA
12. For every ALBEDO_SOURCE accepted source, decide whether it is suitable for normal, height, roughness, AO, metallic, and emission derivation. Record why.
13. Reject fake PBR where roughness is just inverted albedo without material logic, AO is a generic dark vignette, metallic is random noise, or emission is broad neon paint without gameplay/ecology reason.
14. For hard-surface product-face sources, require wear logic: exposed metal only where paint is chipped, salt/scratches directional but not tiled like wallpaper, roughness separated from albedo color.
15. For wet basalt/coastline sources, require wetness, salt, sediment, erosion, waterline, and roughness variation that reads in bright surface/photic lighting.
16. For coral/kelp sources, require porous or fibrous organic logic, constrained biolum masks, thickness/readability notes, and no dense alpha-blend dependence.
17. For foam/waterline sources, require tileable breakup, non-noisy shape control, and mask logic that can be packed without making water muddy.

CHECKPOINT C AFTER TASK 17
- Derivation notes exist for each accepted source.
- Rejections distinguish source-art failure from PBR-channel failure.
- Continuous GlobalQualityWeight consequences are present for each material family.

PHASE 3 - STATIC PREVIEW QA
18. If safe local image tooling exists without code edits, create static contact sheets under Docs/GeneratedAssets/Gemini/QA/1906/** showing source, proposed derived grayscale channels, and 2x2 tile previews.
19. If image tooling is unavailable, do not write ad hoc scripts. Mark PREVIEW_PENDING_TOOL and continue with manual text/image inspection notes.
20. Inspect proposed normals for direction consistency, useful strength, no embossed albedo text, no seam spikes, and no excessive high-frequency shimmer risk.
21. Inspect AO for cavity-only logic. Reject broad darkening that would make surface/photic content muddy.
22. Inspect roughness/smoothness for material-specific logic. Wet basalt may be smoother in wet regions; dry sediment must not become plastic.
23. Inspect metallic for physical plausibility. Organic, stone, foam, and sediment are non-metal unless a cited product-face or ore material requires metal.
24. Inspect emission for constrained system/ecology reason. Reject broad emission that turns coral/kelp into flat neon.
