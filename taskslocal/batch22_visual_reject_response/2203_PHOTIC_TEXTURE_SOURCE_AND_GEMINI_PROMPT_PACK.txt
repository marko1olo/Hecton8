MANDATES
- Gemini/source art is not production-ready until intake passes.
- Prompts must be in strong English with seamless/tileable/orthographic/PBR/source constraints.
- Avoid baked lighting when generating albedo.
- Generate source channels deliberately: albedo source, height source, roughness/mask source, normal only if later derived or explicitly generated.
- Do not use one image as every PBR channel.
