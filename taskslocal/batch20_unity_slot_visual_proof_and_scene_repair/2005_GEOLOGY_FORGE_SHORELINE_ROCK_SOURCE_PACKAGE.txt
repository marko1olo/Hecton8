2. Optimization: LOD/collider/material contracts are explicit and cheap on weak devices.

1. Low: lower density and lower LOD distance, but premium shapes and materials remain.
2. Middle: stable default coastline density.
3. High: more hero formations and better wetness detail.
4. Ultra: additional microdetail and hero silhouette density, with unchanged collision/gameplay truth unless explicitly authored.
