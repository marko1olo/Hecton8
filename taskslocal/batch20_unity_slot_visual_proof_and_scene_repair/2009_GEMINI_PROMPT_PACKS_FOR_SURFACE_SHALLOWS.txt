Three-pillar acceptance:
1. Graphics: prompts demand premium source material that can meet the visual floor.
2. Optimization: prompts and QA rules lead to texture sets that can be compressed, atlased, and LOD-scaled.
3. Gameplay: generated visuals must not confuse affordances, hazards, item identity, or navigation.

GlobalQualityWeight consequences:
1. Low: source derivatives support smaller textures and simpler maps without ugly collapse.
2. Middle: default texture/material set.
