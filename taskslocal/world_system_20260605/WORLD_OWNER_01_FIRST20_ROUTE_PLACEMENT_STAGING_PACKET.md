## Continuous GlobalQualityWeight Consequences

These are labels on one continuous curve, not binary switches:

- Low / compact `0.0-0.25`: preserve route-critical landmarks, return-path silhouette, premium water/sky/terrain composition, material identity, collision proxies, oxygen/HUD readability, and clear negative space. Reduce optional density probability, LOD residency, shadow eligibility, texture residency, and VFX cadence smoothly. No ugly mode, proxy mesh, flat water, muddy sky, smoothed blob, or alpha-blended flora carpet.
- Middle `0.25-0.55`: expected player lane. Maintain full route grammar, believable geology/ecology/industry, stable LOD bands, active material ownership, sparse but meaningful particles, and readable first resource/hazard/evidence decisions.
- High `0.55-0.85`: spend recovered budget on longer LOD0/LOD1 residency, richer wetness/contact response, denser authored near-field biota and wreck traces, stronger material normals/masks, more believable caustic/flow fakes, and improved capture composition. Truth ownership does not change.
- Ultra `0.85-1.0`: add visual overkill only after compact passes: longer sightline richness, denser but authored clusters, richer flora motion/VFX, deeper material layering, better shadows/reflections where proven, and capture-grade shoreline/photic/medium-depth composition. Gameplay truth, collider authority, save identity, DTO layout, material semantics, seeds, and owner routes remain unchanged.

Hysteresis is mandatory for LOD, residency, density, VFX cadence, and diagnostics. Do not flip state immediately at threshold boundaries.

## Regression Model

- CPU: risk comes from renderer count, LOD evaluation, culling, collider/proxy count, streaming dispatch, VFX, and scatter logic. Any single system above `0.1ms` is suspicious until profiler proof and load-shed behavior exist.
- GC: this packet edits no runtime code. Future placement/runtime scatter must prove `0 B/frame` and reject hot `GetComponent`, `Find*`, LINQ, string churn, `Resources.Load`, runtime material clones, runtime mesh/texture generation, and unmanaged readback allocation.
- Memory/VRAM: risk comes from mesh buffers, texture residency, material slots, shadows, VFX atlases, HLOD residency, and Addressables handles. Compact hard ceiling remains 1800 MB VRAM and 900 MB texture budget unless hardware detector and proof justify continuous upscale.
- SetPass/batches: risk comes from material proliferation, shader variants, alpha fields, shadow casters, and unbatched prefabs. Use shared material families, atlases/arrays, SRP Batcher, GPU Resident Drawer, or instancing only when proven.
- Cadence: density, residency, LOD, VFX, diagnostics, and screenshots must scale through continuous `GlobalQualityWeight` with hysteresis. No binary quality switches.
- Correctness: placement must not change route truth, oxygen truth, hazard truth, resource identity, interaction identity, collision truth, save identity, terrain ownership, water ownership, player/HUD ownership, or Addressables ownership.
- Visual: surface, shoreline, ocean surface, Aegir, sky, photic shallows, and medium-depth hero routes must remain bright, legible, beautiful, material-rich, and Subnautica-level or better. Placement that hides failed base art is rejected.
