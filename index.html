3D cave generator utilizing Marching Cubes, Burst Compiler, and Spatial Hashing. Culls hidden geometry to save 60-80% mesh overhead for low-end GPUs.

Diegetic AR Interface

Hecton-OS projects directly onto the suit visor. Uses Screen Space Canvas with physical lag/sway to simulate helmet inertia. No floating 2D menus.

Spatial Inventory
