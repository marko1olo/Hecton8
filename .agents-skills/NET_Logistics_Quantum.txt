## Primary Law

[FORBID] Simulating cable signal propagation, electrons, plasma, or per-meter power waves.
[RULE] "Quantum" logistics sync is a deterministic graph-delta protocol, not a physical model.

---

## Architecture

Long-distance power state is synchronized by dirty graph components:
- Graph topology changes mark affected NetworkID dirty.
- LogisticsTick solves authoritative power on the owning worker job.
- Replication/sync sends compact deltas: NetworkID, node id, potential/load quantized fields, flags, and topology revision.
- Remote visual proxies interpolate locally; they do not own gameplay state.

[RULE] Same-zone gameplay reads authoritative `LogisticsNode` state.
[RULE] Long-distance/nonresident zones store compressed network summaries only.
[RULE] Rehydration on load reconstructs CSR/DSU from edge list and applies last compact summary.

---

## Cinematic Cheat

Instant sync is allowed because player-facing power behavior is binary/low-frequency:
- powered
- brownout
- overloaded
- ruptured
- isolated

The visual travel of sparks, pulsing cables, and panel flicker is shader-time noise driven by a deterministic event timestamp. It is not a simulated signal.

---

## Performance Gate

[REQUIRE] No logistics sync path may run every frame.
[REQUIRE] Dirty summaries flush on LogisticsTick/FrostTick only.
[REQUIRE] Long-distance summaries must be fixed-size or upper-bounded.
