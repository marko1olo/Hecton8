# HECTON-8 | VR Stencil Masking | Fragment Rejection Law

Status: PENDING VERIFICATION
Authority: TECH_SCOUT
Date: 2026-05-11

---

## Law

[RULE] VR visor/HUD masking should use stencil when it rejects expensive fragments before they shade.
[RULE] The mask writer must be cheap: `ColorMask 0`, no texture sampling, no lighting, no transparent blending.
[RULE] The masked pass must use `Stencil Comp Equal` or equivalent and must not write stencil unless explicitly required.
[RULE] XR camera ordering must prove the stencil buffer is preserved between the mask writer and the HUD/visor draw.

[FORBID] Stencil masking as a blanket feature if the writer costs more than the rejected pixels.
[FORBID] Overlay camera clears that erase the stencil before the consuming pass.
[FORBID] Alpha-blended hidden HUD fragments in VR when a stencil/cutout gate can reject them.

---

## Evidence

[SOURCE] Unity ShaderLab stencil documentation states the stencil buffer is an 8-bit per-pixel mask and that failed stencil tests skip the remaining processing for that pixel.
https://docs.unity.cn/Manual/SL-Stencil.html

- no transparent blend HUD overdraw behind the helmet shell.
