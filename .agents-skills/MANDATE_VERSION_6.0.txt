## Math Benchmark Guidance

`math.sin` vs LUT vs parabolic approximation was not benchmarked in Player in this session.

Default law until measured:
- Hot visual oscillation on Low: triangle/parabolic approximation.
- Audio-critical or gameplay-deterministic phase on Low: fixed LUT with integer phase and interpolation if audible.
- High/Ultra cinematic path: `math.sin` only if under profiler budget or Burst-vectorized batch.

Required benchmark shape:
- Burst job, FloatMode.Fast.
- 1M samples.
- warm cache and cold cache passes.
- i3/MX350 target machine.
- report ns/sample and max absolute error.
