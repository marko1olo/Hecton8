## 1. THE SURFACE & PHOTIC ZONES (+200m to -1000m)

### TIER 1 (+200m to 0m): THE SUNLIT REACHES
1. **NORTH (Archipelago Needles)**: Razor-sharp limestone spires rising from the surf. Eroded by ancient winds into jagged, bird-nesting pillars with salt-crusted peaks.
2. **SOUTH (Mesa Plateaus)**: Flat-topped dry land platforms. Massive, perfectly circular sinkholes (Blue Holes) are scattered across, filled with stagnant, nutrient-rich water.
3. **EAST (The Granite Spine)**: A continuous, obsidian-smooth vertical wall. This is the origin of the world's primary mountain range, marking the "end of the sea".
4. **WEST (The Silt Tongue)**: Undulating white sand dunes stretching into the horizon. The surface is constantly rippling under the influence of surface swells.

### TIER 2 (0m to -300m): THE SHALLOWS
5. **NORTH (Sea-Stack Forest)**: Isolated circular pillars forming a literal forest of stone. The floor is a chaotic mix of broken sea-shells and white coral dust.
6. **SOUTH (White Alabaster Pools)**: Terraced basins looking like frozen waterfalls. The minerals here create milky, opaque pools within the grey stone.
7. **EAST (The Tectonic Chute)**: A narrow, straight canyon cut into the granite. It acts as a funnel for the first cold deep-sea currents.
8. **WEST (Sand-Fan Deltas)**: Rippling waves of fine sediment. Tidal forces create complex, fractal patterns that change with every storm.
