## Low, Middle, High, Ultra Use Notes

- Low / compact concept target: strong silhouettes, clear route cues, clean ocean color, limited particles, shared materials, no ugly mode.
- Middle target: richer material identity, more local lights, stronger flora/coral density, better silt and wetness.
- High target: richer shadows, longer visible route detail, stronger water/material response.
- Ultra target: capture-grade density and sensory polish without changing gameplay truth or promising unbuilt systems.
