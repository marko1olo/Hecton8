## Low / Middle / High / Ultra Expectations

Low/compact:

- Must preserve readable ocean color, water volume, shoreline contact, terrain silhouette, sky/Aegir readability, route cue, and HUD/instrument state.
- Allowed reductions: secondary density, raymarch steps, bloom absence, particle count, shadow range.
- Rejected: ugly mode, flat water, muddy sky, proxy terrain, illegible HUD, or darkness as cover.

Middle:

- Expected player lane.
- Must show production player/HUD route, active sky/ocean/terrain material stacks, coherent shoreline breakup, sparse but meaningful particles, and no visible primitive/default/proxy product-face contamination.

High:

- Saved budget must buy richer cloud/Aegir detail, stronger waterline breakup, denser near-field geology/flora, cleaner HUD material response, longer LOD residency, and stronger material normals.
- It must not change gameplay truth, scene ownership, save identity, DTO layout, collision truth, or Crest ownership.

Ultra:

- Visual overkill may add layered atmosphere, surface sparkle, denser route dressing, richer cockpit/visor contamination, better water response, and capture-grade composition.
- Ultra cannot rescue failed compact readability and cannot create a second visual truth owner.
