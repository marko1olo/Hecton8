## Decision 1 - Product Proof Beats Architecture Breadth

Problem: HECTON-8 has huge infrastructure, but current docs repeatedly mark Unity runtime proof, player-build proof, profiler proof, save/load route proof, and visual capture as absent.
Solution: Treat the Copper Wire first-20-minutes route as the product gate. Do not judge progress by subsystem count, H-Phi, source size, or doc breadth.
Rejected Alternatives: Expanding fauna, co-op, extra biomes, Overkill visuals, or broad marketing before route proof. Those increase surface area without improving the first public promise.
Scalability potential: Low tier gets one stable, readable route. Middle/High/Ultra can add richer silt, visor, machinery, fauna, and lighting after the same route is already true.
Hardware Impact: 0us measured runtime impact; strategy only. Expected gain is risk reduction, not frame-time.

## Decision 2 - Public Hook Must Be Agency Under Pressure

Problem: "Underwater survival but darker" is not enough against Subnautica 2's current public momentum and large positive review base.
Solution: First public assets must show one readable player decision under pressure: repair, retreat, reroute, scan, operate, abort, or recover.
Rejected Alternatives: Mood-only abyss shots, anomaly-only shots, creature pose thumbnails, or technical devlog screenshots. These can attract curiosity but do not prove player agency.
Scalability potential: Low budget can test cold-read clarity with few assets. Middle/High/Ultra campaigns can scale once the same agency signal survives Steam/social/creator feedback.
Hardware Impact: 0us measured runtime impact; capture/marketing constraint only.

## Decision 3 - Steam Page Later, Not Weak

Problem: Steam Coming Soon page is valuable for wishlists, but a weak first page can lock in derivative/confusing first impressions.
Solution: Prepare Steam assets now; publish only after 6-10 real screenshots, one 15-20s clip, a tested capsule, official contact preflight, and agency-proof cold reads.
Rejected Alternatives: Launching a page with concept art, vague copy, or no gameplay clip. Steam permits early pages, but project docs correctly block weak page launch.
Scalability potential: Low budget avoids wasting organic traffic. High-tier marketing can use Steam visibility, creator traffic, and event traffic after conversion is measurable.
Hardware Impact: 0us measured runtime impact.

## Decision 6 - Velocity Is Real But Verification Is The Bottleneck
