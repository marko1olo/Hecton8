Rejected Alternatives: Full physical water simulation per predator and GPU texture CPU readback were rejected. They add latency, data transfer, and cross-domain ownership violations.

## Decision 3 - SDF Repulsion Is Immediate

Problem: EWMA smoothing reduces steering jitter but delays wall response if applied to obstacle repulsion.
Solution: Smooth target+flow intent only, then add SDF repulsion unsmoothed with `sdfObstacleRepulsionWeight / max(distanceMeters, 0.25)^2`, clamped by max acceleration.
Rejected Alternatives: Physics raycast obstacle avoidance and smoothing the wall term were rejected. Raycasts violate nav mandate and smoothing the wall term causes late avoidance.
Scalability potential: Low uses nearest clearance/proxy. Middle caps local proxy checks. High/Ultra can use richer SDF gradients without changing the output contract.
