## Speaker Choices

- Black Keel: clipped carrier tender / claim-pool automation. It gives route, debt, and window facts. It does not reassure.
- Deep Reach: sanitized legal packet. The lie is omission and prerequisite language, not villain confession.
- Worker: named colony worker under job pressure. Human through task and object, not family hook.
- Atlas: damaged maintenance telemetry. It collapses categories between cable, tissue, route, seal, and serviceability.
- Capsule black box: formal event reconstruction with timestamp, system ID, confidence, dropped frames.
- Quarantine relay: insurer/custody relay. It can accept extraction while denying release.

## Interruption Grammar

- Interruptions must carry data: dropped frames, carrier lag, alarm priority, pressure state, confidence change, or custody hold.
- Decorative static is rejected.
- Cutoffs are allowed only where they hide a name, receiver, or cause while preserving the gameplay clue.

## Subtitle Grammar

- Keep beats under roughly one breath.
- Split telemetry values, human speech, and system contradiction into separate subtitle beats.
- Source labels are required where voice identity is not obvious.
- Non-English rows remain draft/native-review-needed unless reviewed; no VO readiness claim.

## Black-Box Grammar

- Use event marker, timestamp, system ID, confidence, dropped frames, and contradiction.
- The machine records facts. It does not explain theme.
- Useful clue must survive damage: failed ascent path, conditional return, delayed authorization, category collapse, or custody hold.

## Spoiler Control

- First-hour packets may reveal damaged descent, conditional return, sanitized accident language, and useful-wrong Atlas repair.
- They must not reveal final payload receiver consequences, full Deep Reach liability chain, or Atlas basin outcomes.
