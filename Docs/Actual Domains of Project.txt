ECHELON 1: CORE & MEMORY INFRASTRUCTURE (Engine Foundation)

1. BIOS Bootstrapper: Awaitable boot orchestration, startup state machine,
initialization validation of all systems before removing the boot screen.
2. Global EventBus (SPSC/MPSC): NativeQueue-based signal bus. 0-GC
transport between isolated systems.
3. Data Archivist (MMF Codec): Read/write slot_0.sav, binary
delta packing, checksums (XXHash3), MMF chunk paging.
4. Data Monolith (Static DB): Parses balances/recipes from binary blobs into a
NativeArray at startup. No ScriptableObjects in hot paths.
5. Origin Shift (AUP Manager): 64-bit coordinate shift math (int64x3 + float3). RebaseSignal broadcast at sector boundaries.
6. Native Arena Allocator: Custom UnsafeUtility allocator for bypassing TempJob limits and memory pooling for Burst Jobs.
7. Crash Telemetry (Blackbox): Circular event buffer (32-byte structures).
Dump to .h8dump during crashes or FPS drops on a background thread.
8. Scalability Dictator (Hardware): Dynamic switching of Math LODs between MX350 and RTX based on frame profiling.
9. Platform Abstraction Layer (PAL): IInputProvider. Steam Deck broadcasting of Trackpad/gyro/gamepad inputs without API leaks in Core. 10. Tick Dispatcher & Time Dilation: Cadence control (FastTick 60Hz, SlowTick 10Hz, ColdTick 1Hz, FrostTick 0.2Hz) and Bullet Time effects.

ECHELON 2: WORLD GENERATION & TERRAIN (Topology and Environment)

11. MapMagic Runtime Bridge: 2D Heightmap reader. O(1) height access without allocations. Isolation from third-party SDKs.
12. Voxel SDF Pipeline: 3D distance fields. sbyte density for caves and arches.
13. Marching Cubes Mesher: Asynchronous voxel polygonization on worker threads. 2-frame latency hiding.
14. Voxel Carving (Deformation): Laser drilling, RLE compression of modified cells,
writing deltas to savegames.
15. BRG Scatter Director: BatchRendererGroup instancing of grass and coral. 15Hz
Frustum Culling in Compute Shader.
16. Procedural Wreckage Assembler: Modular assembly of ghost ships (wrecks) based
on WorldSeed and AUP.
17. Geological Node Spawner: Distribution of ore veins. Deterministic LCG
spawning based on a terrain mask.
18. Biome Transition Manager: Mathematical blending of colors, fog, and
biome parameters at boundaries (Dithered blending).
19. Abyssal Flow Fields: 3D vector field of underwater currents. Affects the physics of
submarines, GPU fish, and particles.
20. Thermal Vents & Geysers: Danger zones. Ejects boiling water (physical impulse +
damage) based on TriangleWave cycles.

ECHELON 3: FLORA, FAUNA & BIOTA (Ecosystem and AI)

21. Ecosystem Director (Macro): Lotka-Volterra equations. FrostTick migration
of biomass without GameObjects.
22. Fauna Spatial Hash Grid: O(1) neighbor search. Collision resolution and aggression radii in Burst.
23. Swarm Compute Director (Boids): GPU simulation of small fish schools. Swarm fragmentation
during sonic booms. 24. Predator Cognition (Utility AI): Polynomial scoring of hunger, fear, and aggression. 1km headless fast-fail.
25. Predator Steering & Lunge: Octant-aligned navigation, S-curved attacks, obstacle avoidance (SDF raycasts).
26. A Funnel Smoothing:* 3D voxel-based pathfinding with smoothed "square"
corners (String Pulling).
27. Leviathan Procedural IK: Boneless VAT body blending + burst calculation of tentacles/tails (Constrained S-Curves).
28. Flora Procedural Sway: Vertex shaders for algae animation (interactive bending from submarine propellers).
29. Bioluminescence Sync: Coral pulsation. Glow phases are tied to
global _Time and moon cycles.
30. Fauna Genetics & Mutation: 64-bit trait masks (size, aggression,
coloration) transferred when spawning in biomes.

ECHELON 4: PLAYER, KINEMATICS & TOOLS (Locomotion and Equipment)

31. Kinematic Character Controller (KCC): Asynchronous capsule casts.
Speculative collisions. Complete absence of SphereCastNonAlloc in
the main thread.
32. Hydrodynamic Drag & Buoyancy: Scalar mass and inertia calculation. force *
math.rcp(mass + addedMass).
33. Contextual Hand IK (FABRIK): Procedural sticking of player hands to ladders,
airlocks, and tools.
34. Tether & Cable Physics: Tether physics using acceleration constraints. Without Unity Joints.
35. Equipment Runtime (Tools): Cutter heating math, scanner power consumption (SOA arrays).
36. Scavenging & Harvesting: Convert procedural loot points to ItemIds upon
cutting/interaction.
37. S.O.A. Inventory System: Inventory as a NativeArray<int> (ID,
Quantity, Wear). Search via math.tzcnt.
38. Crafting Fast-Fail Validator: Bitmasks for checking the presence of recipes
for 1 CPU cycle before the discard cycle.
39. VR Somatic Comfort: OpenXR bridge. Virtual horizon locking,
FOV tunneling during jerks, Foveated Rendering.

40. VR Interaction Bridge: Physically grabbing objects with your hands, translating
controllers into the 3D world.

ECHELON 5: COMBAT & SURVIVAL PHYSIOLOGY (Combat and Body)

41. Combat Damage Router: NativeQueue for ForcePackets. Damage distribution to
modules, players, or creatures in a single pass.
42. Armor Penetration LUT: 8x8 penetration tables. Hitboxes based on local
AABB primitives (Headshot multipliers).
43. Status Effects Engine: 32-bit masks. Bleeding, poison, and stun processing
in the SlowTick job.
44. Player Stress & Fear System: Panic buildup from darkness and sounds. Affects
O2 consumption and distortion shaders.
45. Decompression Sickness (Bends): Calculates tissue nitrogen saturation. Instantaneous
damage when ascending > 10 m/s from a depth of > 100 m.
46. Hypoxia & Gas Toxicity: Nitrogen intoxication (introduces noise into controls),
CO2 poisoning (stamina penalty).
47. Crush Depth Integrity: Pressure calculation. Exponential suit destruction
overDepth * math.rsqrt(overDepth).
48. Diet & Metabolism: Hunger, hydration, core body temperature. Affects
regeneration rate.
49. Radiation Scrubber: Contaminated areas from reactors. Rad dose accumulation,
hand texture mutation.
50. Screen-Space Wounds & Decals: Rendering visor cracks and blood through decals/postprocess (Math LODs).

53. Fluid Incursion (Flooding): BFS water distribution between compartments. The mass of

55. Pipe & Sump Pump Logistics: Liquid and oxygen transfer system.

56. Module Deconstruction: Mathematically net return of 50% of resources with a rollback

of the connection graph.
57. Submarine OS (Core): Submarine terminal. Engine temperature, sonar

telemetry, battery consumption.
58. Submarine Navigation (Auto-Level): Physical pitch and roll stabilizers

of the vessel. Buoyancy control (Ballast).
59. Drone Fleet Commander: AI for repair and mining mini-bots.
Stateless solutions based on distances.
60. Scooter (Seaglide) Kinematics: Additional thrust vectors and physics for a manual

submarine tug.
