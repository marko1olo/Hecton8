# HECTON-8 Website Publication Map

Status: working plan.
Purpose: prepare lore for public website, press, wiki, and long-form articles without spoiling the core mystery too early.

## Public Site Pillars

Humanity In 2190:
Shows sparse mature frontier. Safe hook: humanity reached the stars but distance still wins.

Aegir System:
Shows gas giant, remote claim, specialist-known disaster. Avoids Atlas truth and final-zone details.

Moon System:
Shows Aegir as a real operational system with several moons, transfer windows, relays, and HECTON-8 as one dangerous body among many.

HECTON-8:
Shows ocean moon, dead colony, salvage contract, pressure horror. Avoids Deep Reach crime specifics.

Marauders:
Shows player class and salvage culture. Good for marketing because it explains who the player is.

Barnard Yards / Player Origin:
Shows the player as a product of salvage culture, not a random Earth tourist.

Deep Reach:
Shows public corporation profile. Avoids direct confession.

Ships And Travel:
Shows no-FTL hard sci-fi flavor. Good for technical credibility.

Black Keel:
Shows automated salvage carrier / claim-pool pressure. Avoids hidden Deep Reach hooks before release.

## Spoiler-Safe Rules

Do not reveal:

- Atlas-6's damaged classification truth.
- Full Deep Reach evacuation crime.
- Xenon-Omega internal function beyond vague pressure material.
- Final bottom-zone fusion.
- Ending questions.

Allowed before release:

- no FTL;
- remote claim;
- dead colony;
- salvage carrier;
- pressure resource rumors;
- Deep Reach liability smell;
- Marauder profession;
- Aegir as specialist-known claim.
