# HECTON-8 Codex Delivery Map

Status: working delivery plan.
Purpose: decide which lore appears in-game, where it unlocks, and what stays website/writer-only.

## Starting Codex

Unlocked before or immediately after arrival:

- Marauders.
- Player Origin - Barnard Yards.
- Ship Classes.
- Interstellar Travel basics.
- Aegir System public summary.
- Aegir Astronomy Reference public-safe layer.
- Six-domain ledger public-safe layer.
- Aegir host-star light / darkness rule.
- Aegir Moon System public summary.
- Aegir Moon Ladder public-safe layer.
- HECTON-8 public summary.
- Player contract summary.

Tone:
Useful, not explanatory lecture. The player should know enough to work, not enough to understand the crime.

## Early Game Unlocks

Unlocked through shallows, first wrecks, first terminals:

- Salvage Economy.
- Salvage Carrier.
- Black Keel public ownership layer.
- Black Keel custody / claim-pool debt layer.
- Drop capsule damage audit.
- Return vector windows: the carrier can answer only through Aegir timing, weather, and relay geometry.
- Corporate Claims.
- Dead / Cold Claims.
- Aegir Route basics.
- Deep Reach public profile.
- HECTON-8 Geology And Resources basic layer.
- HECTON-8 Drowned Geology route-reading layer.
- HECTON-8 Resource Catalog basic layer.
- HECTON-8 Resource Stack layer.
- HECTON-8 Flora/Fauna shallow ecology layer.
- HECTON-8 Ecology Registry native/adapted/Atlas-altered category layer.
- HECTON-8 Depth Bands, shallow/early version.
- Photic shelf life as the baseline contrast sample.
- Pressure ladder, shallow version: why depth progress is engineering, not courage.
- Worker locker row as the first named human workplace clue.
- Pressure bunk routine as the first proof of ordinary life under pressure.

Tone:
Practical. The player learns why this place is a claim, why it pays, and why the contract is ugly.

## Midgame Unlocks

Unlocked through abandoned modules and deeper resource infrastructure:

- Relay Spine.
- Xenon-Omega public / container records.
- Blue debt / pressure glass slang as Marauder field translation.
- Blue debt containment behavior after first serious sample.
- Cable reef symbiosis as the first useful/horrible ocean-industry merge.
- Seed Program public layer.
- Atlas-6 public industrial layer.
- Named Domains 2190 partial map.
- No-FTL transit math as practical route pressure.
- Relay custody debt chain after first Black Keel debt proof.
- Coward exit chain after the first real ascent repair route: true partial exit, incomplete result.

Tone:
The setting widens. The player sees HECTON-8 as one node in an economy, not an isolated haunted moon.

## Deep Game Unlocks

Unlocked through high-pressure zones, Atlas contamination, sealed Deep Reach records:

- Deep Reach Internal.
- Deep Reach Liability Doctrine.
- Deep Reach return claim / current asset-recovery pressure.
- Seed Ship doctrine / autonomous infrastructure method.
- Xenon-Omega internal model.
- Atlas-6 damaged classification.
- Atlas-6 original directive stack.
- Deep Reach 2147 cover story.
- HECTON-8 geology/resource spoiler layers.
- HECTON-8 Atlas-altered resource and ecology layers.
- HECTON-8 Depth Bands, deep version.
- Abyssal repair fauna as proof that Atlas edits survival, not only kills.
- Factory-temple threshold as the bottom-zone spatial classification.
- Aegir Route priority overrides.
- Black Keel hidden ownership/control hooks.
- Player motive Barnard link.
- Professional motive shift: contract work becoming personal evidence.
- Shift-board route holds as proof that evacuation became scheduling.
- Medical lock delay as proof that quarantine/liability language blocked help.
- Black-box name stack as the physical payload for names, broadcast, dossiers, and ending pressure.
- False ending warning records.
- Material exit warning: real payout, incomplete truth.
- Material payout ledger: successful blue debt certification does not equal truth completion.
- Deep Reach cleanse order: present-tense proof that the company wants resource custody, Atlas access, and evidence deletion.
- Atlas final argument: the bottom-zone question about preservation, severance, sale, exposure, or quarantine.

Tone:
Evidence. Not myth. The horror is that the paperwork makes sense.

## Ending / Post-Ending Unlocks
