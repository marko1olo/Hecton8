## CHAPTER 1: SPACE (FRONTIER LOGISTICS AND LAW)

### [Encyclopedia Entry] Barnard Yards
*Interstellar Industry Catalog, 2189*
Barnard Yards is less of a star system and more of a graveyard of ambitions turned into an industrial hub. It is from here that "black" salvage expeditions launch. The culture of the Yards is built on worn-out pressure hardware and debt. The local marauders do not consider themselves pirates; they are salvage engineers working contracts that legal Tau Ceti crews would consider suicide. Life at Barnard Yards teaches one rule: if a contract looks clean, it means the corporation just hid the body count.

### [Technical Manifest] The "Black Keel" Tender
*Excerpt from Aegir Reclamation Pool Registry*
The "Black Keel" is not a rescue ship. It is an automated claim-tender, jointly owned by the Aegir salvage pool and Keelmark Mutual insurance. Its job is to hang in high orbit over HECTON-8, catch rare acoustic communication windows through the storms, and only drop the cable if the marauders transmit proof of Xenon-Omega substrate extraction. The "Black Keel" has no facilities for immediate rescue. It is a machine that collects debts, not people.

### [Engineer's Manual] High Capacity Tank Module (SuitUpgrades.HighCapacityTank)
*Excerpt from field manual*
Attention greenhorns. The auxiliary O2 reservoir (+4 to max capacity) DOES NOT make your suit more resistant to pressure. It is not a "deep-sea tank." It won't save you from a hull implosion if you wander below 6500 meters without a DepthModuleMk1. All this tank gives you is four extra minutes to realize you're lost in the dark before you suffocate from excess carbon dioxide. Don't confuse air supply with armor.

## CHAPTER 3: SURVIVAL (DEBT AND DEATH)

### [Marauder Note] The Ocean's Audit
*Scratched on the lid of a broken P-63 fabricator*
The corpos teach workers to ask permission from the systems that are killing them. "Await evacuation certification." We marauders learn to ask what exactly the system is trying to hide. If a room is locked from the inside and the log says "atmospheric anomaly"—it means they skimped on filters and vented the air so they wouldn't have to pay severance. Never trust polished screens. Only trust analog gauges. The ocean doesn't forgive lies. The ocean audits every number.

### [Official Memo] Keelmark Loss Desk Policy
*Author: Marek Ibarra, Insurance Adjuster*
Regarding the Great Tide of 2147: we do not have the right to register dead workers as "casualties" without the physical presence of bodies. As long as the bodies remain under the rubble of Sector 44, these 843 people are listed as "Unresolved System Load." Life insurance payouts are voided. If arriving marauders find the corpses and attempt to upload their ID tags to orbit, immediately activate the "Sato-Ren" filters and jam the acoustic communication channel. The water owns these bodies until we say otherwise.

## CHAPTER 4: WATER (ABYSSAL CHEMISTRY)

### [Bioscanner Log] Bloom Collapse Invasion
*Summary of observations from Sectors 11-30*
The ecological collapse on HECTON-8 didn't start with giant leviathans. It started with chemistry. In the flooded factory sectors, toxic algae bloomed, feeding off coolant leaks. Oxygen pockets thinned. Predators stopped hunting in open water and started ambushing near ventilation pipes that create artificial currents. In sectors below 1500 meters, the algae looks like a black film devouring the light from suit lamps. If the scanner doesn't pick up the clicks of small fauna—it means there is no oxygen left here. You're breathing on borrowed time.
