# Humanity 2190 - Game Texture

Status: working world texture.
Purpose: show how the broader human setting appears inside HECTON-8 without turning the game into a political encyclopedia.

## Core Feeling

Humanity is interstellar, but distance still wins.

The player should feel that there are old worlds, other domains, shipyards, routes, courts, claims, schools, and industries behind every object. But none of that arrives in time to open a flooded door.

The useful texture:

- old standards on new disasters;
- domain marks on tools and cargo;
- delayed packets instead of live help;
- jobs that outlive workers;
- machines continuing a contract after the people are dead.

## What The Player Actually Sees

Tool Stamps:
Barnard Yards marks on wrenches, suit clamps, pump housings, pressure doors, and field repairs.

Route Labels:
Luyten Junction packet codes, beam schedule numbers, relay stamps, cargo lane shorthand.

Old Standards:
Sol Core safety plates, pressure-room signage, emergency symbols, medical warning format.

Public Cleanliness:
Tau Ceti-style public audit excerpts or civic condemnation records late in the evidence chain.

Old Hardware:
Centauri Compact manuals, old compatible parts, inherited connector standards, battered but trusted ship components.

Aegir Local Crud:
Deep Reach serials, algae-eaten claim labels, old storm ratings, redacted colony maps, damaged route buoys.

## Domain Texture

Sol Core:
Appears as old labels, law language, safety standards, financial backers, and delayed legitimacy. It should feel powerful but absent.

Centauri Compact:
Appears through older robust hardware and old domain confidence. It knows how to build things that last, but it is not emotionally central to HECTON-8.

Barnard Yards:
Appears through repair culture, salvage slang, tool marks, rough interfaces, debt habits, and the player's personal recognition. This is the most important offworld texture for the player.

Tau Ceti League:
Appears as moral/public pressure if evidence escapes. Good for later articles, public hearings, or post-ending reactions, not early gameplay.

Luyten Junction:
Appears as relay infrastructure, route packets, beam schedules, transfer IDs, cargo staging, and cold machine order.

Aegir Claim:
Appears as weathered Deep Reach hardware, abandoned colony rooms, broken system carriers, old buoys, pressure mining, and people who were too far from help.

## Human Life Texture

People in 2190 are not all sleek future citizens.

Frontier workers:
Patch suits, trade oxygen, reuse old tool families, know contract debt, and judge technology by how it fails under pressure.

Corporate staff:
Trust systems until systems route them into a locked room.

Marauders:
Live by salvage law, rumor, debt, route timing, and bad maps. They are not pirates by default; they are professionals around dead claims.

Domain citizens:
May know HECTON-8 as a distant historical accident or not at all.

Deep Reach operators:
Think in process, asset continuity, recoverable value, exposure control, and delayed accountability.

## In-Game Delivery

Use the broader setting through small repeated objects:

- cargo tags;
- suit patches;
- tool marks;
- route packets;
- old safety icons;
- dialect in field notes;
- incompatible connector standards;
- delayed messages;
- names on manifests;
- stale public articles cached in colony terminals.

Do not stop the game for a history lecture. Let the setting leak through objects.

## Example Object Hooks

Barnard Pressure Wrench:
Scanner: Barnard Yards tool stamp. Heavy jaw, old alloy, repaired twice before it reached HECTON-8.

Luyten Packet Buffer:
Scanner: Relay buffer with stale packet routes. Last clean outbound path predates the colony death record.

Sol Safety Plate:
Scanner: Standard pressure-room warning. Compliant sign, non-compliant room.

Tau Ceti Archive Excerpt:
Terminal: Public inquiry request denied pending remote certification.

Centauri Connector:
Scanner: Old Compact connector standard. Still used because it survives salt better than newer parts.

## Writer Rule

Every offworld reference should do at least one job:

- explain how the object got here;
- show the player is from a bigger world;
- make distance painful;
- tie the player to Barnard/frontier labor;
- make Deep Reach feel old and systemic;
- make HECTON-8 feel like a wound inside a functioning civilization.
