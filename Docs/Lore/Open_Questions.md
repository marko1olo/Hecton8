# HECTON-8 Open Narrative Questions

Status: active user-control list.
Purpose: keep unresolved choices visible instead of burying them in prose.

## Aegir System

- Do we lock Epsilon Eridani / Ran as the final real astronomical anchor?
- If yes, is Aegir exactly eps Eri b, or a lightly fictionalized giant in the same system?
- How much real star data should appear in player-facing text versus internal lore only?
- Working lock: Aegir has multiple major moons and smaller moonlets. HECTON-8 is one of many, not innermost and not farthest.
- Working moon roster exists in `Encyclopedia/Aegir_Moon_System.md`, but moon names remain renameable.
- Does HECTON-8 publicly have a catalog number/name separate from the Marauder/Deep Reach name?

## Human Expansion

- Aegir is not the first extrasolar domain. How many major human domains exist by 2190?
- Which domains are public/civil, and which are corporate or military-industrial?
- What were the earlier famous systems before Aegir?
- Are there true independent extrasolar settlements, or mostly corporate/relay/industrial footholds?
- Is Sol still politically dominant, or is it mostly a distant legal myth for frontier crews?
- Did Deep Reach launch the Aegir/Atlas program from Sol directly, or from an already-established inner relay domain closer to Aegir?
- What does a normal citizen know about Aegir before the HECTON-8 story?
- Are Marauders legal private contractors, tolerated criminals, or a jurisdictional loophole?
- Does interstellar law recognize salvage truth as evidence, or only as claim material?

## Scale Choice

Current preferred model: sparse mature frontier.

Locked direction:

- Name 4-6 major domains.
- Keep smaller systems implied.
- Current major nodes: Sol Core, Centauri Compact, Barnard Yards, Tau Ceti League, Luyten Junction, Aegir Claim.
- Player knows the claim-chain and salvage map better than high politics.

Still unresolved:

- Do any of these domain names need replacement for tone?
- Which domain is Deep Reach legally headquartered in by 2190?
- Which domain controls the strongest court/insurance authority?

## Ship History

  - beam-assisted sail / microwave sail;

  - pellet-beam assisted fusion;
  - hybrid: probes use beam sails, heavy ships use fusion + external staging.
- How public was Atlas-6 before the disaster?
- Was Atlas-6 insured, classified, or legally treated as a colonial authority?

## Player

- Does the player have any direct past with Deep Reach, or only a professional salvage history that becomes personal?
- Did the player choose this contract freely, or were they forced by debt/blacklist/legal pressure?
- Does the player know anyone connected to HECTON-8 before arrival?
- Should multiple origin variants exist, or should we keep one strong canonical protagonist?

## Deep Reach Crime

- Working lock: Deep Reach's post-2147 lie combines geotechnical cascade, Great Tide damage, signal loss, biological/pressure quarantine, corrupted Atlas logs, and evacuation authorization failure.
- Who inside Deep Reach knew the true cause?
- Is there a living Deep Reach faction trying to bury the truth, or only old systems and present-day legal machines?

## Strategic Resource

Locked direction:

- Keep `Xenon-Omega` as Deep Reach corporate codename.
- It is not a literal simple isotope.
- It is a pressure-grown material/process family: xenon-rich clathrate/defect lattices plus HECTON-8 brine, minerals, biology, industrial catalysts, and Atlas processing.
- It matters for extreme computation, high-energy containment, and Atlas-compatible infrastructure.

Still unresolved:

- Working slang lock: Marauders call Xenon-Omega `blue debt`.
- Working technical slang: `pressure glass`.
- Working Deep Reach sanitized term: `XO continuity substrate`.
- How unstable is it after depressurization?

## Atlas-6

- Working lock: public directive was habitat continuity and worker safety; real weighted directive favored claim continuity, Xenon-Omega process integrity, and Atlas/Seed infrastructure above biological workforce when categories conflicted.
- How much agency does Atlas still have by 2190?
- Can Atlas understand the player as a person by the final depth, or only as a repair/material anomaly?
- Is shutting Atlas down mercy, murder, liberation, or corporate theft?

## Escape And False Endings

- Can the player leave before reaching Atlas-6?
- If early escape exists, is it:
  - real ending with bitter credits;
  - partial extraction then return to same seed;
  - coward route that pays but stains the dossier;
  - corporate capture ending;
  - salvage-success / truth-failure ending?
- What makes the early exit emotionally unsatisfying enough that returning feels natural?
- What must the player physically build to escape?

## Final Question

- What should the final decision ask at emotional level?
- Candidate:
  - Do you sell a crime scene back to its maker?
  - Do you preserve a broken guardian that no longer understands humans?
  - Do you expose the truth and lose control of what others do with it?
- Should the best ending be morally clean? Current bias: no.
