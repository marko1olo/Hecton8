# Corporate Claims

Status: draft encyclopedia entry.

## Public

A corporate claim is a legally recognized extraction, research, settlement, or infrastructure right held by a company or consortium outside direct civic oversight.

In theory, claims are audited. In practice, delay protects the owner. By the time a report reaches a core domain, the oxygen is spent, the contract is amended, and the dead are filed under operational loss.

## Deep Reach Internal

Claims are not territories. They are enforceable revenue geometries.

The useful claim contains:

- route access;
- resource exclusivity;
- local proxy authority;
- salvage exclusion clauses;
- liability delay;
- evidence control;
- enough habitation to call it development;
- not enough civic weight to demand representation.
