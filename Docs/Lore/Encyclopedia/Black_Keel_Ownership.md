## Public

Black Keel is registered as an Aegir claim-pool automated tender.

That means it is not the player's private ship and not officially a Deep Reach vessel. It is leased through salvage contract infrastructure: route slots, payload certification, insurance custody, debt handling, and recovery windows.

## Marauder Field Note

Claim-pool means nobody owns it until something valuable is aboard.

Then everybody owns it except you.

## Deep Reach Internal

Black Keel is acceptable because ownership and control are separable.

Control surfaces:

- route certification;
- payload priority;
- quarantine authorization;
- post-recovery billing;
- insurance custody;
- command packet precedence;
- claim continuity clauses.

Direct ownership is unnecessary when the recovery logic already preserves asset continuity.

## Working Truth

Hybrid model:

- public owner: Aegir claim-pool / local salvage infrastructure;
- legal state: debt-impounded tender operating under insurance custody;
- hidden control: Deep Reach priority hooks in payload recovery, route certificates, quarantine, and old claim law;
- player relation: independent contractor with no clean ownership rights.

## Writer Notes

This keeps all useful pressures:

- player is still a Marauder, not a direct Deep Reach employee;
- Black Keel can be useful without being friendly;
- Deep Reach can betray or pressure the player without a cartoon reveal;
- false endings can expose that "neutral recovery" was never neutral.
