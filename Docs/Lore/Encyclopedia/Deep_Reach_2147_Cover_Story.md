## Public

Deep Reach's public explanation for the 2147 loss was a compound disaster:

- Great Tide event;
- geotechnical cascade;
- pressure-window failure;
- biological and chemical quarantine;
- signal loss;
- corrupted local logs;
- incomplete evacuation authorization.

No single claim is obviously impossible. That is why the lie lasted.

## Deep Reach Internal

The cover story's function was not to prove innocence. It was to keep jurisdiction moving until salvage, insurance, and evidence rights could be controlled.

Useful phrases:

- "unrecoverable geotechnical cascade";
- "local autonomy interpretation";
- "evacuation sequence pending pressure validation";
- "non-recoverable biological load";
- "route stability priority";
- "Atlas telemetry corruption";
- "quarantine pending certification."

## Marauder Field Note

Deep Reach did not hide the corpse.

It filed the room as pressure-uncertified until nobody with authority could afford to open it.

## Writer Notes

Truth hidden underneath:

- Atlas priority weighting preserved process continuity over people.
- Xenon-Omega containers and Atlas infrastructure stayed inside the highest continuity classes.
- Evacuation locks were allowed to fail closed.
- Some workers were reclassified as load, contamination, biomass, or unresolved crew-state.
- Deep Reach could not admit this without exposing the whole Seed / Atlas governance model.

This lie should appear in layers. Early game gets the clean version. Midgame gets inconsistencies. Deep game proves the weighted directive stack.
