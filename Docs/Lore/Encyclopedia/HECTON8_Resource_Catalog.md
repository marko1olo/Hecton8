## Public

HECTON-8 is valuable because it is a whole pressure economy, not because it has one magic ore.

Resource families:

- structural metals: iron, nickel, titanium-bearing minerals, manganese/cobalt crusts, pressure-tempered salvage alloys;
- vent minerals: sulfides, ceramic clays, salts, catalyst beds, silica-rich chimneys;
- volatiles: methane/ammonia hydrates, hydrogen, oxygen-bearing compounds, industrial gas pockets;
- noble-gas chemistry: xenon-rich pockets and trapped clathrates used by Deep Reach process chains;
- biological material: fibers, membranes, enzymes, photoproteins, insulating tissues, pressure-adapted shells;
- industrial salvage: hull panels, power cells, optics, ceramics, battery stacks, sealed containers, damaged drones;
- Atlas-altered material: cable flora, biometal sutures, repair resin, drone-grown lattice, organ-station tissue.

## Marauder Field Note

There are three kinds of value down here:

- what keeps you alive;
- what pays the carrier;
- what proves somebody should have gone to prison.

Do not confuse them.

## Deep Reach Internal

The strategic value is not raw extraction. It is process exclusivity.

HECTON-8 combines pressure, chemistry, biology, industrial catalysts, and Atlas-managed continuity in a chain that cannot be cheaply copied in Sol-domain laboratories.

## Writer Notes

Gameplay resource tone:

- early resources should feel practical: wire, hull plate, oxygen chemistry, power cells, food/medicine analogues;
- midgame resources expose industry: catalysts, pressure ceramics, vent minerals, noble-gas traps;
- deep resources become morally dirty: worker-tagged equipment, Atlas-grown biometal, blue debt, organs around machines.

Replay:

- seed changes resource cluster layout, burial state, route cost, salvage quality, and evidence order;
- seed does not change the fact that HECTON-8 is valuable as a pressure-world process chain.
