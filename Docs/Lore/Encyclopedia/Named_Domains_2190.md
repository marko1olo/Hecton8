# Named Human Domains - 2190

Status: locked working set.

## Overview

The setting names six major nodes and implies many smaller claims, depots, habitats, and failed projects.

This keeps the lore large but controlled.

## Sol Core

Earth, Luna, Mars, belt infrastructure, old law, old finance, old institutions.

Sol Core is not irrelevant. It remains the symbolic center of human legitimacy. It is also physically too far away to save frontier workers in real time.

## Centauri Compact

The earliest major extrasolar domain and the proof that human settlement beyond Sol could become ordinary.

It has old infrastructure, political weight, and a habit of treating later frontier systems as messy younger relatives.

## Barnard Yards

Industrial domain, shipbreaking culture, pressure-hardware workshops, salvage brokers, rented hulls, and workers who know how old machines fail.

Working player origin: Barnard Yards or a connected frontier salvage belt.

This makes the player a product of the salvage economy, not an Earth tourist.

## Tau Ceti League

Older civilian domain with stronger civic law, public institutions, and a cleaner self-image.

It is useful as contrast: the kind of domain that can condemn Deep Reach after evidence emerges, but rarely arrives before the damage is done.

## Luyten Junction

Relay/depot/beam infrastructure node. More route machine than society.

It handles transfers, packet buffering, route insurance, beam scheduling, cargo staging, and maintenance claims.

## Aegir Claim

Late Deep Reach corporate claim around the gas giant Aegir and HECTON-8.

Known to specialists, insurers, route authorities, Marauders, and corporations. To ordinary citizens, it is a distant old accident, if they know it at all.

## Writer Notes

Do not overfill the map with names yet. Smaller systems should appear as fragments: claim numbers, route stops, failed depots, insurance zones, and salvage slang.
