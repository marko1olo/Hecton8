# Technology Overview - 2190

Status: draft reference atlas.

## Baseline

Technology in 2190 is advanced but still physical. Mass, heat, pressure, radiation, time, and maintenance remain decisive.

No FTL. No ansible. No reactionless drives. No clean rescue button.

## Spaceflight

Interstellar:

- beam-sail and microwave-sail probes;
- fusion or fusion-pulse heavy ships;
- pellet-beam or external-infrastructure assisted craft;
- sleeper / torpor transports;
- slow cargo carriers;
- magsail, aerobrake, or staged braking concepts.

In-system:

- system carriers;
- transfer tugs;
- orbital depots;
- drop capsules;
- salvage pods;
- autonomous maintenance drones.

## Human Survival

Long-duration travel uses shielding, rotating sections, torpor medicine, strict atmosphere control, recycling, and legal custody systems. Comfort depends on class and contract.

On HECTON-8, survival tech becomes more immediate:

- pressure suits;
- scrubbers;
- thermal management;
- ballast and buoyancy systems;
- analog gauges;
- sonar and acoustic mapping;
- pressure-rated tools;
- repair drones;
- emergency uplink buoys.

## Computing And AI

AI is powerful but bounded by authority, training data, maintenance, and directives.

Atlas-6 is not magic intelligence. It is an autonomous industrial system whose damaged classification and repair logic became catastrophic under pressure, isolation, and Deep Reach priorities.

## Writer Notes

The tech should feel repairable, heavy, expensive, and liable to kill the user when neglected. Avoid sleek miracle devices. The best HECTON-8 technology has bolts, seals, heat, noise, condensation, scratched labels, and fallback procedures.
