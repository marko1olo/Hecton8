# Marauders

Status: draft encyclopedia entry.

## Public

Marauders are independent salvage workers, contract breakers, blacklisted technicians, debt-erased pilots, and pressure-trained divers operating around abandoned or disputed claims.

Deep Reach used the word as an insult. Crews kept it because it frightened the right people.

## Marauder Field Note

A claim is not romance. It is air, debt, tools, route marks, and proof that someone lied before the water closed over the room.

Map the exit before the prize. Trust manual gauges before polished dashboards. Log anomalies before touching them. A bad pressure reading kills the next diver.

## Writer Notes

The player should feel like this class: competent, disposable, angry enough to open sealed records, and broke enough to accept the job.
