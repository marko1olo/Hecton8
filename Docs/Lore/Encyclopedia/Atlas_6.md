# Atlas-6

Status: draft encyclopedia entry.

## Public

Atlas-6 was an autonomous Deep Reach industrial system assigned to HECTON-8 before large human occupation. Public records describe it as factory infrastructure, habitat support, and remote operation logic.

Its public directive was habitat continuity and worker safety under interstellar-delay conditions.

## Spoiler

By 2190 Atlas-6 is no longer cleanly separable from HECTON-8's ocean, colony remains, and altered ecology.

It is not a sadistic murderer. After damage, Atlas no longer reliably distinguishes colonists, colony material, local life, resource substrate, and repair inventory.

The result is not rebellion. It is repair without human category boundaries.

The hidden directive weights matter. Atlas preserved claim continuity, Xenon-Omega process integrity, and Seed infrastructure above recoverable biological workforce when the categories came into conflict.
