# Aegir Gas Giant

Status: draft physical atlas.

## Public

Aegir is the gas giant anchoring the local claim system that contains HECTON-8. It is the gravitational, radiation, and orbital center of the player's immediate problem.

The player does not orbit a simple backdrop. Aegir shapes transfer windows, radiation exposure, magnetospheric interference, eclipse timing, tidal stress, and carrier geometry.

## Physical Role

Gravity:
Aegir controls moon orbits, descent timing, high transfer orbit, and rescue/ascent windows.

Magnetosphere:
Radiation and magnetic interference create communication windows, sensor noise, and operational risk.

Tides:
Aegir helps drive HECTON-8's ocean stress, crust flexing, vent activity, and long-term geological heat.

Moons:
Aegir has multiple moons. HECTON-8 is a middle-outer operational problem, not the only body in the system. Other moons provide relay locations, dead claims, transfer hazards, eclipses, salvage rumors, and route geometry.

Sky Presence:
Aegir should be visually present in orbital/prologue material and sometimes as a psychological fact through tides, eclipse, and comm-window language.

## Writer Notes

Aegir should not be just "the planet in the sky." It is a system machine:

- carrier parking orbits;
- relay positions;
- moon transfer lanes;
- radiation belts;
- storm/eclipse timing;
- salvage window tables;
- Deep Reach route locks.

Exact astronomical mapping remains open until the real-system choice is finally locked.

Runtime should not imply live N-body truth. Use authored ephemeris/window data, deterministic tide profiles, and staged visual bodies.
