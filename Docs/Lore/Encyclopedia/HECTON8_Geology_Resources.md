# HECTON-8 Geology And Resources

Status: draft physical/resource atlas.

## Core Premise

HECTON-8 is not valuable because it is comfortable. It is valuable because pressure, ocean chemistry, tidal heat, geology, biology, and Deep Reach industry create materials and processes that are difficult or impossible to reproduce cheaply elsewhere.

## Moon Structure

Working model:

- global ocean;
- storm-exposed surface and shallows;
- thick pressure layers;
- active seafloor geology;
- tidal flexing from Aegir;
- hydrothermal and cryochemical zones;
- fracture networks;
- industrial ruins embedded into natural vents and ridges.

## Depth Bands

Shallows:
Light, biological color, first salvage, reefs, kelp analogues, exposed wreck fragments, storm damage.

Mid-depth:
Drowned modules, cable runs, mining anchors, pipeline forests, colony infrastructure, early Atlas repair scars.

Deep Abyss:
Low light, pressure hazards, brine basins, silt plains, collapsed structures, predator routes, unstable wreck geology.

Thermal / Tectonic Fields:
Vents, mineral chimneys, heat gradients, chemical blooms, pressure-grown lattices, altered fauna.

Atlas Bottom Zone:
Factory-ship remains, living tissue intrusion, industrial organs, processed geology, Deep Reach hardware fused with ocean systems.

## Resource Families

Metals and structural feedstock:
Nickel, iron, titanium analogues, pressure-tempered alloys, salvage-grade hull materials.

Volatiles and atmosphere chemistry:
Oxygen sources, hydrogen, methane/ammonia pockets if approved, industrial gases, scrubber materials.

Industrial minerals:
Ceramics, catalyst clays, sulfides, salts, superconducting or pressure-stable feedstocks if approved.

Biological materials:
Membranes, enzymes, insulating tissues, pressure-grown fibers, bioluminescent compounds.

Strategic pressure resources:
`Xenon-Omega` is locked as Deep Reach's high-value pressure-process codename. Marauder slang: `blue debt`. Technical field slang: `pressure glass`. It is the apex of the resource chain, not the only resource on the moon.

Atlas-altered materials:
Cable flora, biometal sutures, repair resin, drone-grown lattice, organ-station tissue, and machine-biological composites that become more common with depth.

## Replayable Geology

Seed variation should affect:

- ridge and trench layouts;
- vent field positions;
- resource cluster distribution;
- wreck-module burial state;
- cave/fracture routes;
- brine basin hazards;
- artifact/evidence location order.

Seed variation should not change:

- HECTON-8's identity as Aegir ocean moon;
- Atlas-6 bottom-zone truth;
- Deep Reach responsibility;
- the broad depth-band logic.

## Writer Notes

Avoid one magic ore. The moon should feel like a whole pressure economy. Deep Reach did not come for a shiny rock; it came for a process chain it could own.
