## Public

The player's carrier is the `Black Keel`: an automated claim-tender / salvage carrier operating under contract, debt, and claim-pool logic.

Publicly, it belongs to an Aegir claim-pool. In practice, it is a debt-impounded tender under insurance custody with Deep Reach priority hooks buried in payload, route, quarantine, and old claim-law layers.

It does not sit safely over HECTON-8. It holds around Aegir or a high transfer orbit, bound by orbital geometry, radiation windows, relay priority, and contract locks.

The player reaches HECTON-8 by bathy-drop capsule. That descent package is damaged on arrival. Leaving becomes an engineering chain.

## Marauder Field Note

A carrier is a landlord with engines.

It keeps your tools, tracks your debt, logs your oxygen claims, and reminds you that extraction is a service tier. If it says it is "standing by," ask what window, what orbit, and whose authorization.

Black Keel is not your ship. It is the thing that brought your debt close enough to drown.

## Deep Reach Internal

Carrier autonomy reduces live crew exposure, insurance ambiguity, and direct liability.

Recovery behavior must prioritize certified payload, contract asset continuity, and route security before non-certified biological recovery.

Black Keel-class tender behavior is acceptable when biological recovery remains conditional on certified payload continuity, route clearance, and post-quarantine billing.

## Writer Notes

The carrier should create pressure without becoming a friendly hub.

It can provide:

- rare comm windows;
- contract reminders;
- orbital timing;
- recovery threat/promise;
- delayed Deep Reach packets;
- evidence upload points;
- early false-ending structure.

It should not provide:

- instant rescue;
- comfortable NPC crew;
- a safe social hub;
- free repairs that erase survival.
