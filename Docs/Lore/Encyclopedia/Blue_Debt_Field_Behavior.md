Public wording stays sterile: `extreme-environment process substrate`, `XO continuity substrate`, or `pressure-derived lattice`.

## Marauder Field Note

Blue debt has three prices:

- the pressure bottle;
- the thing that hears it;
- the name attached to the sample.

If a sample is easy to carry, it is either worthless or bait.

## Deep Reach Internal

Field samples must remain in pressure-rated containment. Depressurization causes lattice collapse, spectral drift, and loss of continuity value.

High-quality samples may retain pressure-harmonic behavior compatible with Atlas classification and repair substrate logic.

## Working Truth

Blue debt is not a magic artifact and not a horror virus.

It has three practical behaviors:

1. Pressure dependence:
   - valuable samples need pressure-rated containment;
   - bad storage ruins the material or makes it useful only as evidence.
2. Atlas signal behavior:
   - stable samples can carry or reflect a weak pressure-harmonic signature;
   - Atlas/drones may classify the carrier as repair inventory, stolen process, or route anomaly.
3. Chemical/ecological contamination:
   - samples may carry brine catalysts, biofilm, enzymes, or repair resin;
   - this is industrial/ecological contamination, not supernatural infection.

## Gameplay And Ending Use

Blue debt should make greed physically interesting:

- better sample means heavier/bulkier pressure containment;
- containment can change scanner noise, drone attention, or fauna behavior;
- a false material ending can pay because the sample is real;
- the same ending can be ugly because the sample lets Deep Reach recover Atlas process data.
