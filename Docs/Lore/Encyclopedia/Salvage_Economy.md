# Salvage Economy

Status: draft encyclopedia entry.

## Public

The salvage economy grew around failed claims, abandoned stations, lost cargo, dead habitats, and corporate assets too dangerous or embarrassing for official recovery.

Marauders are one visible part of it. So are claim brokers, route insurers, pressure-rated tool lenders, data buyers, legal cleaners, corpse accountants, and shell carriers.

## Marauder Field Note

Salvage pays because somebody else lied.

The object is never just the object. A power cell is route time. A pressure door is proof. A body is liability. A terminal log is leverage. A sealed crate is only valuable until you learn why it was sealed.

## Writer Notes

This is why the player exists. They are not a random action hero. They are a working specialist in a frontier economy where abandoned infrastructure becomes a profession.

The economy should feel practical and ugly:

- rented tools;
- debt-weighted contracts;
- claim shares;
- salvage bounties;
- forbidden evidence markets;
- payout penalties for damaged assets;
- bonuses for data that never becomes public.
