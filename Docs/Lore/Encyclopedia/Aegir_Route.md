# Aegir Route

Status: draft encyclopedia entry.

## Public

The Aegir route is the chain of launch records, relay windows, depot claims, navigation data, and local carrier operations that make work around Aegir possible.

It is not a clean road. It is a historical accumulation of probes, failed burns, corporate purchases, insurance revisions, and quiet corrections after bad arrivals.

Inside Aegir, route control also means moon-system control: relay moonlets, transfer lanes, radiation windows, eclipses, and Black Keel-class carrier scheduling.

## Deep Reach Internal

Route control determines claim control.

Deep Reach did not need to own every ship. It needed priority access, data precedence, local proxy authority, and enough legal weight to decide who could approach HECTON-8 after the disaster.

## Writer Notes

The Aegir route lets us explain:

- why Deep Reach could build there;
- why the player can arrive in 2190;
- why no immediate rescue exists;
- why old route data can be gameplay-relevant;
- why orbital windows and carrier position matter.
- why HECTON-8 is one moon among many, but the one with the dirtiest claim value.

Use route records as future collectibles: launch manifests, burn corrections, relay delays, missing carrier pings, Deep Reach priority overrides.
