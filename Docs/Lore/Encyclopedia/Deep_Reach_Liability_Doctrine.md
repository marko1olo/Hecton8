## Deep Reach Internal

The doctrine is not "deny everything."

The doctrine is:

- preserve asset continuity;
- preserve claim continuity;
- preserve Xenon-Omega continuity;
- define crew loss as operational variance;
- convert rescue delay into certification delay;
- convert failed evacuation into incomplete authorization;
- convert missing bodies into unresolved accounting status;
- convert Atlas autonomy into local system interpretation;
- convert resource priority into route stability requirement.

A clean lie breaks under audit. A layered procedure survives long enough for jurisdiction to move.

## Marauder Field Note

Deep Reach does not hide bodies by saying they did not exist.

It hides them by asking which department owned them at time of loss.

## Writer Notes

This doctrine is the backbone of Deep Reach horror.

They should rarely sound like villains. They sound like people designing language to survive inquiry:

- "asset continuity";
- "temporary certification failure";
- "non-recoverable biological load";
- "local autonomy interpretation";
- "evacuation sequence pending pressure validation";
- "payload route stability";
- "unresolved crew-state."

This lets the player experience moral disgust through documents, not speeches.
