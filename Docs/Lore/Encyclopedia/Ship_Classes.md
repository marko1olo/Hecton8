# Ship Classes

Status: draft encyclopedia entry.

## Public

Interstellar and in-system ships are not a single category. A probe, a Seed craft, a cargo hauler, a sleeper transport, and a local salvage carrier solve different problems.

## Classes

Needleprobe:
Fast beam-sail or pellet-beam scout. Usually disposable. Designed to send thin truth back across years, not return home.

Seed Ship:
Autonomous factory craft. Arrives before large human settlement and builds anchors, reactors, depots, habitats, and mistakes.

Mass Carrier:
Slow cargo hauler. Shielded, ugly, expensive, and usually more contract than vehicle.

Sleeper Transport:
Crew or corporate asset transport. Uses torpor medicine, rotating pressure decks, radiation shielding, and legal custody systems.

System Carrier:
Local workhorse inside a claimed star system. Moves capsules, cargo, tugs, salvage claims, and crews between depots, moons, and transfer orbits.

Bathy-Drop Capsule:
Atmospheric and ocean descent package. Heat shield, crush frame, buoyancy logic, emergency uplink. Easy to damage. Hard to replace.

## Writer Notes

The player's carrier is a System Carrier. It should feel rented, automated, legally hostile, and physically distant.
