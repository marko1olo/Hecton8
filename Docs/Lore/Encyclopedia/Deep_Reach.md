# Deep Reach

Status: draft encyclopedia entry.

## Public

Deep Reach Extraterrestrial Development Combine built remote colonies, autonomous industry, pressure-world infrastructure, route holdings, and corporate claims. Its public language centered on energy research, habitat autonomy, and frontier development.

HECTON-8 was one of its claims.

## Spoiler

On HECTON-8, Deep Reach wanted strategic pressure-resource extraction, Atlas-6 autonomous governance, pressure-biological exploitation, and control over an Aegir foothold no ethics board could inspect in real time.

Deep Reach did not need to be present every minute to remain dangerous. It designed routes, rules, debt, proxies, and automated systems that kept acting after human judgment failed.

By 2190, Deep Reach's power is interdomain rather than local. It can own parts of a route, hold claim priority, control insurance language, operate proxy systems, and make a dead colony expensive to investigate.

`Xenon-Omega` is locked as Deep Reach's corporate codename for the HECTON-8 pressure process chain. Marauders call the valuable form `blue debt`; technicians call stable sample material `pressure glass`.

After 2147, Deep Reach's public line was not one theatrical lie. It was a layered cover: Great Tide, geotechnical cascade, signal loss, pressure/biological quarantine, corrupted Atlas logs, and evacuation authorization failure.

## Writer Notes

Deep Reach is scarier when it sounds procedural, legal, and tired. Avoid cartoon villain language. The company should usually describe harm as variance, loss, containment, priority, asset protection, or unresolved liability.
