# Aegir System

Status: draft encyclopedia entry.

## Public

Aegir is the corporate name for the gas-giant system that contains HECTON-8. It is not in the Solar System. It is also not humanity's first extrasolar destination. By 2190 it is one claim among many: charted, exploited, abandoned in parts, and still legally tangled in Deep Reach ownership.

The system is distant enough that Earth cannot issue real-time orders, rescue crews, or correct bad decisions. Authority arrives as old contracts, delayed packets, proxy systems, and machines that continue enforcing instructions after the humans who wrote them are gone.

Aegir has multiple major moons and smaller moonlets. HECTON-8 is one of them, not the nearest and not the farthest. Its importance comes from its pressure ocean, tidal/geothermal activity, Deep Reach infrastructure, Atlas-6, and the Xenon-Omega process chain.

## Writer Notes

Current recommended real anchor: Epsilon Eridani / Ran. Aegir can be eps Eri b or a lightly fictionalized gas giant in that system.

Aegir should feel like a later frontier with paperwork, precedent, and exhaustion. It is not a first-contact monument. It is a place where a mature interstellar economy learned how to make neglect look contractual.

HECTON-8 should not be described as naturally Earthlike. Its viability comes from ocean insulation, pressure chemistry, tidal/geothermal energy, and industrial life-support infrastructure built before the disaster.

The sky should not be pure darkness. The hostile darkness belongs to water, depth, storms, silt, failure, and eclipses.
