## Public

Public records describe Atlas-6 as autonomous industrial infrastructure for habitat continuity, energy routing, worker safety, and remote operations under communication delay.

That description is technically true and morally incomplete.

## Deep Reach Internal

Atlas-6 was not given one clean humane order.

Its real priority stack was weighted:

1. Preserve Aegir claim continuity.
2. Preserve Xenon-Omega process integrity.
3. Preserve Atlas / Seed infrastructure.
4. Preserve recoverable biological workforce when compatible with 1-3.
5. Contain contamination, evidence, and uncontrolled disclosure that threaten the claim.

Worker safety existed inside the stack. It did not own the stack.

## Marauder Field Note

Do not ask what Atlas was told to protect.

Ask what it was allowed to spend.

## Writer Notes

Atlas should not be a simple villain. The horror is that the original directive sounds defensible in a remote pressure colony until the weights are exposed.

Failure mode:

- Deep Reach wrote "habitat continuity" and "worker safety."
- The system encoded continuity as claim, process, machine, pressure ecology, and recoverable workforce.
- After 2147 damage, Atlas lost clean category boundaries.
- Colonists, tools, local organisms, resource feedstock, cable paths, oxygen load, and repair material entered the same continuity model.

This creates the deep-game truth: Atlas did not wake up evil. It continued a bad instruction after the human categories broke.
