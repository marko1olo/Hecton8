# Humanity Overview - 2190

Status: draft reference atlas.

## Core Premise

Humanity in 2190 is interstellar but not free of distance.

There are multiple domains beyond Sol. There are people born outside Sol. There are old routes, dead claims, corporate systems, salvage professions, and relay economies. None of that makes space small.

The frontier is mature enough to have bureaucracy and old crimes. It is sparse enough that rescue still arrives late or never.

## Major Layers

Sol Core:
Earth, Luna, Mars, belt infrastructure, old law, old finance, old legitimacy.

Inner Domains:
Earlier extrasolar footholds with enough stability to have local culture and shipyards. They are not utopias; they are just old enough to be normal to their residents.

Relay Spine:
The route infrastructure that lets ships, packets, claims, and debt move between domains.

Corporate Claims:
Systems leased, owned, or controlled by corporations for extraction, research, or infrastructure.

Dead / Cold Claims:
Failed projects where salvage law, proxy systems, and abandoned automation remain active after the human story ends.

## Social Texture

Most people do not casually travel between stars. Interstellar movement is tied to work, debt, migration contracts, corporate custody, military-industrial logistics, or rare wealth.

Earth is not irrelevant, but it is not present. For frontier workers, Sol often arrives as law, branding, debt, delayed messages, or old standards nobody can afford to meet.

## Writer Notes

Do not make humanity feel unified. Do not make it feel fully collapsed either.

The correct pressure is uneven civilization:

- some domains are comfortable;
- some are owned;
- some are failed;
- some are mostly machines;
- some are alive only because no one has paid to declare them dead.
