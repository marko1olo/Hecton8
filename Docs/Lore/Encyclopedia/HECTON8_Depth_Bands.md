## Public

HECTON-8 is explored by depth, not by a flat map. Pressure, light, biology, signal behavior, and industrial damage change as the player descends.

## Working Bands

Shallows:
Bright, storm-touched, biologically rich. First repairs, first salvage, first evidence that Deep Reach public records are incomplete.

Drowned Infrastructure:
Habitat shells, pipelines, anchors, service tunnels, cargo locks, maintenance records. Corporate language still looks orderly here.

Industrial Descent:
Mining scars, processing stacks, cable forests, dead machinery, repair drones, pressure-resource containers. Xenon-Omega begins to matter as process, not loot.

Deep Abyss:
Low light, predators, silt plains, brine, collapsed modules, sonar uncertainty, old evacuation failures. Deep Reach language starts contradicting itself.

Thermal / Tectonic Fields:
Vents, mineral chimneys, heat gradients, pressure lattices, altered organisms, Atlas repair scars. Geology and industry are no longer cleanly separate.

Atlas Bottom Zone:
Factory-ship remains, industrial organs, living tissue intrusion, processed seafloor, Deep Reach hardware fused with ocean systems.

## Marauder Field Note

Depth is not distance. Depth is cost.

At one band you pay in battery. Then scrubber heat. Then hull fatigue. Then memory. Then names.
