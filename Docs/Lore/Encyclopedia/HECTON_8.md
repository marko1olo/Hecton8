# HECTON-8

Status: draft encyclopedia entry.

## Public

HECTON-8 is an ocean moon in the Aegir claim zone. Deep Reach built a pressure colony there decades before the player's arrival. Public records describe the settlement as lost after a major environmental and infrastructure failure in 2147.

It is one of several major Aegir moons, not the innermost and not the farthest. Its specific value comes from pressure ocean chemistry, tidal/geothermal activity, Deep Reach infrastructure, Atlas-6, local ecology, and a rare industrial process chain.

Most surviving data is incomplete, delayed, or legally contaminated. The colony is known to be dead. The cause is not known cleanly at the start of the game.

## Marauder Field Note

The useful version is shorter: drowned modules, sealed liability records, bad pressure history, and enough salvage value for someone to pay a contractor instead of sending a clean corporate team.

The place is not empty. It is just no longer organized around human survival.

There is more than one prize down here. Wire, oxygen chemistry, old hull, blood kelp fiber, pressure glass, worker IDs, and black-box truth can all be worth more than a clean conscience on the wrong day.
