# Relay Spine

Status: draft encyclopedia entry.

## Public

The Relay Spine is the loose name for the chain of beam stations, navigation nodes, depots, comm buffers, and maintenance habitats that make interstellar work possible between major human domains.

It is not one continuous road. It is a broken economic nervous system: expensive nodes separated by years of flight, owned by different authorities, patched by old treaties, and guarded by insurance clauses.

## Deep Reach Internal

The route is the asset.

A ship can be replaced. A crew can be replaced. A claim can be refiled. A functioning relay node changes what a corporation can reach, bill, deny, and recover.

## Marauder Field Note

Never trust a clean route map. If the map is clean, somebody paid to remove the wrecks.

## Writer Notes

The Relay Spine explains how a place like Aegir can be reached without making travel casual. It also explains why rescue is not immediate: route ownership, window timing, relay condition, debt locks, and local carrier availability all matter.
