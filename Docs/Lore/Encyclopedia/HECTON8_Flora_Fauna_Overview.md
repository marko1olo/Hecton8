## Public

HECTON-8 supports a pressure-adapted ocean ecology. The shallow bands can be bright, alive, and beautiful. That contrast matters: the horror deepens because the world is not dead everywhere.

Known scanner families already used by the project include:

- fauna: Bladderfish, Shore Skimmer, Kelp Raylet, Silt Drifter, Wall Glider, Brine Siphoner, Lantern Sifter, Glassworm Juvenile, Iron Minnow, Cold Veil Eel, Pocket Ambusher, Brine Stalker, Shadow Interceptor;
- flora/biota: Tubular Coral, Halo Sargassum Mats, Blood Kelp, Thermal Tubeworm, Ghost Weed, Iron Coral, Cathedral Kelp, Rift Ribbon, Lantern Grass, Static Thicket.

## Ecological Gradient

Shallows:
Bright, readable, colorful, useful. Life looks alien but not corrupted by default.

Mid-depth:
Industrial interfaces appear. Flora grows around cables, vents, pipeline leaks, abandoned farms, and coolant discharge.

Deep Abyss:
Fauna becomes scarred, route-aware, vibration-driven, and tied to oxygen pockets or pressure shadows.

Thermal Fields:
Heat, chemistry, mineral growth, and biological survival overlap. Food, poison, oxygen, and light stop being clean categories.

Atlas Bottom Zone:
Life and industry lose boundary. Stations behave like organs. Cables grow like roots. Repair drones carry biological material. Fauna can contain machine intrusion without becoming simple robots.

## Marauder Field Note

If it is pretty in the shallows, scan it.

If it is pretty below two kilometers, ask what it is eating.

## Writer Notes

Atlas alteration should be progressive, not universal:

- shallow ecosystems must keep wonder and contrast;
- early corruption should be ambiguous: cable growth, strange scars, organisms using warm metal;
- deep corruption becomes undeniable: biometal sutures, drone resin, tissue around machines, animals with industrial inserts;
- final-zone biology is not "zombie nature." It is repair logic using every available category.

Runtime note:

Lore can support many species, variants, and scan notes while runtime keeps deterministic spawn tables and visual fakes. Do not require individual biological simulations for every creature or plant.
