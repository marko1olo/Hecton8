## Purpose

False endings must be real outcomes, not fake game-over screens.

They should respect player agency while making the player feel the cost of leaving before understanding HECTON-8.

## Family A - Material Ending

Condition:
Player repairs enough ascent/recovery chain to extract a certified blue debt payload without exposing the core truth.

Outcome:
Black Keel recovers the player and sample. Contract pays. Credits can roll.

Cost:
Deep Reach or its legal hooks gains process coordinates, sample continuity data, and enough paperwork to bury the colony again.

Tone:
Successful theft. Failed witness.

Use:
Best Stalker-like false ending. The player "wins" as a salvage contractor and loses as a human being.

## Family B - Partial Exit / Same Seed Return

Condition:
Player reaches a limited recovery window or orbital uplink but lacks final evidence, final payload, or full escape readiness.

Outcome:
Player can return to the same HECTON-8 seed.

Cost:
World state worsens:

- Deep Reach pressure rises;
- Black Keel contract language tightens;
- route windows narrow;
- drones or Atlas attention can increase;
- some evidence becomes harder to access.

Tone:
You got air. You did not get free.

Use:
Best for long campaign pacing. It makes early escape real without ending the deep game.

## Family C - Corporate Capture

Condition:
Player uploads or carries enough value/evidence through a compromised recovery chain without controlling release.

Outcome:
Black Keel recovers the player as witness, asset, or liability.

Cost:
Player survives under custody. Truth becomes negotiable property.

Tone:
The ship was never neutral.

Use:
Strong if Black Keel ownership hooks are revealed late.

## Family D - Coward Exit / Bad Dossier

Condition:
Player escapes with minimal payload and little truth.

Outcome:
Alive, unpaid or underpaid, marked unreliable, haunted by unresolved evidence.

Cost:
Marauder dossier records survival without witness value.

Tone:
Not failure. Worse: unfinished.

Use:
Optional. Use carefully; do not moralize too loudly.

## Working Structure

Minimum false-ending set:

- Material Ending: real credits, bitter success.
- Partial Exit: same seed continuation with worsened pressure.

Optional:

- Corporate Capture if Black Keel/Deep Reach hooks become strong enough.
- Coward Exit if escape engineering supports a low-truth route.
