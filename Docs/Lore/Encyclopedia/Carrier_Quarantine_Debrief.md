## Source Brief

Carrier quarantine is where rescue can stop looking like rescue.

On paper, the Black Keel needs quarantine for good reasons. HECTON-8 can return pressure trauma, unknown biological residue, blue debt contamination, damaged suit seals, corrupted storage, Atlas-compatible signal traces, and cargo that should not enter a shared bay. A carrier that does not isolate recovered mass is a bad carrier.

The danger is that the same room can isolate a witness. Quarantine can separate the player's body from the payload, the payload from the evidence, the evidence from the names, the names from public custody, and the voice from any receiver outside Black Keel's contract logic. A medical delay can become a debrief delay. A debrief delay can become billing. Billing can become custody.

Use quarantine as a procedural threat, not a theatrical trap. The stronger line is: "Recovery accepted. Medical quarantine and contract debrief authorized." It sounds normal. That is why it works.

## Public / Website Article

The Black Keel does not need a crewed brig to become dangerous. It only needs a quarantine protocol.

Every salvage carrier in Aegir space has some version of post-recovery isolation. The reasons are real: pressure injury, biological contamination, unstable cargo, compromised suit seals, damaged black boxes, and samples that can ruin a ship if they are handled like ordinary salvage.

On HECTON-8, those reasons become leverage. A recovered Marauder can be held apart from the payload while the payload is certified. Evidence can be copied, locked, or declared contaminated before a public receiver sees it. A witness statement can be delayed until contract counsel, Keelmark, or a Deep Reach priority handler has classified the route.

Quarantine does not have to announce betrayal. It can announce care, safety, procedure, and billing. The player should learn to read the order of operations. If the carrier certifies cargo before it asks whether the recovered person can breathe, the recovery has already chosen what matters.

## Spoiler / Writer Truth

Carrier quarantine debrief is the narrative mechanism that makes corporate capture, false material success, and compromised recovery feel procedural. It should not imply every recovery is automatically capture. It should imply that the carrier has enough legitimate reasons to isolate the player that abuse can hide inside the checklist.

Use it when payload certification, debt billing, medical hold, evidence access, and witness statement routing need to collide. The player should fear being saved incorrectly.
