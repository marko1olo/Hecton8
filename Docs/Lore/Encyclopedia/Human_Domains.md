# Human Domains

Status: draft encyclopedia entry.

## Public

By 2190, humanity is no longer confined to Earth or even Sol. Interstellar expansion exists, but it is uneven, expensive, and politically fragmented.

Some domains are old enough to have local law, shipyards, relay stations, and children who have never seen Earth. Others are corporate claims: industrial systems with enough infrastructure to extract value, but not enough public attention to protect workers. Some are dead claims, where automation and legal ownership survived longer than the people.

## Domain Types

Sol Core:
Earth, Luna, Mars, belt industry, and the old legal center of human civilization. Still powerful. Not physically close.

Inner Domains:
Earlier extrasolar footholds and settlements. More stable than frontier claims, but not necessarily free.

Relay Domains:
Beam, communication, depot, fuel, navigation, and repair infrastructure. Often more machine than society.

Corporate Claim Systems:
Legally owned or leased systems where companies build extraction, research, and logistics networks far from public oversight.

Dead / Cold Claims:
Failed projects where contracts, automated systems, and salvage rights outlive the original colony.

## Writer Notes

Aegir belongs to the later frontier. It is not the first star, not the first planet, and not the first gas-giant claim. Its importance comes from pressure-derived strategic resources, Atlas-6, Deep Reach liability, and the fact that a mature interstellar system can still abandon people cleanly.
