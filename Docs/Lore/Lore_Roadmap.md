# HECTON-8 Lore Roadmap

Status: working expansion plan.
Purpose: grow the setting into a massive lore base without losing control.

## Core Model

The setting is a sparse mature frontier.

Humanity has already reached multiple star systems by 2190. This does not mean civilization is fast, safe, or dense. It means the frontier has enough age to accumulate law, debt, contracts, abandoned claims, route maps, insurance doctrines, salvage professions, and corporate crimes.

HECTON-8 is not a first step. It is a late-stage wound.

## Expansion Layers

- no FTL;
- no real-time interstellar command;
- travel by infrastructure, slow ships, launch windows, braking, shielding, and route ownership;
- local Aegir orbital mechanics matter more to gameplay than full interstellar simulation.

## Rule

Every lore expansion must answer at least one of these:

- what does the player do differently because of this;
- what does the player understand differently because of this;
- what can be discovered, missed, sold, hidden, or misread;
- what pressure does it add to survival, escape, or final choice.
