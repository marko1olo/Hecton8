# Final Payloads Gameplay Map

Status: working endgame structure.
Purpose: turn final choices into physical payloads, evidence states, carrier routes, and consequences.

## Core Question

The final question is not only "do you escape?"

The stronger question:
What has the right to leave HECTON-8?

Possible answers:

- the player;
- money;
- names;
- proof;
- blue debt;
- Atlas signal;
- living ocean material;
- Deep Reach process knowledge;
- nothing.

## Payload Rule

Every ending payload should be physical or data-physical:

- stored in a pressure casket;
- transmitted through a limited window;
- carried in ascent hardware;
- burned into a black box;
- hidden in carrier logs;
- destroyed before extraction.

No abstract morality button.

## P0 - Self Only

Meaning:
The player escapes with minimal evidence and no major cargo.

Requirements:
Repair ascent path, hit carrier window, avoid deep proof chain.

Outcome:
Survival, but little leverage. Deep Reach can frame the run as failed salvage or private breach.

Tone:
Not a joke ending. A human but hollow exit.

World Consequence:
HECTON-8 stays mostly buried.

## P1 - Material Payload

Meaning:
The player exports blue debt, pressure glass, rare salvage, or process hardware.

Requirements:
Find high-value sample, stabilize it, send/sell through Black Keel or broker route.

Outcome:
Money and contract success. Truth remains partial or suppressed.

Risk:
Deep Reach gets what it came back for.

World Consequence:
The crime becomes resource flow again.

## P2 - Evidence Payload

Meaning:
The player exports names, manifests, Atlas directive fragments, Deep Reach process records, and colony death chain proof.

Requirements:
Recover cross-source evidence: colony records, Deep Reach memos, Atlas fragments, Black Keel logs.

Outcome:
Lower immediate material value, higher external pressure against Deep Reach.

Risk:
Evidence can be discredited if incomplete or routed through compromised carrier systems.

World Consequence:
HECTON-8 becomes a public wound instead of a buried claim.

## P3 - Names Payload

Meaning:
The player prioritizes personal/human records over strategic proof or money.

Requirements:
Recover Barnard marks, evacuation manifest, worker logs, colony personal records.

Outcome:
Not enough to destroy Deep Reach alone, but enough to give the dead identity.

Risk:
May be emotionally strong but politically weak unless paired with evidence.

World Consequence:
The player changes why the story matters, even if the system survives.

## P4 - Atlas Signal Payload

Meaning:
The player lets part of Atlas communicate or leave in data form.

Requirements:
Deep Atlas interface, signal stabilization, final trust/bargain/containment choice.

Outcome:
Potential proof, potential contamination, potential new intelligence, potential repeat disaster.

Risk:
Deep Reach can exploit it, or Atlas can continue damaged logic outside HECTON-8 context.

World Consequence:
The ocean's post-crime life may become a voice or a weapon.

## P5 - Living Sample Payload

Meaning:
The player exports altered biological material from HECTON-8.

Requirements:
Containment, ethical choice, scan chain, carrier payload capacity.

Outcome:
Can prove new life/repair ecology exists, or hand Deep Reach a portable process.

Risk:
Biosecurity, misclassification, corporate recovery.

World Consequence:
Raises the hardest question: is this evidence, survivor, contamination, or resource?

## P6 - Continuity Substrate Payload

Meaning:
The player exports high-value bottom-zone substrate or Atlas-compatible lattice.

Requirements:
Deep resource access, pressure casket, Black Keel route, or compromised extraction path.

Outcome:
Maximum material/strategic value.

Risk:
This is the payload Deep Reach most wants.

World Consequence:
Deep Reach may recreate the Aegir process elsewhere.

## P7 - Burned Route / Containment

Meaning:
The player prevents critical payloads from leaving and may sacrifice payout, evidence completeness, or rescue certainty.

Requirements:
Destroy carrier route data, vent/destroy samples, collapse final link, or transmit warning instead of cargo.

Outcome:
Deep Reach loses direct recovery path. Player outcome can vary from escape to stranded to unknown.

Risk:
Truth may remain trapped with the moon.

World Consequence:
HECTON-8 remains wounded but harder to exploit.

## P8 - Deep Reach Capture

Meaning:
The player leaves through a route that Deep Reach controls.

Requirements:
Compromised Black Keel path, high-value payload, incomplete evidence, or accepting a clean contract exit.

Outcome:
Extraction, payout, silence, or disappearance.

Risk:
The player becomes another contained record.

World Consequence:
The system eats the witness.

## Ending Combination Logic

Endings should combine payloads, not only branch once.

Examples:

- Self Only + Names: survivor with human record but little proof.
- Material + No Evidence: successful salvage, ugly truth failure.
- Evidence + Names: strongest public wound route.
- Evidence + Atlas Signal: powerful but dangerous proof.
- Living Sample + Deep Reach Capture: catastrophic corporate recovery.
- Burned Route + No Escape: containment martyr or ambiguous survival.

## Required Trackers

Authoring-level trackers:

- evidence completeness;
- names recovered;
- blue debt state;
- Atlas interface state;
- Black Keel compromise level;
- carrier window readiness;
- ascent package readiness;
- Deep Reach attention;
- living sample containment;
- route burn state.

Runtime should store these as flags/counters/enums, not interpreted prose.
