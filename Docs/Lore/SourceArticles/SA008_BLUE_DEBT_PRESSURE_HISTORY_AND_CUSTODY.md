## Source Brief

Blue debt must feel like a handled pressure artifact, not a collectible crystal.

Player-facing truth:

- `Xenon-Omega` is Deep Reach's corporate codename for a pressure-grown material/process family.
- Marauder slang is `blue debt` because the sample carries payout, attention, and consequence.
- Technical field slang is `pressure glass`.
- Value depends on pressure history: where it formed, how long it stayed sealed, what brine/vent/biological/process systems touched it, and whether decompression damaged the lattice.
- Good samples require pressure-rated containment and telemetry. Bad handling can turn value into evidence, contamination, bait, or junk.
- The material can carry weak Atlas-compatible pressure-harmonic behavior. That is route and classification pressure, not magic communication.
- Contamination is chemical, industrial, and ecological: brine catalysts, biofilm, enzymes, repair resin, or broken process substrate.

## Public / Website Article

No honest Marauder judges blue debt by color.

A blue edge on the seafloor can be worthless. A dull gray casket can buy a carrier window. The useful question is pressure history: where the sample grew, what held it closed, what vent cycle annealed it, which brine pocket fed it, which organism touched it, which Atlas route heard it, and how much of that state survived the hand that picked it up.

Deep Reach called the material `Xenon-Omega` when it wanted ownership to sound clean. Technicians called the stable lattice `pressure glass`. Marauders call it `blue debt` because every good sample creates two ledgers: what it pays, and what it lets somebody demand afterward.

The material is not a single ore vein. It is a pressure-world process family: noble-gas pockets trapped in brine, mineral defects opened by tide and heat, biological catalysts grown in hostile water, industrial concentrators left by Deep Reach, and Atlas-compatible repair substrate that learned to move through the same damaged system.

This is why containment matters. A sealed sample can remain viable, harmonic, or custody-grade. A careless sample can drift, fracture, bloom, or die while leaving live contamination behind. The difference may be one clamp, one bad warm cabin, one vent repressure, or one telemetry packet that tells the wrong office what the player carries.

Blue debt pays because it is real. It ruins people because value is never the only thing it carries.
