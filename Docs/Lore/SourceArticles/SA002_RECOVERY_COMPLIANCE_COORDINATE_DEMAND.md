## Source Surface Texts

### Website / Wiki

Recovery Compliance Office is the part of Deep Reach that still answers in 2190.

It does not answer like a rescue desk. It answers like a department that has learned to survive distance. A packet arrives late, clean, stamped, and limited to what the route can bill: coordinates, payload class, sample state, quarantine receiver, custody hash, contractor identity, silence clause. If the message contains concern, concern is below the routing header.

That is the important distinction. Deep Reach is not leaning over HECTON-8 with a live hand on every valve. Aegir is too far from core authority and too dirty for that. Present pressure moves through old route rights, Black Keel clauses, Keelmark Mutual custody language, automated receiver logic, and the few relay windows that survive weather, orbit, and local interference.

Recovery Compliance uses those windows carefully. A living contractor is useful because a living contractor can still sign, certify, carry, sell, or leak. A dead contractor is useful if the body tag, recorder, or custody mark survives. Coordinates are useful either way. That is why the first clean corporate packet may ask where the player is before it admits what the player is.

Vera Sato-Ren's office does not need to write "leave them." It can write: route safety requires payload declaration before biological recovery language. It can write: quarantine receiver pending. It can write: contractor welfare review awaits custody confidence. Each sentence is defensible at a desk. Underwater, each sentence costs air.

For the player, the packet order matters. If Recovery Compliance asks for coordinates before acknowledging the bathy-drop loss, that is evidence. If it asks for sample class before medical state, that is evidence. If it routes through Keelmark before a public relay, that is evidence. The office is dangerous because it keeps making the old disaster countable in Deep Reach's favor.

### In-Game Codex

Recovery Compliance Office is the active Deep Reach pressure channel in 2190. It cannot issue instant rescue from core space. It acts through delayed packets, Black Keel recovery clauses, Keelmark custody language, quarantine receivers, and route-notary hashes.

Its clean replies are not neutral. A request for coordinates, payload class, sample state, or custody confidence before biological recovery language can prove priority ordering. Preserve packet spools and relay receipts. The order of questions may matter more than the words.
