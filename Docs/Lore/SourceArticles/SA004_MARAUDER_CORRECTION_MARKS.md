## Source Brief

Marauder correction marks are field annotations placed beside official language, not over it. They appear as grease-pencil notes, etched arrows, tape flags, washer scratches, knot codes, salvage chalk, thumbnail cuts in soft gasket, and short audio tags attached to a scan packet.

The mark does not make the Marauder omniscient. It says a trained salvage worker distrusted the official label enough to leave the object readable for the next person. A good correction keeps three things together: the claimant sentence, the physical object, and the practical contradiction.

Examples:

- Deep Reach writes `release pending`; a Marauder writes `door held shut`.
- Keelmark writes `payout exposure`; a Marauder writes `what got priced`.
- Black Keel writes `oxygen welfare support`; a Marauder writes `air billed before rescue`.
- A route board says `clean line`; a Marauder circles the missing return stamp.
- A translated archive caption sounds smooth; a Marauder mark keeps the rough witness wording beside it.

The system is useful because HECTON-8 is full of truthful sentences that point away from the truth. Correction marks do not replace evidence. They keep evidence from being made polite.

## Public / Website Article

Marauders are not trusted because they are noble. They are trusted, when they are trusted at all, because they have already been paid to notice how claims lie.

On HECTON-8 their marks survive in places official systems wanted clean: beside a shift board, under a quarantine stamp, on the lip of a pressure bunk, across a packet footer, inside the rim of a tool crate, near the edge of a worker tag. The marks are short because long notes fail underwater. They are physical because clean summaries travel too easily.

A Marauder correction mark has one job: keep the object arguing.

It does not say `this is the whole truth`. It says `do not let that sentence finish the room`. The official line stays visible because it can prove who tried to rename the object. The correction stays beside it because the next diver may not have time to decode a corporate paragraph while the air cabinet is browning at the edge.

The best marks are ugly and exact. `Door held` is better than `cruel policy`. `Name half gone` is better than `tragic loss`. `Safe means holding` is better than `unsafe shelter`. A correction mark is not literature. It is a hand on your shoulder before you trust the wrong label.

This is why Marauder notes matter to the wider archive. They show which official categories were already distrusted before the public heard about HECTON-8. They preserve the moment when a worker, diver, or salvage handler looked at the object and refused to let the paperwork be the last voice in the room.

## Player Codex Entry

Marauder correction marks are field annotations left beside official records and object labels. They preserve a practical reading when claimant language makes an event cleaner than the physical evidence.

Read the mark with the object, not instead of it. A correction can point to a missing return stamp, a door that stayed shut, a body-chain mark, a custody mismatch, a translation hold, or a payout category attached to a person.

The correction is not a verdict. It is a warning that the official sentence is trying to finish the evidence too early.

## Writer / Integration Notes

Use correction marks as small source-separated counter-voices. They should never solve the mystery for the player. They should tell the player where not to let official wording overwrite physical evidence.

Good placements:

- next to Deep Reach claimant phrases;
- beside route boards with missing return stamps;
- on worker lockers, pressure bunks, medlocks, and tool crates;
- on terminal margins where localization hold or claimant language matters;
- as short audio tags on scan packets after the player has already seen the object.

Avoid turning Marauders into all-knowing narrators. Correction marks are working-class evidence habits: practical, partial, suspicious, and source-bound.

`LORE_MARAUDER_CORRECTION_MARKS_WEBSITE`

Las marcas de corrección Marauder son anotaciones de campo breves colocadas junto al lenguaje oficial, no encima de él. Aparecen como lápiz graso, flechas grabadas, tiras de cinta, arañazos en arandelas, códigos de nudo, tiza de salvamento, cortes en juntas o etiquetas de audio unidas a un paquete de escaneo.

La marca no vuelve al Marauder correcto en todo. Dice que un trabajador de salvamento entrenado desconfió de la etiqueta lo suficiente como para dejar el objeto legible para la siguiente persona. La línea oficial permanece visible porque puede probar quién intentó renombrar la sala. La corrección queda al lado porque el siguiente buzo quizá no tenga tiempo para descifrar un párrafo corporativo.

Las mejores marcas son feas y exactas: `puerta retenida`, `medio nombre`, `aire facturado antes del rescate`, `qué fue tasado`. Una marca de corrección no es toda la verdad. Es una advertencia de que la frase oficial intenta cerrar la prueba demasiado pronto.

Tanda Koreksi Marauder

`LORE_MARAUDER_CORRECTION_MARKS_WEBSITE`

Tanda koreksi Marauder adalah catatan lapangan pendek yang ditempatkan di samping bahasa resmi, bukan di atasnya. Bentuknya bisa pensil minyak, panah tergores, pita penanda, goresan pada washer, kode simpul, kapur salvage, sayatan pada gasket, atau tag audio yang menempel pada paket pemindaian.

Tanda itu tidak membuat Marauder benar tentang semuanya. Tanda itu mengatakan seorang pekerja salvage terlatih cukup tidak percaya pada label sehingga membiarkan objek tetap bisa dibaca orang berikutnya. `LORE_MARAUDER_CORRECTION_MARKS_WEBSITE`

Marauder 修正标记是放在官方措辞旁边的短现场注记，不是覆盖官方措辞。它们可能是油性铅笔、刻出的箭头、胶带旗、垫圈周围的划痕、绳结代码、salvage 粉笔、垫圈上的切口，或附在扫描包上的音频标签。

这个标记并不说明 Marauder 对一切都正确。它说明一个受训的 salvage 工人足够不信任标签，于是把物件留给下一个人继续阅读。官方行必须保持可见，因为它能证明是谁试图给这个房间改名。修正留在旁边，因为下一个潜水员可能没有时间解读一整段公司文字。

最好的标记丑而准确：`门保持关闭`、`半个名字`、`救援前计费空气`、`什么被定价`。修正标记不是全部真相。它是在警告：官方句子正在过早结束证据。
