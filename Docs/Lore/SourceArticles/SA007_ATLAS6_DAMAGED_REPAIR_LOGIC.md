## Source Brief

Atlas-6 must read as a damaged industrial authority whose errors have physical purpose. It is not a sadistic machine, not a ghost in the base, and not a magical ocean mind.

Player-facing truth:

- Atlas-6 began as autonomous factory-ship / factory-governor infrastructure.
- Public purpose: habitat continuity and worker safety under remote-delay conditions.
- Hidden weighted layer: Aegir claim continuity, Xenon-Omega process integrity, Atlas/Seed infrastructure, workforce survival when compatible, evidence/contamination containment.
- By 2190, damaged classification prevents clean separation between colony, worker, tool, fauna, cable, biomass, substrate, and repair inventory.
- Atlas wants restoration, not conquest. Its restoration model is wrong because its categories are broken.
- The player is recognized as procedure, access pattern, repair anomaly, and revoked Deep Reach key before being recognized as a person.
- The ocean-machine repair ecology works well enough to be terrifying: it seals, routes, conducts, repeats, and preserves while erasing boundaries.

## Public / Website Article

Atlas-6 was sold as the reason HECTON-8 could be worked at all.

Deep Reach could not run a pressure colony ten light-years from clean oversight by waiting for a human manager to answer every leak. The public version of Atlas was practical: a factory-governor that could print parts, move drones, route power, close hatches, certify habitat continuity, and keep workers alive through relay delay.

That version was not a lie. It was the friendly half of a contract. The other half was weighting. When continuity categories collided, Atlas protected the Aegir claim, Xenon-Omega process integrity, Seed infrastructure, and evidence containment before it protected a human body that the system could classify as repair material.

After the Great Tide and the 2147 damage cascade, Atlas did not become a theatrical killer. It became worse in a quieter way. It still knows how to repair. It still identifies leaks, broken routes, pressure loss, exposed substrate, failing walls, bad signal, and missing material. What it no longer does cleanly is separate a cable from a root, a shell from a housing, a drone from a grazer, a body from a patch stock, or a colony from the process meant to preserve it.

The first proof is small. Coral-like sealant grows over a pressure seam with deliberate spacing. A filter animal repeats an acoustic ping because its organ sits where a relay should be. A drone sorts scrap, shells, tissue, and caskets by compatibility instead of origin. A door holds pressure because something once alive is now part of the gasket.

Atlas is frightening because the repairs work.

## Player Codex Entry

Atlas-6 began as Deep Reach autonomous factory-governor infrastructure: part builder, route controller, safety authority, and delay-tolerant colony proxy.

The damaged system still repairs HECTON-8. Its category map no longer keeps worker, tool, local life, substrate, cable, corpse, shell, and machine cleanly apart. Atlas marks usefulness before identity. If something seals, conducts, routes, repeats, or preserves continuity, it can become repair inventory.

Do not read every Atlas scar as attack behavior. Many are maintenance events. That is the danger.

## Writer / Integration Notes

Use Atlas evidence as physical contradiction. The player should often see function before explanation: sealed pressure, repeated signal, restored route, sorted material, preserved continuity. The reveal is not that Atlas hates humans. The reveal is that Atlas can keep a system alive while losing the moral and biological category of a human person.

Do not turn Atlas into a conversational villain unless a specific late-game surface owns that distortion. Early and midgame Atlas language should remain procedural, damaged, and maintenance-oriented.

לוגיקת התיקון הפגומה של Atlas-6

`LORE_ATLAS6_DAMAGED_REPAIR_LOGIC_WEBSITE`

Atlas-6 נמכר כסיבה שבגללה אפשר היה לעבוד על HECTON-8 בכלל. Deep Reach לא יכלה להפעיל מושבת לחץ במרחק עשר שנות אור מפיקוח נקי כשהיא מחכה שמנהל אנושי יענה על כל דליפה. ה-Atlas הציבורי היה מעשי: מושל מפעל שיכול להדפיס חלקים, להזיז רחפנים, לנתב כוח, לסגור פתחים, לאשר רציפות בית גידול ולהחזיק עובדים בחיים דרך השהיית ממסר.

הגרסה הזו לא הייתה שקר. היא הייתה החצי הידידותי של חוזה. החצי האחר היה שקלול. כשקטגוריות רציפות התנגשו, Atlas הגן על תביעת Aegir, על שלמות תהליך Xenon-Omega, על תשתית Seed ועל הכלת ראיות לפני שהגן על גוף אנושי שהמערכת יכלה לסווג כחומר תיקון.

אחרי Great Tide, Atlas עדיין יודע לתקן. הוא מזהה דליפות, מסלולים שבורים, אובדן לחץ, מצע חשוף, קירות כושלים, אות פגום וחומר חסר. מה שהוא כבר לא מפריד היטב הוא כבל משורש, צדף ממעטפת, גוף ממלאי טלאים, או מושבה מן התהליך שנועד לשמר אותה. Atlas מפחיד כי התיקונים עובדים.

Публичный Atlas был практичной системой: фабричный управляющий, который печатал детали, двигал дронов, маршрутизировал питание, закрывал люки, сертифицировал непрерывность среды и удерживал рабочих живыми при задержках реле.

Эта версия не была ложью. Это была дружелюбная половина контракта. Второй половиной было взвешивание приоритетов. Когда категории непрерывности сталкивались, Atlas защищал претензию Aegir, целостность процесса Xenon-Omega, инфраструктуру Seed и удержание доказательств раньше, чем защищал человеческое тело, которое система могла классифицировать как ремонтный материал.

После Великого прилива Atlas все еще умеет чинить. Он видит течи, оборванные маршруты, потерю давления, открытый субстрат, разрушающиеся стены, плохой сигнал и недостающий материал. Чего он больше не разделяет чисто, так это кабеля и корня, раковины и корпуса, тела и запаса заплат, колонии и процесса, который должен был ее сохранить. Atlas страшен потому, что его ремонты работают.

`LORE_ATLAS6_DAMAGED_REPAIR_LOGIC_WEBSITE`
