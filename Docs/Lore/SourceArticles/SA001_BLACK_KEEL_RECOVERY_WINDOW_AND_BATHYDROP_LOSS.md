## Source Surface Texts

### Website / Wiki

Black Keel can hear a bad descent and still refuse a burn. That is not a contradiction. It is route math with a contract stapled to it.

The carrier that brings a Marauder to HECTON-8 is not parked above the moon with a warm hatch open. Black Keel works from Aegir transfer space, where the safe line between radiation, storm scatter, Kestrel relay shadow, propellant margin, and cargo custody keeps moving. A recovery call is one signal in that moving geometry. A verified recovery is a different thing: a burn slot, a guidance solution, a pressure-rated pickup path, a clean receiver, and mass the carrier is allowed to lift.

The player's bathy-drop was not a reusable shuttle. It was a descent package with a recovery interface. On impact it spent the part of the system meant to leave: warped crush frame, flooded ascent avionics, lost high-gain antenna, torn flotation and heat-shield ring, emergency buoy returning partial pings. The capsule did its first job by not becoming paste. It failed the job that matters afterward.

That failure turns escape into work. The route back needs an acoustic relay spine to talk through water and weather, pressure seals and clamp rings that will hold above local rating, a guidance timing core that can meet Black Keel instead of missing it by an orbit, an ascent charge or thermal store that can climb without tearing the payload, and a quarantine/legal handshake that gives the receiver permission to open a door.

The numbers stay ugly because the debt is physical. The starting lien is 4.8 tonne-window before oxygen, welfare addenda, sample custody, and evidence payload adjustments. Tonne-window means mass through a recovery opportunity, not a credit joke. A sealed recorder, a pressure-glass sample, a living contractor, and a corpse tag all compete for the same narrow promise.

Black Keel's voice is useful because it is bounded. It can send a window. It can log a buoy. It can price a payload. It can repeat that biological recovery is not certified on the present route. It cannot make Aegir stop turning, cannot make HECTON-8 stop storming, and cannot make Keelmark Mutual forget which kilogram was bonded.

When Recovery Compliance asks for coordinates before rescue language, the carrier is not malfunctioning. The old claim system is working exactly as designed.

### In-Game Codex

Black Keel received at least part of the bathy-drop loss signal, but reception is not recovery. The carrier holds in Aegir transfer space and can only recover during narrow burn, relay, weather, mass, and legal windows.

The damaged bathy-drop spent its ascent capacity during survival: warped crush frame, flooded avionics, torn flotation ring, failed high-gain antenna, and partial emergency buoy. Leaving HECTON-8 now requires a rebuilt chain: acoustic relay, pressure seal, guidance timing core, ascent energy, and quarantine/legal handshake.

Preserve carrier receipts and buoy logs. They prove what Black Keel knew, what Keelmark counted, and which payload class was prioritized.
