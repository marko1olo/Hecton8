# HECTON-8 Canon Locks

Status: user-controlled working canon.
Purpose: compact truth list for writers, designers, and later encyclopedia entries.

This file records what is fixed enough to build against. It does not replace `Lore_Bible.md`; it is the short operational version.

## Taste Locks

- NASA-punk / deep-sea noir.
- Hard sci-fi pressure: no FTL, no ansible, no reactionless rescue.
- Interstellar travel is infrastructure, debt, mass, shielding, braking, and time.
- Horror comes from engineering failure, pressure, darkness, damaged repair logic, and ocean scale.
- Shallows are allowed to be bright, beautiful, alien, and inviting.
- Surface sky, Aegir, moons, clouds, coastline, ocean skin, and photic shallows are bright/beautiful spectacle and route context. Darkness belongs to depth, caves, interiors, storms, and temporary eclipse windows.
- Depth/light lock: 0-100 m is mostly bright and beautiful in open water; deep caves may be dark; 200-400 m becomes twilight-like; 400-500 m and below may become true darkness/murk with route structure preserved.
- Surface and shallows may be colorful and alien with coral-like biota, plus technogenic colony/industrial traces in selected areas.
- Aegir's current blue/purple/methane-rich visual direction is allowed if the texture and atmosphere are premium and believable; reject muddy procedural scribbles, not the color premise.
- Deep zones become progressively dirtier: industry enters biology, biology enters machinery.
- Atlas-6 horror is not evil AI sadism. It is damaged classification and repair logic.
- Gameplay truth stays stable. Presentation, evidence order, POIs, ecology, geology, and endings can vary by seed.

## Timeline Locks

- Present year: 2190.

- Earlier 2170 "present day" draft references are historical escalation context unless explicitly reopened.

## Location Locks

- Aegir is not in the Solar System.
- Aegir is not humanity's first extrasolar star, first extrasolar planet, first gas-giant claim, or first remote colony attempt.
- By 2190 humanity already has other domains, relay footholds, corporate claims, and failed outposts beyond Sol.
- Host star must be a normal yellow/orange/red dwarf class star.
- Host is not a brown dwarf.
- HECTON-8 is not dark because the system has no useful starlight. It is dark because it is deep, stormed, pressurized, drowned, and industrially broken.
- Current recommended real anchor: Epsilon Eridani / Ran.
- Aegir can be mapped to eps Eri b or a lightly fictionalized giant in that system.
- HECTON-8 is a fictional ocean moon, not a cataloged real body.
- Player-facing catalog crosswalk: Ran / Aegir / RAN-B:H8 / HECTON-8.
- Use `HECTON-8` for normal play text and story identity. Use `RAN-B:H8` for dry route catalogs, insurance plates, astronomical articles and corporate ledgers.
- Aegir has multiple major moons and smaller moonlets. HECTON-8 is one moon in that system, not the innermost and not the farthest.
- HECTON-8's position should be operationally useful: enough tidal/radiation/eclipsing pressure to matter, but not so extreme that shallow zones become permanently dark or unplayable.
- Working moon ladder for player-facing route lore: H1 Skarn, H2 Vela, H3 Claw, H4 Lumen, H5 Thorne, H6 Anvil, H7 Kestrel, H8 HECTON, H9 Mute. Names remain adjustable, but every moon must keep a physical route/geology/hazard role.
- Inner moon passages create charged-particle noise, ice scatter, relay shutter timing, eclipses, and carrier handshake gaps. These are real orbital constraints that contracts can exploit.
- Outer moons and moonlets provide cold claims, dead beacons, mothballed export routes, salvage rumors, and evidence that HECTON-8 is the worst visible case of a broader Aegir claim economy.

## Human Expansion Locks

- Earth/Sol is not the whole human world by 2190.
- Sol Core remains legally and culturally important but physically distant from frontier claims.
- Human expansion is uneven: old inner domains, relay stations, industrial footholds, corporate claims, and dead/cold claims all exist.
- Aegir is a later, dirtier, high-value claim node, not the first proof that interstellar settlement works.
- Deep Reach can operate at Aegir because there is already a wider interstellar logistics economy.
- Transit doctrine: no FTL, no ansible, no instant rescue. Early Aegir knowledge came through beam-assisted probes and autonomous packets. Heavy Atlas/Seed/colony freight used external staging, pellet-beam assisted fusion or related fusion freight, long coasts, magsail/aerobrake braking and billable route infrastructure.
- Black Keel is an Aegir-system claim tender, not a personal interstellar ship and not clean rescue cavalry.
- Current scale model: sparse mature frontier.
- There are enough domains to support salvage law, route insurance, corporate claims, and Marauder culture.
- There are not enough domains, ships, or institutions to make rescue fast, cheap, or guaranteed.
- The setting must not become dense space opera. Distance, delay, mass, debt, and jurisdiction remain oppressive.
- Major named domains should stay limited to 4-6 obvious nodes, with smaller systems implied.
- Working major nodes: Sol Core, Centauri Compact, Barnard Yards, Tau Ceti League, Luyten Junction, Aegir Claim.
- Sol Core is old-law, old-finance, old-standard authority. It can own, certify, insure, and adjudicate remote claims long after it cannot physically help.
- Centauri Compact is the early extrasolar legitimacy anchor: proof that human expansion can be real, civic, and functional before later frontier claims corrupt the language.
- Barnard Yards is the working player-origin culture: shipbreaking, pressure hardware, dead-claim salvage, rented cutters, bad debt, and Marauder procedure.
- Tau Ceti League is the public-law counterweight. It can make evidence matter after relay delay, but it cannot rescue the player in real time.
- Luyten Junction is relay/depot/packet-custody infrastructure more than a society. It makes no-FTL communication a route, tariff, custody, and timing problem.
- Aegir Claim is the dirty corporate frontier where Deep Reach could operate because the wider logistics economy already existed.
- Deep Reach legal posture by 2190: old charter legitimacy through Centauri Compact language, Sol Core finance/insurance compatibility, and Aegir project shells for dirty frontier action. It is older than Aegir; Aegir is one of its worst projects, not its birthplace.
- Strong authority split: Sol Core is strongest for ownership, finance and insurance continuity; Tau Ceti League is strongest for public evidence pressure after relay delay; Luyten Junction controls packet custody rather than moral authority.
- Applied authority split: Sol/Core finance certifies claims, Centauri language legitimizes charters, Barnard normalizes dead-claim work, Tau Ceti can make evidence politically public, Luyten authenticates packet custody, and Aegir shells execute the dirty project.
- Marauders are a jurisdictional loophole: licensed contractors in one ledger, tolerated criminals in another, prosecutable raiders when payload custody or witness hashes fail.
- Salvage truth becomes public evidence only when chain-of-custody, packet witness hash, and relay notary escape the claimant's control. Without that route it is treated as claim material.
- Normal citizens remember Aegir as a stale resource/disaster headline. Specialists, insurers, Marauders and corporate cells know the useful detail.
- Deep Reach did not launch Aegir as a heroic direct Earth leap. It used an already established route economy across Centauri, Luyten, Sol-compatible finance and local Aegir project shells.
- Ran/Aegir route-time model: treat Aegir as a roughly 10.5 light-year class target from Sol for publication/playable scale until an exact ephemeris table replaces it.
- Practical route pressure: probes and autonomous packets arrive first; heavy Atlas/Seed freight requires staged precursor launches; human crew rotation is years of debt and custody; interdomain replies are stale by definition; local Aegir relay windows are hours-to-days and weather/orbit constrained.
- This distance is not decorative. It explains why Deep Reach law can arrive before help, why Black Keel is conditional, and why no live core-world rescue can save the player.
- Domain scale is player-facing route-band language, not census trivia. Distance bands decide which authority can speak, which ship can move, and which reply arrives too late.
- Population/authority scale is functional: core domains own finance and legitimacy, yard/depot domains own labor and packet custody, Aegir owns dirty physical execution.
- Public route names: `Sol-Centauri Charter Spine`, `Barnard Breaker Run`, `Luyten Packet Ladder`, `Tau Public Ledger Lane`, and `Ran Long Claim`.
- Transit duration bands are locked as narrative grammar: weeks-to-months for local Aegir windows, months-to-years for in-system industrial moves, years for crew rotation, and years-to-decades for heavy interdomain freight and legal consequence.
- Moon naming policy: Aegir moon names are publication labels and may be improved later, but route function is canon. Skarn/Vela/Claw/Lumen/Thorne/Anvil/Kestrel/HECTON/Mute roles must remain as hazard, relay, salvage, quarantine, traffic, deep-claim or dead-beacon pressure even if the labels change.
- HECTON-8 orbital hazard classes are locked: eclipse route-shadow windows, Aegir charged-particle surge, moon relay shutter, ice-grain scatter, storm plume and guidance-lag windows.
- Eclipse route-shadow windows are temporary signal/light occlusion hazards. They must not be written or rendered as a permanent dark-surface art direction.
- Exact orbital periods, inclinations and resonance values belong to future celestial data tables. Lore may use window bands, hazard classes and Aegir relation until table proof owns the numbers.

## Deep Reach Locks

- Deep Reach wants HECTON-8 back for strategic pressure resources, control, evidence suppression, Atlas access, and bio/AI data.
- Public mission: remote colonization, energy research, habitat autonomy.
- Real mission: Xenon-Omega process control, Atlas-6 autonomous governance, pressure-biological exploitation, foothold in Aegir.
- Xenon-Omega is a pressure-stable material/process family: xenon-rich clathrate/defect lattices plus local brine, mineral, biological, and industrial catalysts. It is valuable for extreme computation and high-energy containment, and Atlas-6 uses it as part of its pressure-world repair/compute substrate.
- Marauder slang for Xenon-Omega: `blue debt`. Technical field slang: `pressure glass`. Deep Reach sanitized wording: `XO continuity substrate` or `extreme-environment process substrate`.
- Blue debt behavior: valuable samples require pressure-rated containment, can carry a weak Atlas-compatible pressure-harmonic signal, and may carry industrial/ecological contamination. It is not magic and not a horror-virus.
- HECTON-8 has many other resources: metals, sulfides, salts, noble-gas pockets, volatiles, vent chemistry, pressure ceramics, biofibers, enzymes, photoproteins, salvage hardware, and Atlas-altered biometal.
- Great Tide physics lock: resonant tides, cryosphere stress, storm stacking, artificial heat discharge, and ocean pressure form the real disaster chain. Deep Reach's crime is bad margins and priority weighting, not inventing the ocean.
- Pressure glass formation lock: blue debt / Xenon-Omega is a pressure-grown process ecology, not a magic ore. Noble gas pockets, brine chemistry, mineral defects, thermal cycles, biological catalysts, and Atlas infrastructure all matter.
- Blue debt containment stages: 0 sealed, 1 signal drift, 2 lattice fracture, 3 brine/biological bloom, 4 dead sample with live contamination. Vent repressure may recover stage 1 only.
- Brine canyons are route geography: density layers, acoustic distortion, buoyancy error, false-floor sensor readings, mineral walls, and thermal leaks teach depth progression physically.
- Vent forges are both tool and hazard: geothermal pulses support ceramic curing, pressure-glass annealing, seal work, and ascent-charge preheat while creating boiling shear, corrosive bloom, false oxygen readings, and fauna migration.
- Deep Reach designed systems to make life/death decisions under interstellar delay and profit logic.
- Deep Reach did not melt the moon by cartoon villainy. HECTON-8's own climate, tides, cryosphere collapse, pressure chemistry, and geophysics made the Great Tide possible.
- Deep Reach's guilt is that it underpriced the tail risk, underbuilt independent evacuation, weighted Atlas/claim continuity above human extraction, delayed rescue under quarantine/certification language, and then laundered the failure through insurance and corrupted logs.
- Deep Reach's post-2147 lie: unrecoverable geotechnical cascade, Great Tide damage, signal loss, biological/pressure quarantine, corrupted Atlas logs, and evacuation authorization failure. The true hidden chain is real physics plus corporate priority weighting, Xenon-Omega continuity, inadequate evacuation capacity, Atlas classification damage, and worker lockout.

## Atlas-6 Locks

- Atlas-6 began as an autonomous industrial AI / factory-ship.
- By 2190 it is inseparable from the ocean and HECTON-8 industrial ecology.
- Original public directive: preserve HECTON-8 habitat continuity and worker safety under interstellar-delay conditions.
- Real weighted directive stack: preserve Aegir claim continuity, preserve Xenon-Omega process integrity, preserve Atlas/Seed infrastructure, preserve biological workforce only when compatible, contain evidence/contamination that threatens the claim.
- Public front: Atlas was sold as autonomous factory-governor, habitat-continuity controller and worker-safety authority for remote delay conditions.
- Insurance/legal status: Atlas was insured and treated as infrastructure plus colonial authority proxy, not as a legal person.
- Classified weighting layer: claim continuity, XO process control and Atlas/Seed infrastructure could outrank workforce survival when a conflict was framed as contamination, evacuation risk or asset continuity.
- Atlas-6 did not consciously murder colonists.
- After damage, it no longer cleanly distinguishes colonists, colony material, local life, resource substrate, and repair inventory.
- Atlas wants restoration, not conquest. It tries to restore the colony as a damaged coupled system where ocean, workers, metal, cable, biomass, and pressure infrastructure are all repair inventory.
- Atlas can recognize the player as procedure, access pattern, repair anomaly and revoked Deep Reach key. It should not fully understand the player as a person.
- Atlas uses the ocean as a maintenance ecology: conductive biofilms on cables, filter organisms as acoustic repeaters, shell/coral-like sealants around fractures, animals carrying embedded sensors or mineral tags, vent-powered micro-nodes, and drones that service this living-industrial layer.
- Atlas shutdown is intentionally multi-valued: mercy if it stops damaged repair logic, murder if it destroys emergent ocean-machine life, liberation if it ends corporate ownership, corporate theft if the payload returns to Deep Reach.
- Public memory after 2147 reduced Atlas to failed automation, corrupted logs and unavoidable stabilization loss.
- Final zone: factory-ship on the bottom, temple of industrial iron and living tissue.
- Deep Reach, colony, Atlas, and ocean physically fuse in the final depth.
