# CP01 - Arrival And Shallow Water

Status: source draft.
Packet Family: arrival / survival / bright ecology.
Canon Owner: Lore_Bible, Gameable_World_Packets P01/P03.
Runtime Layer: Narrative for codex/terminal, World for scanner/object labels.
Primary Surfaces: scanner, PDA, first shelter UI, website public.

## Player Experience

The player does not start in a dead horror pit.

They wake in a damaged bathy-drop on a bright, violent shelf. The water is beautiful, shallow enough for color, full of living motion, and still trying to kill them through pressure leaks, broken power, weather, and bad oxygen discipline.

The first emotional contrast:

- outside: bright alien water, reefs, life, Aegir light through storm haze;
- inside: cracked glass, emergency foam, burnt wiring, one dead automated voice, no clean rescue.

## Core Objects

Bathy-Drop Shell:
The player's descent vehicle. It should be usable as a temporary shelter and first evidence object.

Crush Frame:
Bent but not failed. Shows why the player survived and why leaving is not simple.

Emergency Float Crate:
First salvage container. Contains repair staples, battery slab, sealant tape, signal mirror, or small fabrication stock.

Heat Shield Teeth:
Scattered ceramic/ablative plates embedded in reef and sand. Useful salvage and visible crash trail.

First Reef:
Bright ecology. Harvestable, scannable, not corrupted by default.

Broken Uplink Core:
A physical reason the player cannot call the Black Keel cleanly.

Shallow Service Buoy:
Old Deep Reach infrastructure. It can receive weak pings but cannot pass a full rescue request without repair.

## First 30 Minute Beat Chain

1. Wake in damaged bathy-drop.
2. Seal leak or power life support.
3. Recover first tool from float crate or wreck panel.
4. Step into bright shallows.
5. Scan first harmless organism.
6. Find heat-shield debris trail.
7. Reach shallow service buoy or aquaculture frame.
8. Try uplink; receive only partial Black Keel handshake.
9. Discover first Deep Reach public marker.
10. Build/repair temporary shelter route.

## Seeded Variants

Impact Site:
Reef shelf, kelp shallows, storm trench, aquaculture grid, broken service platform.

First Tool:
Cutter, sealant injector, battery tap, portable sonar, manual tether spool.

First Threat:
Weather surge, oxygen leak, small predator, electrical short, low battery, hull breach timer.

First Beauty:
Halo sargassum, bladderfish cloud, kelp raylet, glassworm bloom, shallow bioluminescent wake.

First Lie:
Deep Reach marker claims "temporary suspension zone" or "inactive colony asset" instead of dead colony.

## Text Drafts

Title:
Crash Shelf.

Scanner Short - Bathy-Drop:
Bathy-drop wreckage. Crush frame intact, ascent package destroyed. This craft can keep you alive, not take you home.

Scanner Short - Heat Shield:
Ablative ceramic from the descent burn. Good insulation, bad omen.

Marauder Field Note:
If the drop shell is still holding pressure, do not strip it bare. A bad shelter is worth more than a clean inventory.

PDA Codex Body:
The bathy-drop was built for one violent job: enter atmosphere, survive storm shear, hit ocean, shed heat, and keep one operator alive long enough to begin work. It was never a ship. It was a disposable argument with physics.

The Black Keel is still above the moon somewhere in Aegir traffic, but the drop's uplink core is cracked and the ascent hardware is gone. A clean rescue requires power, timing, an orbital window, and a transmitter that can push through weather, water, and Aegir's magnetic noise.

Terminal Fragment - Drop Diagnostic:
ASCENT PACKAGE: missing.
UPLINK CORE: fracture detected.
PRESSURE SHELL: partial.
MISSION HANDOFF: incomplete.
CARRIER CONTACT: delayed.

Audio Beat:
Black Keel handshake received. Packet integrity below recovery threshold. Repeat: recovery threshold not met.

Website Public:
HECTON-8 begins at the waterline: a damaged descent capsule, a bright alien shelf, and a carrier too distant to answer cleanly. The first mystery is not what lives below, but why the rescue chain already feels hostile.

## Gameplay Use

This pack supports the first survival loop:

- get air stable;
- get tool access;
- learn scanning;
- see beauty before horror;
- establish Black Keel distance;
- establish Deep Reach sanitized language;
- make the player feel stranded without removing agency.
