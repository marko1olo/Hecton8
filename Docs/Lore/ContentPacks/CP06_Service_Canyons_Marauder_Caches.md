# CP06 - Service Canyons And Marauder Caches

Status: source draft.
Packet Family: route salvage / other scavengers / mid-depth navigation.
Canon Owner: HECTON8_Field_Atlas Z03, Gameable_World_Packets P10.

Primary Surfaces: scanner, Marauder note, route map, cache inventory.

## Player Experience

The player starts finding evidence of other post-disaster professionals.

Not armies. Not NPC crews walking around. Caches, bad maps, cut tethers, private marks, sealed lockers, and route warnings from people who came for money and learned part of the truth.

## Core Objects

Dead Claim Buoy:
Old salvage marker with corrupted owner ID and half-valid route tags.

Marauder Cache:
Hidden locker with tools, bad maps, warnings, sample scraps, or bait.

Cut Tether:
Physical trace of a failed route or deliberate abandonment.

Route Scratch Map:
Low-confidence map of a canyon, pipeline, or drone-safe path.
