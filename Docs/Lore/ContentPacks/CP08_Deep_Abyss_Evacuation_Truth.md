Status: source draft.

## Player Experience

The deep abyss should feel empty until the player finds records that make it crowded with absence.

This is where the disaster stops being environmental and becomes procedural: holds, routes, priorities, locks, and authorization delays that killed people while preserving process.

## Core Objects

Evacuation Manifest:
Names, categories, route holds, medical delays, substrate protection entries.

Crushed Transit Gate:
Physical route that should have moved people and did not.

Medical Lock:
Evidence of triage delayed by command routing.

Black Box Stack:
Deep record package, partly corrupted, valuable for evidence payload.
