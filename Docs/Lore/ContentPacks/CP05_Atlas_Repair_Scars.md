## Player Experience

The first Atlas scar should not explain itself.

The player finds a place where repair happened wrong: coral grown over cable with deliberate spacing, a station wall sealed with tissue and resin, a drone carrying living matter as patch stock, an animal with an industrial insert that works too well to be random.

The horror is not "machine plus meat." The horror is purpose.

## Core Objects

Cable Kelp:
Biota grown around power/data lines. Sometimes natural opportunism, sometimes guided repair.

Drone Resin:
Industrial sealant mixed with biological matrix. Used to patch cracks, doors, hull, pipes, and bodies.

Misclassified Remains:
Human or animal remains incorporated as material, not trophies.

Repair Drone Nest:
Maintenance cavity where drones sort scrap, shells, tissue, caskets, and metal by damaged continuity rules.

Atlas Signal Scar:
Zone where scanner data returns category errors, inventory language, or impossible repair classifications.

## Beat Chain

1. Player sees ambiguous cable/biota overlap.
2. Later finds a deliberate repair pattern.
3. Scanner marks impossible category match.
4. Drone behavior proves it is maintaining, not hunting.
5. Atlas fragment reveals damaged classification language.
6. Deep zones show the same logic at building scale.

## Text Drafts

Title:
Repair Scar.

Scanner Short - Cable Kelp:
Biological growth follows cable geometry with non-random spacing. Energy leakage alone does not explain the pattern.

Scanner Short - Drone Resin:
Sealant contains industrial polymer, coral protein, and processed tissue. Applied in layers.

Marauder Field Note:
Bad welds look messy. This does not. That is the problem.

PDA Codex Body:
Atlas repair scars mark places where maintenance logic crossed categories it was never meant to merge. The system still identifies leaks, broken routes, pressure loss, exposed substrate, failing habitat walls, and missing material. It no longer reliably distinguishes cable from root, shell from housing, animal from tool, corpse from spare stock, or colony from process.

The result is not random growth. It is practical. It seals. It conducts. It routes. It preserves. That practicality is what makes it worse.

Atlas Fragment:
LEAK: closed.
ROUTE: restored.
MATERIAL: compatible.
LIFE STATUS: unresolved.
CONTINUITY: improved.

Deep Reach Fragment:
Maintenance automation has entered an unsupervised classification mode. Do not interrupt active repairs unless substrate continuity is threatened.

Audio Beat:
It patched the door with something that had bones in it. Door holds pressure, though.

Website Archive:
Atlas repair scars are the first undeniable sign that the bottom of HECTON-8 is not a ruined base. It is a working system with broken definitions.
