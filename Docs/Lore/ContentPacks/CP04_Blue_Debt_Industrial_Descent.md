# CP04 - Blue Debt And Industrial Descent

Status: source draft.
Packet Family: resource temptation / industrial descent / pressure material.
Canon Owner: Xenon_Omega, Blue_Debt_Field_Behavior, HECTON8_Resource_Catalog.
Runtime Layer: Narrative for notes, World for scanner/resource records.
Primary Surfaces: scanner, inventory, contract screen, terminal, ending dossier.

## Player Experience

Blue debt must feel valuable before it feels cursed.

The player finds it in pressure containers, mining scars, thermal processing stacks, broken Atlas-compatible hardware, and deep industrial caches. It is not glowing magic ore. It is a difficult material/process residue that only stays useful under hostile conditions.

The temptation:

- it sells well;
- it unlocks upgrades;
- it attracts contract pressure;
- it changes signal behavior;
- mishandling it can damage ecology, gear, and endings.

## Core Objects

Pressure Casket:
Small sealed container with readable warnings, old serials, and cold exterior frost or pressure skin.

Substrate Scrape:
Mineral/process residue in a scar, pipe, or reactor throat.

Stabilizer Clamp:
Device used to keep a sample from decompression damage.

Blue Debt Buyer Tag:
Contract marker proving somebody wanted the sample more than rescue.

Contaminated Fauna:
Not a monster category by itself. An organism carrying industrial intrusion or pressure-material exposure.

## Beat Chain

1. Player sees sealed pressure casket before understanding value.
2. Scanner warns not to open in warm/low-pressure environment.
3. Black Keel or contract system requests sample state.
4. Player can stabilize, sell, hide, use, or vent.
5. Deeper zones reveal why Deep Reach and Atlas needed it.
6. Endings depend on what payload leaves.

Blue debt pays because it bites twice. Once when you carry it. Again when somebody learns you have it.

PDA Codex Body:
"Blue debt" is Marauder slang for pressure-kept Xenon-Omega material and related process residue. It is not a single mineral. It is a family of pressure-grown lattices, clathrate structures, contaminated substrates, and industrial leftovers that Deep Reach used for high-energy containment, extreme computation, and Atlas-compatible infrastructure.

The useful state matters. A sample that looks inert on the seafloor can become worthless, dangerous, or noisy after decompression. Good salvage is not just finding it. Good salvage is keeping it in the condition that made it valuable.
