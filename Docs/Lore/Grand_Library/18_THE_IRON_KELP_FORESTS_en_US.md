# THE IRON KELP FORESTS

> **Source:** Deep Reach Xenobiology Division, Hazard Log.
> **Speaker:** Automated Botanical Analyst.
> **Reviewer Note:** The bench file on the flora that kills more Marauders than the predators do, with its loss-cause fields intact.

---

## 1. Sample Class And Growth

Iron kelp keys out as a chemotrophic colonial organism with a mineralized fibre skeleton. Sample IK-114 came off a vent apron on the sinter-line approach and has never been reclassified.

It does not photosynthesize. It pulls sulfide salts and dissolved metal ions out of the vent field and lays them down as fibre, which is where the mass comes from. Bench values on IK-114: frond length 31 m, dry mass 84 kg, breaking load 41 kN at mid-frond and 6 kN at the holdfast. The holdfast is the weak point, and the holdfast is the part buried in silt.

Colour runs rust to near-black with the metal available. The fronds move continuously in current, and a mature stand grinds against itself loudly enough that the first survey pass logged it as running machinery.

## 2. The Hazard Of Entanglement

This log ranks entanglement as the leading loss cause inside kelp volume, above the pressure, thermal and predation classes together.

Fronds foul joint cages, tool ports and thruster guards. Load from a servo joint draws the frond tighter instead of shearing it, because 41 kN sits above any assist tolerance a Class-IV is certified to run. Case IK-114-C7: contractor recovered after 41 minutes, envelope intact, wafer lot spent, both arms fouled at the elbow cage. The loss-cause field reads ENVIRONMENTAL. In the annotation column beside it, a hand has written the tool that would have worked, and where: cutter contact, holdfast, not frond.

Crews now cut low and blind in the silt, and clear the return line before the first cut. This Division has never issued that procedure.

## 3. An Abyssal Hunting Ground

Kelp volume anchors the local food web, which is also why the Division's predation returns were all collected there. Prey concentrates where the fibre is thickest.

The thickets hide mass. Trench Stalkers hold inside the fronds and strike from about a body length, and the grinding of the fronds covers small-movement acoustics well enough that a passive listen comes back empty. Sub-40 Hz carrier events logged in kelp volume have no matching body on any imaging pass in this file.

## 4. Corporate Harvesting

Harvest contracts for kelp fibre still issue as secondary work against the same claim.

Processed fibre goes into backing cloth and tile binder for ablative layups, including the heat-tile stacks on Styx descent shells, and into corrosion-rated cable jacket. Both certifications, heat and corrosion, were written off sample IK-114: one frond, cut on a bench, in air.

Payment posts against the lien as recovered mass, and recovered mass is weighed dry. A bundle cut wet is carried wet and billed after the receiving desk has spun it. Contractors who cut the frond instead of the holdfast bring up more mass, more fouling damage, and a return card with a longer equipment column than the payout line.
