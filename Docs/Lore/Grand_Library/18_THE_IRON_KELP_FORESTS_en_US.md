# THE IRON KELP FORESTS

> **Source:** Deep Reach Xenobiology Division, Hazard Log.
> **Speaker:** Automated Botanical Analyst.
> **Reviewer Note:** An analysis of the seemingly harmless, yet incredibly lethal, abyssal flora of HECTON-8.

---

## 1. What is Iron Kelp?

Iron Kelp is not a plant in the traditional terrestrial sense; it is a metallic, chemotrophic organism that thrives in the crushing pressure of the abyssal trenches.

It does not rely on sunlight for photosynthesis. Instead, it metabolizes the highly radioactive Xenon-Omega particulate suspended in the water, converting it into dense, metallic bio-fibers. A single frond of Iron Kelp can grow up to thirty meters in length and possesses the tensile strength of braided steel cable. It is rust-colored, incredibly heavy, and constantly undulating in the deep-sea currents.

## 2. The Hazard of Entanglement

To a Marauder, a forest of Iron Kelp is a death trap.

The kelp grows incredibly fast, especially around the active hydrothermal vents where the Xenon-Omega concentration is highest. The primary danger it poses is entanglement. The motorized servos of a Class-IV diving suit are powerful, but they can easily become fouled if metallic kelp fronds wrap around the joints or the hydro-jet thrusters. Once a Marauder is entangled, struggling only causes the kelp to wrap tighter. Many Marauders have slowly suffocated on the ocean floor, hopelessly bound by the heavy metallic vines while their oxygen scrubbers slowly ran out.

## 3. An Abyssal Hunting Ground

The Iron Kelp forests act as the foundation of the local ecosystem rather than a mere navigational hazard.

The dense, metallic thickets provide perfect camouflage for the apex predators of HECTON-8. Trench Stalkers frequently use the kelp forests as ambush points, weaving their chitinous bodies through the fronds to strike at unwary Marauders. The constant grinding sound of the metallic fronds rubbing against each other in the current masks the sonar signatures of approaching Leviathans, making early detection nearly impossible.

## 4. Corporate Harvesting

Despite the extreme danger, Deep Reach occasionally issues secondary contracts for the harvesting of Iron Kelp.

The metallic bio-fibers of the kelp are highly valued in aerospace manufacturing due to their natural resistance to radiation and extreme heat. In fact, the ablative heat shielding used on the Styx drop pods is woven from processed Iron Kelp. A Marauder who takes the time to harvest a bundle of kelp using their thermal lance can earn a significant bonus to their ledger, provided they survive the attempt and don't attract a Stalker in the process.
