# THE ABYSSAL MINING RIGS

> **Source:** Deep Reach Extraterrestrial Development Combine, Heavy Machinery and Salvage.  
> **Speaker:** Automated Asset Tracker.  
> **Reviewer Note:** The crawler fleet register with its disposition codes intact. Nothing on it has been built since 2147.

---

## 1. Mobile Extraction Crawlers

Asset class MEC. Thirty-one units on this register, all commissioned before 2147, none after.

A Mobile Extraction Crawler is a self-propelled refinery on spiked track, rated for abyssal pressure and sized to work a vent apron without returning to dock. Spec sheet on MEC-14, the pattern most of the fleet followed: track gauge 21 m, sinter line on the second deck, sealed fission-electric plant, rated berths 96, cargo torpedo tubes 4. Refined material left the hull upward by torpedo. Personnel left by hoist, on a rotation set against the sinter line rather than against the shift.

## 2. The Crawling Graveyard

Disposition on 27 of 31 units reads STRANDED, NOT RECOVERED.

The hulls sit where they stopped. Plant containment on the older units is degrading and several are venting warm coolant, which is a containment fault and a power-leak fault in that order. Where the leak is electrical, iron kelp has rooted on the hull and conductive biofilm has grown along the exposed trunk runs: Atlas maintenance routing reads a live conductor as a repair surface without regard for what the conductor is bolted to. Trench Stalkers den in the cover that produces. Crews call the densest concentration of these hulls the Crawling Graveyard. This register calls it grid block 7.

## 3. High-Risk Salvage

Asset Reclamation contracts issue against stranded units through the claim pool.

Scope is entry, cut, and recovery of certified hardware: receiver coil sets, pressure-rated switching plate, clamp-ring stock, and any process plate off the sinter line carrying custody-grade pressure history. Sealed plant cartridges remain company property under custody hold and may not be moved. That clause is enforced by the receiving desk, not by the water.

Contractor loss rate against this contract class is entered as 0.85. Payout posts as recovered mass against the lien, and the recovered mass on a coil set is small. Crews take the work because a coil set is certifiable, and a certifiable object moves lien mass in a way that a beautiful dead machine never does.

## 4. The Hollow Rigs

Four units are entered as CEASED TRANSMIT, CAUSE NOT ESTABLISHED.

MEC-6, MEC-19 and MEC-22 were located intact. No external breach, no plate deformation, blast doors sealed from the inside on the internal latch, no remains recovered from any of the three. On MEC-19 the door tracks carry shell sealant growth 40 mm thick, laid over the latch and cured hard. The route stamp board inside shows every card returned to its hook mid-shift.

MEC-30 has not been located. Its last packet is a plant status line and a manifest update. That update reclassified the unit's onboard inventory, and in the personnel column it entered the crew as material, at unit mass. The Atlas governor holds write access to manifests. That is the limit of what this register can establish.
