# THE ABYSSAL MINING RIGS

> **Source:** Deep Reach Megacorporation, Heavy Machinery and Salvage.  
> **Speaker:** Automated Asset Tracker.  
> **Reviewer Note:** Dead behemoths littering the sea floor. We don't build them anymore; we just strip the corpses.

---

## 1. Mobile Extraction Crawlers

Before the catastrophic collapse of Sector 44, Deep Reach utilized colossal, nuclear-powered Mobile Extraction Crawlers to harvest Xenon-Omega. 

These massive rigs, the size of city blocks, crawled along the ocean floor on reinforced, spiked treads. They were designed to endure the crushing pressure of the abyss and withstand attacks from all but the largest Leviathans. Housing crews of over two hundred personnel, the Crawlers acted as mobile refineries, dredging the sea floor and processing raw Xenon-Omega on-site before firing it to the surface via automated cargo torpedoes. 

## 2. The Crawling Graveyard

The golden age of the Crawlers ended when the local fauna began to adapt. 

Today, the ocean floor is littered with the rotting hulks of dozens of these abandoned rigs. Their massive nuclear reactors, left unmaintained for decades, are slowly breaching, leaking intense, localized radiation into the water. This radiation has aggressively mutated the surrounding ecosystem, accelerating the growth of the metallic Iron Kelp forests and spawning hyper-aggressive variants of Trench Stalkers that use the irradiated husks of the Crawlers as nesting grounds. The area where the highest concentration of these dead rigs lies is known to Marauders simply as the "Crawling Graveyard."

## 3. High-Risk Salvage

Deep Reach never abandons valuable assets entirely. 

While raw Xenon-Omega is the primary objective of most drops, experienced Marauders are occasionally given high-risk "Asset Reclamation" contracts. Their objective is to navigate the irradiated, monster-infested ruins of an abandoned Crawler, cut through the blast doors, and manually extract the highly valuable, weapons-grade fissile material from the decaying reactor cores. These missions have a staggering 85% fatality rate, but the corporate payout applied to the Marauder's Debt Ledger is often enough to buy their family an extra decade of rations on the core worlds.

## 4. The "Hollow Rigs"

Not all Crawlers were destroyed by Leviathans or structural failure. 

Corporate salvage logs detail at least three instances where a Crawler simply stopped transmitting. When salvage teams eventually located the rigs, they found the outer titanium hulls completely intact, with no signs of external breach or combat. The internal blast doors were sealed from the inside. However, the entire crew of two hundred was simply gone. There were no bodies, no blood, and no signs of struggle—only deep, inexplicable claw marks gouged into the *inside* of the heavily reinforced airlock doors, suggesting whatever took the crew was already inside the rig with them.
