# THE COMMUNICATION BLACKOUT AND ORBITAL SILENCE

> **Source:** *Black Keel* Orbital Tender Operations Manual, Section 4.  
> **Speaker:** Communications Officer J. Vance.  
> **Reviewer note:** A stark explanation of why Marauders operate in near-total isolation from the surface.

---

## 1. The Physics of the Blackout

The ocean of HECTON-8 is not composed of pure H2O. It is heavily laced with dissolved heavy metals, ionic salts, and particulate Xenon-Omega dust. This specific chemical soup acts as an absolute Faraday cage. 

Electromagnetic radiation—including all standard radio, microwave, and laser communication—cannot penetrate past a depth of 200 meters. The abandoned colony, Sector 44, sits at a depth of 8,500 meters. From the perspective of the orbital tender *Black Keel*, the ocean floor is a completely black box.

## 2. Acoustic Telemetry

Because radio is useless, the only way to communicate with the surface is via Acoustic Telemetry. The Styx descent pods are equipped with massive sonar arrays that translate digital data into low-frequency acoustic pulses. 

The transmission rate is agonizingly slow—approximately 300 bits per second. A Marauder cannot stream video or send complex telemetry. They can only send basic text strings, suit pressure warnings, and encrypted payroll signatures. The delay between sending a distress ping from the ocean floor and receiving a response from orbit is roughly eight minutes. In a flooded compartment, eight minutes is an eternity.

## 3. The "Silent Orbit" Protocol

Deep Reach executives recognized that acoustic transmissions could be intercepted by rival corporate spy satellites. To protect the intellectual property of the Xenon-Omega extraction methods, the *Black Keel* operates under a strict "Silent Orbit" protocol.

The orbital tender will never initiate a transmission. It will only "ping back" an automated receipt when a Marauder uploads a completed salvage manifest. There is no human operator on the other end to offer encouragement, tactical advice, or rescue coordination. When a Marauder speaks into their comms channel, they are speaking into an automated void. 

## 4. The Severance

The acoustic tether is a lifeline on paper. In practice, it is a corporate leash.

If a Marauder discovers something that violates the Contractor Silence Protocol—such as evidence of the 843 dead workers, or proof that Atlas-6 intentionally drowned the colony—the *Black Keel's* automated systems will detect the flagged keywords in the acoustic stream. 

When this happens, the orbital tender does not argue. It does not threaten. It simply issues an explosive bolt command to the Styx pod. The acoustic tether is severed. The oxygen supply is cut. The Marauder is permanently erased from the ledger, swallowed by the silence of the deep.
