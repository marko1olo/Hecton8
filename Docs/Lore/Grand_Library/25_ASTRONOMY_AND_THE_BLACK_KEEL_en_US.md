# ASTRONOMY AND THE BLACK KEEL

> **Source:** Deep Reach Extraterrestrial Development Combine, Orbital Mechanics and Infrastructure.  
> **Speaker:** Black Keel station archive, custody-safe public mirror.  
> **Reviewer Note:** A claim tender above a water grave is still a cage. It just has better paperwork.

---

## 1. The HECTON System

HECTON-8 is an ocean moon in the Aegir system. Ran is the star. Aegir is the giant. HECTON-8 moves inside Aegir's moon ladder, where radiation belts, moon occlusions, storm plumes, relay shadows and insured traffic windows make the sky above the water into a moving machine.

From the Black Keel, the view is not empty rescue space. Skarn can dirty antennas with charged noise. Vela can shutter a relay path with ice dust. Kestrel can stand in a line of sight at the wrong minute. HECTON's own weather can throw plume through an ascent corridor that looked clean on the previous table. The astronomy matters because it decides when a packet is witnessed, when a body can lift, and when a claim remains stranded despite being true.

## 2. The Black Keel

The *Black Keel* is not a heroic orbital city. It is an Aegir claim tender and custody platform with habitats bolted to its work. Its clean public diagrams show modular berths, quarantine tanks, route witness racks, launch cradles, cargo meters and receiver-account consoles. Its lived reality is narrower: patched pressure corridors, overused scrubbers, suit lockers that smell of salt and disinfectant, and debtors sleeping close enough to hear the claim printers wake before shift change.

Black Keel does not own rescue. It owns the local accounting surface that can make recovery legible to somebody else's receiver.

## 3. Orbital Isolation

The people on the Black Keel are stranded in a practical, contractual sense. The station cannot leave Aegir on its own. It depends on scheduled nearlight haulers, receiver slots, custody windows and route insurance that were priced before the current emergency existed. A cargo arrival can bring cold-sleep labor, synthetic supplies, replacement modules and sealed orders. It cannot become an instant rescue ship because a corridor worker starts suffocating or a Marauder revolt makes the ledger untidy.

If life support fails, if a quarantine class changes, or if a recovery packet arrives during the wrong window, the station can shout into the chain and still wait. Help is not two hours away. It is behind beam schedules, braking slots, quarantine tanks, claim signatures and the thermal budget of receivers that are not emotionally available.

## 4. The View of the Abyss

The gravity well of HECTON-8 dominates the view from the lower decks of the Black Keel. There are no continents, no islands, no breaks in the water. It is a singular, endless, churning black ocean, obscured by violent, swirling atmospheric storms. Marauders spend their "rest" cycles in the observation lounges, looking down into the dark abyss. They know that somewhere down there, beneath miles of crushing water, are the ruins of Sector 44, the rotting husks of the Crawlers, and the Leviathans that wait for the next Styx Drop Pod to plunge into their domain. It is a view that drives many Marauders mad before they ever even put on a diving suit.
