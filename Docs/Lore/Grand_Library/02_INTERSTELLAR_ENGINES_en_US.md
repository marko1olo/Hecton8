## 1. No Magic, Only Physics
In the HECTON-8 universe, there are **NO Faster-Than-Light (FTL) drives**. There are no jump gates, hyperspace, ansibles (instant communication), or reactionless drives that violate the conservation of momentum.

Humanity's expansion is slow, difficult, and costs colossal amounts of money. This is exactly why "deep space" belongs to megacorporations—only they can afford to wait decades for a return on their investments.

## 2. Ship and Engine Types
Interstellar logistics relies on three main types of transit:

### A. Beam-Assisted Probes and Autonomous Packets
The first to arrive in the Aegir system (HECTON-8) were automated probes. They were accelerated using colossal orbital pellet-beam lasers installed in the Solar System or at intermediate nodes like Luyten Junction. Probes are small, fast, unmanned, and cheap enough to "throw" across a route before anyone confirms it's safe. It was these probes that brought back the first data about the Xenon-Omega isotope.

### B. Fusion-Pulse / Fusion-Electric Cargo Craft
Heavy colonial ships of the "Atlas" class, carrying factories, infrastructure, and a crew sleeping in torpor, use fusion-electric or pulse engines. The flight to the Aegir system takes years.
Such a ship is literally a "mortgage with engines." The corporation does not run a heavy transport just to rescue one or two marauders. A ship is only dispatched when the route is profitable.

### C. Magsails and Aerobraking
The main problem with near-light speed flight isn't accelerating, but stopping. Deep Reach's heavy ships deploy giant magnetic sails (Magsails) stretching for hundreds of kilometers to brake against the interstellar medium as they approach the Aegir system. In the final stage, they use aerodynamic braking against the atmospheres of gas giants or the dense storm envelope of HECTON-8 itself.

## 3. Transit and Rescue Doctrine
Because there is no FTL travel, rescue from the core of human worlds is **impossible**. If a disaster occurs on HECTON-8, the response from Earth or Tau Ceti will arrive 10-15 years later.

This is precisely why Deep Reach deployed the Atlas-6 AI as a "Factory-governor." It was supposed to maintain the colony's autonomy.
When a marauder descends to the ocean surface, they are dropped from an orbital tender (Black Keel) in a "Styx" class pod. This pod is designed for a one-way descent. Its frame deforms under pressure, and its avionics flood. To return to orbit, the marauder must find, repair, or steal thermochemical ascent charges from the dead colony.

You are not a passenger. You do not own a ship. You are expendable material at the end of a very long, very slow rope stretched across the void.
