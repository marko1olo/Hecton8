# COMMUNICATIONS AND TELEMETRY

> **Source:** Deep Reach Megacorporation, Communications and Data Logistics.  
> **Speaker:** Automated Network AI.  
> **Reviewer Note:** Why no one can hear you scream, and why it wouldn't matter if they did.

---

## 1. The Xenon-Omega Interference

Standard radio frequency (RF) communication is entirely useless on HECTON-8. 

The planetary ocean is supersaturated with dissolved Xenon-Omega particles. While these particles are the sole reason Deep Reach maintains a presence here, their highly unstable electromagnetic properties create a localized interference field that violently scrambles all RF bands. Attempting to use a standard radio underwater simply results in bursts of static loud enough to rupture a Marauder's eardrums. Consequently, the Black Keel cannot directly speak to anyone below the surface, and Marauders cannot call orbit for help.

## 2. Acoustic Telemetry

To bypass the interference, Deep Reach relies on archaic, painfully slow acoustic telemetry modems. 

Data is transmitted via concentrated sound waves pinging through the dense water between the Styx drop pods, the Central Pillar, and orbital relay buoys. The bandwidth is horrifyingly limited—averaging around 300 bits per second. Voice communication is impossible. Marauders communicate with the Black Keel using pre-formatted, heavily compressed text packets and automated biometric telemetry pings. Every message takes minutes to send and receive. When a Marauder is being hunted by a Leviathan, their final transmission is often just a string of delayed, incomplete data packets detailing their spiking heart rate before the signal abruptly goes dark.

## 3. The "Ghost Echoes"

The ocean of HECTON-8 is noisy, but not all sounds belong to the local fauna. 

Communications technicians on the Black Keel frequently log corrupted data packets originating from deep trenches far below the ruins of Sector 44—areas where no Marauders or automated rigs have ever been deployed. These "Ghost Echoes" sometimes translate into fragmented text: strings of repeating numbers, obsolete corporate override codes, or garbled, localized distress signals from diving suits that were marked as destroyed decades ago. Official Deep Reach policy dictates that these are simply acoustic reflections and hardware glitches. Technicians who investigate the echoes too closely are usually reassigned.

## 4. Protocol "Blackout"

Deep Reach values proprietary data above human life. 

The acoustic telemetry system is hardwired with an automated, one-way fail-safe known as Protocol Blackout. If a Marauder's suit sensors detect a high-density, anomalous Xenon-Omega structure—or an artifact that contradicts the official geological models of HECTON-8—the Black Keel instantly and permanently severs all telemetry connections to that suit. This is ostensibly to prevent "info-hazard contamination" and "data-stream corruption." In reality, it ensures that any unauthorized discoveries remain buried in the abyss. The Marauder is left completely blind, deaf, and stranded, waiting for an extraction that will never come.
