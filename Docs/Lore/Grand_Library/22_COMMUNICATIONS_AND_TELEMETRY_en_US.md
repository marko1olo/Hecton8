# COMMUNICATIONS AND TELEMETRY

> **Source:** Deep Reach Extraterrestrial Development Combine, Communications and Data Logistics.  
> **Speaker:** Automated Network AI.  
> **Reviewer Note:** The network's own account of why the water is quiet, including the clause that keeps it quieter.

---

## 1. Why Radio Fails

Radio frequency service is not provisioned below the surface at this site and no waiver for one has ever been issued.

The cause is the water. This ocean is cold, conductive, mineral-heavy, and carries suspended metal dust off four decades of unmaintained industry. Attenuation certification for the standard suit RF set records usable range at 3.1 m at 100 m depth and no usable range below the shelf. The set is still fitted to every Class-IV because the harness is common stock, and most contractors still key it once on arrival.

The carrier therefore cannot address anyone below the surface directly. Whatever reaches Black Keel out of the water reaches it as sound.

## 2. Acoustic Telemetry

Service runs by acoustic modem: hull transducer to relay, relay to the Support 3 riser antenna, riser to orbital receiver.

Rated throughput on the contractor lane is 300 bits per second, degrading with duct state, brine layering and vent shear. That class of bandwidth carries pre-formatted packets and biometric pings. Voice is provisioned only through a certified pod uplink above 200 m, in clipped four-second frames, and the frame budget posts against the descent.

Round trip on a nominal window is minutes. Where the lane degrades, the network delivers what arrived and marks the gap. Recovered logs from loss events read accordingly: heart rate climbing, suit pressure delta, wafer state, then a sequence number with no payload behind it. The last complete field in most of them is the biometric one, because the biometric lane holds priority over the text lane. That priority was set to protect the record.

## 3. The Ghost Echoes

The network logs unattributed inbound packets from below the surveyed volume, where no contractor and no rig is deployed.

The strings are short and repetitive. Verbatim, from the log: `44-P-118 44-P-118 44-P-118`, a door identifier from a level flooded since 2147; `OVERRIDE CLASS 2 REQUEST` in a code format retired before the collapse; and three suit beacon IDs whose equipment records were settled as destroyed. One string carries a partial custody hash that still validates against the original issue.

Standing classification is ACOUSTIC REFLECTION / HARDWARE FAULT. That classification has never been reviewed, because review requires a physical inspection request and inspection requests for the volume route through the Quarantine Review Gate. Two technicians filed the reflection classification as inconsistent with a validating hash. Both of their transfer orders are in this file, dated the same shift.

## 4. Custody Hold

Telemetry service is conditional, and the condition sits in the contract rather than in the hardware.

Under the custody hold clause, a suit that reports an unlisted high-density Xenon-Omega structure, or any structure inconsistent with the filed geological model, has its lane suspended pending review. Suspension is automatic. Review is not: it opens at the Quarantine Review Gate, consumes a receiver slot, and is scheduled by interval.

The interval is longer than a suit's endurance. The clause does not claim otherwise and does not use the word abandon. The lane is described as held, held lanes are restored in full when the review closes, and the restoration report files against the suit ID.

Contractors who have come back from a hold describe the same sequence: the carrier acknowledges, the acknowledgement repeats on cadence, the packet count stops advancing. The Asset Silence Board summary of the clause runs to one line. PROTECTS FILED MODEL INTEGRITY.
