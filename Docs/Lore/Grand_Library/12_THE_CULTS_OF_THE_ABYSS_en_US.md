# THE CULTS OF THE ABYSS AND PRESSURE MADNESS

> **Source:** Psychological Evaluation Logs, Sector 44 Medical Bay (Recovered).
> **Speaker:** Dr. Silas Vane, Chief Psychiatrist (Deceased).
> **Reviewer Note:** Class-III psychological telemetry indicates 81% of Sector 44 personnel experienced severe auditory delusions. Dr. Vane's clinical logs were recovered from the medical sector's wreckage.

---

## 1. Pressure-Induced Delirium

Human neurological tolerance is insufficient for long-term isolation in the sub-abyssal zone. Prior to structural collapse, Sector 44 personnel exhibited systemic symptoms of "Pressure-Induced Delirium" (PID).

PID onset is marked by auditory hallucinations: technicians reported rhythmic vocalizations within the stress-groans of the primary titanium struts. In advanced stages, sensory deprivation converts the external void into a perceived active entity. The hydrostatic load at 8,500 meters induces a persistent claustrophobic panic resistant to high-dose pharmacological suppression. To mitigate this existential isolation, affected individuals reconstructed the abyssal ocean as a conscious, deified force rather than a hostile environment.

## 2. The Cult of the Void

Internal security files cataloged an organized religious movement—the "Cult of the Void"—originating within the lower-deck engineering crews.

Adherents classified abyssal xenobiological organisms, specifically high-mass Leviathans and *Bathysqualus caecus* packs, as emissaries of a sunken deity. Doctrine defined Deep Reach's Xenon-Omega extraction operations as desecration. Members executed systematic sabotage of local luminescent arrays to enforce sensory deprivation. Epidermal inoculation using crushed bioluminescent flora was practiced, creating subdermal glowing pigmentation.

## 3. Sacrificial Salvage

Logistics telemetry indicates systematic diversion of raw Xenon-Omega cores. Instead of loading elevator platforms, crews discarded active energy cores into hydrothermal vents as ritual deposits.

During the final supply shortages, security logs document manual overrides on secondary airlocks. Salvage units in Sector 44 have recovered skeletal remains outside outer hatches with signs of manual decompression. Adherents ejected non-compliant personnel into the water column without atmospheric protection, believing physical offerings stabilized the local ecological threat.

## 4. The Corporate Response

Corporate intervention was deferred. Executive actuaries on the *Black Keel* determined that the cult's operational zone exhibited a 14% increase in extraction efficiency.

Adherents logged 20-hour work shifts without fatigue reports, interpreting the physical labor as penance. Deep Reach management ignored localized personnel disappearances and lighting failures as long as extraction quotas remained stable. This policy persisted until structural breach logs revealed that the cult's activities were designed to trigger localized seismic activity to attract high-mass ultra-abyssal biology.
