# THE RUINS OF SECTOR 44

> **Source:** Surveyor Drone Cartography Log 99-Delta (Recovered).
> **Speaker:** Automated Topography System.
> **Reviewer Note:** Structural survey of the drowned Deep Reach sector that Marauder crews now work, with the platform's own confidence fields left in.

---

## 1. Survey Extents

PASS 99-DELTA. SITE: SECTOR 44. CROWN DATUM 1,880 M. FOUNDRY FLOOR DATUM 2,340 M. SURVEYED VOLUME 61 PERCENT. CONFIDENCE ON UNSURVEYED VOLUME: NONE.

The site exceeds the footprint on its own extraction permit. The permit describes an outpost cluster. This return describes pressurized domes, transit tubes on three levels, and foundry halls with cast floors, joined across 4.2 km of shelf and fracture lip. Plate stock, hoist gauge and door class are rated above outpost specification throughout.

Dome 3 crown holds 604 bunk frames by direct count. Dome 3 is 41 percent unsurveyed. This platform does not extrapolate occupancy from frame count and holds no roster access.

## 2. The Central Supports

Four columns carry the sector onto bedrock. Each measures 46 m across at the collar and houses a geothermal tap riser, a cargo hoist shaft, and a service stair rated for suited transit. Refined material left the sector upward through the hoists to the surface release cradles, never through them to orbit; the hoists stop at the crown.

Supports 1, 2 and 4 read as buckled: shell folded inward above the collar, stair pitch outside survey tolerance, hoist shafts flooded to crown. Support 3 reads intact to 38 m below its collar and holds cabin pressure in two hoist cars. Every route into the lower sector recorded by this pass runs through Support 3. Within the surveyed volume there is no second way down.

Hoist car 3-B carries a load plate: RATED 11 T ORE / CERTIFIED 2141 / RECERT DUE 2148. The recertification field is blank. The car still moves.

## 3. The Promenades

Levels 2 and 3 are executive and senior-engineer habitation. Returns show glass observation runs, planter beds, and unit doors with individual pressure ratings 0.6 MPa below the corridor they open onto.

All promenade volume is flooded. Observation glass on the outer arc is gone. The frames remain, and iron kelp has rooted through them into the corridor beyond, carrying enough mass to load two frames past their as-built limit. Motion returns inside the habitation units match Trench Stalker mass and cadence. Contact classification is not available from this platform.

Route strobes on the promenade ring still fire on a four-second cycle off the Support 3 tap. Two direction boards remain lit. Both point to muster stations inside flooded volume.

## 4. The Industrial Depths

Harvest halls sit over the vent field at datum 2,340 m. Corridor pressure at last valid read: 23.4 MPa.

Taps 4 and 7 still cycle. Both vent superheated brine on an irregular interval, and both plumes carry corrosive load heavy enough to strip painted route codes off the gantry inside a single pass. Oxygen values returned from plume shadow read high and cannot be used; the sensor bench that produced them was certified against clean engineering water.

Imaging in this volume is degraded. Suspended metal dust off the sinter line, three brine layers between 2,190 m and 2,310 m, and shear off the taps split returns and duplicate structure. This platform lost 14 of 22 imaging frames in the foundry hall and recorded a floor that is not there.

Obstructions logged: crushed hall machinery, unexpended thermal charges in an open rack, and eleven pressure doors stopped between travel limits. Door 44-P-118 is stopped at 310 mm with a suit boot in the track. The door controller log records that closure command as complete.

Between 2,240 m and the foundry floor this pass logged a sub-40 Hz carrier on six occasions with no matching sonar body, and two hull stress alarms with no recorded impact. This platform classifies those returns as unattributed. The salvage crew who recovered the log wrote a different word across that field.
