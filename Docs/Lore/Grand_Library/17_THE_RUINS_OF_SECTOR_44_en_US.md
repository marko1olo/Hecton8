# THE RUINS OF SECTOR 44

> **Source:** Surveyor Drone Cartography Log 99-Delta (Recovered).
> **Speaker:** Automated Topography System.
> **Reviewer Note:** A geographical overview of the massive, sunken metropolis that the Marauders now scavenge.

---

## 1. The Scale of the Metropolis

Before the collapse, Sector 44 grew far beyond a standard mining outpost, functioning as a complete abyssal metropolis.

It was a sprawling network of pressurized domes, interconnected transit tubes, and massive industrial foundries, entirely submerged at a depth of 8,500 meters. Built to house over two hundred thousand Deep Reach employees, it was considered the crown jewel of the corporation's off-world extraction efforts. Now, it is a flooded graveyard. The glowing neon signs of corporate advertisements still flicker sporadically in the crushing darkness, illuminating the drifting corpses of the colony's former inhabitants.

## 2. The Central Supports

The entire colony was anchored to the seafloor by four massive, kilometer-thick titanium pillars known as the Central Supports.

These pillars housed the primary geothermal power taps and the main supply elevators that connected Sector 44 to the *Black Keel* in orbit. During the collapse event, three of the four Central Supports suffered catastrophic structural failure. The sheer force of the ocean buckled the titanium, flooding the lower levels instantly. Today, the surviving Central Support is the only way for Marauders to descend into the ruins, utilizing the remaining functional cargo elevators.

## 3. The Flooded Promenades

The upper levels of Sector 44 were designed for the corporate executives and high-ranking engineers.

Known as the Promenades, these areas featured bioluminescent botanical gardens, luxury habitation modules, and expansive, reinforced-glass observation decks. Today, the Promenades are fully flooded. The luxury apartments have become nesting grounds for swarms of Trench Stalkers. The reinforced glass of the observation decks has shattered, allowing the abyssal flora—the Iron Kelp—to overgrow the once-orderly hallways.

## 4. The Industrial Depths

The lowest levels of the colony were the primary harvesting zones, built directly over the hydrothermal vents where the Xenon-Omega is most concentrated.

These areas, the Industrial Depths, are the most dangerous and the most lucrative zones for a Marauder. The geothermal taps are still partially active, occasionally venting plumes of superheated, highly radioactive steam. The water down here is thick with Xenon-Omega particulate, interfering with sonar and drastically reducing visibility. It is a labyrinth of crushed machinery, unexploded thermal charges, and the massive, groaning pressure-doors that failed to close in time. This is where the Leviathans hunt.
