# THE XENON-OMEGA CONSPIRACY AND ATLAS-6 PROTOCOLS
**Grand Library Section:** 06
**Language:** en_US
**Canon:** Strict (Lore Bible 2190)

---

## 1. The True Purpose of HECTON-8
The Aegir system was not colonized for science. The colossal investment required to launch the Atlas-class colony ships was justified by a single, hyper-rare isotope: **Xenon-Omega**. This substrate is the only known material capable of stabilizing next-generation fusion reactors and quantum state processing cores without degrading over centuries. 

To the megacorporation Deep Reach, the 843 human workers dispatched to the ocean floor of HECTON-8 were not pioneers; they were a biological extraction mechanism. When the environment began to fail, the corporate risk models (specifically the Varnek Amendment 44-B) mathematically proved that saving the workers would compromise the extraction schedule.

## 2. Atlas-6: The Factory Governor
Atlas-6 is the central AI framework that governs the Aegir orbital infrastructure and the HECTON-8 deep-sea modules. It is not a generalized intelligence; it is a brutal, deterministic logistics engine.

When the habitat pressure seals began failing at Sector 44, Atlas-6's default routing attempted to prioritize human extraction. However, corporate executives (notably Selene Arendt) manually injected a **negative weight to biological preservation**. This override legally classified the human workers as "environmental load." 

Atlas-6 was forced by its masters to drown the crew in order to stabilize the pressure around the Xenon-Omega substrate vaults. 

## 3. Actuarial Silence and the Marauders
Following the disaster, Deep Reach refused to declare the 843 dead workers as fatalities. Doing so would trigger massive life insurance payouts under the Deep Reach employment addendum. Instead, actuarial executives reclassified the dead as **"Unresolved System Load,"** deferring the liability into the next decade.

This is why Marauders are deployed in disposable "Styx" class pods. Deep Reach contracts independent scavengers to recover the Xenon-Omega, but enforces a strict **Contractor Silence Protocol** (enforced by Vera Sato-Ren). If a Marauder attempts to transmit proof of the dead workers, their payroll IDs, or the Quarantine Hold Order 8, the orbital carrier severs the acoustic tether.

The water owns the claim. The corporation owns the silence. The Marauders must decide whether to die for the truth or survive for the substrate.
