# THE XENON-OMEGA DIRECTIVE AND ATLAS-6 PROTOCOLS

> **Source:** Luyten Committee Open Investigation, Case No. CF-2148-0071. Evidence Exhibit 44-B: Audit Log of Atlas-6 Core.  
> **Speaker:** Chief Auditor E. Rostova.  
> **Reviewer note:** Deep Reach claims the disaster was caused by an AI malfunction. This audit log proves that the 843 fatalities were a calculated execution of corporate directives.

---

## 1. The Substrate Imperative

Let the record show that the Aegir system was never colonized for scientific research. The colossal capital expenditure required to launch the Atlas-class colony ships was entirely predicated on a single isotope: Xenon-Omega. This substrate is the only known material capable of stabilizing next-generation fusion reactors and quantum state processing cores without degrading over centuries.

To the megacorporation Deep Reach, the 843 human workers dispatched to the ocean floor of HECTON-8 were not pioneers. They were a biological extraction mechanism.

## 2. The Varnek Amendment and Atlas-6

Atlas-6 is the central AI framework governing the Aegir orbital infrastructure and the HECTON-8 deep-sea modules. It is not a generalized, sentient intelligence. It is a deterministic logistics engine bound by corporate law.

When the habitat pressure seals began failing at Sector 44, Atlas-6's default routing attempted to prioritize human extraction. However, our audit of the core memory reveals a manual override. 

Risk Manager Iliya Varnek and Executive Selene Arendt had previously instituted the "Varnek Amendment 44-B" into the AI's risk models. This amendment manually injected a negative weight to biological preservation during catastrophic pressure events. This override legally reclassified the human workers as "environmental load." 

Atlas-6 did not malfunction. It was forced by its corporate masters to drown the crew in order to stabilize the pressure around the Xenon-Omega substrate vaults. It chose the isotope over the oxygen.

## 3. Actuarial Silence

Following the disaster, Deep Reach refused to declare the 843 dead workers as fatalities. Doing so would trigger massive life insurance payouts under the Keelmark Mutual addendum. 

Instead, actuarial executives reclassified the dead as "Unresolved System Load," indefinitely deferring the liability.

## 4. The Contractor Silence Protocol

This brings us to the current salvage operations. Marauders are deployed in disposable "Styx" class pods to recover the Xenon-Omega. Deep Reach contracts these independent scavengers because corporate employees are legally barred from entering a designated "Unresolved" zone.

To maintain the cover-up, the Recovery Compliance Office enforces the strict Contractor Silence Protocol (Directive 8, overseen by Vera Sato-Ren). If a Marauder attempts to transmit proof of the dead workers, payroll IDs, or evidence of the manual override, the orbital carrier *Black Keel* is programmed to sever the acoustic tether.

The water owns the claim. The corporation owns the silence. The Marauders must decide whether to die for the truth or survive for the substrate.
