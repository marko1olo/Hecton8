# XENON-OMEGA: REFINEMENT AND PROPERTIES

> **Source:** Deep Reach Megacorporation, Classified Chemical Manifest.
> **Speaker:** Automated Chemical Analyzer.
> **Reviewer Note:** The scientific breakdown of the substance that makes all this death and suffering financially viable.

---

## 1. What is Xenon-Omega?

Xenon-Omega is a pressure-stable clathrate family, a complex xenon-rich defect lattice found only within the abyssal trenches of HECTON-8.

In its raw form, it appears as a crystalline, bioluminescent blue solid. The lattice is grown through a specific coincidence of the planet's extreme gravitational pressure, superheated mineral water from hydrothermal vents, and a rare sub-crustal fungal bloom that acts as a biological catalyst. It is highly radioactive, dense, and violently reactive when exposed to standard atmospheric oxygen.

## 2. The Refining Process

The Marauders do not refine Xenon-Omega; they merely harvest the raw cores from the flooded ruins of Sector 44 or the surrounding seabed.

Once a core is sent up the supply elevator to the *Black Keel*, it undergoes a brutal automated refinement process. The raw crystals are crushed, super-cooled to near absolute zero, and bombarded with ionized radiation to stabilize the isotope. This process is so dangerous and prone to catastrophic explosions that the refining bays on the *Black Keel* are designed to routinely blow out into space and be automatically rebuilt by repair drones.

## 3. Application in High-Density Processing

Why is Deep Reach willing to expend billions of credits and thousands of lives for this substance? Next-generation computation and high-energy containment.

Standard silicon and optical processing substrates fail under intense cosmic radiation and gravitational shear of interstellar transit. Modern neural cores and Atlas-compatible deep-system architectures require an exotic, pressure-stable medium to prevent quantum decoherence. Refined Xenon-Omega clathrates function as the ultimate high-density compute substrate. A single, fist-sized core of stabilized Xenon-Omega can process petabytes of neural telemetry per millisecond without thermal leakage, serving as the core computing lattice for interstellar colony ships, remote drones, and the Atlas-6 system itself. It is the foundation of the modern automated interstellar economy.

## 4. The Cost of Extraction

A standard inquiry in corporate audits: if the *Black Keel* is fully automated, why not use automated drones to harvest the Xenon-Omega instead of human Marauders?

The answer, as always, is found in the corporate ledger. The chemical composition of HECTON-8's ocean, combined with the extreme pressure and the electromagnetic interference from the Xenon-Omega itself, completely degrades sophisticated drone circuitry within hours. A hardened, pressure-resistant AI drone costs 40 million credits. A desperate, indebted human in a mechanical diving suit costs practically nothing. To Deep Reach, human life is the cheapest, most renewable resource available for abyssal extraction.
