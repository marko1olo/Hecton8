# THE ECONOMY OF SURVIVAL AND KEELMARK DEBT

> **Source:** Keelmark Independent Contractor Agreement, Addendum 14-B (Financial Obligations).
> **Speaker:** Automated Legal Voice-Prompt.
> **Reviewer Note:** A stark breakdown of the financial trap that keeps Marauders returning to the abyss.

---

## 1. The Debt Trap

No one comes to HECTON-8 voluntarily. The Marauders are the dregs of the Sol System—disgraced military, bankrupted asteroid miners, and corporate exiles who owe massive debts to the Deep Reach Megacorporation.

Instead of serving time in a penal colony, debtors are offered a "Lucrative Independent Contracting Opportunity." The catch? They must purchase their own transportation, Styx drop pods, Neodymium tethers, and Class-IV diving suits from Keelmark, the financial arm of Deep Reach. By the time a Marauder touches the water for their first dive, they are already millions of credits in debt. Interest accrues daily, compounded by the astronomical cost of life support on the *Black Keel*.

## 2. Salvage Value

To pay off this debt, Marauders must scavenge Sector 44. Everything has a price.

A waterlogged corporate ID badge from a dead worker might fetch 50 credits (proof of death clears an insurance file). A pristine Class-A Xenon-Omega core is worth 500,000 credits. However, Keelmark sets the exchange rates. If a Marauder brings back too much scrap metal, the market price artificially plummets. Deep Reach ensures that a contractor earns just enough to survive, but never enough to buy their freedom.

## 3. Oxygen as Currency

In orbit, the official currency is the Universal Credit. But down in the ocean, the only currency that matters is O2.

Oxygen tanks are the most expensive consumable sold by the *Black Keel* quartermaster. Marauders constantly face a gruesome calculus: do they spend their hard-earned salvage credits on repairing their suit armor, upgrading their thermal lance, or buying an extra five minutes of compressed oxygen-helium mix? Many have suffocated because they chose to prioritize weapon upgrades over air, hoping the extra firepower would secure a massive payday.

## 4. The Keelmark Ledger

The Keelmark Ledger is the massive, unfeeling quantum computer that tracks every contractor's debt.

When a Marauder dies—whether by suit puncture, predator attack, or a severed tether—the Ledger doesn't forgive the debt. Keelmark seizes any salvage the deceased contractor managed to send up, liquidates their remaining assets, and passes the remaining negative balance onto their next of kin. On HECTON-8, even death doesn't clear your tab.
