# SUBMARINE TECHNOLOGY AND MARAUDER DIVING SUITS

> **Source:** Keelmark Loss Desk, Actuarial Risk Assessment for Independent Salvage Operations.  
> **Speaker:** Underwriter H. Toth.  
> **Reviewer note:** Deep Reach refuses to issue high-grade pressure armor to independent contractors. Marauders must rely on modified industrial gear.

---

## 1. The Styx-Class Descent Pod

We do not provide Marauders with Atlas-class transport shuttles. They are inserted via surplus Styx-class orbital descent pods. These units were originally designed to drop automated mining drills through the HECTON-8 atmosphere. 

They have no thrusters for atmospheric braking. They utilize a highly ablative heat shield and a terminal velocity water impact to crack the planet's surface tension. The survival rate of a Styx drop is 88.4%. Keelmark considers this an acceptable risk margin for contractors. Once the pod strikes the ocean, it sinks like a stone to the operating depth, acting as a temporary staging point.

## 2. The Neodymium Acoustic Tether

Radio frequencies do not penetrate deep water. Marauders are completely cut off from the orbital carrier *Black Keel* the moment they hit the water. Their only lifeline is the Neodymium Acoustic Tether—a physical, braided fiber-optic cable spooled out from the Styx pod.

This tether serves three purposes:
1. It transmits low-frequency sonar bursts back to the pod.
2. It pumps a highly compressed oxygen-helium breathing mix.
3. It acts as a physical winch to drag a disabled suit back to the airlock.

If the tether is severed by debris, local fauna, or sudden currents, the Marauder has precisely 4.5 minutes of emergency bailout air. In 99% of severed tether incidents, Keelmark closes the file as a "Total Asset Loss."

## 3. Deep-Sea Weapons and Water Resistance

Standard kinetic firearms are useless at 400 atmospheres of pressure. The water friction slows bullets to a halt within two meters. 

Contractors are equipped with pneumatically driven spear guns and thermal lances. The thermal lance is an industrial cutting tool repurposed for defense; it ignites a magnesium-thermite reaction that burns at 4000°C underwater. It is highly effective against the armored plating of local predators, but a careless swing will instantly vaporize the water in a confined space, causing a lethal steam explosion that cooks the diver inside their own suit.

## 4. The "Lazarus" Suit

Marauders wear repurposed Class-IV heavy industrial diving suits. They are not built for combat; they are built to withstand the crushing dark. The suits consist of a titanium-carbide articulated exoskeleton overlaid with synthetic neoprene bladders.

The primary cause of contractor death is not predators. It is suit puncture. At operational depth, water breaching a damaged suit seal does not merely leak. It enters as a hypersonic jet of water that slices through bone and tissue like a laser cutter. Keelmark actuaries have noted that death by high-pressure injection is nearly instantaneous.
