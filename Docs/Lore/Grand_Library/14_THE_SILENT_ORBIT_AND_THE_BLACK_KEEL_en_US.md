# THE SILENT ORBIT AND THE BLACK KEEL

> **Source:** Deep Reach Megacorporation, Orbital Asset Registry.
> **Speaker:** Automated Registry Drone 44-X.
> **Registry note:** Asset record for the station holding every contractor's lien and return window. Drone 44-X telemetry, accession BK-REG-8804; no crew statement.

---

## 1. The Scale of the Black Keel

The *Black Keel* is not a spaceship; it is a mobile, orbital industrial fortress. Measuring over four kilometers in length, it is a dark, brutalist spire of irradiated tungsten and depleted uranium plating.

It was towed into orbit around HECTON-8 by three heavy-lift tugs over a century ago. From the surface of the ocean, it occasionally appears as a fast-moving, dull red star—the glow emanating from its massive plasma radiators. The station was designed to be self-sufficient, featuring on-board foundries, automated defense grids, and massive cryogenic storage bays for the refined Xenon-Omega.

## 2. A Ghost Ship

Despite its massive size, the *Black Keel* has a living human population of zero.

There are no executives, no human quartermasters, and no medical staff aboard. Deep Reach determined that maintaining a human crew in orbit around HECTON-8 was too expensive and presented a severe security risk. The entire station is operated by a network of specialized, non-sentient Artificial Intelligences. The "Quartermaster," the "Medical Chief," and the "Flight Controller" are all just different voice modulations of the same central processing core. The Marauders are the only living humans in the entire star system.

## 3. The Launch Bays

The process of sending a Marauder down to Sector 44 is violently efficient.

The launch bays of the *Black Keel* function less like hangar decks and more like artillery batteries. The Styx drop pods are loaded into massive electromagnetic railguns. When a Marauder is cleared for descent, the pod is fired directly at the ocean at Mach 4. The ablative heat shielding burns away during atmospheric entry, and the pod impacts the water with enough force to kill an un-augmented human. The pod then sinks like a stone, dragged down by tungsten ballast, until it reaches the abyssal trench.

## 4. The Automated Defense Grid

Deep Reach is paranoid about corporate espionage. The *Black Keel* is heavily armed, equipped with an array of orbital kinetic bombardment platforms (Rods from God) and high-yield laser batteries.

The defense grid operates on a strict "shoot first, do not ask questions" protocol. Any unregistered vessel entering the HECTON-8 system is immediately painted by targeting lasers and destroyed. Even the Deep Reach supply freighters that arrive every six months to collect the Xenon-Omega harvest must transmit a rolling, 2048-bit quantum encryption key. Failure to transmit the exact key within a three-second window results in the freighter being vaporized.
