# DEAD CLAIMS AND SALVAGE LAW

> **Source:** Keelmark Mutual, disputed recovery doctrine, Black Keel mirror copy.  
> **Speaker:** Claim-License Tutor, contractor-facing version.  
> **Reviewer Note:** Rescue is a duty. Salvage is a permission. The whole profession lives in that difference.

---

## 1. What Makes A Claim Dead

A dead claim is not abandoned property. Abandoned property is simple. A dead claim still has owners, insurers, creditors, missing workers, contaminated rooms, unpaid equipment, disputed logs and a reason every clean office would rather call it inactive.

On HECTON-8, death did not settle ownership. It made ownership harder to touch. Sector 44 drowned with pressure doors locked, maintenance ledgers open, Atlas state corrupted, evacuation stamps delayed and bodies missing from the return lists. Deep Reach could not simply write the place off without admitting too much. Keelmark could not pay every loss without proving what had been lost. Aegir Reclamation Pool could not sell the assets cleanly while the ocean kept producing evidence.

So the claim was left dead enough to deny rescue, alive enough to invoice, and dangerous enough to require disposable specialists.

That is where the Marauder enters.

## 2. The Marauder License

A Marauder license does not make the holder a pirate. It also does not make the holder a rescuer.

A pirate steals outside the ledger and hopes nobody can enforce the line. A rescuer arrives under public duty, crew protection and an obligation to preserve life before property. A Marauder enters the space Keelmark prefers: limited salvage access, lien recognition, recoverable-mass accounting, evidence custody options and enough personal liability to keep the larger offices clean.

The license is narrow by design. It permits entry into a disputed asset, not freedom inside it. It permits recovery of materials, tools, route packets, personal effects and certain sealed records if the chain of custody can survive the water. It does not require Black Keel to open a rescue window because a contractor found a living voice in a bad room. It does not require Deep Reach to admit that an old evacuation order was delayed. It does not require Keelmark to classify a body as a person before it can price the equipment attached to that body.

This is why the word Marauder stays ugly. The law needs the work but refuses the cleaner name.

## 3. Custody Is The Real Cargo

Most beginners think salvage law is about what an object is worth. Veterans learn that value arrives only after custody survives.

A pressure glass core without origin is scrap. The same core tagged to a sealed hatch, a pressure log, a route board and a delayed evacuation stamp can become liability evidence. A nameplate is sentimental junk until it is tied to a room, a shift, a missing worker line and a witness hash. A Xenon-Omega sample can pay debt. A Xenon-Omega sample with context can make an office answer questions.

Black Keel teaches this in the least human way possible. Every recovered object wants a custody path: who found it, where, under what pressure state, in which route window, with which contamination class, against which claim. The ocean tries to erase that path with salt, silt, impact, corrosion and living growth. Deep Reach tries to erase it with cleaner words.

A careful Marauder fights both.

## 4. Field Marks

Marauder marks are not decoration. They are field law scratched fast.

A black ring around a hatch means the owner record is hostile or lying. A pressure arrow means the door clock cannot be trusted. A slash through a corporate label means the label describes the office's preferred version, not the room's condition. Three short cuts beside a name mean keep the name with the object. A white smear near a relay case usually means do not move it before copying the local hash. None of these marks are official. That is why they work.

The marks are rude because polite language already failed. A flooded workroom does not give the next diver time to read a legal manual. It gives them a wall, a marker, three breaths, and maybe the last honest instruction anyone left.

## 5. Useful Theft

Marauder work is not clean redemption. It is theft performed inside a licensed wound.

Marauders steal because the system recognizes a stolen object faster than an abandoned person. A cut-open cabinet can prove a safety lock was never wired to open, and a recovered badge lifts a worker off the unresolved-load line. Samples buy oxygen and carry the signature that makes Recovery Compliance reopen a closed file. A route packet pays the lien and proves why the lien existed, and which fact the desk reads first depends on custody.

That is the hard moral texture of HECTON-8. The ocean is not only a monster. It is also the last archive. The law is not only an enemy. It is also the tool that can make a wet truth survive past the next window.

A Marauder who only loots gets paid until the debt changes shape. A Marauder who preserves custody can make the dead claim speak.
