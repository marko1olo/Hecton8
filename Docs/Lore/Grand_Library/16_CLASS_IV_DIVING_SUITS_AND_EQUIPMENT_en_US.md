# CLASS-IV DIVING SUITS AND ABYSSAL EQUIPMENT

> **Source:** Deep Reach Megacorporation, Equipment Quartermaster Manual.
> **Speaker:** Quartermaster AI Unit.
> **Reviewer Note:** A technical breakdown of the only thing standing between a Marauder and being instantly pulverized by 400 atmospheres of pressure.

---

## 1. The Class-IV "Iron Coffin"

The standard-issue equipment for any Marauder descending into the abyssal trenches of HECTON-8 is the Class-IV Mechanical Diving Suit.

Colloquially known by Marauders as the "Iron Coffin," the suit is essentially a wearable submarine. It is constructed from overlapping plates of irradiated tungsten and a polymerized titanium alloy, designed to withstand the crushing 400 atmospheres of pressure at the ocean floor. The suit is bulky, slow, and severely limits peripheral vision, utilizing a single, reinforced quartz viewport.

## 2. Life Support and Chemical Heaters

The ocean of HECTON-8 is pitch black and hovers just above the freezing point. The suit's life support systems are rudimentary but effective.

Oxygen is provided by an automated chemical scrubber system that recycles exhaled carbon dioxide. However, these scrubbers degrade over time and must be routinely swapped out—an expense the Marauders must pay out of pocket. To prevent the diver from freezing to death in the sub-zero waters, the interior of the suit is lined with chemical thermal-mesh. When the suit's primary battery runs low, the thermal-mesh is the first system to be automatically disabled to conserve power for the life support scrubbers.

## 3. Mobility and Propulsion

Walking on the ocean floor in an environment with such immense pressure is a slow, grueling process.

The Class-IV suit relies on motorized servos in the joints to assist with movement, giving the Marauder an unnatural, lurching gait. The boots are weighted with solid tungsten ballast to keep the diver firmly planted on the seabed and prevent them from being swept away by sudden deep-sea currents. For vertical mobility, the suit features twin hydro-jet thrusters mounted on the back. These thrusters are not designed for sustained flight; they provide short, powerful bursts to allow the Marauder to leap over ravines or scale the collapsed architecture of Sector 44.

## 4. Abyssal Armament

Traditional kinetic firearms are entirely useless on HECTON-8 due to the extreme water density and the heavily armored carapaces of the local fauna.

The primary weapon of the Marauder is the Thermal Lance. Functioning essentially as an underwater flamethrower, it ignites a concentrated stream of magnesium-thermite that burns at 4000°C. It is the only weapon capable of melting through the chitinous armor of a Trench Stalker or slicing through a jammed titanium bulkhead. For long-range engagements, Marauders use pneumatically driven kinetic harpoons. These harpoons fire solid tungsten spikes designed to pin aggressive fauna to the seafloor, buying the Marauder enough time to escape.
