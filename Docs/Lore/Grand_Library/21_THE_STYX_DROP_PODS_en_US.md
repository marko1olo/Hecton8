# THE STYX DROP PODS

> **Source:** Deep Reach Megacorporation, Orbital Logistics Division.
> **Speaker:** Automated Logistics AI.
> **Reviewer Note:** A detailed look at the terrifying commute between the Black Keel and the ocean floor.

---

## 1. The One-Way Elevator

The *Black Keel* orbits high above the stormy atmosphere of HECTON-8. The only method of deployment for a Marauder from the safety of orbit to the crushing depths of the ocean is the Styx-Class Atmospheric Drop Pod.

Affectionately referred to by Marauders as the "Coffin Ride," the Styx pod is a cramped, uninsulated, unpowered titanium cylinder just large enough to hold a single Marauder wearing a Class-IV diving suit. It has no windows, no pilot, and no manual controls. It is a completely automated, ballistic delivery system designed for maximum cost-efficiency, not comfort.

## 2. The Descent Profile

A deployment begins with a violent, magnetically accelerated launch from the *Black Keel's* orbital bays.

The pod plummets through HECTON-8's violent upper atmosphere, relying on an ablative heat shield woven from processed Iron Kelp to prevent the occupant from being incinerated. After a jarring, bone-rattling atmospheric entry, the pod strikes the raging surface of the ocean at terminal velocity. The impact is severe enough to cause concussions even inside the heavily padded Class-IV suit. From there, the pod simply sinks. For the next three agonizing hours, the Marauder sits in absolute darkness, listening to the groaning of the titanium hull as the pressure builds, plunging 8,500 meters down into the abyss.

## 3. Structural Integrity and Failures

The Styx pods are designed to be cheap and reusable. They are frequently not cheap enough, and they are reused too often.

While the core hull is made of dense titanium alloys, repeated atmospheric re-entries and exposure to 400 atmospheres of pressure create microscopic stress fractures. Deep Reach maintenance drones patch these fractures with quick-curing resin, but failures are an accepted statistical probability. If a pod's integrity fails during atmospheric entry, the Marauder is flash-boiled. If it fails during the deep descent, the hull instantly collapses inward, pulverizing the occupant into a bloody paste before they even realize what happened.

## 4. The Extraction Sequence

Getting down is terrifying, but getting back up is a logistical nightmare.

Once a Marauder's shift is over, they must locate a designated extraction zone. The Styx pod deploys a high-tension, mono-molecular tether that fires upward, attempting to latch onto the remaining functional cargo elevators of the ruined Central Pillar, or to automated drone barges hovering near the surface. Once latched, massive winches slowly reel the pod back up through the miles of water. It is a slow, agonizing ascent that leaves the pod completely defenseless against predatory fauna. If a Leviathan decides to bite the tether, or if a Trench Stalker fouls the winch mechanism, the pod falls back to the ocean floor. The Marauder inside is simply written off the corporate ledger as a total loss.
