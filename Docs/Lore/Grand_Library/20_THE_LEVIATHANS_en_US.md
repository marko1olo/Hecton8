# THE LEVIATHANS OF HECTON-8

> **Source:** Deep Reach Megacorporation, Xenobiology Threat Assessment.
> **Speaker:** Automated Threat Analyst.
> **Reviewer Note:** Do not engage. Do not observe. Shut down all power and pray it swims past.

---

## 1. The Apex Predators

HECTON-8 is an oceanic hellscape, and the Leviathans are its undisputed lords.

They are biological anomalies, growing to scales that defy conventional understanding of deep-sea gigantism. The smallest confirmed specimen measured over 120 meters in length, while anomalous sonar shadows suggest the existence of creatures more than three times that size lurking in the deepest, unexplored trenches. They move with an unnatural, sinuous grace through the crushing pressure of the abyss, completely unbothered by the 400 atmospheres of force that would instantly pulverize a human. To a Leviathan, a Marauder in a Class-IV diving suit is not a threat; it is barely even a snack.

## 2. Abyssal Anatomy

The anatomy of a Leviathan is a nightmare of evolutionary adaptation.

They lack eyes, having evolved in pitch-black waters. Instead, their massive, elongated heads are covered in highly sensitive electro-receptors and lateral lines capable of detecting the minute electrical impulses of a beating heart from kilometers away. Their bodies are encased in thick, overlapping plates of chitinous armor that are naturally infused with metallic minerals from the hydrothermal vents, making them virtually impervious to thermal lances or kinetic harpoons. Their maws are vast, cavernous crushers lined with hundreds of razor-sharp, continually regenerating teeth designed to snap the titanium hulls of submarines in half.

## 3. The Mechanics of the Hunt

Leviathans are blind, but they are far from oblivious.

They navigate and hunt using a sophisticated form of low-frequency biological sonar. The clicking and groaning of a Class-IV suit's servos, the hiss of a thermal lance, or the clatter of a mining drill echo for miles in the dense water, ringing like a dinner bell. When a Leviathan locks onto a sound, it does not rush. It circles in the darkness, emitting deep, concussive vocalizations that vibrate through the water with enough force to shatter reinforced quartz viewports and liquefy internal organs. By the time a Marauder actually sees the bioluminescent glow of a Leviathan's maw emerging from the gloom, it is already too late.

## 4. The Futility of Combat

Deep Reach corporate doctrine regarding Leviathan encounters is brutally simple: Combat is prohibited.

Standard operating procedure dictates that upon detecting a Leviathan-class sonar signature, a Marauder must immediately power down all suit systems, including life support and thermal meshes, sink into the mud, and remain absolutely motionless. If the Leviathan detects the Marauder regardless, corporate policy chillingly suggests that the Marauder activate their suit's scuttling charge. This ensures a quick death for the employee and, more importantly, damages the Leviathan's digestive tract enough that the beast might eventually spit out the highly valuable, indigestible titanium diving suit for later corporate recovery.
