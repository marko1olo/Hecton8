# THE LEVIATHANS OF HECTON-8

> **Source:** Deep Reach Extraterrestrial Development Combine, Xenobiology Threat Assessment.
> **Speaker:** Automated Threat Analyst.
> **Reviewer Note:** Recovered with the redactions intact. Do not engage, do not observe, kill power and hold still.

---

## 1. Classification

FILE CLASS: CONVERGENCE LABEL. TAXONOMIC STATUS: NOT ESTABLISHED. SPECIMEN IN CUSTODY: NONE.

Leviathan is not a species in this file. It is the label the Assessment applies when four indirect returns arrive together and no body is recovered: a sub-40 Hz acoustic carrier, prey blackout across a band, flow disturbance with no mapped source, and hull stress alarms with no logged impact.

The form has fields for mass, length, dentition and plating. On every recovered copy those four are redacted, and the redaction is older than the collapse. What is left is instrumentation and testimony.

## 2. What The Instruments Return

The carrier arrives as pressure in the frame and in the chest before any display resolves it. Logged duration runs 4 to 90 seconds. Bearing resolves; range does not. Twenty-two of twenty-two events returned a bearing with the range field blank or flagged in error.

Prey blackout runs ahead of the carrier in eleven cases and behind it in four: grazing and schooling acoustics stop across a band, then resume from a different depth. Flow shows up on strain gauges rather than current sensors. Sector 44 foundry gauge 44-S-9 recorded 3.1 mm deflection twice in one shift with taps 4 and 7 both cold. Nine hulls have filed stress alarms with no impact record, no penetration, and plate deformation the bench reads as pressure loading instead of strike.

## 3. Testimony And Its Limits

Survivor statements in this file are not clean data, and the file says so on every page.

Each deposition in the annex was taken from a contractor recovered hypoxic, narcotic under partial pressure, or both. The Assessment's qualifier is stamped across all of them: STATEMENT CONTAMINATED BY GAS STATE / NOT ADMISSIBLE AS SPECIMEN DESCRIPTION.

The statements agree on behaviour and not on anatomy. Contractors who describe a shape describe different shapes. Contractors who describe the order of events describe the same order every time: blackout, then carrier, then a long hold at distance, then nothing worth recording. The Assessment treats the second agreement as the more serious finding and files the label under route hazard rather than fauna.

## 4. Standing Procedure

Doctrine on contact is short because the alternatives were priced and rejected.

On carrier detection: cut power to servos, lamp, thermal mesh and active sonar, settle into silt, hold. Running has never appeared in a recovery record as a successful action. It appears in the equipment column as a spent lift bottle.

Where contact continues, the next numbered step is not about the contractor. Step 4 requires the recorder seal and custody beacon to be triggered before any other action, on the stated ground that recovery of the record establishes loss class. Step 5 is the suit charge. Step 6 is the receiving desk form.

The procedure has six numbered steps. None of them is retrieval, and the annex that would have held one is listed in the index as withheld.
