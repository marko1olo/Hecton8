# APEX PREDATORS AND ABYSSAL BIOLOGY

> **Source:** Fragmented Xenobiology Report, Sector 44 Research Lab (Recovered).
> **Speaker:** Dr. Aris Thorne, Lead Xenobiologist (Deceased).
> **Reviewer Note:** Sector 44 telemetry confirms 94% bio-threat density. Dr. Thorne's recordings were salvaged from the flooded biological containment ring.

---

## 1. The Chemosynthetic Web

HECTON-8 receives no sunlight at the operational depths of Sector 44. The entire ecosystem is sustained by black smoker hydrothermal vents. Massive hydrothermal vents spew superheated, mineral-rich water into the ambient 1.8°C ocean column.

Around these vents grow sprawling fields of *Siderophycus ferreus* ("Iron Kelp"), a biomineralized flora depositing iron sulfide within its cellular walls. This kelp forms the foundation of the food web, sustaining massive swarms of blind, bioluminescent krill. Scarcity of caloric energy drives hyper-aggressive hunting patterns. Sub-abyssal organisms display extreme adaptations: hyper-thickened silica-titanium carapaces, highly concentrated neurotoxins, or gigantism driven by slow metabolic rates.

## 2. The Trench Stalkers

Operational logs catalog *Bathysqualus caecus* ("Trench Stalker") as the primary hazard during extravehicular salvage. They are pack-hunting predators reaching 6–8 meters in length, with complete ocular atrophy.

In place of eyes, their cranial plates house a dense network of electro-receptors (cranial ampullae) capable of registering microvolt fluctuations. The active electromagnetic signature of a Class-IV atmospheric diving suit is detectable up to 2.8 kilometers. Any systemic power spike—including the ignition of a magnesium-thermite thermal lance—will trigger immediate predatory routing toward the emitter.

## 3. Chitinous Armor

Kinetic firearms are useless at depth, both due to hydrostatic drag and the density of local biological shells.

At 400 atmospheres, organic structures require reinforced structures. The outer carapace of the Stalker consists of a biomineralized silicate-titanium matrix. Standard high-tensile harpoons deflect off the plates without structural penetration. Tactical guides mandate the use of magnesium-thermite thermal lances, operating at 4000°C, to burn through the pressure-forged chitin.

## 4. The Leviathans

Deep Reach classified all high-mass biological acoustics prior to the evacuation of Sector 44.

Passive sonar arrays periodically record low-frequency signatures exceeding 180 decibels, initially cataloged as seismic displacements. These organisms inhabit the ultra-abyssal trenches beneath the continental shelf. Acoustic modeling indicates that a close-range vocalization within the 5–15 Hz band is capable of inducing fatal internal tissue cavitation in divers. Surviving engineering crews suggest that the catastrophic collapse of Sector 44's primary structural struts was caused by the hydrodynamic wake of a passing high-mass organism, rather than tectonic shear.
