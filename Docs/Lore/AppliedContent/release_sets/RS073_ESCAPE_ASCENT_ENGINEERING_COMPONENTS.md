# Escape Ascent Engineering Components

Status: production-facing AppliedLore source.
Runtime rule: source/export only; runtime consumes baked static data and string-pool offsets.

Make the escape chain concrete: relay, seal, guidance, ascent energy and legal quarantine handshake all force deeper repair instead of convenient rescue.

## Packets

- `P361_ACOUSTIC_RELAY_SPINE_ASSEMBLY` - Acoustic Relay Spine Assembly: Escape starts with an acoustic route spine because high-gain radio and the capsule mast are physically broken.
- `P362_PRESSURE_SEAL_CLAMP_RING_ASSEMBLY` - Pressure Seal Clamp Ring Assembly: Ascent requires certified clamp rings because the capsule survived descent by damaging its own pressure interface.
- `P363_GUIDANCE_TIMING_CORE_ASSEMBLY` - Guidance Timing Core Assembly: The escape system needs a timing core because rescue is an Aegir geometry problem, not a button.
- `P364_ASCENT_ENERGY_CHARGE_ASSEMBLY` - Ascent Energy Charge Assembly: Ascent energy must be pressure-certified, not looted fuel, because the charge has to survive launch depth and carrier custody checks.
- `P365_QUARANTINE_LEGAL_HANDSHAKE_ASSEMBLY` - Quarantine Legal Handshake Assembly: Escape is not free until the player chooses which legal receiver is allowed to parse the payload.
