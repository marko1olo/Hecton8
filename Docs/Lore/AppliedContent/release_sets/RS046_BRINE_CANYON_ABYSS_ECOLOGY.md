# Brine Canyon Abyss Ecology

Status: production-facing draft pending native localization.

Lock mid/deep ecology as route pressure, density physics and creature telegraphing.

## Packets

- `P226_BRINE_VANE_FORESTS` - Brine Vane Forests: Brine Vane Forests define HECTON-8's canyon ecology. These membrane-like growths mark density boundaries, route hazards and the chemical economy that makes the moon's deep ocean feel engineered by pressure.
- `P227_DENSITY_SKATER_FAUNA` - Density Skater Fauna: Density Skater Fauna show HECTON-8's hard-sci-fi creature logic. They do not break physics; they exploit brine interfaces, pressure layers and sonar blind spots as habitat and hunting routes.
- `P228_VENT_ANCHOR_COLONIES` - Vent Anchor Colonies: Vent Anchor Colonies explain why deep exploration is mechanically necessary. They turn thermal vents into resource engines, biological shelters and pressure hazards, giving the player a reason to move down instead of simply repairing upward.
- `P229_WIDE_FILTER_BODY_TRACE` - Wide Filter Body Trace: Wide Filter Body Trace keeps HECTON-8's largest fauna grounded. The creature is not a boss reveal; it is moving ecology that clears plankton, bends sonar and changes how the player reads the deep.
- `P230_SILT_AMBUSHER_TELEGRAPH` - Silt Ambusher Telegraph: Silt Ambusher Telegraph is a creature-design rule for HECTON-8's low-visibility zones. Threats stay frightening because they are systemic and readable: missing microfauna, wrong surface geometry and acoustic warning before impact.
