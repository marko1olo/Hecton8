# Fauna Encounter Grammar

Status: production-facing AppliedLore source.
Runtime rule: source/export only; runtime consumes baked static data and string-pool offsets.

Define fauna encounters as route grammar and replay pressure, not boss lists or mystical ocean speech.

## Packets

- `P411_PREDATOR_SHADOW_ENCOUNTER_GRAMMAR` - Predator Shadow Encounter Grammar: Predator shadows teach route risk through absence, sonar cuts and prey behavior rather than direct boss presentation.
- `P412_GLASS_GRAZER_CLEARING_ENCOUNTER_GRAMMAR` - Glass Grazer Clearing Encounter Grammar: Glass grazers create temporary safe readings by clearing growth, but their absence can be a stronger warning than attack.
- `P413_LANTERN_DRIFT_FALSE_SAFE_ENCOUNTER_GRAMMAR` - Lantern Drift False Safe Encounter Grammar: Lantern drifts can mark oxygen, signal noise or predator displacement; brightness is information, not safety.
- `P414_BRINE_VANE_NAVIGATION_ENCOUNTER_GRAMMAR` - Brine Vane Navigation Encounter Grammar: Brine vanes turn plant-like growth into navigation hardware by bending currents and sonar readings.
- `P415_SENSOR_TAGGED_FAUNA_PURSUIT_ENCOUNTER_GRAMMAR` - Sensor Tagged Fauna Pursuit Encounter Grammar: Sensor-tagged fauna are not mind-controlled pets; they are feedback carriers in a damaged maintenance network.
