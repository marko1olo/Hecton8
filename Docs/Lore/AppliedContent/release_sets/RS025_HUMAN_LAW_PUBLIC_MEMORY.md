- `P123_SALVAGE_TRUTH_EVIDENCE_STATUS` - Salvage Truth Evidence Status: Salvage Truth Evidence Status turns lore into mechanics: a recovered log matters only if the player protects the packet route.
- `P124_NORMAL_CITIZEN_AEGIR_MEMORY` - Normal Citizen Aegir Memory: Normal Citizen Aegir Memory defines the public distance of HECTON-8: not secret, not understood, and not close enough to save.
- `P125_DEEP_REACH_ORIGIN_CHAIN` - Deep Reach Origin Chain: Deep Reach Origin Chain anchors the corporation in older human expansion, keeping Aegir as one of its worst projects rather than its birthplace.
