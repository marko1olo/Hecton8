# RS099 Public Evidence Governance Bridge

Status: source_ready
Date: 2026-06-08
Evidence class: AppliedContent source packet

## Intent

This release set turns the public-evidence arc into player-readable material instead of a bare archive warning. It covers the part of the lore where proof becomes dangerous after publication: a public packet can raise the cost of corporate denial, but it can also become a broker map, an insurer delay tool, a claimant-language laundering surface, or an ending spoiler.

## Player-Facing Packets

- P496_PUBLIC_EVIDENCE_MISUSE_WARNING_BRIDGE: publication is leverage, not shelter.
- P497_EVIDENCE_RELATION_GRAPH_DOSSIER_BRIDGE: relation edges are review cues, not verdicts.
- P498_TERMINAL_CLAIMANT_LANGUAGE_AUDIT_BRIDGE: procedure terms can replace people, rooms, bodies, and route clocks.
- P499_PUBLIC_INDEX_SPOILER_CAP_BRIDGE: category titles are shelf labels, not receiver chains or ending reveals.

## Route Moment

POST_PUBLIC_LEDGER_ACCESS. This is outside the first 20 minutes, but it protects the same route promise: the player can learn what kind of evidence exists without being handed the final receiver, Atlas consequence, or ending result before earning the proof path.

## Localization

All 15 supported locales are present in the packet source. en_US is source-authority text. Other locales are complete draft rows marked `draft_machine_or_llm` and require native review before final publication.
