# Long Campaign Act Spine

Status: production-facing AppliedLore source.
Runtime rule: source/export only; runtime consumes baked static data and string-pool offsets.

Turn the tens-of-hours campaign into depth-act route cards that connect survival, proof, POI escalation and final payload pressure.

## Packets

- `P381_CONTRACT_APPROACH_ACT_GATE` - Contract Approach Act Gate: The campaign begins as a job: contract, lien, claim pressure and a bad orbital window.
- `P382_PHOTIC_SHELF_SURVIVAL_ACT` - Photic Shelf Survival Act: The first playable hours should be beautiful, practical and mechanically trapped.
- `P383_BRINE_CANYON_LIABILITY_ACT` - Brine Canyon Liability Act: Midgame proof should move from accident rumors to physical liability chains.
- `P384_ABYSSAL_MACHINE_FIELD_REPAIR_ACT` - Abyssal Machine Field Repair Act: Late midgame should make Atlas maintenance ecology useful, frightening and mechanically expensive.
- `P385_ATLAS_BASIN_PAYLOAD_ACT` - Atlas Basin Payload Act: The final act is a receiver problem: what leaves the ocean, who receives it and what remains damaged.
