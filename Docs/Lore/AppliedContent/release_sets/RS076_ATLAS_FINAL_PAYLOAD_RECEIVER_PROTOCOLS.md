Status: production-facing AppliedLore source.

Make final choices readable as payload receiver rules: sell coordinates, sever Atlas, preserve quarantine, publish ledger or leave Deep Reach blind.

## Packets

- `P376_PAYLOAD_SELL_COORDINATES_RECEIVER_PROTOCOL` - Payload Sell Coordinates Receiver Protocol: Selling coordinates is a real ending: money, recovery and corporate return to the crime scene.
- `P377_PAYLOAD_SEVER_ATLAS_RECEIVER_PROTOCOL` - Payload Sever Atlas Receiver Protocol: Severing Atlas can be mercy, murder, liberation, or theft depending evidence and receiver.
- `P378_PAYLOAD_PRESERVE_QUARANTINE_RECEIVER_PROTOCOL` - Payload Preserve Quarantine Receiver Protocol: Preserve/quarantine denies simple ownership transfer but leaves the player with consequence and delay.
- `P379_PAYLOAD_PUBLIC_LEDGER_RECEIVER_PROTOCOL` - Payload Public Ledger Receiver Protocol: Publishing proof prevents clean erasure but removes player control over consequences.
- `P380_PAYLOAD_WITHHOLD_BLIND_RETURN_PROTOCOL` - Payload Withhold Blind Return Protocol: Withholding payload can deny Deep Reach, but it also withholds proof, payout and clean recovery.
