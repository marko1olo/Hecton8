# RS024_RESOURCE_RECIPE_TAXONOMY

Status: production-facing draft pending native localization.
Runtime contract: source content only; game consumes baked packet hashes and string-pool rows.

Separate natural, Deep-Reach-amplified and Atlas-altered resources into gameplay-readable categories.

## Packets

- `P116_NATIVE_SULFIDE_SALT_STACK`: Native Sulfide Salt Stack defines the natural resource layer of HECTON-8: dangerous, useful and independent of Deep Reach intent.
- `P117_NOBLE_GAS_BRINE_POCKETS`: Noble Gas Brine Pockets explain the hard-sci-fi root of Xenon-Omega: rare pressure chemistry that only becomes strategic material through containment and industrial process.
- `P118_DEEP_REACH_PRESSURE_CERAMICS`: Deep Reach Pressure Ceramics separates corporate engineering from pure evil. The company made working materials and then used that competence to justify lethal priorities.
- `P119_ATLAS_BIOFIBER_SEALANT`: Atlas Biofiber Sealant is one of HECTON-8's clearest biomechanical resources: a crafting material that makes the player benefit from the same misclassification that horrified them.
- `P120_BIOMETAL_SENSOR_TAGS`: Biometal Sensor Tags connect resources, ecology and Atlas agency. They are not generic collectibles; they are material proof that the repair network has entered living systems.
