Status: source_ready
Date: 2026-06-08

## Intent

RS100 extends the public evidence arc after RS099. It teaches the player that receiver labels, cleanup bids, and claimant-safe summaries are evidence lanes, not verdicts. The articles are spoiler-safe: they expose conflict categories without revealing final receiver, Atlas consequence, legal result, protected names, coordinates, or ending branch.

## Player-Facing Packets

- P500_PUBLIC_ARCHIVE_RECEIVER_AMBIGUITY_BRIDGE: receiver labels can diverge because address, relay, quarantine, claimant, and archive lanes are separate.
- P501_EVIDENCE_MARKET_CLEANUP_BID_BRIDGE: cleanup bids prove pressure around evidence, not truth or buyer guilt.
- P502_CLAIMANT_SAFE_SUMMARY_CONFLICT_BRIDGE: claimant-safe summaries can protect people or hide leverage; the omission itself is evidence.
