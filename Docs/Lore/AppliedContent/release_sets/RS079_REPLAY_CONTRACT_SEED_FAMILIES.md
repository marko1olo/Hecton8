# Replay Contract Seed Families

Status: production-facing AppliedLore source.
Runtime rule: source/export only; runtime consumes baked static data and string-pool offsets.

Define replay contract families that vary route pressure, custody and evidence order without turning HECTON-8 into power roguelite progression.

## Packets

- `P391_QUIET_SALVAGE_CONTRACT_SEED` - Quiet Salvage Contract Seed: A quiet seed lowers early pressure but hides stronger evidence custody and payout compromise.
- `P392_STORM_WINDOW_RUSH_CONTRACT_SEED` - Storm Window Rush Contract Seed: A rush seed pays for speed by making orbit, weather and repair timing hostile from the first hour.
- `P393_HIGH_CUSTODY_SAMPLE_CONTRACT_SEED` - High Custody Sample Contract Seed: A sample-focused seed increases blue-debt value while tightening containment, mass and quarantine pressure.
- `P394_EVIDENCE_FIRST_CHARTER_CONTRACT_SEED` - Evidence First Charter Contract Seed: An evidence-first seed lowers material payout and increases route friction, but makes public proof easier to preserve.
- `P395_RECOVERY_COMPLIANCE_BAIT_CONTRACT_SEED` - Recovery Compliance Bait Contract Seed: A bait seed looks profitable because Deep Reach wants the player to carry a receiver route back to them.
