The source knows the locker seal failed inward from outside pressure and the contents were not removed first. It does not know the final liability chain, the worker's name, or the last movement of the occupant.

The article should make damage direction meaningful. The room is not a loot pile and not a melodrama beat. The object proves that pressure reached an ordinary personal space before anyone had time to convert it into salvage.
