## Source Brief

The source knows the lockout tag was applied recently enough for ink to smear under water. It does not prove who applied it or the final reason. The article should make lockout state readable without turning it into a dramatic confession.

Player use: supports access gating, worker-route evidence, and scanner caution.

Forbidden facts: no final culprit, no secret note, no universal key, no runtime readiness.

## Surface Texts

### Scanner

LOCKOUT TAG // Wet ink smear. Access held by procedure, not broken latch.

### Codex

The latch is not the first problem. A lockout tag still hangs from it, ink smeared by water before it dried. Someone marked this access as held while the route was still being worked.

Procedure survived just long enough to become evidence.

### PDA Log

Lockout tag transcription:

- Ink: wet smear.
- Initials: unreadable.
- Latch: intact.
- State: procedural hold.

Do not cut latch until lockout reason is checked.
