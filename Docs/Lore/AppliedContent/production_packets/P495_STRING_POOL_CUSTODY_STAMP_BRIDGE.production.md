**Codex note**

A localized line is not safe just because it reads clean. The custody stamp is the part that ties the sentence to its source: LocID hash, spoiler byte, packet origin, source authority, draft translation state, and the hold that says a native reviewer has not signed the wording.

Deep Reach can make a worker's sentence sound like a maintenance category. A draft translation can make the same damage quieter in another language. The stamp does not make the text true. It keeps the text from being treated as finished evidence before the chain says who carried it.

**Marauder annotation**

Keep the LocID. Keep the spoiler byte. Keep the ugly source line. Let the reviewer fight the language after the packet proves where it came from.
