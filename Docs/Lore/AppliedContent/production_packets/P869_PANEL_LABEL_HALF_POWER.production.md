## Source Brief

The source knows the label authorizes support loads at half power and warns against high-load restoration. It does not claim current panel state is valid. The article should create a clear player-facing distinction: support systems first.

Player use: supports first shelter UX and future panel labels.

## Surface Texts

### Scanner

PANEL LABEL // Half-power support loads only: scrubber, light, condenser test.

### Codex

The label is old, but useful. Half-power means support loads: scrubber test, dim light, condenser check. It does not mean workshop comfort, heavy pumps, or room heat. The warning tape says the same thing with less patience.

Power has categories before it has comfort.

### PDA Log

Panel label transcription:

- Mode: half-power.
- Allowed: scrubber, light, condenser test.
- Rejected: heavy pump, room heat, workshop load.
- Tape: stale but legible.

Use support loads before comfort loads.
