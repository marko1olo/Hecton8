## English Authority Surface Texts

### External Site / Wiki Article

Title: Black Keel Claim Window

Black Keel can hear a signal before it can lift a body.

That difference is the claim window. In Aegir, a carrier response is not a rescue promise; it is a timed lane through relay geometry, weather, quarantine review, payload acceptance, and mass recovery. The same window prices cargo, evidence, oxygen debt, and operator welfare because every kilogram that leaves HECTON-8 spends the same orbital opportunity.

Keelmark Mutual records the window as custody timing. Black Keel records it as receive confirmed, lift pending. Deep Reach clauses can still push a sample, coordinate packet, or quarantine hold ahead of the operator if the priority language is valid. None of that requires a villain confession. It only requires a ledger that treats recovery mass as scarce and people as one class of recoverable material.

P-63 makes the rule visible early. A relay board can show a clean acknowledgement while the bathydrop still has no ascent package, no quarantine handshake, and no allocated tonne-window. The carrier knows the operator is alive. It can bill the operator. It can price a packet. It still cannot ignore HECTON-8 pressure, Aegir timing, or the contract order.

Contact is a route fact. Rescue is a completed chain.

### In-Game Codex

Recovered Black Keel claim-window notice. Carrier contact means a signal reached the Aegir relay lane; it does not mean recovery is available. Extraction needs an ascent-capable package, quarantine/legal handshake, accepted payload state, and tonne-window allocation. Preserve timestamp strips and custody tags. A received packet can still be converted into claim material if lift remains pending.
