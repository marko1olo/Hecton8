### External Site / Wiki Article

Title: Xenon-Omega Public Material

Public Aegir records do not agree on what Xenon-Omega is. Some call it an extreme-environment process substrate. Some call it a pressure-derived lattice. Deep Reach procurement tags call it a continuity material and move on before explaining the word.

That vagueness is part of the record. Xenon-Omega is useful enough to create sealed handling rules and expensive enough for Black Keel to price it against a recovery window, but the open public layer does not prove what Deep Reach found under HECTON-8 or how the material works. The safe public fact is narrower: any viable sample is treated as pressure-sensitive, custody-sensitive, and politically expensive.

The first player-facing evidence should look boring before it looks valuable. A P-63 label warns against venting a bottle in warm cabin air. A mass ledger puts the sample in a tonne-window column instead of a treasure column. A scanner refuses to grade an opened case. A Deep Reach notice asks for containment state and receiver authority before it asks who recovered the material.

Marauders usually translate that into blue debt. The phrase is not public science. It means the same object can be payout, contamination problem, custody evidence, and claim bait. A clean shine means less than the pressure record, seal history, and tag that survived with it.

Public pages may name Xenon-Omega as a rumor and handling category. They must not explain the internal process, final Atlas-6 basin relation, or final payload receiver route. Those truths need recovered evidence, not a public glossary.

### In-Game Codex

Recovered Xenon-Omega handling note. Public records call it a pressure material, procurement class, or continuity substrate. Treat the wording as incomplete. A viable sample needs pressure-rated containment, custody tags, and receiver authority before Black Keel will price it above scrap. Do not vent, heat, or open a case for curiosity. An opened sample can become dead material with live liability.
