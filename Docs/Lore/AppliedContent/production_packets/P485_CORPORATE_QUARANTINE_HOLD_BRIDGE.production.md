## Canon Summary

Quarantine hold is a route consequence surface. It can delay, contain, or capture a person, a sample canister, a witness hash, or an evidence packet under physically plausible quarantine language. The hazard remains real: pressure biology, containment-stage failure, Atlas-compatible signal drift, and Xenon-Omega sample custody can justify containment. The abuse is also real: Deep Reach and its claim-chain offices can use the same language to stall release until Recovery Compliance, Keelmark Mutual, or a controlled receiver sees the payload first.

This packet improves the mid/deep evidence route moment where brine-canyon liability proof and Atlas-service evidence become receiver/custody pressure. It does not change first-20-minute survival content; it removes a later evidence-route blocker by defining how partial/corporate/quarantine recovery can be written without contradicting canon.
