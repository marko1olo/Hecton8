## Canon Summary

Contract Continuity Desk is not a rescue office. It is the Deep Reach surface that keeps every recovery verb contract-valid while preserving room to delay extraction, narrow the recovered subject, or route the result to a corporate receiver. The language can be true and hostile at the same time:

- Recovery can mean recovery of the contractor, capsule, sample, coordinates, evidence, or legal claim package.
- Return can mean return to Black Keel, Aegir Reclamation Pool custody, Recovery Compliance, Keelmark loss review, quarantine hold, or a certified receiver queue.
- Contractor welfare can remain listed as a live term while oxygen, ascent mass, quarantine, weather, payload proof, or receiver legality makes it conditional.
- Payload can include a living worker, a body, data, blue debt containment, Atlas continuity state, coordinates, or claim evidence.
- Receiver is the party allowed to accept the recovered package; it is not automatically the player, a public authority, or a neutral rescue service.

## Surface Texts

Terminal document:

DEEP REACH EXTRATERRESTRIAL DEVELOPMENT COMBINE
Aegir Continuity Holdings / Recovery Compliance
Contract Continuity Desk
CCD-480 / Language Bridge Addendum

Subject: recovery-language continuity for Black Keel contractor package.

Use "recovery" when the action restores claim-valid custody over any certified package component. Do not narrow the term in packet text unless the receiver has already certified living extraction mass.

Use "return" only with a named receiver. Acceptable receivers include Recovery Compliance, Aegir Reclamation Pool notary custody, Keelmark Mutual loss review, quarantine hold, and any carrier queue carrying a valid Aegir continuity seal.

"Contractor welfare" remains accurate when listed as an active consideration, even if it is sequenced behind payload proof, contamination review, orbital window, oxygen accounting, evidence custody, or receiver certification.

"Payload" may include contractor body, contractor testimony, capsule telemetry, coordinates, XO continuity substrate, Atlas access state, chain-of-custody hash, or salvage value. Do not promise that the payload is the contractor.

Black Keel may acknowledge distress. Acknowledgement is not receiver acceptance. Aegir Reclamation Pool may log the claim. Logging is not extraction release. Keelmark may preserve a welfare line while holding recovery mass against unresolved payload debt.

Desk note: if the contractor challenges wording, answer with the active receiver list, not with intent.

PDA codex:

The Contract Continuity Desk keeps Deep Reach recovery language usable after the words have stopped meaning rescue. A packet can promise recovery while asking for coordinates first. It can say return while naming Recovery Compliance as the receiver. It can list contractor welfare while holding oxygen mass behind payload proof.

That does not make the packet false. It makes it dangerous. The Black Keel carrier, Aegir Reclamation Pool, Keelmark, Aegir Continuity Holdings, and Recovery Compliance all read the same words through different ledgers. The player must preserve receiver terms, chain-of-custody hashes, and welfare clauses before trusting any return action.
