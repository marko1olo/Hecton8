## English Authority Artifact

DR-ASI/BOARD-04 is a routing screen for evidence conflicts. It compares worker-safety exposure, Atlas-6 continuity state, Xenon-Omega custody, quarantine release, and Tau Ceti public-ledger risk before a record leaves Deep Reach custody.

S-17 holds the record until Recovery Compliance receives coordinates or pressure-grade custody proof. S-22 sends only a sealed copy to the Packet Notary Interface. S-41 keeps recovery language inside quarantine review. S-58 converts a missing worker to asset/load accounting while leaving the name recoverable through sealed evidence request.

The board does not delete proof. It decides which office can hear it, which route can carry it, and whether a contractor can later turn the notary seal into leverage.
