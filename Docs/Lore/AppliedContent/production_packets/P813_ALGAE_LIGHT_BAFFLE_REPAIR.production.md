## Source Brief

The source knows the baffle was used to shape light over algae growth and that the bent foil now overexposes one edge. It does not define a full growth simulation. The article should make lighting part of practical ecology repair.

The object should support player-facing route readability: shade, light, bloom health, and repair tabs.

Player use: tells the player that aligning a baffle can protect growth and improve intake clarity without harvesting the patch.

Forbidden facts: no growth-sim claim, no instant oxygen engine, no perfect tank recovery, no runtime readiness.

## Surface Texts

### Scanner

LIGHT BAFFLE // Shade foil bent. Realign tab to protect algae edge and clear intake glare.

### Codex

The baffle is a cheap piece of foil with serious work to do. It shades the algae edge so growth stays slow enough to screen water without choking the mesh. Bent wrong, it burns one strip and blinds the intake view.

Light is a repair tool here.

### PDA Log

Baffle repair note:

- Shade foil: bent.
- Repair tab: reachable.
- Algae edge: overlit.
- Intake glare: high.

Realign before cutting filter growth.
