## Surface Texts

**Website article seed:**

After HECTON-8, evidence did not move only through courts, archives, or rescue offices. It moved through cleanup bids. A cleanup bid is a paid request to sort a fragment into a safer category: damaged telemetry, claimant correspondence, obsolete repair notice, salvage lot attachment, insurance duplicate, or inadmissible route debris. The bid may be legal, predatory, desperate, or fraudulent. Its existence proves that someone wanted the record handled.

The bid does not prove the record true. It does not prove it false. It does not prove the bidder caused the event. It shows pressure around the fragment and names the office willing to spend currency, favor, or leverage to change how the fragment travels. Deep Reach used cleanup bids as a public-safe shield. Marauder cells used the same market to recover trails before they were erased. Atlas-linked relays sometimes produced cleanup bids without knowing what the recovered packet meant.
