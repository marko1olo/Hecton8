## English Authority Surface Texts

### External Site / Wiki Article

Title: Tau Ceti Public Ledger Pressure

Tau Ceti cannot pull an operator out of HECTON-8.

It can make a receipt hard to bury.

That is the public ledger pressure route. A witness hash leaves Aegir through a custody packet, crosses Luyten Junction as a notarized delay record, and enters a Tau Ceti public docket after the relay interval. The docket does not stop a flood, patch a bathydrop, or put oxygen in a suit. It changes what Deep Reach can deny later.

The route is slow enough to be useless as rescue and fast enough to damage a lie. Aegir stale-law timing means the public record may open while the operator is still fighting local pressure, Black Keel claim windows, Keelmark mass accounting, and Recovery Compliance language. Tau Ceti sees a packet. The player still sees a broken seal, a route timer, and water against the hatch.

Deep Reach can survive many private discoveries. It can reclassify claim material, contest sample custody, and buy time through quarantine wording. A public docket is harder. Once the witness hash and custody packet escape the claimant's control, the same evidence becomes political exposure, insurance pressure, and a future liability trail.

That future does not save the present body. It can stop the present body from becoming only cargo.

### In-Game Codex

Recovered public-ledger notice. Tau Ceti can publish verified evidence after relay delay, but it cannot alter Aegir rescue timing. Preserve witness hashes, custody packet seals, and Luyten notary marks if you want Deep Reach liability to survive outside the claim window. A public docket changes later denial, not current pressure.
