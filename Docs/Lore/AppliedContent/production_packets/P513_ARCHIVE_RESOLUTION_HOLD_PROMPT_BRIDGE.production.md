## Surface Texts

**Website article seed:**

A resolution hold is the archive refusing to finish a sentence too early. It is used when a packet family has enough evidence to show conflict but not enough evidence to name the route conclusion. The public page may show the dispute reason and the missing proof class: witness hash absent, custody route split, receipt body unread, route alias untested, claimant-safe line unresolved, or legal hold blocking source comparison.

The hold is not a cover by itself. It is also not innocence. It is a procedural stop that says the archive can see the shape of the missing field. That matters for HECTON-8 because public readers often arrive after the labels have been cleaned. The hold keeps a clean label from becoming a finished answer before the object, route, and witness fields have done their work.

**Wiki article seed:**

Archive resolution hold: a public evidence state used when a packet dispute has a known missing proof class. The hold should list the reason code, missing proof type, current confidence state, and next-proof target. It must not reveal final route, final receiver, protected claimant, Atlas consequence, or legal result before the later proof gate.

**PDA / codex entry:**

PDA prompt: conclusion held. The archive knows what is missing, not what the ending means. Find the missing proof class before trusting the public label. If the hold says witness hash, chase the second hash. If it says custody route, separate object custody from office custody. If it says receipt body, compare header against body.

**Scanner entry:**

Resolution hold active. Evidence conflict has a named missing proof class. Confidence: partial. Required next proof: witness hash, custody route, receipt body, route alias, or claimant-safe line.

**Terminal note:**

ARCHIVE RESOLUTION HOLD

Do not close.

Reason code present.

Missing proof class present.

Conclusion field locked until next proof target is recovered.

**Evidence caption:**

Resolution-hold card. The archive names the missing proof class and refuses to turn a dispute into a conclusion.

**Spoiler policy:**

Resolution-hold language may appear early. Exact missing proof result, final receiver, protected claimant, Atlas consequence, suppressed drawer, and legal result stay masked until later proof gates.
