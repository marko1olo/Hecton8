## Source Brief

The source knows the bag still has usable evidence, but the trapped bubble makes volume uncertain. The article should turn the defect into a useful player action: mark suspect volume, draw a line, store it separately.

Player use: supports first water checks, ecology reads, and later PDA sorting without claiming lab implementation.

## Surface Texts

### Scanner

SAMPLE BAG // Air bubble trapped. Mark volume as suspect; draw a fill line before storage.

### Codex

The trapped bubble does not ruin the bag. It changes what the bag can prove. Volume is suspect, but color, sediment, and odor notes can still matter if the defect is marked before storage.

Good samples are honest about their damage.
