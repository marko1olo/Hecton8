## Source Brief

The source knows the bag still has usable evidence, but the trapped bubble makes volume uncertain. The article should turn the defect into a useful player action: mark suspect volume, draw a line, store it separately.

Player use: supports first water checks, ecology reads, and later PDA sorting without claiming lab implementation.
