## Surface Texts

External site / wiki candidate:

Material payout is one of the ugliest Aegir claim routes because it can look like survival from the inside. Black Keel can quote a lien reduction against custody-grade pressure glass. Keelmark Mutual can fold the same packet into load conversion. Aegir Continuity Holdings can receive the value as continuity recovery. None of those words require a worker name to survive.

The practice depends on custody. Blue debt is not valuable because it shines in a locker. It becomes valuable when pressure history, sample chain, route window, contamination state, and receiver identity survive transit. The same witness hash that could help prove a delayed evacuation can be priced as exposure, mass allowance, or silence risk if it remains inside the payout packet.

In-game codex:

Black Keel calls it payout. Keelmark Mutual calls it load conversion. Aegir Continuity Holdings calls it continuity recovery.

The route is the same: custody-grade pressure glass, blue-debt receipts, and Xenon-Omega sample hashes can reduce a 4.8 tonne-window lien while turning worker evidence into claim value. If locker names, route permissions, and witness hashes stay inside the payout packet, the dead become mass allowance and liability noise.

A paid exit can still bind the Marauder to silence, quarantine review, or future return action. It is an exit route, not absolution.
