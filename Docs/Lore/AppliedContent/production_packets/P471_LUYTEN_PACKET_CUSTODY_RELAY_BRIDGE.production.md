### External Site / Wiki Article

Title: Luyten Packet Custody Relay

Luyten Junction does not make HECTON-8 closer. It makes a packet harder to erase.

No one at Luyten can pull a body out of Aegir water on command. The Junction is relay and depot law: beam schedules, packet tariff, custody lanes, witness hash comparison, notary spools, and slow freight queues. A fact that leaves Black Keel with a readable seal may still take stale-law time to reach Tau Ceti public pressure. That delay is the point. It is also the cruelty.

Deep Reach can argue with a local relay. It can price a sample, stall a lift, dispute a welfare class, or push a quarantine clause through Black Keel's Aegir window. It cannot easily rewrite a packet once Luyten has logged the custody lane, tariff jump, notary spool, sender hash, receiver hash, and damage state. Luyten does not decide justice. It proves which ledger touched the evidence before justice has a chance to arrive.

P-63 teaches this early in small, ugly hardware. A relay board can show Black Keel receive confirmed, Luyten spool pending, Tau Ceti public lane delayed, and recovery lift absent on the same strip. The packet is alive. The operator is still underwater.

That is no contradiction. It is no-FTL civilization doing exactly what it can do: authenticate a fact years before anyone can physically answer for it.

### In-Game Codex

Recovered Luyten custody-lane mark. Luyten Junction can authenticate packet route, tariff queue, witness hash, and notary spool transfer after Black Keel receives local evidence. This can make a fact harder to erase and useful to Tau Ceti public pressure later. It does not allocate lift, oxygen, quarantine clearance, ascent hardware, or rescue mass now. Preserve witness hash strips before cutting relay hardware.
