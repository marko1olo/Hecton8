## Source Brief

The source knows the salt feathering points upstream and the grate frame remains intact. It does not know every upstream blockage. The article should teach the player to read water marks before cutting a frame that may still be needed.

The object is a repair clue, not a loot node. It should make current, blockage, and frame preservation legible.

Player use: tells the player that clearing a blockage can be better than cutting the grate. It can support scanner hinting and future repair-route ordering.

Forbidden facts: no magic current map, no instant full drainage, no total route answer, no runtime readiness.
