## Surface Texts

**Website article seed:**

A source voice label is the small line that keeps an archive sentence honest. It names whether the sentence came from a Deep Reach office filing, a terminal receipt body, an object mark, a scanner read, a public-safe summary, or a Marauder field note. The label does not decide truth. It tells the reader whose truth-claim is being tested.

Without the label, a contradiction turns into fog. With the label, the archive can say that an office line claims one route while the object mark preserves another. The player can then follow proof order instead of treating the cleanest public sentence as final canon.

**Wiki article seed:**

Source voice label: a provenance field that names the speaker lane behind an evidence sentence. Valid lanes include office filing, terminal receipt body, object mark, scanner read, public-safe summary, claimant-safe line, and Marauder annotation. The label is not a verdict and must not reveal final receiver, protected claimant, Atlas consequence, or legal result.

**PDA / codex entry:**

PDA voice label: read who is speaking before you trust the line. Office filing, object mark, scanner read, receipt body, public summary, and Marauder note carry different proof weight. If two voices disagree, keep both and follow the next-proof target.

**Scanner entry:**

Scanner tag: source voice unresolved. Compare voice lane, affected field, confidence state, and proof order before accepting the cleaner line.

**Terminal note:**

Terminal provenance note: this sentence is not anonymous archive truth. It carries a source voice. Preserve the voice label until the next proof target confirms or downgrades it.

**Evidence caption:**

Source voice label preserved: office, object, scanner, public summary, and Marauder note must not collapse into one neutral sentence.

**Spoiler policy:**

Safe to expose source voice type and affected field. Hold final receiver, protected claimant, final route branch, Atlas consequence, legal verdict, and rescue conclusion behind later proof gates.
