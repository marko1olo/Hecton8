## Surface Texts

**Website article seed:**

A public HECTON-8 custody trail can split without becoming false. The object may sit in one drawer, the office record may name another desk, the public summary may carry a claimant-safe cover line, and a legal hold may freeze the packet under a fourth authority. The archive calls this custody divergence. It is not a verdict. It is a warning that the same packet moved through different rules.

Object custody begins with the thing that can still be touched: seal, plate, bag, crate, sample sleeve, damaged tag, or stamped casing. Office custody names the desk that filed, stored, copied, or forwarded the record. Claimant-safe custody removes or softens a public name so the archive can show a summary without exposing a protected party. Legal custody stops movement under a hold, quarantine review, or dispute flag. These lanes can agree. On HECTON-8 evidence, they often do not.

When they diverge, the public archive keeps all lanes visible. A clean office label does not erase a salt-stained object seal. A claimant-safe line does not prove who owned the packet. A legal hold does not reveal the final receiver. Read the lanes separately before building a public relation edge or evidence caption.

**Wiki article seed:**

Custody divergence is the condition where one evidence packet carries separate custody lanes: object custody, office custody, claimant-safe custody, and legal custody. Each lane answers a different question. Object custody asks where the physical item went. Office custody asks which desk handled the record. Claimant-safe custody asks what can be shown without exposing a protected claimant. Legal custody asks which hold prevented movement. Divergence is handling evidence, not final ownership.

**PDA / codex entry:**

Archive rule: do not merge custody lanes. Object custody follows the seal and damage. Office custody follows the desk stamp. Claimant-safe custody follows the public-safe wording. Legal custody follows the hold tag. If the lanes disagree, keep the split until a witness hash, relay timestamp, or receipt body proves which lane carried the route.
