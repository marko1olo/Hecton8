## Source Brief

The source knows the tool wrap is dirty enough to isolate from clean stock. It does not claim a contamination system. The article should give tool care and inventory sorting one shared habit: wrap dirty gear separately.

Player use: supports tool-care and inventory UI language later.

Forbidden facts: no contamination-system claim, no automatic cleaning, no tool durability system, no runtime readiness.

## Surface Texts

### Scanner

DIRTY WRAP // Grit fold and oil stain. Keep tools isolated until wiped.

### Codex

The wrap is doing its job by looking bad. Grit and oil stayed in the fold instead of spreading into med stock or battery contacts. A dirty wrap is not trash if it keeps the rest of the kit clean.

Quarantine can be a piece of cloth.

Tool-wrap note:

- Oil stain: present.
- Clean-stock contact: avoid.
- Use state: isolate until wiped.
