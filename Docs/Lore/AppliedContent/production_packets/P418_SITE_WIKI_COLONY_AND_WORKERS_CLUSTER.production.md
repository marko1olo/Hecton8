## English Authority Text

### External Site / Wiki Article

Title: Colony and Workers

Deep Reach's public packet kept the failure clean: storm, geological collapse, autonomous evacuation failure, Atlas rescue attempt, data loss. The recovered worker chain keeps the dirty routing attached.

P63 WATER LEDGER / MARA VENN

The ledger is printed for ration volume and corrected by hand for turbidity, corridor pressure and medical reserve. Venn's signature sits beside a moved drinkable-water buffer, a locked medical allotment and a pump cadence mark. Deep Reach later filed the same page under autonomous allocation. The page still shows a person choosing which route stayed dry.

RELAY WITNESS TRAY / JUNO KADE

The tray holds packet seals, witness stamps and corrosion-bent relay slips. Kade notarized fragments that delay, brine damage and corporate pressure could have made unsigned. The object does not read as a communication miracle. It reads as the work needed before a message was allowed to survive.

BRINE ROUTE PINBOARD / REN OKOYE

Okoye's density slate maps brine as a moving road. The pinboard marks pressure turns, route permissions and the names allowed to cross them. Beside the liability act, the route stamps stop looking like navigation help. They become proof that the colony sent workers into a moving hazard because the route still had value.

TRIAGE TORQUE BOARD / SAHANA IQBAL

The board shares repair torque values with cot route tags and quarantine release marks. Iqbal kept triage repairs moving while custody language slowed patient movement. The surviving line is not mercy. It is seal, torque, patient route, hold.

VENT FORGE REJECT TRAY / LIAN TORRES

Torres appears in forge glove wear, anneal timer cards and pressure-glass rejects. The material became salvage later. In the colony record it was skilled labor first: heat, timing, fracture check, failed shard, repeat.

WORKER INDEX

Taken together, the records make HECTON-8 legible through jobs. A locker tag points to a route. A tool board points to clearance. A water ledger points to a pressure decision. A relay seal points to custody. A triage tag points to a door that could be safe open and legal closed.

The returning field-systems specialist does not need a family coincidence to understand the place. The language is already familiar: route, clearance, sign-off, delay, override, hold. That professional proximity is enough. Every failed evacuation left an operator trail. Every repair passed through someone's hands.

Deep Reach did not create the tide with a button. Glacial melt, tidal physics and geological failure did real damage. The company's record is worse in quieter places: accepted delay, changed Atlas weighting, withheld information, quarantine argument, loss conversion. Claim paperwork arrived where rescue language should have remained.

The colony was not a number. It was a set of jobs under pressure, and the records still know who held them.

Mara Venn, Juno Kade, Ren Okoye, Sahana Iqbal and Lian Torres are not quest mascots. Their records show which route stayed dry, which packet survived, which brine crossing was permitted, which patient movement stalled, and which pressure-glass batch failed before it became salvage.
