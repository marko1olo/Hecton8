## Surface Texts

**Website article seed:**

The confidence ladder exists because HECTON-8 evidence fails in different ways. A scanner can be confident about material and weak about route. A terminal receipt can preserve the useful body while lying in the header. A public-safe summary can protect a person while cleaning an office. One flat trust score would hide the problem the player needs to solve.

The ladder names useful states instead: provisional, object-supported, receipt-body-supported, downgraded, held, contradicted, and resolved by later proof. These states are safe to show early because they explain how to read evidence without revealing who finally received the packet or what the final consequence becomes.

**Wiki article seed:**

Evidence confidence ladder: a spoiler-safe set of confidence states for public and PDA evidence. The ladder can show provisional, object-supported, receipt-body-supported, scanner-downgraded, claimant-held, contradiction-open, and later-proof-resolved states. It must not replace proof order or expose final outcomes.

**PDA / codex entry:**

PDA confidence panel: do not treat confidence as one meter. Read the source voice, then the confidence state. Object support, receipt-body support, scanner downgrade, claimant hold, and contradiction-open states point to different next-proof targets.

**Scanner entry:**

Scanner confidence tag: confidence applies to a field, not the whole story. Material, route, receiver, custody, and claimant fields can disagree.

**Terminal note:**

Terminal confidence note: receipt body support can outrank a clean header. Keep confidence tied to the field that was actually observed.

**Evidence caption:**

Confidence ladder active: trust the field state, not the cleanest public sentence.

**Spoiler policy:**

Safe to show confidence state names and next-proof class. Hold final receiver, protected claimant, final route branch, Atlas consequence, legal verdict, and rescue conclusion behind later proof gates.
