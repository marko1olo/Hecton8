### External Site / Wiki Article

Title: Barnard Yards Salvage Origin

Barnard Yards sells work before it sells hope. A cutter tech leaves the breaker wall with a rented handle, a pressure-clamp certificate, a debt mark, and a list of dead claims that still have payable metal inside them.

The yards are not a clean academy. They are shipbreaking docks, pressure-hardware benches, claim offices, cheap bunk blocks, and tool cages built around old hulls. A worker learns which welds can be cut, which seals need rating proof, which clamp rings can be trusted below pressure, and which paper makes a salvage run legal until a better ledger calls it theft.

That is why a Barnard Marauder can step into HECTON-8 damage and read the room before the room becomes safe. Flood line first. Power next. Pressure path. Cutter only on service metal. Clamp before lift. Witness hash before payout. The habit looks like courage from a distance. Up close it is labor taught by debt.

Black Keel can use that history. The Aegir Reclamation Pool does not need a hero. It needs someone who already accepts bad gear, delayed rescue language, mass-through-window accounting, and the rule that a dead claim is still owned until the right office fails to answer. Keelmark Mutual can price that worker as a 4.8 tonne-window problem before oxygen, sample custody, welfare addenda, or evidence payload adjustment.

Barnard Yards is the reason the operator knows procedure. It is also the reason procedure does not free them.

### In-Game Codex

Barnard yard record matched against the operator's cutter tag. The mark fits dead-claim salvage work: rented induction cutter, pressure-clamp certification, debt window accounting, and Marauder procedure. This background explains the operator's first repair order on HECTON-8: drain, isolate, patch within rating, cut only service metal, preserve evidence, then move. It does not cancel the Black Keel lien, the damaged bathy-drop, or Aegir local custody terms.

Issued: low-power induction cutter, clamp set, seal gauge, witness tag sleeves.

Lien note: tool loss, hull contamination, uncertified cut, or failed payload witness may convert contract work into recoverable debt.

Procedure note: drain before cut. Rate before lift. Tag before sale. A dead claim remains owned until custody fails.
