### External Site / Wiki Article

Title: Keelmark Tonne-Window

Keelmark Mutual does not start the player with a balance. It starts the player with a mass problem.

The first lien is recorded as a 4.8 tonne-window equivalent before oxygen, welfare addenda, sample custody, and evidence payload adjustment. In Aegir claim language, a tonne-window is the amount of recoverable mass Black Keel may lift through a usable orbital opportunity after carrier fuel, storm position, quarantine review, and claimant priority have already taken their cuts.

That makes the debt physical. The operator's body, spare oxygen, pressure-rated welfare support, sealed samples, damaged capsule hardware, and packeted evidence all compete inside the same recovery queue. A sample that keeps pressure history can improve payout. An evidence payload can alter who is allowed to answer the lift. A welfare addendum can keep the operator alive long enough to owe more. Keelmark Mutual writes those categories cleanly because clean categories survive court review. HECTON-8 turns them back into air, mass, and route cost.

Aegir Reclamation Pool sells the window as claim infrastructure. Black Keel enforces it as queue math. Deep Reach can still touch the order through priority hooks and recovery clauses, but the first lesson is simpler: contact is not rescue, and debt is not only credits. The carrier has to decide what leaves the ocean when the window opens.

### In-Game Codex

Recovered Keelmark tonne-window notice. The starting lien is 4.8 tonne-window equivalent before oxygen, welfare addenda, sample custody, and evidence payload adjustment. Treat it as recovery mass, not currency. Operator lift, sealed samples, capsule parts, and custody packets can all consume or alter the same Black Keel window. Preserve evidence tags before taking high-mass salvage.
