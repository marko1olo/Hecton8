## Source Brief

The source knows the spool has enough contact length for a small cut or access release, not reckless stripping. It does not define all tool mechanics. The article should make cutting a decision with cost.

Player use: supports tooltips, scanner warnings, and route logic where clearing/repairing is better than cutting.

Forbidden facts: no unlimited cutter, no all-lock bypass, no tool combat implication, no runtime readiness.

## Surface Texts

### Scanner

CONTACT CUTTER // Short spool. One careful cut; avoid structural ribs.

### Codex

The spool is short by design. It gives a field crew one careful cut where a latch, cable tie, or light cover blocks repair. It is not enough to carve through a room, and it should not touch structural ribs unless the route is already lost.

A cutter is a key with teeth, not a plan.

### PDA Log

Tool-locker note:

- Spool: short.
- Use class: access cut.
- Structural ribs: avoid.
- Refill: unavailable.

Mark cut target before spending spool.
