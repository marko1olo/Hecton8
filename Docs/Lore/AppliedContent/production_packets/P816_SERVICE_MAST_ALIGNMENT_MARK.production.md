## Source Brief

The source knows the mast is bent off its old alignment notch and can be reseated if the hinge is not forced. It does not know signal success. The article should make alignment visible and repairable.

Player use: supports scanner hints, prop decals, and future interact prompts for mast alignment.

Forbidden facts: no guaranteed clear channel, no remote autopilot fix, no full comms restoration, no runtime readiness.

The mast is not broken enough to be useless. Storm load bent the hinge off its alignment notch and left the old mark exposed. Forcing the bracket would shear the mount. Reseating it gives the receiver a real direction again.
