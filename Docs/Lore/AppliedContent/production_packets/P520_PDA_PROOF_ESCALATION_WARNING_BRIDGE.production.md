**Website article seed:**

A proof escalation warning appears when the next correct question would answer too much. It can happen when a contradiction card points toward a protected claimant, when a route alias starts revealing the final route branch, when a receipt chain approaches the final receiver, or when an Atlas-linked consequence would turn an early clue into an ending dossier.

The warning is not a dead end. It tells the reader which safe comparison remains available: source voice, affected field, scanner confidence, object mark, receipt body, or custody route. The archive keeps the player moving without printing the answer before the route has earned it.
