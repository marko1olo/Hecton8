## Source Brief

The source knows the cold sealant cured under pressure and the patch lasted beyond its intended temporary window. It does not know whether the responsible office understood the final risk. The object should carry the contradiction: a cheap patch held, but it should not have been asked to hold this long.

The article should support repair logic and evidence logic at the same time.

Player use: tells the player where sealant, clamps, and pressure scars matter. It can also connect to Deep Reach language about acceptable margins without making a full accusation.

Forbidden facts: no final liability proof, no all-purpose sealant, no permanent miracle patch, no runtime readiness.
