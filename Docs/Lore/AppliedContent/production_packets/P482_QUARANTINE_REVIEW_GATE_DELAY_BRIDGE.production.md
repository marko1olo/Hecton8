## Canon Summary

The Quarantine Review Gate is a lower Deep Reach claim-chain surface. It delays evacuation, recovery, and payload release by converting real hazards into review gates: biological contamination, pressure-chain custody, damaged Atlas-6 classification, Xenon-Omega sample behavior, and Black Keel receiver legality.

The delay is physically plausible. Great Tide damage corrupts logs and route evidence. Atlas-6 can misclassify bodies, machinery, local life, and pressure substrate as one contaminated inventory. Xenon-Omega and blue debt samples can carry brine, biological bloom, signal drift, or weak Atlas-compatible pressure-harmonic noise. A recovery carrier has a legitimate reason to demand pressure seals, packet witness hashes, and custody proof before taking mass aboard.

The crime is not that the hazards are fake. The crime is that Recovery Compliance uses those hazards to keep the player, samples, coordinates, and worker proof in review until Deep Reach's custody position improves.

## Surface Texts

External site / wiki:

Quarantine Review Gate is the Deep Reach name for a release screen attached to contaminated recovery claims. It does not read like a prison order. It reads like safety: pressure seal incomplete, biological chain uncertain, Atlas classification unstable, packet witness hash disputed, Black Keel receiver not yet certified.

That language can be true and still lethal. After the Great Tide, HECTON-8 had broken evacuation routes, corrupted Atlas logs, pressure-grown Xenon-Omega substrate, and living contamination carried through brine, tools, seals, and bodies. A careless recovery could spread evidence, ruin custody, or bring a bad sample into a carrier cabin.

Deep Reach kept the gate because it bought time. Recovery Compliance can hold a survivor, sample, or coordinate packet in review until Keelmark Mutual, the Packet Notary Interface, and Black Keel recovery terms all point back toward Deep Reach control.

In-game codex:

The Quarantine Review Gate is why acknowledgement is not rescue. Black Keel can see a recovery window and still refuse extraction if the packet has no clean pressure seal, no acceptable witness hash, or no receiver that will accept biological and Atlas-classified risk.

The gate is not nonsense. Blue debt can drift, fracture, bloom, or carry weak Atlas-compatible signal noise. Atlas-6 damage can mark workers, tools, animals, and pressure substrate under the same contaminated inventory class. Great Tide records give the office enough broken evidence to write a legal hold.

The useful question is who benefits from the delay. In the recovered chain, the answer is Recovery Compliance.
