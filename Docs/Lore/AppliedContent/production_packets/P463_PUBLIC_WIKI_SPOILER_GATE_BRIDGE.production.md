Title: Spoiler Gate

The gate protects five things: the player's first descent, the colony worker evidence, the Deep Reach liability chain, the Atlas-6 repair-network truth, and final payload outcomes. Public pages can point toward those subjects. They cannot carry the final route, receiver, or consequence.

Use public articles as maps. Use recovered evidence as proof.
