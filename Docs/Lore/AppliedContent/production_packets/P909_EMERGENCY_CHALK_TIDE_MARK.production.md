The source knows the chalk mark shows old tide reach and the lower band smeared under later water. It does not predict current tide. The article should make timing and route caution local, not magical.

Player use: supports tide-window reading, shelter approach, and service buoy timing.

### Codex

The chalk line was drawn for a reason and then partly disproved by water. The upper mark still holds. The lower band smeared when the tide or storm reach climbed past the old assumption.

Trust the mark enough to learn from it, not enough to obey it.
