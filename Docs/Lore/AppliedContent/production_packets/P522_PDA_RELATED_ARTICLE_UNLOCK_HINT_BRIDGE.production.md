## Surface Texts

**Website article seed:**

A related article unlock hint explains a lock without printing the locked answer. The PDA can say that the entry needs a source voice check, a confidence ladder state, a receipt-body comparison, a claimant-safe audit, or a route alias comparison. It must not say the final receiver or route branch that waits behind the lock.

Good hints keep the player moving. They name the safe action, not the spoiler. They also stop the public archive from pretending a missing link is a broken page.

**Wiki article seed:**

Related article unlock hint: a label for locked or partially visible evidence links. It should name visible requirement, held proof class, safe preview label, and next-proof target. It must not expose the hidden relation or final outcome.

**PDA / codex entry:**

PDA unlock hint: related entry held. Check the visible requirement first: source voice, confidence state, receipt body, claimant-safe audit, route alias, or custody route. The locked answer remains protected.

**Scanner entry:**

Scanner unlock tag: related entry held until safe proof requirement is met.

**Terminal note:**

Terminal hint note: link is not broken. It is held by proof class. Complete the safe comparison before opening the relation.

**Evidence caption:**

Related article held: safe requirement visible, protected answer hidden.

**Spoiler policy:**

Safe to show visible requirement, held proof class, and next-proof target. Hold exact hidden relation, final receiver, protected claimant, Atlas consequence, legal verdict, and rescue conclusion behind later proof gates.
