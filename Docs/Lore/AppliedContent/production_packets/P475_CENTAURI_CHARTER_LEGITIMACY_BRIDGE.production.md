## English Authority Surface Texts

### External Site / Wiki Article

Title: Centauri Charter Legitimacy

Centauri Compact made extrasolar charters sound civilized before the frontier learned how to weaponize them.

The Compact was not a rescue fleet and not a clean moral court. It was the early proof that a human domain beyond Sol could keep registries, audits, civic seals, route standards, and inheritance law stable under interstellar delay. That mattered. A charter with a Centauri audit seal could make a remote project look older than a land grab and cleaner than a contractor's ledger.

Deep Reach used that inheritance well. Its Aegir papers cite old Centauri language for continuity, worker welfare, civic liability, and autonomous habitat duty. The words are respectable. The plate is usually beautiful: etched alloy, double-ring audit seal, a witness hash sleeve, a stamped route clause from the Sol-Centauri Charter Spine. Then the same plate appears on a drowned P-63 cabinet, a Black Keel receiver page, or an Aegir shell docket that prices recovery mass before operator survival.

That is the abuse. Deep Reach did not need to forge legitimacy from nothing. It could cite a real civic language and hollow it out one rider at a time. "Continuity" could mean habitat care in a Centauri registry and claim preservation on HECTON-8. "Worker welfare" could mean audited extraction capacity in one charter and delayed quarantine release in another. The paper stays lawful while the intent rots under pressure.

For the player, the charter seal is a warning, not a comfort. If Centauri language is present, the document may survive court review. It does not prove the machine beside it was built to save anyone.

### In-Game Codex

Recovered Centauri charter plate. Centauri Compact language gave early extrasolar projects civic legitimacy: audit seals, route standards, welfare clauses, and witness hashes. Deep Reach can cite that language on Aegir hardware while violating its intent. Preserve charter plates and rider strips. They can prove which shell claimed lawful continuity before the room proved otherwise.
