## Surface Texts

**Website article seed:**

Hecton-8 scanner confidence drops when the instrument sees more than one story attached to the same object. A label may name a receiver. A stamped plate may name an older office. A witness hash may repeat under a different category. A terminal receipt may carry the old drawer route under a safer header. A route alias may show that the object moved before the archive learned its public name.

The downgrade is not a failure message. It is a custody warning. The scanner keeps the conflict visible so a clean label cannot erase a cracked seal, salt-bloomed tag, scorched adhesive, receipt body, or repeated hash. Public readers may see "partial confidence." Field users read it as: do not throw away the dirty mark because the neat line is easier to file.

**Wiki article seed:**

Scanner confidence downgrade reason: a qualified scanner note applied when evidence fields conflict. Common downgrade causes include label/object mismatch, object mark/receipt mismatch, witness hash under multiple labels, route alias conflict, and terminal receipt rewrite. The note does not decide final custody. It preserves the conflict until another evidence channel explains which field changed.

**PDA / codex entry:**

Scanner rule: partial confidence means the object is still useful. If the label, physical mark, witness hash, terminal receipt, or route alias disagree, keep every field. A clean name tells you how someone filed the object. A damaged mark may tell you where it moved before that name appeared.

**Scanner entry:**

Confidence downgraded. Label, object mark, witness hash, receipt echo, or route alias conflict detected. Result: partial. Required next evidence: matching mark, receipt body, repeated hash, or alias trail.

**Terminal note:**

SCANNER CONFIDENCE DOWNGRADE

Cause: evidence fields disagree.

Do not promote label over object mark.

Do not discard receipt body, witness hash, or route alias.

Action: preserve partial confidence until conflict source is resolved by another evidence channel.

**Evidence caption:**

Partial-confidence scan. The scanner kept a label conflict visible because the object mark, witness hash, receipt echo, or route alias did not agree with the clean field.
