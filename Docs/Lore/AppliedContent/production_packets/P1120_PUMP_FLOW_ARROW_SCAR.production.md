## Source Brief

The source knows the arrow is a maintenance mark, not a UI prompt. The scar shows intended flow direction and warns against reversing a hose because the room is wet and labels are damaged.

Player use: supports early pump repair, utility-room readability, and salvage discipline.

Forbidden facts: no pump-system claim, no automatic repair, no full pressure assurance, no runtime readiness.

## Surface Texts

### Scanner

PUMP MARK // Scratched arrow marks flow direction. Match the hose socket before reseating.

### Codex

The painted label is gone, but the scratch survived. Someone cut the flow arrow into the casing where salt and handling could not wipe it away. It is crude, useful, and meant to be read before the hose moves.

The pump can be wrong even when the part fits.

### PDA Log

Pump mark:

- Arrow: scratched into casing.
- Hose socket: wet scrape on outflow side.
- Label paint: gone.
- Action: align hose direction before reseating.

Do not treat socket shape as proof of direction.
