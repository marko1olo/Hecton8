## English Authority Surface Texts

### External Site / Wiki Article

Title: Packet Custody

A message from HECTON-8 is not useful because it was spoken. It is useful because the route can prove who carried it, when it left, what it crossed, and which claimant failed to erase it.

Black Keel's contract window treats cargo and testimony the same way: mass, seal, serial, receiver, delay. A sample that reaches the carrier without a custody strip is just claim material. A body name without a witness hash becomes a loss category. A pressure record without route stamps can be filed as sensor noise before the next relay window opens.

That is why early salvage work keeps returning to ugly small objects: bent packet plates, wet notary strips, route stamps, relay slips, salt-swollen connector tags. They look like paperwork until the ocean makes them physical. The player is not only collecting lore. They are preserving the chain that lets a fact survive Aegir.

Packet custody is not justice. It is the minimum condition for justice to have a route.

### In-Game Codex

Recovered packet seal. Black Keel can receive cargo, but cargo is not the same as proof. A valid route record needs source, seal, witness hash, relay mark, receiver lane, and damage state. Deep Reach can dispute an unsealed packet as corrupted material. Preserve custody tags before stripping hardware.

### Scanner

PACKET SEAL // Witness hash strip damaged but readable. Preserve before salvage cut. Unsealed evidence converts to claim material.

### Terminal / Document

KEELMARK PACKET NOTARY INTERFACE

Action: preserve seal plate, witness hash, route stamp, and connector tag before sample transfer. Unstamped material may be processed under claim-loss conversion.

Seal first, cut second. A clean sample with a broken tag belongs to whoever owns the ledger.
