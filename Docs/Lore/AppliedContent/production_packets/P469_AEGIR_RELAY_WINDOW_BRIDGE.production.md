### External Site / Wiki Article

Title: Aegir Relay Window

An Aegir relay window is not a miracle channel. It is a short, dirty alignment between HECTON-8 weather, moon shadow, magnetic noise, relay queue, and Black Keel carrier geometry.

The window can carry a message before it can carry help. A packet has no wet mass, no cracked ascent frame, no quarantine body state, and no tonne-window claim against the carrier. It can leave through a narrow receive lane while storm plumes still blind optical relay, while charged-particle noise dirties the carrier handshake, or while P-63 still marks the bathydrop as ascent-incapable.

That timing is why HECTON-8 can answer and still feel abandoned. Black Keel may confirm receipt. Deep Reach may send a sanitized line through a legal relay. Keelmark may price the next custody step. None of those states repairs the ascent package, clears the weather top, verifies quarantine, or moves a recovery mass lane into reach.

Public route maps make the delay sound clean: receive, review, recover. The player sees the ugly version on a relay board: receive confirmed, uplink unstable, handshake stale, recovery blocked.

Contact is information. Recovery is machinery, weather, orbit, law, and mass agreeing at the same time.

### In-Game Codex

Recovered Aegir relay forecast. A receive window means a signal can pass through local storm, moon-shadow, and magnetic noise. It does not mean Black Keel can recover the operator. Extraction still needs ascent-capable hardware, clean carrier geometry, quarantine/legal handshake, accepted payload state, and tonne-window allocation. Keep relay strips and timing boards intact; stale replies can still affect contracts.

### Scanner

RELAY WINDOW // Signal receive lane opening. Storm plume and Aegir magnetic noise remain high. Recovery blocked until ascent package, quarantine handshake, and tonne-window allocation clear.

### Terminal / Document

P-63 AEGIR LOCAL RELAY FORECAST

Receive lane: partial.

Uplink quality: intermittent; storm-top occlusion and magnetic noise above clean-carrier threshold.

Carrier state: Black Keel acknowledgement possible. Recovery mass lane unavailable.

Required before lift: ascent-capable package, verified quarantine handshake, accepted payload state, clean carrier geometry, and tonne-window allocation.

Operator note: receipt of message or evidence packet is not recovery authorization.

### Audio / Transcript

Black Keel relay: "Signal lane open. Recovery lane closed. Do not spend air on the wrong window."

### Marauder Field Note

When the board says window open, ask which window. A voice can cross bad sky. A body needs the sky, the ship, the seal, and the bill to agree.

### Black-Box / Telemetry Fragment
