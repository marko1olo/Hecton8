## Surface Texts

**Website article seed:**

A spoiler-safe crosslink label is a controlled name for a related article. It can say that two entries share an object mark, a source voice, a scanner downgrade, a custody route, or a proof-order dependency. It must not say that the link proves the final receiver, the protected claimant, the Atlas consequence, or the ending branch.

The label keeps navigation useful without turning the sidebar into a confession. The reader can follow evidence families, but the relation edge stays procedural until the route earns the answer.

**Wiki article seed:**

Spoiler-safe crosslink label: a public/wiki relation label that names a safe relation class and hides the exact spoiler relation. It should include visible relation class, held relation class, confidence state, and safe click target. It must not expose final route, claimant, receiver, Atlas consequence, or ending result.

**PDA / codex entry:**

PDA related-entry label: this link is safe to open because it shares a visible evidence feature. The exact relation is held. Read source voice and confidence before treating the link as proof.

**Scanner entry:**

Scanner relation tag: safe relation visible. Exact relation held behind proof gate.

**Terminal note:**

Terminal link note: related entry is visible by safe feature match. Do not infer final relation from the link alone.

**Evidence caption:**

Crosslink label: safe relation shown, spoiler relation held.

**Spoiler policy:**

Safe to show visible relation class and safe click target. Hold exact spoiler relation, final receiver, protected claimant, Atlas consequence, legal verdict, and rescue conclusion behind later proof gates.
