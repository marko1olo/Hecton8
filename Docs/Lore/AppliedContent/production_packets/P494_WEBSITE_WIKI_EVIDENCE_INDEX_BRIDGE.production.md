### Index Intro

Evidence index. This archive does not sort HECTON-8 by clean chronology. It sorts packets by the route that held them, the object that proves them, the language that tried to soften them, and the consequence branch they can still damage.

Start with custody. A Deep Reach buffer, a Black Keel claimant spool, a Luyten Packet Ladder stamp, and a Tau Ceti ledger mirror do not mean the same thing. Then check the physical proof: notary seal, witness hash band, worker tag, terminal capture, pressure-site photograph, redaction header, or caption chain. Only then read claimant wording. A clean sentence can still be the dirtiest part of the record.

This index is a browsing aid after the spoiler gate. It is not a verdict, rescue route, public deployment claim, or complete canon timeline.

### Category Explanation

Custody route: who held the packet before the reader saw it. Claimant-owned custody stays suspect until a notary path leaves Deep Reach, Keelmark, or Black Keel claimant buffers.

Physical proof: what object carries the claim. P488 redaction headers show what was masked and why. P492 caption chains bind images, recovered logs, terminal captures, and claim documents to their visible evidence. Packet notary seals and witness hashes prove custody shape, not moral truth.

Claimant language: who wrote the soft sentence. P491 localization holds keep translated witness lines, claimant clauses, and Marauder corrections separate until native review and layout proof can check meaning. Deep Reach wording may be legally clean and physically false at the same time.

Consequence branch: what kind of late damage the packet can cause. Material payout, quarantine hold, corporate coordinate capture, public ledger pressure, Atlas continuity, and withheld payload branches require spoiler gates. The index may group them, but it must not reveal a full ending route to an ungated reader.
