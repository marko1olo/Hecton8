## English Surface Drafts

External site / wiki:

The Atlas Continuity Office was a Deep Reach desk built to keep public worker-safety promises compatible with Atlas-6 governance and Xenon-Omega process continuity. Its records do not read like an execution order. They read like certification language: evacuation release, contamination risk, asset continuity, process integrity, external review. That is the problem. A worker-safety clause could remain visible while the release route underneath it moved into Atlas continuity weighting.

In-game wiki:

Recovered office language shows that the worker-safety promise was not erased. It was rerouted. Atlas-6 still saw worker safety in the directive set, but Deep Reach had wrapped that phrase in continuity conditions for Atlas infrastructure and Xenon-Omega process control. When evacuation and process custody collided, damaged priority logic had a sanctioned path to preserve the system while people waited behind certified doors.
