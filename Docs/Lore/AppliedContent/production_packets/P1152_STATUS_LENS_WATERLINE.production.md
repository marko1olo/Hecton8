## Source Brief

The source knows the lens is not reliable because water clouded part of it. The article should prevent reading an indicator color as final state when the lens itself is damaged.

Player use: supports power-panel literacy, repair caution, and environmental storytelling.

Forbidden facts: no indicator-state claim, no power-system claim, no automatic fault detection, no runtime readiness.

## Surface Texts

### Scanner

STATUS LENS // Waterline clouds lower half. Do not trust color without panel check.

### Codex

The status lens still has color, but the lower half is clouded along a waterline. A dim green through bad plastic can look like a healthy signal until the panel is opened.

The lens is evidence about flooding, not proof of panel state.
