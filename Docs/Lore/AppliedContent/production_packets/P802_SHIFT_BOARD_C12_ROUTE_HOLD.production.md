## Source Brief

The board knows work assignments and missing return stamps. It does not know why the workers failed to return. It should read like an operational artifact: route holds, work lanes, postponed drill, and maintenance debt.

The source hides the full disaster chain. The article can hint at Atlas bureaucracy through a pending maintenance request, but it cannot make Atlas omniscient or a single-note villain.

Player use: gives the room a routing function. Names such as pump spine, south lock, and aquaculture intake can later label spaces, pings, scanner entries, or blocked doors.

Forbidden facts: no final cause, no direct villain confession, no secret evacuation order, no exact corpse count, no instant help arriving from orbit.

### Codex

Route Board C-12 lists ordinary work: pump spine check, south lock grease, aquaculture intake filter, heater coil inventory. Two return stamps are missing. The evacuation drill line is clipped under a maintenance request and never reissued.

This was not a memorial board. It became evidence because the shift did not close.
