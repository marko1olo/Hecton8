## Surface Texts

### Public Archive Disclaimer

Some HECTON-8 archive captions carry translated draft text because the recovered source is not always English. A worker's spoken line, a claimant clause, and a Marauder correction do not fail in the same way when they cross languages.

Deep Reach records often sound cleaner after translation than the room they describe. This archive keeps those lines marked until native review and layout proof can check tone, direction, font coverage, caption length, and the legal meaning of claimant wording. The mark is not a downgrade of the witness. It is a warning against laundering evidence through smooth language.

### In-Game Codex Disclaimer

This entry contains translated draft evidence. Treat claimant wording as contested, especially where Deep Reach terms replace a worker's direct description. Marauder notes preserve the practical reading: who spoke, what object carried the line, what was missing, and what the translation may have softened.

Native review and layout proof are pending. Do not treat the translated phrasing as final voice or final caption fit.

### Terminal Translation Stamp

### Marauder Annotation

If the claimant sentence reads kinder than the hatch, keep the hatch. Workers say "door would not open." Deep Reach writes "release pending." A bad translation can make both sound like weather.

Do not polish witness language until it stops bleeding. Mark the draft, keep the source line, and let the archive caption say who still has to check it.
