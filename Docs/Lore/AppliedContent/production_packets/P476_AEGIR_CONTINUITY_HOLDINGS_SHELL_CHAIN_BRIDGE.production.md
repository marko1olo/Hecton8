## English Surface Drafts

External site / wiki:

Aegir Continuity Holdings is not a headquarters, a throne room, or a name anyone paints on a hull. It is the dull shell ledger that made Deep Reach's Aegir work look old enough, solvent enough, and lawful enough to survive relay delay. Centauri Compact charter language gave the venture a respectable accent. Sol claim law gave the seals and insurance grammar. Keelmark Mutual converted damaged lives and missing workers into loss categories. Aegir Reclamation Pool made local recovery hardware look like shared claim-pool infrastructure. Black Keel then moved inside that paper: useful, conditional, and always asking what can be recovered before asking who is alive.

In-game wiki:

Recovered custody notes identify Aegir Continuity Holdings as the shell that lets Deep Reach keep a dirty Aegir claim under older legal language. Treat the name as a receiver surface, not a person. When Black Keel asks for payload class, receiver authority, or tonne-window mass before offering recovery language, it is acting inside this chain. Anything routed through this shell can become claim material. If the player wants a fact to become evidence, the packet needs custody marks, witness hash, and a relay route the claimant cannot silently own.
