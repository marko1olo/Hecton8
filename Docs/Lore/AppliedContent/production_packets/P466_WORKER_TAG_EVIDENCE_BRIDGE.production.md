## English Authority Surface Texts

### External Site / Wiki Article

Title: Worker Tag Evidence

A worker name on HECTON-8 is not proof by itself. A name can be copied from an old shift board, found on a loose tag, filed on a body-name hash, or repeated by a damaged terminal. Deep Reach can call it stale. Keelmark Mutual can call it unresolved load. The ocean can separate the tag from the room that explains it.

The stronger record is physical. A worker tag needs a shift slate, a route stamp, a damage state, a witness hash, and a receiver path. The slate says the worker was assigned. The route stamp says where the worker was sent. Damage state says whether the tag, body, locker, tool, or slate survived the same event. The receiver path says whether the record escaped the claimant's control through Black Keel, a relay notary, or another custody lane.

Without that chain, a name can be converted into a loss category. It becomes missing load, unverified body material, contaminated claim residue, or an accounting line that can be disputed before the next Aegir packet window. With the chain intact, the same name can resist a clean Deep Reach report and a Keelmark Mutual desk that would rather price the loss than carry the witness.

This is why worker evidence is small and ugly: etched tags, route slips, cracked slates, stained locker plates, salt-cured hash strips. They do not make rescue clean. They make erasure harder.

### In-Game Codex

Recovered worker-tag rule. A name is not proof until it agrees with route, shift, damage state, witness hash, and receiver path. Preserve worker tags, shift slates, body-name hash strips, and route stamps before cutting hardware. Deep Reach can dispute an isolated name as stale data. Keelmark Mutual can convert an unrouted name into a loss category.

### Scanner Short

WORKER TAG // Body-name hash present. Route stamp damaged. Preserve slate and receiver strip before salvage cut. Isolated name converts to loss category.

### Terminal / Document

KEELMARK MUTUAL LOSS DESK

Subject: worker-tag reconciliation, HECTON-8 local packet

If worker name, shift slate, route stamp, damage state, and receiver path do not agree, retain the line as unresolved claim material. Do not elevate to witnessed person record without external custody, witness hash continuity, and claimant-challenge review.

Approved interim classes: missing load, contaminated body material, route-unverified tag, unresolved welfare exposure.
