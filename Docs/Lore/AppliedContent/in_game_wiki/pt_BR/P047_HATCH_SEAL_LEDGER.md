# Ledger de Selo de Hatch

Hatch frames guardam mentiras uteis. Green seal light significa que o gasket aguentou, nao que a closure foi fair, safe ou reversible. Seal ledgers preservam pressure cuts, override stamps, hinge strain e direction da ultima manual closure. Em abandoned colony spaces, um hatch pode ser route blocker, survival tool, worker evidence e accusation no mesmo objeto. A pergunta raramente e se ele abre. A pergunta e quais pressure, water e human decision ele manteve separados.

## Scanner

Seal memory recovered. O gasket sofreu three pressure cuts e one evacuation override; return-side access locked from the wet side.

## Terminal

SEAL LEDGER / FRAME H-17: gasket deformation above service tolerance. Manual closure accepted under EVAC OVERRIDE. Return-side count unresolved. Reopen only after pressure equalization, hinge inspection e wet-side witness check.

## Audio

It sealed. Isso e tudo que o hatch prometeu.

## Field Note

Um sealed hatch e um receipt. Antes de abrir, pergunte qual lado pagou.
