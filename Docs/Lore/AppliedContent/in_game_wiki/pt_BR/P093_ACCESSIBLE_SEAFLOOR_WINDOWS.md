# Janelas de Seafloor Acessiveis

Accessible seafloor windows nao sao safe places. Sao rare permissions escritas por broken geology. Um collapsed shelf lowers the descent angle. Uma reheated brine column carries pressure differently por algumas centenas de metros. Um exposed ridge deixa sonar hold shape onde open water swallow it. Por essas windows, old floor fica reachable: mineral seams, crushed habitat anchors, pressure-glass, dead machines e evidence que o main ocean manteria out of human range. Rush a window e ela closes with the same pressure that made it valuable.
