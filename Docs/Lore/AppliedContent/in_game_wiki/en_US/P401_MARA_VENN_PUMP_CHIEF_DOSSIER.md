# Mara Venn, Pump Chief

Mara Venn was the pump chief who kept the lower annex alive after the intake meters began lying. Her dossier is built from work objects: a salt-pitted badge, a cadence board with hand-ticked strokes, a water ledger corrected in pressure ink, and a bypass handle worn smooth by repeated use. Together they show a worker forcing the colony to admit that water loss was not background ambience. It was a countable machine failure attached to human lungs.

## Scanner

Dossier: Mara Venn. Badge edge, cadence board, and corrected water ledger all point to Pump Room P-63.

## Terminal

PUMP ROOM P-63 / VENN, MARA: bypass handle polished through the glove layer; cadence board pinned beside intake loss marks. Ledger correction converts missing water from scenic leakage into machine load.

## Audio

Venn did not leave a farewell. She left a rhythm on the pump board so scared people could count the next breath.
