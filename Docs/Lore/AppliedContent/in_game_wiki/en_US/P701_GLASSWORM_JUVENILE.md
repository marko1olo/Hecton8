# Glassworm, Juvenile

Where the bloom is thickest, the plate behind it is cracked.

The colony grows on a fracture, not near one, and thickens where flow through it is strongest, so the healthiest patch marks the worst leak. Every tube mouth points upstream. Read the mouths and you have the leak axis without crossing it.

The bloom is soft. Behind it, 22.9 megapascals is working against a dry void, and the inflow at the crack will take a glove seam off at the wrist. Cut a loaded patch and the tubes fail in sequence, a run of ticks along the plate. That run is the crack taking its load back.

Every specimen logged in Sector 44 is a juvenile. The 2147 survey logged the same stage.

Assessment: harvest off the downstream face, and back off the plate the moment it ticks.

## Scanner
