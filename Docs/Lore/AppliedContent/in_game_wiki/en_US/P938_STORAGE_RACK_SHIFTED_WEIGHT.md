# Storage Rack Shifted Weight

The storage rack leaned after heavy crates were moved from lower shelves to upper ones during water rise. The shift saved dry stock and made the rack dangerous. Preservation and collapse can share the same decision.

## Scanner

SHIFTED WEIGHT // Heavy crates moved upward. Rack lean increasing.

## Terminal

STORAGE // Brace rack before pulling upper crates or lower salvage.
