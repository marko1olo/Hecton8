# No-FTL Transit Math

No-FTL travel keeps HECTON-8 hard sci-fi. Ships and messages move by infrastructure, velocity, shielding, braking and custody. This makes rescue a chain of windows and repairs, not a button.

## Scanner

Route math rejects live rescue. Flight is years to decades plus launch, braking and custody delay.

## Terminal

Transit note: no FTL, no ansible. Aegir packets move through relays, buffers and orbital windows; response authority can be older than the emergency.
