# Pressure Ladder

The pressure ladder is the practical campaign spine. It explains why tens of hours pass before Atlas-6: not by arbitrary locks, but by oxygen, hull, power, insulation, route knowledge and orbital timing.

## Scanner

Depth is divided by pressure ratings, not level names. Each band needs a different engineering answer.

## Terminal

Depth ladder: shelf equipment, canyon reinforcement, brine insulation, abyss hull discipline, factory-zone signal hardening.

## Audio

You do not descend because you are brave. You descend because the next seal holds.

## Field Note
