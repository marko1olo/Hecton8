# P-63 Manual Pump Chain

A recovered P-63 work order turns first repair into labor. The field fabricator can print valve gaskets, cutter contacts, pinger floats and sealant clamps after the room is pumped low enough for gauges to agree. It cannot print a clean escape, a new ascent sleeve, or a carrier handshake. The pump chain teaches the first rule of HECTON-8: water leaves only when pressure, tool rating and human patience line up.
