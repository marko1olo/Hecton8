# Density Skater Fauna

Density skaters are mid-depth animals that make the brine canyon feel like layered machinery instead of empty volume. Their feet, fins or hooked pads press on density interfaces strong enough to hold small bodies for seconds at a time. To the eye they seem to skate through open water; to the scanner they leave ripples where open water should have no surface. That contradiction is the clue. A skater track can mark a shear window, a brine step, a sonar blind edge or a hunting lane used by larger creatures. The animal is not magic and not a promise of safety. A suit is heavier, louder and badly shaped for the same trick. Use skater wakes to read the layer. Do not assume you can follow.

## Scanner

## Terminal

FAUNA BASELINE: skater contact rises near torn brine vanes, warm brine steps and sonar dead edges. Their wake marks a density shelf, not safe footing. Do not chase across opaque layers.

## Field Note

They are not levitating. They are walking on a boundary your suit can fall through.
