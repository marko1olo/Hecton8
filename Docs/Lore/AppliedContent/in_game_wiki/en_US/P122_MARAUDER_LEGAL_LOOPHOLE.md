# Marauder Legal Loophole

Marauders are not a tribe with one flag. They are contractors, debt crews, wreck divers and desperate mechanics working inside a loophole built by delay. A dead colony still has ownership, but ownership cannot patch hulls, pump brine or pull recorders out of crushed service decks. Deep Reach needs crews it can disown. The domains need evidence they do not have to collect themselves. The crews need enough paper to survive the next patrol.
