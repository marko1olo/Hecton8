# Stitch Kit Bent Needle

The needle bent against something harder than skin. Field medics learn that shape from sewing suit mesh in a hurry.

## Scanner

Thread fibers hold rubber dust and a trace of blood. The kit tray has saltwater pits near the emergency clamp.

## Terminal

The kit was logged for personal injury, then cross-charged to equipment repair. That saved one worker from a penalty fee.

## Audio

A medic says hold still. A second voice answers through clenched teeth that the suit is worth more than the leg.

## Field Note

Bent needles show which body the crew valued first: human, suit, or company asset. In deep water, that order shifts by hour.
