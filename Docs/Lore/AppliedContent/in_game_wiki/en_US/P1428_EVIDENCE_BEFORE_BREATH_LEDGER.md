# Evidence Before Breath Ledger

A distress hash can leave HECTON-8 before the player does because proof is built to be small. It is checksum, custody, witness time and a narrow claim about what existed. It can cross a relay window in pieces. It can be stored, priced, challenged and copied without asking anyone to keep it warm.

A person is not that kind of payload. A breathing body needs temperature, pressure, medicine, contamination handling, route authority, legal custody and a receiver willing to own the stop. The route can accept your evidence and still refuse your mass. That is not a contradiction in HECTON-8. It is the cruelty of a system that knows exactly what a human is worth in parts.

This is why the first answered packet should not feel like rescue. Black Keel can acknowledge the hash. A broker can rank the sample. A return desk can ask for coordinates, substrate and proof integrity while your air timer keeps counting. The player is not trying to make civilization aware. Civilization is aware. The work is to make the living body harder to ignore than the data it produced.

## Scanner

DISTRESS HASH NOTARY // Salt-pitted cradle with intact witness seal. Packet accepted: identity hash, sample hash, time window, pressure telemetry. Person not accepted. Life support remains a separate bill.

## Terminal

RETURN ACTION QUEUE // Evidence chain verified. Rescue language suspended until route ownership, breathing-mass custody, quarantine berth and lift reservation are assigned. Do not confuse acknowledgement with extraction.

## Audio

Proof travels because it does not cough, panic, freeze or need a seat.

## Field Note

If the terminal says accepted, read the next noun. Evidence accepted is not person accepted.
