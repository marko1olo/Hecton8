# Salvage Hardware Custody Plate

A recovered custody plate keeps salvage from becoming anonymous scrap. Hardware value depends on origin, pressure exposure, contamination state and who can prove the chain. A relay core from a worker route can be repair material, evidence or claim property depending which tag survives.
