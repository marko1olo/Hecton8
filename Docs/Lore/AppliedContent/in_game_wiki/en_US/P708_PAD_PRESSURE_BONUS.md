# Depth Band Bonus Schedule

Schedule DB-3 pays thirty-one percent more for working to 1400 metres, and the supplement is described as voluntary and elected by the worker.

At 1400 metres the water reads 14 MPa. The word voluntary is accurate: nobody was ordered deeper, so nobody has to be accounted for going deeper.

Beside the printed bands somebody kept a grease-pencil tally of elected Band C shifts. The marks run in blocks of five and there are far more than ninety before the next review mark appears.

The locker row behind the board is worn unevenly. The three lockers nearest the dive hatch have the paint rubbed off the handles. Assessment: the tally is a better depth record than the schedule, and the deep lockers are the ones worth opening.

## Scanner

Painted board, grease-pencil annotation, partially washed. Confidence 66 percent. Hazard: none. Limit: tally marks cannot be attributed to a name.

## Terminal

AEGIR CONTINUITY HOLDINGS / SCHEDULE DB-3
DEPTH BAND SUPPLEMENT, PER SHIFT
BAND A TO 400 M: BASE
BAND B TO 900 M: BASE +14 PERCENT
BAND C TO 1400 M: BASE +31 PERCENT
SUPPLEMENT IS VOLUNTARY AND ELECTED BY THE WORKER.
MEDICAL REVIEW: EVERY 90 SHIFTS.

## Field Note

Voluntary. You elect it, so when your ears go it is a lifestyle choice. Count the tally blocks against the review mark and you will know why the third locker is empty.
