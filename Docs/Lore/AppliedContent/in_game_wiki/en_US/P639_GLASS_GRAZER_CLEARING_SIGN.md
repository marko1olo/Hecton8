# Glass Grazer Clearing Sign

A recovered fauna scan makes glass grazers useful without making them tame. Their feeding trails clear photic mats into pale lanes where visibility improves and predator shadows stand out early. A fresh clearing can guide a diver toward safer water. An empty clearing can also mean the grazers left because pressure, bloom or a larger shape arrived first.

## Scanner

GRAZER CLEARING // Fresh scrape marks in photic mat. Visibility improved. Check for sudden silence before following the lane.

## Terminal

FAUNA ROUTE NOTE // Glass grazer feeding lanes increase line-of-sight and reduce mat drag. Abandoned lanes may indicate bloom shift, predator pressure or Atlas-tagged fauna disturbance.

## Audio

Marauder route note: if the grazers are still eating, the lane bought you a minute. If they are gone, ask why before you swim.

## Field Note

Follow fresh teeth. Fear quiet teeth.
