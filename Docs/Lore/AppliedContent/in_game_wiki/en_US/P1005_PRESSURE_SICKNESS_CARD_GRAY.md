# Pressure Sickness Card Gray

The gray card lists symptoms in plain blocks: joint ache, tunnel vision, sour breath, anger without target. Crews memorize the order.

## Scanner

Laminate bubbles around the word anger. Repeated thumb pressure wore the corner where supervisors initialed cleared returns.

## Terminal

Health notices were reissued after each depth push. The final version removed rest language and kept output quotas intact.

## Audio

A recorded briefing sounds calm until the compressor kicks in. Then the speaker repeats the symptom list at twice the pace.

## Field Note

Cards shape what workers admit. If the listed symptom costs pay, it vanishes from speech and moves into broken tools.
