# Pressure Ceramic Flux Card

A recovered flux card explains why ceramic parts need local chemistry. Clamp rings, seal seats and cutter contacts survive because the flux was cooked under the right brine and pressure history. A clean-looking blank without the card may shatter under ascent load.

## Terminal
