# Rubber Gasket Ring Pressure Seal

Pressure gaskets are the quiet part of HECTON-8 engineering. Plates and ribs take the visible load, but black rubber decides whether the ocean gets through the last millimeter. A good gasket is not decorative trim. It is a compressed argument between a room and the sea.

The material keeps memory. A latch-side lip flattened into one direction means the hatch closed under load many times. Salt packed deep in the groove means a slow leak, not a single collapse. Clean rubber under a torn outer edge can mark a forced opening after flooding. A glossy swollen ring means chemical damage; a dry gray center can mean the room behind it held a pocket longer than the wall around it deserved.

For a Marauder, gasket reading changes the order of work. Scan before cutting. Check whether the ring is still carrying pressure. Preserve a seal trace if it proves a door opened after the official evacuation window. Salvage the gasket only when the pocket is dead, the line is safe and the evidence has already been read.

## Scanner

## Terminal
