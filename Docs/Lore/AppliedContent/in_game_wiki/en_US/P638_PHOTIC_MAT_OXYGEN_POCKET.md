# Photic Mat Oxygen Pocket

A recovered shallow scan shows why the bright shelf feels alive before it feels safe. Photic mats can raise oxygen in thin pockets, feed glass grazers and make a damaged corridor look welcoming from outside. The same mat can hide bitter bloom, trap heat near metal and make a sensor read comfort where a suit should read caution.
