# Pressure Tape Repair Date

A recovered pressure-tape date turns a patch into a maintenance history. The tape shows repair age, worker initials, humidity bleed and whether the seam was meant to hold a room, a route or a sample cradle. A fresh-looking patch can still be old enough to fail the next pressure swing.

## Scanner

PRESSURE TAPE // Repair date legible. Adhesive bleed shows patch exceeded service window before flood.

## Terminal

MAINTENANCE NOTE // Tape repair accepted for local compartment only. Do not route pressure load or sample custody through expired seam.

## Audio

Marauder note: date first, shine second. Pretty tape lies better than old tape.

## Field Note

Every patch has a birthday and a death date. Read both.
