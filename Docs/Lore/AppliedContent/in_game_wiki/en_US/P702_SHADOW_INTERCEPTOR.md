# Shadow Interceptor

It does not follow you. It arrives where you were going.

On an open crossing the contact holds a wide bearing, matches your speed without matching your heading, and then leaves the display. That gap is the attack. It has stopped returning sound because it is now on your bearing, and a diver reading the plot sees an empty screen and relaxes.

Speed does not help. It has already solved for your speed. Changing your destination does, because the solution was computed against the old one.

Three suit beacons from Shift D stopped inside forty metres of each other on an open crossing with no structure to shelter in. The recovery queue listed the coordinates and closed the entry.

Assessment: when contact drops on a crossing, change where you are going, not how fast you get there.

## Scanner

Contact intermittent. Confidence falling. Hazard: closes on projected position, not present position. Limit: loss of contact does not indicate departure.

## Terminal

SPECIMEN FIELD REF 2147-D-09. CLASS: RECOVERABLE BIOMASS. INCIDENT LINK: SHIFT D TRANSIT LOSS, 3 UNITS. CAUSE OF LOSS: ROUTE SELECTION. RECOVERY: COORDINATES FILED, ENTRY CLOSED.

## Field Note

Cause of loss, route selection. Three people picked the same straight line across open water and the form blamed the line. Break your heading when the screen goes clean.
