# No-FTL Route Delay

Distance is an operating limit on the Aegir route. A packet can outrun a ship. A legal answer can reach HECTON-8 before the spare part, tender crew, pressure bottle, or human rescuer that would make the answer useful. Relay contact therefore changes paperwork before it changes the room around you. When the archive says help has acknowledged the claim, check packet age, carrier mass, braking slot, and local descent weather before you trust the sentence.
