# Muster Two Queue Log

Every card in the Muster Two box sends its holder into Corridor 6, which took water at 02:00 while the cards were still being handed out.

The board line to the print shop was down and the plate was never struck. One steward stood at the head of the queue, read the correction into a recorder, and shouted it at each person in turn, which holds for exactly as long as a single voice holds against an alarm loop and a crowd that is already moving.

Roughly 900 cards are left in the box. Twelve of them carry a second corridor number in pencil, in a different hand.

Assessment: a card recovered here is printed proof of a route that is not there. Take the room numbers off the ink and route yourself.

## Scanner

Steward's recorder in a card box, 900-count stock, most cards unissued. Confidence 92 percent. Hazard: printed route enters flooded corridor. Limit: no issue tally on the tape.
