# Atlas Final Question Cold Light

The cold light appears on the basin panel when all receiver paths remain possible. It gives no advice and refuses no input. Its value is that it holds the question open long enough for consequence to feel physical.

## Scanner

COLD LIGHT // Receiver paths unresolved. Panel awaiting final input.

## Terminal

## Audio

Panel tone: waiting without comfort.

## Field Note

The last honest interface may be one that refuses to help.
