# Hush Belt Orbital Silence Chart

Marauders call the overlap the Hush Belt. It is not a place on a map. It is the hours when four separate locks close at once: Aegir floods the band with magnetic noise, Kestrel breaks line of sight, HECTON-8's mineral ocean eats electromagnetic signal, and Black Keel accepts only custody-tagged acoustic packets.

That is why a sky contact can exist and still be useless. The carrier may know the storm. It may know the relay buoy answered. It may even receipt a salvage manifest. None of that means a human operator heard you. Below the upper water, communication becomes a slow legal object moving through pressure, salt, buoys and contract filters. A distress call is not a scream. It is a packet asking permission to be believed.
