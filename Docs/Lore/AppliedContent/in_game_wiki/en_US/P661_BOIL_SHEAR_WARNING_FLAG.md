# Boil Shear Warning Flag

A recovered vent flag marks where useful heat becomes a cutting force. Boil shear forms when hot plume, brine layer and structural edge meet at the wrong angle. It can cure a part in one pocket and strip a glove seal in the next.

## Scanner

BOIL SHEAR // Thermal plume crossing brine edge. Keep suit seals outside the white turbulence band.

## Terminal

VENT WARNING // Shear pocket active. Use clamp arm or sample tether. Direct suit contact may exceed gasket tolerance before pain response.

## Audio

Worker recorder: flag means the water is cutting sideways.

## Field Note

If the plume turns white, treat water like a blade.
