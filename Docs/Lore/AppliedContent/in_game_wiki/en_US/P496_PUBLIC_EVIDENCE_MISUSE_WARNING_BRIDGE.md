# Public Evidence Misuse Warning

The public ledger release is not a rescue flare. It is a price mechanism. When a Tau Ceti packet reaches a public mirror with its redaction header, caption chain, witness hash, and Luyten notary mark still attached, Deep Reach cannot cheaply pretend the room never existed. Aegir shell offices cannot sell a clean timeline without buying every attached contradiction. That is the value of publication.

The danger is that the same packet becomes a directory. Brokers use names and body marks to price a bounty. Insurers use timestamps to delay payment. Claimant offices use route plates to find families before advocates do. Cleanup teams use caption order to decide which witness can still be frightened, bought, or erased. Publication changes leverage; it does not make the people in the record safe.

## Scanner

PUBLIC LEDGER RISK // Redaction header, caption chain, witness hash, and Luyten notary mark are intact. Publication makes Deep Reach and Aegir denial expensive; it also exposes names, route plates, injury marks, and claim targets.

## Terminal

PUBLIC RELEASE WARNING TL-2190-AEGIR-77
Required attachments: redaction header, caption chain, witness hash, Luyten notary mark, original route plate.
Effect: denial becomes costly.
Secondary effect: bounty brokers, insurers, claimant cleanup teams, and market desks receive the same map.
Do not strip captions for bandwidth. A naked image is a rumor with a victim attached.
