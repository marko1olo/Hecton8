# Quarantine Review Gate Signatures

Quarantine Review Gate records are how safety language becomes delay. The gate does not need a line ordering anyone to die. It keeps extraction pending while pressure-biology risk, external review, and custody ownership remain unresolved. In HECTON-8's evidence chain, the ugly detail is that some evacuation hardware still had power when the hold was applied. Ikenna Sor's signature marks the gate that treated an open route as unsafe until certification arrived. Certification could not arrive fast enough across Aegir relay delay, and Deep Reach did not reserve enough independent lift to wait the delay out.
