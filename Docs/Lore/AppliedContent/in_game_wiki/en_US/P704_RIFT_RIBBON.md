# Rift Ribbon

It sets only within twelve metres of an open fissure, which makes a ribbon line a fissure map at lamp range.

Sonar cannot draw that edge. Fissure walls return nothing and the warm outflow bends the ping upward, so the plot draws continuous floor over a gap that takes a suit whole at 4,180 metres. The ribbons draw it because they grow nowhere else.

The lean is the gauge. Forty degrees off vertical is a working vent and a safe standoff. Flat blades with the holdfasts stripping mean outflow rising, and the surge lands about ninety seconds after that.

The blades plate with harmonic-class substrate, which is why anyone works this edge.

One ribbon in forty carries a pressed tag at the holdfast, part number 6-441-C, which appears in no survey. Assessment: harvest upcurrent of the lean, and never straddle a ribbon line.

## Scanner

Colonial strap growth, holdfast density rising toward bearing 210. Confidence 52 percent. Floor continuous. Hazard: thermal, 88 C intermittent. Limit: sub-metre voids below acoustic resolution.
