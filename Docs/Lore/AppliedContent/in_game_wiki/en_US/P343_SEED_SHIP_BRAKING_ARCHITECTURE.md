# Seed Ship Braking Architecture

Seed ships did not arrive like exploration cruisers. They were industrial payloads planned around deceleration mass, shielding loss, automated construction, and years of cold staging. Aegir had hardware before it had a settled human community because machines could survive the braking budget better than people.

## Scanner

Braking archive: arrival depended on sacrificial mass, magnetic sail capture, fusion catch burns, and shield ice consumed on schedule.

## Terminal

SEED BRAKE LOG: primary magsail deployed. Pellet catch partial. Shield ice margin spent. Cargo accepted for automated infrastructure insertion only.

## Audio

Getting there was not the hard part. Stopping with enough machine left to build was the hard part.

## Field Note

Braking records are colony prehistory. Every early habitat began as a decision about what mass could be spent.
