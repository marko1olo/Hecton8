# Best Route: Residue Left Visible

Residue Left Visible is the standard for the strongest route. The ending should save something concrete without turning HECTON-8 into a reward screen: a public record, denied Deep Reach routing, a preserved basin, a freed operator, worker names, or an Atlas proof hash.
