# Triage Ledger Page, Medical Post 2

Medical Post 2 sorted casualties into four printed categories: ambulatory, assisted, stretcher, deferred. Deferred was a movement category. Once the quarantine gate held extraction, nothing could be moved, and every row on the form resolves to four.

So the page is column four, forty times. Petra Nkemdirim ruled a fifth column with the edge of her ID card - every added rule line on that page is fifty-four millimetres - and tallied the injuries the form had no row for. She continued on the reverse, then on a dressing wrapper.

Her triage authority was provisional. Sort, do not sign. She signed the bottom of a column she had drawn herself.

Assessment: the cabinet under the board holds four sealed scrubber cartridges, and the hinge will take one pass.

## Scanner

Clipped ledger board, printed form, hand-ruled fifth column. Confidence 76 percent. Hazard: none. Limit: reverse-side entries and wrapper are dry-side paper.

## Audio

Post two, second tier, dictating for the shift file. Row thirty-one is a crush to the left forearm. Row thirty-two is the same. Row thirty-three is a scald and I have no row for scalds. All three go down as four, because four is what four means today. Anything past row forty goes in the margin. The margin is not a category. Sign it anyway.

## Field Note

Four was the gate, not the wound - the form was doing custody and she was doing medicine on the back of it. Cartridges or the wrapper, one trip: the cartridges are air and the wrapper is the only casualty count anyone ever made.
