# Recycler Bin Spoon Tick

The bin ticks because a spoon slipped past the magnet gate. Everyone hears it, and nobody wants to stop the cycle.

## Scanner

Steel contact marks circle the drum. Food gel hardened around the spoon bowl, sealing a small scrap of folded label.

## Terminal

Waste cycle notes show repeated utensil losses after ration portions shrank. Replacement cost was charged to galley staff.

## Audio

Tick, scrape, tick, scrape. The rhythm follows the drum until the machine coughs and the room falls quiet.

## Field Note

Annoying sounds become community clocks. When the tick stops, someone either fixed the machine or stole the evidence.
