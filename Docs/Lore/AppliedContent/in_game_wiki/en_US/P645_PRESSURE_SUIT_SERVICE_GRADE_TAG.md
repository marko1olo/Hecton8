# Pressure Suit Service Grade Tag

A recovered service tag keeps suit progression procedural. The tag names seal fatigue, scrubber load, thermal tolerance, contamination handling and pressure service band. It is not a hero rank. It is a warning label for which rooms can kill the suit before the diver understands why.

## Scanner

SUIT GRADE TAG // Service band readable. Seal fatigue and scrubber load exceed shallow-safe margin under current contamination.

## Terminal

SUIT SERVICE BOARD // Upgrade clearance requires pressure seal proof, oxygen loop inspection, thermal tolerance check and contamination rinse. Do not certify by depth label alone.

## Audio

Marauder locker note: grade tag is not permission. It is the smallest bad news the suit could print.

## Field Note

Read the tag like a debt notice. It tells you where the suit starts charging blood.
