# Pressure Bunk Routine

Pressure bunks show the colony as a maintained machine for daily life. The horror comes from normal systems obeying bad priorities.

## Scanner

The bunks were engineered for sleep under stress: seal checks, oxygen ration cards, condensation drains and shift alarms.

## Terminal

Hab routine: bunk seal cycle stable. Wake alarms cancelled by station priority override. Crew status remained recoverable for thirty-one minutes.

A bed is worse evidence than a corpse when the checklist still passes.
