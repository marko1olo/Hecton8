# Locker 44-C-17, Crown Muster Row

Teodora Ilves fitted pressure doors on the crown run, 1,904 metres down to Foundry Gallery 4. Her file records Band B and perfect attendance.

The locker argues with the file. Three scrubber cartridges inside are stamped to other names. The gloves are two sizes off her issue. Taped to the inner door skin is her own copy of the roster, with a grease-pencil swap chart running eleven weeks: who took whose deep shift, and which lapsed seal certification the swap was keeping off the roster. Extension 44-E-19 is hers.

The chart is drawn on the door skin, and the door is 31 kilograms of rated alloy.

Assessment: the door shims stacked in the base are salvage and the chart is not. Cutting the panel free takes the pencil with it.

## Scanner
