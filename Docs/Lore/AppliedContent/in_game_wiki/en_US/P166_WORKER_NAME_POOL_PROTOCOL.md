# Worker Name Pool Record

A worker name in HECTON-8 is a small chain of proof. It tells the player which domain sent a body into the ocean, which job kept that body useful, which route the worker could legally enter, and which badge fragment later survived in a locker, manifest clip or shift board.

The name pool exists so repeated runs can feel inhabited without rewriting canon. A pump technician might appear beside a gasket ledger in one seed and beside a medlock refusal in another, but the Great Tide, Deep Reach liability and station geography do not change. Variety changes the order of discovery, not the crime.

Good names stay grounded. They are not jokes, collectibles or melodrama hooks. They should sound like people who expected wages, pressure bonuses, meal credits and a next shift. When a Marauder reads a name strip before taking the tools below it, the room briefly stops being a container and becomes a workplace.
