# Deep Reach Present Windows

Deep Reach Present Windows explains how an absent corporation can still be an active antagonist in 2190. There is no faster-than-light rescue desk and no live executive steering HECTON-8 from a core-world chair. The distance is real: windows are rare, delayed and filtered through relay spines, local proxies, automated legal notices and Black Keel claim language. That does not make Deep Reach harmless. By the time a signal crosses the gulf, the colony is already shaped by old contracts, quarantine defaults, payout thresholds and custody rules. When the player holds evidence, the company's voice becomes audible as an offer: surrender Atlas authority keys, accept payload custody, submit to review, and maybe receive recovery instead of abandonment.

## Scanner

Deep Reach cannot steer HECTON-8 by radio, but its delayed windows, proxy notices and Black Keel clauses still decide what help costs.

## Terminal

LIVE WINDOW / DEEP REACH PROXY: operator recovery negotiable after evidence review, payload custody, quarantine compliance and Atlas authority-key transfer. Refusal preserves claimant status but voids immediate extraction priority.

## Audio

When their voice reaches you, it is already a contract.

## Field Note
