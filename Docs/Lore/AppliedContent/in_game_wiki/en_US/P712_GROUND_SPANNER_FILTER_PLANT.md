# Ground Spanner, Filter Plant 3

The spanner at Filter Plant 3, 1,340 metres, has a ground box end, a handle bowed four degrees from being struck, and jaws spread from 24 millimetres to 25.4.

Clamp bolts sit forty millimetres off the housing wall; the tool for that gap is a crowfoot on an extension. Nine requisitions came back not in current catalogue: the stocking module had been stripped for material retention. Yusuf Brannick's fitting ticket stayed current. The plant's clamp torque procedure was never signed off, because signing it needed the tool.

So the clamps were turned with an open jaw, angled, from one side, for years. He struck his initials into the shank twice. The first set wore away.

Assessment: every clamp bolt in there is worn to one profile and will not take a rated socket. Bring his spanner or bring a cutter.

## Scanner
