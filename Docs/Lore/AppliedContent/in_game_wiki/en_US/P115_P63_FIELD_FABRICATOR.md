# P-63 Field Fabricator

Shallow Annex P-63 contains the first field fabricator. It should feel industrial, constrained and useful. Early recipes are valve gaskets, cutter contacts, pinger floats and sealant clamps. The fabricator refuses dangerous pressure material until the player earns containment knowledge.
