# P-63 Field Fabricator

Shallow Annex P-63 contains the first field fabricator, and it behaves like repair infrastructure instead of a magic printer. Early recipes are valve gaskets, cutter contacts, pinger floats and sealant clamps: small parts that make damaged tools trustworthy again. The fabricator refuses dangerous pressure material until the player has the certification and containment knowledge to handle it.

## Scanner

## Terminal
