# Oxygen Ledger Cutoff Claim Hold

An oxygen ledger is a life-support account. It records scrubber cartridge time, suit reserve delta, room pump handoff, compressor feed and cutoff authority. When that row stops, the room has stopped supporting breath. It does not prove the exact second of death; it fixes the last point where the room still accepted an air load.

A claim hold becomes suspect when it outlives the oxygen row. Route desk can leave a worker active, and claim desk can hold recovery authority, but neither field can add air back to the room. If oxygen stopped first and the return mark stays blank, the active worker state is paperwork over a closed body path.

The useful contradiction sits across four things: scrubber cutoff, suit tag presence, route permission stamp and later claim exception. If the route remains open after the ledger cutoff, Deep Reach can bill delay, retain salvage authority or reclassify the carrier as recoverable mass while avoiding a clean loss closure.

Field handling is narrow. Preserve the ledger strip before rinsing brine. Photograph the cutoff row, cartridge serial, suit reserve delta and any hand stamp crossing salt. Do not let claim status overwrite the life-support limit.
