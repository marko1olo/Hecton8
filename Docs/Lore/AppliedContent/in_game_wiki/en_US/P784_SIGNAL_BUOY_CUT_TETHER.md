# Signal Buoy Cut Tether

The buoy tether was cut with a dull edge after deployment, leaving stretched fibers before the final snap. A clean storm tear would fray differently. Someone wanted the buoy free, late or silent, and used poor steel to say it.

## Scanner

## Terminal
