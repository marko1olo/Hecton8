# Pressure Bunk Shift Routine

A pressure bunk is not a bedroom in the old surface sense. It is rented warmth, restraint webbing, dry foam if the pumps are kind, and a small proof chain that says a worker returned from pressure. The C-12 slate records wake tone 03:40, suit-seal check, pressure note, meal-water token, tool custody and descent assignment. No sunrise reached the module. The pump tone did the work of morning.

The routine seems harsh until the chain is followed. A missed seal check touches the bunk lead. A late pump key touches the tool crib and canteen ledger. A pressure note can move a worker from route duty to medlock before a bad decision becomes a flooded corridor. The bunk was a bed, but it was also an address, a schedule, a custody point and a quiet alarm.

Small evidence matters here: damp sock tape on a rail, an abbreviated name, a meal token clipped beside a pressure note, one key due before tone 2. None of it was written for memorial. It was ordinary station life trying to keep a tired body alive long enough to answer the next tone.
