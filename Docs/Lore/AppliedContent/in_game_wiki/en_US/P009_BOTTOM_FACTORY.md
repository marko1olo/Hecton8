# Bottom Factory

The Bottom Factory sits at the lower return of the Atlas basin. Deep Reach pipework still moves brine and heat there, but carbonate sleeves and filter growth have closed over many service runs. Blue debt residue stains the seams where old steel, cable trays, and pressure-grown seals now share the same maintenance path.

The risk starts with the useful jobs. The place patches leaks, keeps pumps in circuit, and carries signal through routes that were never cleared for people. A door can stay shut because the gasket is gone and the pressure growth has taken the load. A cable tray can still relay data while its cover works as a filter. A suit ring, sample canister, or tool can disappear into the same repair sleeve if its custody tag is buried before anyone reads it. Treat every pale seam as active infrastructure until the flow marks and tag drawers are checked.
