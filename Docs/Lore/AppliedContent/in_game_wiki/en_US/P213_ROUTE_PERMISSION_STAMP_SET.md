# Route Permission Stamp Set

The Route Permission Stamp Set gives movement a visual language. PUMP, SEAL, RELAY, BRINE, FORGE, TRIAGE, BOTANY, and WITNESS stamps are not badges of rank. They are pressure permissions: who could cross a hatch, claim a suit, release a crawler, or enter a maintenance lock when the colony was failing. The stamp should always pair with a physical state: open, sealed, denied, overwritten, flooded, or later corrected by a Marauder hand. This lets the player reconstruct colony movement without needing a character to explain it.

## Scanner

A route stamp is stronger than a diary line because it proves who was allowed through the pressure boundary.

## Terminal

STAMP RULE: route permission must connect one worker, one door or vehicle, one job family, and one later contradiction. A stamp without a physical boundary is decoration. A stamp on a hatch, suit rack, crawler rail, or lift tag becomes movement evidence.

## Audio

Access is biography under pressure.

## Field Note

Stamps: PUMP, SEAL, RELAY, BRINE, FORGE, TRIAGE, BOTANY, WITNESS. Pair with hatch state and job object.
