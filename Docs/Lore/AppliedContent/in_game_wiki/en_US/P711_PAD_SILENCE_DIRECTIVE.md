# Silence Directive AS-19

Directive AS-19 sets what a lien holder may put on an open channel in Aegir Reclamation Pool water. No personal names against uncleared assets. No depth and bearing pairs for held lots. No accession codes in clear.

Bearings broadcast in clear draw uncontracted salvage into rooms that kill people, which is the stated ground. Breach suspends the tender window pending review.

AS-19 is bolted to the mast at Shallow Annex P-63. Three metres above the plate a muster beacon has been repeating a 2147 recall in clear since the tide. The recall reads out eleven names.

Assessment: the beacon runs off the mast bus and will mask a hail on the same band. Isolate it at the bus before transmitting.

## Scanner

Enamel plate, bolted, four fixings of five. Confidence 93 percent. Hazard: mast bus live. Limit: appended term list absent from plate.

## Field Note

Saying a dead worker's name on open channel costs you a tender window. The board that says so is bolted three metres under a beacon that has been saying eleven of them for forty-three years.
