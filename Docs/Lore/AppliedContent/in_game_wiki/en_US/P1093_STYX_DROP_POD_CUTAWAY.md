# Styx Drop Pod Cutaway

The Styx pod is a disposable pressure shell, not a shuttle. It moves one suited operator from Black Keel to a legal worksite with the least insured mass possible. It can survive atmosphere, storm shear and water impact well enough to leave shelter, power trickle, a terminal and a proof object.

Its failure is specific: heat tiles spent, uplink mast sheared, compressor flooded, recovery sleeve no-lock, claim handshake below recovery threshold. The capsule kept the operator alive while proving that delivery and rescue are different contract events.

## Scanner

BATHY-DROP SHELL // Ablator spent. Crush frame intact. Uplink mast sheared at hinge. Recovery sleeve no-lock. Survival bus local only.

## Terminal

DESCENT RECEIPT // Operator delivered to insured worksite. Dropcraft unrecoverable. Ascent package not certified. Carrier recovery requires window, clean hash and recoverable mass class.

## Audio

The pod did its cheap job. It got you down. Everything after that costs more than Deep Reach wanted to spend.

## Field Note

Do not strip the shell first. Read it. The broken parts tell you which systems can still become a way out.
