# Recorder Latch Glass Fracture

READ-ORDER CARD, recorder bay 4. Glass, teeth, wheel, stamp, in that order.

The latch glass is a pressure-rated cover over the bay latch, fitted so a technician could confirm seating without opening the recorder. Cheap, replaceable, ignored, and therefore intact.

Glass takes force differently from metal.

  PRESSURE STAR, short legs, no scrape on the tongue .. door shock
  CRESCENT BITE at the outer edge before the run ...... hand tool
  GLOVE POLISH, salt bloom, hinge-side chips .......... opened from inside a wet room
  CLEAN HOOK through settled mineral dust ............. late pry

Teeth record strip pull, the wheel records wet or dry, and the glass records access.

Bay 4 carries a pry hook cut through old salt. The recovery filing describes the bay as sealed on retrieval. One of the two is wrong and the glass has no motive.

Scan before salvage. Broken early, the bay is parts.

## Scanner

LATCH GLASS FRACTURE // Clear pressure glass over a recorder bay latch. Crack origin, inner salt, hinge-side chips and pry arcs show whether the bay opened under pressure, after flooding or by tool.

## Terminal

MAINTENANCE NOTE // Read latch glass before lifting the cassette. Interior bloom means the room breathed wet. A clean outer hook means late pry. A pressure star without latch travel means impact, not access.

## Field Note

If the glass is cleaner than the latch, the hand came later.
