# Receiver Counterstamp Room Number

A receiver counterstamp is the moment a blank berth becomes a named door. It can make recovery harder to ignore, but it also names who owns the body after lift. Check the room class, medical owner and receiver strip before trusting the stamp.

## Scanner

COUNTERSTAMP READ // Quarantine room number present. Verify body custody class; sample-only rooms do not clear living recovery.

## Terminal

RECEIVER ROOM COUNTERSEAL // Blank berth overwritten by owned custody. Lift allocation may proceed only under named room, owner and body class.

## Audio

Room number written. Receiver owns the door. Confirm body custody before ascent burn.

## Field Note

A room number is a handle. Pull it carefully; some doors are cages.
