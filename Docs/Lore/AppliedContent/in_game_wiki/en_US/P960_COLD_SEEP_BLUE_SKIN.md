# Cold Seep Blue Skin

The cold seep paints a blue skin over nearby metal where dissolved minerals freeze out slowly. It looks like corrosion but behaves like insulation. The skin can protect a seam and make a valve too stiff to turn.

## Scanner

BLUE SKIN // Cold mineral layer over metal. Insulating valve stiffness.

## Terminal

COLD SEEP // Warm valve gently before scraping mineral skin.

## Audio

Technician note: blue metal is not always sick, but it is stubborn.

## Field Note

Protection and obstruction can share a color.
