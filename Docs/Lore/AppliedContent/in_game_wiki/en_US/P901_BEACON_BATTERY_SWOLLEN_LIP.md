# Beacon Battery Swollen Lip

The swollen lip on the beacon battery prevents full latch closure even while voltage remains usable. Pushing harder risks venting the cell. The better repair is ugly: strap the latch, lower duty cycle and stop pretending the case is square.

## Scanner

SWOLLEN LIP // Voltage usable, latch closure blocked by cell deformation.

## Terminal

BEACON POWER // Strap latch and reduce duty cycle before deployment.

## Audio

Field note: power left, shape gone.

## Field Note

Capacity and safety age at different speeds.
