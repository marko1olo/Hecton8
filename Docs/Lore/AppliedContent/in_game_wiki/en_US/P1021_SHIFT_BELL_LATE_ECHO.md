# Shift Bell Late Echo

The shift bell echoes late in the east corridor because a panel cavity swallowed the first tone. People there learned to run.

## Scanner

Wall screws around the bell are mismatched. A foam pad stuffed behind the housing dampens vibration from a cracked pipe.

## Terminal

Scheduler complaints cite late arrivals from one corridor. Facilities closed the issue as behavioral after one remote test.

## Audio

The bell rings, then returns as a thin second sound. Boots start after the echo, not the first tone.

## Field Note

Timing faults become discipline records. The station punishes bodies for acoustics nobody budgeted to fix.
