# Pellet Wake Braking Ledger

A burned foil strip, a route-school fragment and a mass account put interstellar travel in the player's hand. Aegir was first reached by small pellet-beam scouts fired out from Luyten Junction. Those probes were cheap enough to lose. Heavy human freight came later, on Atlas-class carriers that burned fusion-electric pulses, slept their crews in torpor and treated every kilogram as debt.

The brutal part is arrival. A ship that spends years climbing toward near-light transit still has to shed velocity. Magsails bite the interstellar medium. Aerobraking skims gas giants and storm envelopes. A bad fraction in the margin leaves plasma where cargo used to be. HECTON-8 can be owned, billed and indexed by human law while still being unreachable as a rescue scene. The corporation can inherit the claim before a ship can cross the dark.
