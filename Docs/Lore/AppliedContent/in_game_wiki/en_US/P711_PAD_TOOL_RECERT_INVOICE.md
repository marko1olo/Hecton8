# Recertification Invoice TC-3390

Invoice TC-3390 bills 4,120 units to recertify one door jack. The same pattern costs 2,650 units new. Recertification needs a witnessed pressure test on a rated bench, and the nearest rated bench is off-site.

Section 11 deferred. Deferral is a section decision, and an unlisted tool cannot generate a process incident, which is what sections were measured on.

Jack 44-J-217 is clipped to the cage in Corridor 11, three metres from this invoice, cert sticker four years expired. One jaw is sheared through the pin boss.

Door 11-4 stands forty millimetres open at 2,240 metres, 22.4 MPa on the wet side.

Assessment: the jack body is salvage-grade steel. The door will not move for you either.
