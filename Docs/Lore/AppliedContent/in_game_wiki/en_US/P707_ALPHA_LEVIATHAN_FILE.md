# Alpha Leviathan: Convergence File

Nine of twenty-two events were merged under one designation because nine hulls filed stress alarms and the loss form has one line for cause.

SEC 2 MORPHOLOGY, SEC 4 MASS AND SPAN - WITHHELD (RCO/hv)
SEC 6 ACOUSTIC - CARRIER BELOW 40 Hz, 1340 m; UPPER BOUND WITHHELD
SEC 7 ROUTE IMPACT - HEADING ONLY, LINE 214
SEC 9 CONTACT EVENTS - APPENDIX K, NOT ENCLOSED, NO REMOVAL STAMP
SEC 11 SURVIVOR STATEMENTS - RETAINED, NOT COUNTED (HYPOXIA, NARCOSIS)
SEC 12 SPECIES MATCH - INCONCLUSIVE, 22 OF 22

What survives is instrument behaviour: hull stress alarms with nothing on sonar, bearings without range, flow turning across a corridor with nothing to turn it.

The quiet comes first in most of them: small feeders stop together, stay stopped, and one end of a corridor goes silent before the other.

Assessment: the end that goes quiet first is the end it came from. Leave on any other bearing.

## Field Note

Twenty-two bearings went into that office and nine came out with a name on them, because nine hulls filed stress alarms and a filing needs a cause. They cut the mass and span and left every survivor statement in, because there is no form line for removing fear. I don't trust Section 9 - a bearing with no range is a scared crew reading a hull alarm - but the quiet is real, so learn what a busy corridor sounds like.
