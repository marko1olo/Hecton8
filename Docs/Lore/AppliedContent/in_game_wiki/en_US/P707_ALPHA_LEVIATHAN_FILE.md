# Alpha Leviathan: Convergence File

Nine sightings were merged under one designation because the loss form has one line for cause.

SEC 2 MORPHOLOGY, SEC 4 MASS AND SPAN - WITHHELD (RCO/hv)
SEC 6 ACOUSTIC - CARRIER BELOW 40 Hz, 1340 m; UPPER BOUND WITHHELD
SEC 7 ROUTE IMPACT - HEADING ONLY, LINE 214
SEC 9 CONTACT EVENTS - SEE APPENDIX K
SEC 11 SURVIVOR STATEMENTS - RETAINED, NOT COUNTED (HYPOXIA, NARCOSIS)
SEC 12 SPECIES MATCH - INCONCLUSIVE, 9 OF 9

What survives is instrument behaviour. Hull stress alarms fire with nothing on sonar, and the bearing that returns carries no range. Flow turns across a corridor with nothing in it to turn the flow.

Before any of that, the water goes quiet. Small feeders stop together and stay stopped, and the quiet reaches one end of a corridor first.

Appendix K is not enclosed and has no removal stamp. Assessment: the end of the corridor that goes quiet first is the end it came from. Leave on any other bearing.

## Scanner

Contact unresolved. Material unknown. Confidence 94 to 12 percent in nine seconds. Hazard: hull stress rising, no contact logged. Limit: bearing returned without range.

They cut the mass and span and left every survivor statement in, because there is no form line for removing fear. I don't trust Section 9 - a bearing with no range is a scared crew reading a hull alarm - but the quiet is real, so learn what a busy corridor sounds like.
