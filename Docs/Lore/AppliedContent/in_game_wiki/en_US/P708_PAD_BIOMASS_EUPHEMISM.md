# Sector Reconciliation Strip, Q4 2147

Sector 44's biomass total rose eighteen percent in the quarter the colony drowned, and the reconciliation records the sector as productive.

The mechanism is one word. Unrecovered personnel were moved to organic inventory pending determination of status. Inventory does not have to be searched for. It has to be counted, and counting it made the quarter look better than the one before.

Ibarra signed it. His other work is careful and his arithmetic is correct throughout.

The strip was printed inside a room whose door is still sealed from the corridor side. Assessment: the payroll cabinet behind that door holds worker IDs, and carrying them changes who answers a recovery call.

## Scanner

Printed reconciliation strip, thermal paper, legible. Confidence 79 percent. Hazard: none. Limit: reverse side blank, no counter-signature.

## Terminal

KEELMARK LOSS DESK / RECONCILIATION 44-Q4
SECTOR 44 BIOMASS TOTAL: +18 PERCENT ON Q3.
BASIS: RECLASSIFICATION, UNRECOVERED PERSONNEL TO ORGANIC INVENTORY, PENDING DETERMINATION.
SECTOR STATUS: PRODUCTIVE.
ADJUSTER: M. IBARRA

## Field Note

Eighteen percent up and the sector reads productive. If you pull one thing out of this room pull the ID cabinet, not the terminal - the terminal only knows what it was told.
