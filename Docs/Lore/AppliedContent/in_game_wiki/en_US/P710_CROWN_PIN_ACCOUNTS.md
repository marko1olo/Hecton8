# Two Accounts of the Crown Pin

Two notes on one clip describe the same pin test on the same shift, and they do not agree.

The printed one: CROWN HOIST PIN 3, TEST TO 1.4 T, HELD, SERVICEABLE, D-31, initialled TA. The grease-pencil one: pin 3, aborted at 0.6, shoulder already flat on one side, four punch dots where a signature goes.

Both are dated 160190. Both name the same pin. Neither is a lie that helps anybody. The printed note gets the crew a hoist that will take their cases off the crown, and the pencil note leaves those cases at 1,880 metres with nothing above them but water.

Pin 3 is still in the crown frame. Assessment: the pin is inspectable, and whichever note you believe, gauge the shoulder cold before you hang mass on it.

## Scanner
