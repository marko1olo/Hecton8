# Escape Component Tuning Rules

Escape Component Tuning Rules keep the end of HECTON-8 from becoming a shopping list with a launch button. A believable escape route needs parts, but every part must prove something beyond its material cost. The relay spine proves that the player can speak past the water and magnetic noise. Clamp rings prove the capsule or ascent object can keep shape under pressure. The guidance core proves timing, not destination fantasy: it must hit a window, not wish for orbit. The ascent charge proves energy and risk. The legal handshake decides who answers, who owns the recovery channel and whether quarantine accepts the payload. Evidence payload changes consequence: the player may leave with currency, accusation, sample custody or leverage. If one proof is missing, the result should not feel like a disabled button. It should feel like a different ending: partial recovery, custody hold, bad receiver, failed claim or escape bought with silence.

## Scanner

Escape parts should force depth and evidence, not grind. Leaving HECTON-8 is a chain of proofs, not one expensive craft.
