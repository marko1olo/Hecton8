# Route Permission Slip RP-2209

Slip RP-2209 grants Marit Osei entry to Transfer Gallery 9, 2,058 metres to 2,190 metres, for the shift beginning 06:00. Gallery 9 came down eleven days before the slip was printed.

Slips issue against the route table. The table is a controlled document, and striking a route from it takes a change-control cycle: thirty days at site, longer where the route is a numbered evacuation path. Gallery 9 was numbered. Until the cycle closed, the corridor stood open on paper and the issuing desk had no field to enter otherwise.

Osei folded her slips into thirds and carried them under the wrist cuff. This one has the crease and the abrasion ring. Her confined-space ticket had lapsed nine shifts earlier; her route competence was current.

Assessment: the debris fan starts eighteen metres in. Nothing past the fan has been surveyed.
