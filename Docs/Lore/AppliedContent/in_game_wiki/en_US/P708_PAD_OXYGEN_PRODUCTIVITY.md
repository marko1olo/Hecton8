# Scrubber Allocation Sheet, Shift Office 3

Form SC-19 sets how much scrubber media each section of Sector 44 receives, indexed to throughput. Sections that fell below index received 0.7 of standard issue.

The last line does the work. Media reclassified as a consumable rather than a safety item moves it out of the safety budget, which is audited, and into the operating budget, which is not.

Someone in Shift Office 3 added a column in pencil down the right edge: section number, media received, and hours of usable air. The pencil column runs eleven sections. Four of them are under nine hours.

The pencil is not signed. Assessment: the printed sheet is the record, the pencil is the truth, and the room's scrubber bed is two metres away if the arithmetic needs checking.

## Scanner

Laminated allocation sheet, water-swollen. Confidence 88 percent. Hazard: none. Limit: pencil column not machine-readable.

## Terminal

AEGIR CONTINUITY HOLDINGS / FORM SC-19 REV 4
SUBJECT: SCRUBBER MEDIA ALLOCATION, Q3
METHOD: ALLOCATION INDEXED TO SECTION THROUGHPUT.
SECTIONS BELOW INDEX: MEDIA ISSUE AT 0.7 OF STANDARD.
APPEAL ROUTE: CONTRACT CONTINUITY DESK.
NOTE: MEDIA IS A CONSUMABLE, NOT A SAFETY ITEM.

## Field Note

Whoever kept that pencil column knew exactly what the form was doing and filled it in anyway, shift after shift. Check the bed before you trust either number.
