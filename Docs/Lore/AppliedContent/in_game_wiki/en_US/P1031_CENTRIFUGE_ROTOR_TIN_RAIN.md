# Centrifuge Rotor Tin Rain

The centrifuge makes a tin rain sound when the rotor spins down. Balanced samples should end in a smooth whine, not loose weather.

## Scanner

Rotor wells show mismatched wear. One cup carries dried sealant, likely used to fake the mass of a missing vial.

## Field Note

Bad balance points to missing material. A silent gap in a sample rack can be louder than any alarm.
