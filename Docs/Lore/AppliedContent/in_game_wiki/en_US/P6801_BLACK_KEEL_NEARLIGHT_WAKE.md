# Black Keel Nearlight Wake

Black Keel is an Aegir-system claim tender, not a rescue ship from Sol. A data packet can fit through a dirty relay pass while a living body still fails ascent seal, storm ceiling, Kestrel shadow, quarantine and tonne-window checks. Treat carrier contact as route evidence: log the window, keep proof tagged, and do not spend ascent hardware until the mass line accepts the payload.
