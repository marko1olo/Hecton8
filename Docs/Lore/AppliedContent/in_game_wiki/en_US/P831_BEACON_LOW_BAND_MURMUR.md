# Beacon Low Band Murmur

The beacon has lost its clear ping but still murmurs in the low band. That makes it useless for fast rescue and useful for slow navigation. A patient receiver can follow the murmur through brine noise.

## Scanner

Beacon playback: almost nothing, then almost home.
