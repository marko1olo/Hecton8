# Keelmark Loss Desk Coin

The desk coin is a cheap weight used to hold loss forms flat in moving gravity. Its rim is stamped with claim categories: cargo, equipment, personnel, disputed. Personnel has the thinnest wear because those forms moved fastest away.

## Scanner

DESK COIN // Claim category rim. Personnel segment lightly worn.

## Terminal

LOSS DESK // Use coin wear only as contextual evidence, not formal proof.

## Audio

Clerk room: the coin keeps paper still while everyone else moves.

## Field Note

Small weights teach large priorities.
