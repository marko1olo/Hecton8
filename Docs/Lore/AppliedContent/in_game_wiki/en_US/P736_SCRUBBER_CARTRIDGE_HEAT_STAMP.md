# Scrubber Cartridge Heat Stamp

A recovered cartridge stamp shows CO2 capacity as heat history. A scrubber that ran hot may still click into place, but its bed can break through early under work load. The stamp keeps the player from treating oxygen time and scrubber time as the same number.

## Scanner

SCRUBBER STAMP // Heat band exceeded on last cycle. CO2 breakthrough risk increased.

## Terminal

SUIT SERVICE // Cartridge accepted for short shelter work only. Replace before long route or heavy exertion.

## Audio

Marauder suit note: oxygen lies if the scrubber is cooked.

## Field Note

Breathable gas still needs somewhere for the bad part to go.
