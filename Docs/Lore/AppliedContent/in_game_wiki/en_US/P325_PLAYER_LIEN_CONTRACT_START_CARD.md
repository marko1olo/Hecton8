# Player Lien Contract Start Card

CONTRACT CONTINUITY DESK / SALVAGE ENGAGEMENT SC-4471-B. Two laminated pages, chest pocket.

  1.1  Operator engaged as independent salvage contractor. Worksite RAN-B:H8, Sector 44.
  2.3  Prior Deep Reach field access recognised, status inactive. Recognition is not authorisation.
  3.1  Lien active. Beneficiary: Aegir Reclamation Pool.
  4.2  Recovery mass window: 4.8 tonnes gross.
  4.7  Counted against 4.2: recovered mass, samples, tooling, Class-IV suit and consumables, and the Operator.
  7.4  Where the Operator is unrecovered, the lien does not lapse. It settles against the estate.

Clause 4.7 is the working clause. Ninety-one kilos of Operator and three hundred forty of suit and consumables come off the window before a crate is lifted.

The signature box on page one was pressed hard enough to emboss page two.

## Scanner

The start card does not make you a chosen victim. It makes you a former Deep Reach professional with a lien, a key and a reason to lie to yourself.
