# Biolumen Patch Cold Spark

The patch sparks blue when touched by cold current. It makes the hull look awake where metal should stay dull.

## Scanner

Lumens cluster along a micro-scratch. Nutrient residue in the scratch suggests the patch was fed by a leak.
