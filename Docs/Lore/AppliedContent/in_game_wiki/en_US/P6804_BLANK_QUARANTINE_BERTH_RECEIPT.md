# Blank Quarantine Berth Receipt

A blank quarantine berth receipt is the visible difference between being heard and being received. Black Keel has a live body line and a priced tonne-window, but no receiver has named a quarantine room, medical owner or custody door. Treat the receipt as evidence: the route is active, the refusal is procedural, and the missing room is the blocker.
