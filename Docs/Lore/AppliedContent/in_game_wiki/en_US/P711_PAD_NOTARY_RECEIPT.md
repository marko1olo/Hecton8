# Packet Notary Receipt 44-EV-0006

Receipt 44-EV-0006 is proof that a priority-one evacuation assistance request left HECTON-8 at 0207 on 4 November 2147. It is proof of nothing else, and it says so on its face: a notary receipt attests transmission, not receipt, not reading, not action.

The distinction is standard notarial wording and it is also the whole of the 2190 defence. Transit to the Tau Ceti desk runs a little over four years.

The slip was never torn from the cutter. The 2190 endorsement was printed onto it in place through the carrier link and reads DUTY DISCHARGED AT NOTARISATION.

Assessment: the interface still holds power and will print on demand. Its clock runs 43 years behind, so every slip it issues carries the wrong date.

## Scanner

Receipt slip, uncut, in cutter. Confidence 99 percent. Hazard: none. Limit: printed timestamp conflicts with device clock by decades.

## Field Note

It proves the message went out, and it was built to prove nothing past that. Compliance endorsed the slip in 2190 without ever putting a hand on it - print a copy, wrong clock and all, because it is the only dated record that the request existed.
