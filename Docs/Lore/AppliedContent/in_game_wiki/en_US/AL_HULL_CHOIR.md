# Rib Survey, Frames 22 to 29

Voices arrive in rooms that share no air, no door and no sightline, because the pressure ribs carry them there.

Steel conducts sound near 5,000 metres per second against roughly 1,500 in water. The frames are welded continuous through the factory, so a shout in Bay 22 lands in Bay 6 ahead of itself and arrives with no direction on it whatever. Crews called the effect the choir and used it to find casualties, which sent rescue parties into empty rooms.

Part way through this survey, Rib 6 began carrying the pump deck, which hangs off a separate run. The dye check on Frame 26 came back clean.

Assessment: one clamp on a rib gives you no bearing at 2,240 metres. Two clamps on separate frames and the arrival difference will.
