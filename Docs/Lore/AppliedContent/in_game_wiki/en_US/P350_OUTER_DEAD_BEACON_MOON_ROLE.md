# Mute Outer Dead Beacon Role

Mute is the outer dead-beacon moon in Aegir's ladder. Its relays are unreliable, cold, and partly missing from current public maps. They still appear in old solvers, insurance clauses, and recovery tables. That makes communication with HECTON-8 rare and ugly rather than magical. A message can be delayed by physics, bad data, or a custody rule that pretends the two are the same.
