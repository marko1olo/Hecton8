# Scrubber Three Bed Swap, Habitat Tech

A sintered scrubber bed runs warm and reads on the panel exactly like a working one.

Carbon dioxide absorption gives off heat, so the plant treats a warm bed as an active bed. Media regenerated past its cycle limit fuses into a grey ash that still holds temperature and holds no capacity at all. Beds Three and Five at the atmosphere plant were ash and green on the same morning, which cost the habitat something near forty hours of breathable margin that nobody had budgeted for.

Eleven regenerations above rating were signed off against a requisition number that returns nothing.

Assessment: weigh the canister. Dry mass at or under 4.1 kilos means spent, cycle count over forty means spent, and temperature means nothing whatsoever.

## Scanner

Belt recorder, filter-plant issue, casing scorched. Confidence 81 percent. Hazard: spent absorbent, residual heat and caustic dust. Limit: bed weights not stored on the unit.
