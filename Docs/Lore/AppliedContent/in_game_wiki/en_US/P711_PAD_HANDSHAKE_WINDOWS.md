# Receiver Handshake Schedule RH-4

Window C is recovery assist. It displaces the next cargo tender and the tonnage credit is forfeit.

Schedule RH-4 sets the rest of the Black Keel's handshake windows: cargo tender at 0140, 0940 and 1740 with credit at close, transfer line on demand and metered. Receiver time is finite and the schedule attributes the cost of a displaced tender, which is defensible arithmetic. It also prices a call for help in the only currency a lien holder carries.

RH-4 is bolted over the 2147 tender board at P-63. The old board's Window C punch card is full at sixty-one, and the forfeit column beside it carries sixty-one.

Assessment: forfeit is per request, not per tonne. Windows do not stack.

## Scanner

Schedule plate over older board, two layers. Confidence 76 percent. Hazard: none. Limit: revision 3 overlays revision 1, lower layer only partly legible.

## Terminal

RECOVERY COMPLIANCE OFFICE / SCHEDULE RH-4 REV 3
CARRIER: BLACK KEEL, AUTOMATED CLAIM-TENDER
WINDOW A, CARGO TENDER: 0140 / 0940 / 1740. TONNAGE CREDIT AT CLOSE.
WINDOW B, TRANSFER LINE: ON DEMAND, METERED.
WINDOW C, RECOVERY ASSIST: DISPLACES NEXT WINDOW A. CREDIT FORFEIT.
WINDOWS ARE NOT CUMULATIVE.
CLASSIFICATION: TENDER SCHEDULING, BILLABLE.
QUEUE: RETURN ACTION QUEUE.

## Field Note

A C window takes the whole next cargo window with it and the credit never comes back, so if you are going to call one, call it once and clear the room while you are in there. Sixty-one punches on the old board.
