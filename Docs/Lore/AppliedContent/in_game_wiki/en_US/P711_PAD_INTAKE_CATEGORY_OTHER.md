# Intake Form MC-2, Bed 4

Pressure injury has no box on Intake MC-2. The form offers six categories and it enters as category six, other, non-specific.

Loss Desk Schedule 9 fixed the list so every Aegir site returned comparable figures. Category six is recorded and not aggregated, and site injury rate is one input to a tide-margin review.

Forty-one forms are spiked on the wall above Bed 4. Twenty-nine are category six.

The recompression chamber in the next bay carries an hour meter on the compressor housing. It reads 1,940 hours.

Bed 4's regulator manifold has been stripped back to the wall bracket. Assessment: the chamber is rated to 60 metres equivalent, 0.6 MPa, and its hatch is dogged from the inside.
