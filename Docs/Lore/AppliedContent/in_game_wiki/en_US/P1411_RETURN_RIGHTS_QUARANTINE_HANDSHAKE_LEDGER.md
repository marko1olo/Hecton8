# Return Rights Quarantine Handshake Ledger

Recovery Compliance Office, Form RR-6 Rev 2. One sheet, wired to the handshake console at Receiver Desk 2, Shallow Annex.

  ACCESSION RR-6/2190/0417
  CARRIER MEDICAL OFFICER ....... signed
  PRESSURE SEAL CERTIFIER ....... signed
  CLAIM OWNER, KEELMARK MUTUAL .. signed
  PAYLOAD CUSTODIAN ............. signed
  RECEIVER AUTHORITY ............ ____
  CLAUSE 9: WHERE NO RECEIVER AUTHORITY IS ENTERED BEFORE ASCENT BURN, THE RETURNED PARTY AND ACCOMPANYING PACKETS ENTER NEUTRAL HOLD.

Four parties sign for objects: a rated seal, contained samples, an acknowledged lien, a counted packet archive. The fifth line has to name an institution willing to take delivery of a living body.

No settle interval is printed against neutral hold. The blank half of the split tag is still seated in the slot.

## Scanner

RETURN RIGHTS TAG // Split custody seal. One half is stamped by Recovery Compliance, the other left blank for receiver authority. Biohazard color is crossed out; evidence quarantine remains active. The tag is not asking whether the survivor is alive. It asks who inherits the living body.

## Terminal

RECOVERY COMPLIANCE RECEIVER DESK // Quarantine/legal handshake pending. Required parties: carrier medical officer, pressure seal certifier, claim owner, receiver authority, and payload custodian. If no receiver signs before ascent burn, returned body and packets enter neutral hold. Neutral hold may be contested by Keelmark Mutual, Deep Reach, public ledger notary, or Atlas continuity office.

## Audio

Never sign the first receiver that answers fast. A fast receiver wants the cargo. A real receiver wants the record, the body, and the trouble in the same room.

## Field Note

Receiver first, burn second. If you rise without a receiver, you rise as property.
