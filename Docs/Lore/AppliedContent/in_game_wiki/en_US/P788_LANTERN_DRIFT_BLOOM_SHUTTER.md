# Lantern Drift Bloom Shutter

The bloom shutter closes when a lantern drift is touched by hard light. That makes the animal vanish from sight while its charge remains in the water. A bright lamp can remove the warning and leave the hazard.

## Scanner

BLOOM SHUTTER // Hard light closes lantern surface. Residual charge remains.
