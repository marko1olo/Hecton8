# Suboffice Personnel Seeds

Suboffice Personnel Seeds gives Deep Reach a human paperwork layer without turning the company into a neat boss chart. These names are not there to create a new ruling cabal. They are there so signatures, door logs and memo fragments can feel like things a person touched at three in the morning while alarms kept losing priority. A risk intake clerk can mislabel a tide margin. An evacuation counsel can hold a release. A packet notary can certify a witness hash without asking why the room is flooding. The system remains bigger than any one worker, but the evidence gains fingerprints.

## Scanner

The lower Deep Reach names matter because the disaster was carried by hands, initials and inboxes, not by an abstract company logo.

## Terminal

PERSONNEL SEED TABLE: Risk Margin Intake / Evacuation Queue Counsel / Atlas Continuity Analyst / Packet Notary / Recovery Custody Clerk. Use names as signature anchors, witness conflicts and room evidence, not as a simple command ladder.
