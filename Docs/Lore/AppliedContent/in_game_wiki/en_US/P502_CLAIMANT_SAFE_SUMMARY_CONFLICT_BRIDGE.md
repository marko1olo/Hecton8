# Claimant-Safe Summary Conflict

A claimant-safe summary is a protected rewrite, not a neutral short version. It may remove names, round coordinates, replace injury detail with a category, soften a route verb, or substitute the office allowed to answer public mail. Those edits can protect people; Deep Reach can also use the same safety language to bury leverage. The conflict between summary and raw object is not a continuity error. It is evidence: what was removed, who benefited, and what object can restore context.

## Scanner

SAFE SUMMARY CONFLICT // Public wording omits or softens facts raw evidence still implies. Omission is an evidence lane.

## Terminal

CLAIMANT-SAFE SUMMARY CONFLICT
Summary text is safe for public handling. Raw object may not be safe.
Do not restore protected names or coordinates from guesswork.
Preserve omission list, route proof, and claimant risk marker.
