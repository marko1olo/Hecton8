# Bottle Rack, Foundry Floor

Four sockets, four certified bottles, and the pencil column on the rack tag still closes.

Two bottles are vented. Two hold 480 minutes each, 960 on the tag total. The media case beside them is tagged for two scrubber beds and the seal is unbroken. A bed strips CO2 and makes no oxygen, so a bed short is a diver short, whatever the bottles read.

The last three lines of the column are in the air tech's hand. Minutes only, no litres. Bed one against TA, bed two against four punch dots, and no line carries her own mark.

The tag header is dated 190190. Three suits came down this shaft.

Assessment: crack the case for the beds before you touch the rack, and check each gauge against the tag rather than the tag against the gauges.

## Scanner

Four-socket rack, two bottles charged, two vented. Confidence 81 percent. Hazard: charged bottles at 23.4 MPa ambient. Limit: media case seal unbroken, contents counted by tag only.
