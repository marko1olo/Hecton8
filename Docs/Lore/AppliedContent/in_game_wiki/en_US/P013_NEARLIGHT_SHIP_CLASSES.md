# Nearlight Ship Classes

Nearlight ships built human space without breaking physics. Probes arrived first, then seed ships, mass carriers, sleeper transports, local system carriers, tugs, and tenders. Their scale explains why Black Keel is local infrastructure, not a personal escape yacht.

## Scanner

## Terminal
