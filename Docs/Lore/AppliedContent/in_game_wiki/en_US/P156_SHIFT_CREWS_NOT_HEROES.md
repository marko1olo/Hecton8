# Shift Crews, Not Heroes

HECTON-8 should make the colony human through work before tragedy. A pressure tech who swaps a valve gasket, a cook who tracks water tokens, a route clerk who stamps the wrong return, a diver who signs out a cutter and never signs it back in: these details make people legible without forcing a family-revenge hook. The drowned colony is strongest when it feels like a place interrupted during routine, not a museum built after death.

## Scanner

HUMAN LAYER // The colony becomes real through schedules, worn tools, missed returns, and names on duty boards. Not speeches.

## Terminal

WRITING RULE: record workers as pump techs, medics, route clerks, divers, cooks, fabricator operators, and tired supervisors. A roster with one missing return can hurt more than a monument.

## Field Note
