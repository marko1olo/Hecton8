# Bathydrop Cradle Squeal

The cradle squeal appears only when a capsule is miscentered under load. It is a high, brief complaint before the locks engage. Ignoring it saves seconds and spends frame life.

## Scanner

CRADLE SQUEAL // Miscentered capsule under load before lock engagement.

## Terminal

BATHYDROP // Recenter cradle if squeal repeats across two lock cycles.

## Audio

Deck mic: short scream, green locks.

## Field Note

Acceptance lights can arrive after damage starts.
