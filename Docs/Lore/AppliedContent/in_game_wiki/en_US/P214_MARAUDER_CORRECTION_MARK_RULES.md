# Marauder Correction Mark Rules

Marauder Correction Mark Rules define the second human hand on colony evidence. Deep Reach wrote categories to bury workers; later claim crews wrote over those categories to survive, bargain, or warn each other. A correction mark is useful only when it changes what the player can infer. A circled name can rescue a person from a loss category. A crossed route stamp can make a safe door look suspicious. A price mark can turn a biological sample into a debt object. A hidden witness mark can protect a voice fragment until the player has the right context. The marks should feel fast, practical, and tired, not theatrical. They are not graffiti for mood. They are work done by people who knew the record was dirty and still needed to eat.

## Scanner

A correction mark can rescue a name, poison a claim, or warn the next diver that the official category is a lie.

## Terminal

CORRECTION RULE: Marauder marks must change evidence interpretation, route risk, or payout pressure. Valid marks preserve a worker name, challenge an insurance category, warn a route, price a sample, hide a witness, or mark debt. Empty graffiti breaks the evidence chain.
