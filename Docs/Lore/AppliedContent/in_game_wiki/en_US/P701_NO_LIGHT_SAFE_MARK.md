# No-Light Safe Mark

A recovered no-light mark tells the player when visibility is the danger. Some routes wake fauna, drones or bad human memories when swept with full lamps. Marauders mark those passages for dim pings, tactile line and short scans.

## Scanner

NO-LIGHT MARK // Lamp discipline required. Use pinger and line touch through next passage.

## Terminal

FIELD MARK // Full light sweep may trigger fauna movement or repair-drone response. Use low lamp and acoustic check.

## Audio

Marauder route note: if the mark says no light, believe the mark before fear asks for brightness.

## Field Note

Darkness is a tool when light makes noise.
