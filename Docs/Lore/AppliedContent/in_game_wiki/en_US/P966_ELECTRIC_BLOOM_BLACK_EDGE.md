# Electric Bloom Black Edge

The black edge around an electric bloom marks where current burned biology away from a live cable. The center still glows. The edge is a warning that the hazard is powered from beneath, not from the visible tissue.

## Scanner

BLACK EDGE // Current burn around bloom. Live cable feed below.

## Terminal

ELECTRICAL HAZARD // Isolate cable before cutting glowing bloom center.

## Audio

Suit mic: crackle under the green.

## Field Note

Bright hazards often hide their power source.
