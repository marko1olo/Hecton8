# Lantern Drift Bloom

Lantern drift bloom is a plankton visibility event on the photic shelf. It paints current lines, suspended debris, cable arcs and toothy hazards before a lamp would find them. That makes the bloom useful for navigation, but it is not comfort lighting. Disturbed cells pulse and pass vibration through the food web; a cutter, pump, pinger or careless thruster can leave a bright acoustic trail behind the player. The glow can also flatten contrast, hiding small spines and broken shell clamps inside too much beauty. Treat a bloom as a route decision: drift with it when you need outlines, cut across it when silence matters, and do not work loud in the middle unless you want company.

VISIBILITY EVENT: bloom improves silhouette read and current tracing, but reduces stealth. Agitation by cutter, pump, pinger, thruster or hull impact can draw filter-feeders, grazers and shallow predators along the illuminated wake.

## Field Note
