# Marker Inventory, P-63 Back Wall

The tape on a doorframe in Sector 44 is worth more than the contents of the room behind it.

Colony practice was a last-shift marker: shift code, hour, and the method a crew used to leave, taped or scratched at eye height beside the exit they actually took. D-shift, eleven hundred, lower stair on a hand line. Deep Reach recovery listed the markers as personal effects, which kept them out of the safety record and left them on the walls for forty-three years.

Marauder practice is to transcribe one word for word before disturbing anything else in the room.

Three markers in the same hand sit in bays four hundred metres apart, dated the same hour.

Assessment: a marker giving an hour is a tide window. One giving a method is a route somebody survived.

## Scanner
