# Silent Orbit Receipt Ledger

The first Black Keel receipt gives a Marauder acknowledgement without help. Radio dies below the upper ocean. Laser and microwave links scatter or vanish. At depth, a contractor talks through slow acoustic pulses that carry text, hashes, pressure warnings and payable mass records. Black Keel answers for contract custody, not for rescue.

Kestrel, storm windows and HECTON-8's mineral ocean can age a message into stale evidence before orbit clears. A receipt proves the carrier heard enough to price the run. It proves no extraction, mercy or live command. The first real reply can feel colder than silence: the system can count you before it can save you.
