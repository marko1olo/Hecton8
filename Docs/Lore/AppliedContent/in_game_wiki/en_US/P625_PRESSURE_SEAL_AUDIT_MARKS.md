# Pressure Seal Audit Marks

A recovered seal board explains why a patch is not a rescue system. Deep Reach stamp paint marks the ring class, pressure history, clamp torque, worker signoff and quarantine routing state. A cold sealant bead can stop the room from drowning; only a rated ring can carry ascent pressure, evidence mass and a living body through a recovery window.

## Scanner
