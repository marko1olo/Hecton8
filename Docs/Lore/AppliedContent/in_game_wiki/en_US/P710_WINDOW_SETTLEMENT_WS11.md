# Window Settlement Worksheet WS-11

WS-11 settles a tonne-window against what a crew actually lifts, and its arithmetic is why this camp is empty.

Printed: consumables spent on an aborted lift book as a debit at full issue, no credit. Recovered mass credits at the depth class it came from. Custody-grade off the foundry floor carries 2.8.

The margin does the rest in pencil. Window left to clear, 2.1. Abort and ascend, 0.9 debit, no credit, balance 3.0. Two cases at 0.55 from the floor, 1.10 lifted, credited 3.08, balance clear with 0.98 spare.

Going home cost them three tonnes of window. Going to 2,340 metres cost them nothing on paper.

The consumables line is filled for three: three transfer lines, three beds, 620 minutes each. Assessment: the multiplier still stands, and it still prices the floor below the surface.
