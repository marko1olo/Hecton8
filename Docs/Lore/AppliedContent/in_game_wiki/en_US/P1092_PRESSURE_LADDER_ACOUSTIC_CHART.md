# Pressure Ladder Acoustic Chart

Marauders keep this chart clipped beside the oxygen table because HECTON-8 is not one ocean. The photic shelf keeps a little Ran-light and storm life. The cable reef snags tools and pingers in old Deep Reach infrastructure. Brine canyons bend buoyancy and make sonar report false floors. Machine fields are pressure-crushed factories, still answering to Atlas repair logic. The Atlas Basin is dark, hot, metallic and alive with bad categories.

Sound is the only practical sightline below the upper ocean, but sound is never honest for free. Salt layers trap it. Thermal vents shear it. Cable mats echo it. A clean return can be a wall, a predator wake, a pipe memory or a relay copying your own panic back at you. Descent needs seals, oxygen, battery, return ping and doubt.

## Scanner

BATHYMETRIC CHART // 0-250 m photic shelf; 250-1200 m cable reef; 1200-2800 m brine canyon; 2800-4300 m machine field; 4300-5600 m Atlas Basin. Sound-speed profile broken by salt, heat and metal debris.
