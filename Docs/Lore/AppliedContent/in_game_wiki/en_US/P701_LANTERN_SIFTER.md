# Lantern Sifter

Ping a sifter colony and your ping comes back twice.

The first return is the wall. The second arrives a fifth of a second later, off bearing, and louder than the range explains. Every animal carries one hard grain in its gill basket, trace-class glass the size of a rice pellet, and nothing about a filter feeder needs one. Atlas grew a repeater network into an animal and never had to service it.

The wrong bearing points at the next colony. Walk the chain and it ends at cable still under load, which is worth the swim. The first pulse also gave your position to everything on that route, and the route runs past forced-flow vents.

One chain out of Bay 31 points into solid rock.

Assessment: ping once, then clear the vent ahead before you follow the second bearing.
