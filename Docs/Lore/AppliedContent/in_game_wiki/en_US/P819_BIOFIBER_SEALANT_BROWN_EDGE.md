# Biofiber Sealant Brown Edge

The brown edge forms when biofiber sealant cures too fast against warm metal. It grips at first, then peels under vibration. The color is a warning that a repair solved the leak briefly and stored the failure for later.

## Scanner

BROWN EDGE // Over-fast cure on warm metal. Vibration peel risk.

## Terminal

PATCH CHECK // Cool metal or brace sealant before pressure cycling.

## Audio

Repair note: brown means it held long enough to betray you later.

## Field Note

Temporary repair likes to dress as success.
