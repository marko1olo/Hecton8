# Black Keel Custody

Black Keel is not a luxury personal ship. It is a nearly automated salvage carrier operating through claim-pool custody, insurance debt and delayed orbital windows. It gives the player a real route back to orbit without making rescue simple or loyal.

## Scanner

Carrier custody is public salvage infrastructure with private priority hooks.

## Terminal

Custody file: hull owner masked, claim-pool debt active, Deep Reach priority channel present but not declared to contractor.

## Audio

Black Keel can recover you. It can also recover everything before you.

## Field Note

A lifeline can still be a leash.
