# Xenon Trophic Repair Ecology Fieldguide

HECTON-8 biology becomes dangerous when it is treated as scenery. The same vent plume can feed iron kelp, grow a xenon-rich lattice, warm a brine step, mask a predator and power an Atlas repair pulse. A scanner label that says resource, plant or animal is only the first guess.

Blue Debt is formed by process, not by placement. Heat lifts minerals. Salt sorts density. Microbial bloom edits the lattice. Grazers crop the edges. Biofilm carries weak current. Atlas learns which surfaces seal, conduct or repeat sound. When a Marauder cuts a sample loose, they may be taking fuel, food web, route marker and evidence from the same living joint.

## Scanner

XENON TROPHIC TRACE // Vent heat, sulfide salt, blind grazers, conductive biofilm and Atlas repair pulses share one gradient. Valuable blue growth is never clean ore; it is a living process with a ledger tag.

## Terminal

BIORESOURCE FIELD NOTICE // Do not classify HECTON-8 life as flora, fauna or contamination until route behavior is observed. If a sample carries current, filters sound, seals a crack or attracts tagged fauna, treat it as ecology and infrastructure.

## Audio

If it feeds on a vent, grows over a cable and answers a repair ping, stop asking whether it is alive or useful. Ask who is using it.

## Field Note

Blue Debt is not lying in the mud. It is being made, eaten, sealed, harvested and renamed.
