# Tape Seal, Gland Collar G-77

Gland collar G-77 carries a hairline fracture ninety millimetres long and eleven turns of pressure tape, half-lapped, with the turn count written on the outer lap in marker. LUBO PETRIC, 14-9.

Half lap over a collar shoulder is the correct wrap. The collar carries the load, the tape carries the seal, and a count written on the outside lets the next hand check tension without cutting it open. He did all of that. He was a Grade 1 greaser with no pressure certification, the seal card wanted a Class 2 signature, so he signed the Class 2 line and the office refused the repair.

Week 41 did not come.

Assessment: the trunk behind G-77 is dry at 1,940 metres, 19.4 MPa, forty-three years on. Dry, not breathable. It will take a person and a tool bag clear of the water.
