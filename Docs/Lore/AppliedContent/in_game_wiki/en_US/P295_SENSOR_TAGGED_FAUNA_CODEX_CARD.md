# Sensor-Tagged Fauna Codex Card

Sensor-tagged fauna are one of the cleanest ways to see HECTON-8’s machine ecology without turning the ocean into a mind. Deep Reach tagged animals, shell colonies and route species for migration, pressure tolerance and contamination studies. Years later, some tags are buried in scar tissue, shell sockets, mineral caps or growth rings. Atlas can still hear a weak return and may treat the moving body as a diagnostic landmark: pressure packet, contamination hint, route occupancy, drone sweep anchor, stale maintenance witness. That does not make the animal obedient. It may graze, flee, hunt or panic for ordinary reasons while the tag makes its path matter to broken infrastructure. Do not read tag motion as intent. Do not cut a live tag loose unless the route cost is worth the cruelty and the dirty data. The useful question is not “what does it want?” It is “what system still believes this scar is a sensor?”

## Scanner

Sensor-tagged fauna. Old Deep Reach biometal tag embedded in scar, shell or growth ring. Weak Atlas route echo; animal intent not confirmed.

## Terminal

SPECIMEN / SENSOR-TAGGED FAUNA / role: living telemetry carrier, stale survey landmark, repair-route contaminant / safe read: old tag pulse, scar tissue growth, non-command movement / reject: treating behavior as obedience, cutting tag from live body, trusting occupancy echo / route note: the tag can report a room the animal crossed, not the reason it crossed.

## Audio

It is not obeying Atlas. It is carrying an old question that Atlas still asks.

## Field Note

The tag is a scar, not a leash. It may report you, misplace you, or wake something that never saw the animal.
