# Noble Gas Pocket Seal

A recovered pocket seal protects a pressure history, not a loose gas prize. Noble-gas brine pockets matter because their pressure, mineral defects and thermal route can feed pressure-glass process work. Venting one for quick payout destroys the part that proves value.
