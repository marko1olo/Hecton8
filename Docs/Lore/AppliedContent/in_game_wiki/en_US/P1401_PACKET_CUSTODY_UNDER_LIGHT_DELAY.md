# Packet Custody Under Light Delay

Finding truth on HECTON-8 is the easy failure. A terminal can still boot, a badge can still carry a name, and a wet camera spool can still show who was locked behind a pressure door. None of that makes evidence by itself. Until the packet has a source clock, a hash, a second witness and a custody route outside the claimant's hand, Black Keel can treat it as salvage mass and Deep Reach can call it contaminated project material.

The old human frontier made law out of delay. There is no FTL reply and no live court above the ocean. A useful packet has to survive the local relay window, the Black Keel queue, a Luyten Packet Ladder receipt and, if the packet is meant to hurt Deep Reach, a receiver like the Tau Public Ledger Lane. That chain is dull on purpose: clock, duplicate, seal, queue, receipt. Break one link and the same truth becomes a sample, a rumor or a liability file.

Deep Reach knows this. Recovery Compliance does not need to erase every document. It only has to poison the route: flag the payload as quarantine material, challenge the clock, demand original custody, price the mass window too high, or route the packet through Keelmark before a public receiver sees it. The Marauder is not preserving reading material for a museum. They are trying to make the drowned colony count as people before the claim system counts it as material.

A good Marauder keeps ugly evidence in two forms: one clean enough for a notary and one dirty enough to prove where it was found. The first gets the case moving. The second keeps the corporation from saying the truth arrived without a place, a pressure state or a body behind it.

## Scanner

PACKET CUSTODY // Wet relay seal. One readable file is not evidence. Preserve clock, hash, duplicate witness, custody mark and receiver path before salvage compression.

## Terminal

LUYTEN NOTARY CHAIN // A packet becomes admissible when source clock, witness hash, carrier custody, relay receipt and independent receiver all agree. One claimant-controlled copy is cargo, not proof.

## Audio

A true file is not proof until it gets out under a name Deep Reach cannot quietly own.

## Field Note

Copy it twice. Keep the wet copy. Clean packets win hearings; dirty packets prove you did not invent the room.
