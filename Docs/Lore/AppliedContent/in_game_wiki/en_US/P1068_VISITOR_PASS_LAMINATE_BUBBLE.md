# Visitor Pass Laminate Bubble

The bubble in the pass laminate sits over the date field. Heat loosened it, or a careful thumbnail did.

## Scanner

Ink beneath the bubble is feathered. The pass photo is real, but the expiration line was warmed and pressed flat.

## Terminal

Visitor control shows a single-day escort. Badge pings continue three shifts past the escort window.

## Audio

The front desk scanner chirps uncertainly. A guard says the plastic is always like that near the heater.

## Field Note

Temporary access becomes permanent through small tolerance. Bad laminate can be a weather problem or a prepared excuse.
