# Tubular Coral

A tube run on a factory wall means the wall behind it is cracked. The growth tracks fractures and fills them; it has held Room 6 of the West Wet Store at 20.1 MPa since 2147.

Live runs are pale at the tip, iron-stained at the base; dead ones keep their shape for decades. Read colour, not outline.

The plate stamp is under the coral. A room whose panel number cannot be read cannot be certified, so the Room 6 file closed on exterior survey and one signature.

Cut a run crossing a seam and you cut the patch, not the plant. A torch took forty-three years of seal off in under a minute.

Every filled fracture is closed from the pressure side first. Yield: biofiber sealant, heaviest at the stained base. Take it off sound plate only.

## Scanner

Colonial tube growth on load-bearing plate. Coverage 60 percent. Confidence 84 percent. Hazard: none at contact. Limit: cannot separate surface deposit from filled fracture.

## Terminal

REPAIR RECORD 44-W-0612. CLASS: STRUCTURAL REMEDIATION, MATERIAL SOURCED LOCALLY. PANEL IDENTIFICATION: NOT RECOVERABLE. ROOM 6: CERTIFIED ON EXTERIOR SURVEY. INSPECTION INTERVAL: WAIVED. FILED: ATLAS CONTINUITY OFFICE.

## Field Note

Certified on exterior survey means somebody walked the outside of a flooded room, could not find the panel number, and signed for it anyway. Colour-check the tips before you trust a wall, and never put a torch through a run that crosses a seam.
