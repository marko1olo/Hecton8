# Winch Cable Flat Spot

The winch cable flat spot formed where a loaded line froze against a guide roller. It will pass through a casual inspection and fail under shock load. A smooth-looking cable can hide a remembered crush.

## Scanner

FLAT SPOT // Loaded freeze against guide roller. Shock-load failure risk.

## Terminal

WINCH // Rotate cable by hand before lifting capsule mass or sealed crate.

## Audio

Deck note: touch the line before trusting the drum.

## Field Note

Round things can carry flat damage.
