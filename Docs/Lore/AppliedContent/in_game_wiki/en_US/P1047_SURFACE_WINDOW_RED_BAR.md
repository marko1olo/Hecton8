# Surface Window Red Bar

The red bar across the surface window is thin, but it cancels resupply, med lift, and every promise made before breakfast.

## Scanner

Screen glass is polished under the forecast strip. Operators rubbed that place while waiting for the bar to shrink.

## Terminal

Dispatch records show two manual window extensions. Both were approved after cargo shortage alerts hit finance.

## Audio

The console chirps once when weather updates. Nobody speaks until the room fan cycles down.

## Field Note

Weather bars become moral documents under pressure. They decide which risk sounds like nature and which sounds like management.
