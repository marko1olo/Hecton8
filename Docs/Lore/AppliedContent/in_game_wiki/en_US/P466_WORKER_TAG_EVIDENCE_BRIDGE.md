# Worker Tag Evidence Chain

A worker tag is not proof by itself. It becomes hard to erase only when shift slate, route stamp, damage state, witness hash, and receiver path stay attached. Loose badge: weak. Badge plus shift slate, route stamp, damage mark, witness hash, and non-claim receiver path: evidence. Do not pocket the name and cut the room apart. Preserve the tray, the slate, and the ugly route around it.
