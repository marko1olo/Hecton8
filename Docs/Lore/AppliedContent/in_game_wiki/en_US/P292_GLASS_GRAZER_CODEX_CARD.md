# Glass Grazer Codex Card

Glass grazers are not pets, loot, or decoration. They are small transparent shell-feeders that make the photic shelf readable by scraping mineral film from pressure glass, loose photic mat edges, old cable jackets, and limestone lips. A feeding school leaves pale lanes in the growth. Those lanes can show recent current, a clean handhold, a seam that is not too warm, or a patch where predators have not crossed in the last few minutes. The useful clue is not only movement. It is interruption. If food remains and the shell-ticks stop, the school has heard pressure change, shadow motion, cutter vibration, or a larger animal passing outside the scanner cone. Treat the scraped lane as evidence, not permission. Follow the edge. Leave when the edge folds inward.

## Scanner

Glass grazer school. Non-hostile shell-feeders scraping mineral film. Active ticking means work water; sudden silence means something larger has changed the clearing.

## Terminal

SPECIMEN / GLASS GRAZER SCHOOL / role: shallow film scraper, current witness, predator-displacement cue / safe read: feeding edge, fresh pale scrape lane, steady shell ticks / reject: abandoned food field, tight frozen school, no return ticks after pinger / route note: absence after feeding is hazard data.

## Audio

When the little mouths stop ticking and the food is still there, do not be the next thing that waits.

## Field Note

Feeding school: read the lane. Frozen school: read the water. Empty lane with food left behind: leave.
