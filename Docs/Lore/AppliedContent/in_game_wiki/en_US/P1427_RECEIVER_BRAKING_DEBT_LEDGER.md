# Receiver Braking Debt Ledger

Nearlight travel did not make distance harmless. It made distance accountable. Human ships cross between stars because old routes are owned in pieces: beam arrays push, pellet lanes feed, fusion stages spend, shields lose mass, radiators dump heat, magsails bite into fields, and receiver stations agree to catch what the route sends.

That last verb is the ugly one. To catch a body, a receiver must accept braking debt before the body arrives. It must reserve heat margin, quarantine volume, medical custody, legal exposure, and the right to say who pays when the stop goes wrong. A signal packet does not need most of that. A sealed evidence hash can ride a relay. A freight crate can ride a schedule written years earlier. A living operator in a broken ocean claim is a different kind of mass.

This is why HECTON-8 can feel connected and abandoned at the same time. Black Keel can hear you through a relay window. A broker can price your sample. A public ledger can record that your distress hash existed. None of those facts create braking rights for your body. Rescue becomes possible only when signal, custody, receiver liability, quarantine, sky timing, lift mass and actual hardware line up. Until then, the system is not silent. It is worse: it is legible.
