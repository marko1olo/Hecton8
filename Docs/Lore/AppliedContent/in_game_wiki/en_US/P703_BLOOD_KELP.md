# Blood Kelp

Your work lamp cannot see it. The pigment absorbs the blue-green band lamps are weighted for, so a stand reads as open water at 12 m and a wall at 3.

The amber emergency lens brings it up solid. It roots only on hard structure: a stand in open water at 1,900 m sits on a roof slab, whatever the chart says.

Contact fires the photophores, edge first, travelling inward, never outward from the touch. It holds near ninety seconds, long enough to show anything at the rim where you entered.

Stipes carry a diver's weight; a fouled harness will not tear free. Cut from the edge in; cutting from inside is how the four Bay 17 entanglements ended.

Yield: bioluminescent compound, heaviest in blades that have not discharged. The edge is always spent. Cut on the amber lens.

## Scanner

Anchored frond growth, dark pigment. Confidence 49 percent. Return sparse. Hazard: none logged. Limit: pigment absorbs survey lamp band; extent understated.

## Field Note

Four entanglements and the classification still reads non-hazard, vegetation, because nothing on that form has a box for a plant that holds. Run the amber lens on approach; white light shows you nothing until you are already inside it.
