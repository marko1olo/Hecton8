# Wide Filter Body Trace

Wide filter bodies are the first proof that the open abyss is not empty between landmarks. They move through deep water as slow plankton harvesters with bodies wider than small annex frames. The animal does not hunt the Marauder; it changes the map around the Marauder. When it passes, suspended life thins, sonar returns smear along the body edge, and buried industrial outlines can appear for a few minutes before the silt closes again.

This makes the organism both danger and tool. Its shadow can hide a service frame or make a route look open where the water is actually full of moving tissue. The correct play is to read the wake: clear water behind the mouth, dirty falloff at the rim, acoustic quiet in the body's lee. Never swim under the shadow just to see the creature. Follow the gap it leaves only if you have power, line and a way back.

Do not call it a boss. Call it weather with a stomach: slow, huge, useful and indifferent.
