# Wall Glider

It only travels on manufactured surface. Plate, cable run, conduit, handrail, never bare rock and never across an open fracture.

That refusal is a survey. Where a track runs unbroken, the plate under it is sound. Where it turns back on itself, the plate is opening.

The film it leaves is conductive. On energised plate it is live, and a factory-governor continuity check will run straight through it, so a hairline crack under a glider track tests sound. A tool laid flat on fresh film sticks for a second and blanks your gauge for ten.

They will not cross the yellow shift-lane paint on Annex decking. Most of that paint is forty-three years gone and they still will not.

Assessment: trust the track over the continuity read, and stop where the track stops.

## Scanner

Surface-adherent grazer on manufactured plate. Confidence 77 percent. Hazard: conductive residue, low current. Limit: track age not resolvable. A dead track reads as a live one.
