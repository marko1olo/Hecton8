# Density Skater Wake Wrinkle

Density skaters leave narrow wrinkles where two brine layers meet. The wake can survive longer than the animal remains visible. Reading the wrinkle gives a cheap current estimate and a warning that the surface layer is thin.

## Scanner

WAKE WRINKLE // Layer boundary disturbed. Thin surface brine detected.

## Terminal

ECOLOGY ROUTE // Use wrinkle angle for drift estimate before crossing.

## Audio

Field recorder: the animal is gone, but the water kept its handwriting.

## Field Note

Some tracks are made from water refusing to mix.
