# Quarantine Berth Receipt

A quarantine berth receipt is a room claim, not a comfort note. The card names air, filters, waste handling, med locks, custody cameras, chain seals and the office that owns the liability line. A survivor coming out of a sealed ocean site arrives as living mass with exposure, injury, contamination risk, debt and evidence still attached.

The hard field is receiver berth. Distress hash, sample receipt and Black Keel acknowledgement can all be valid while that line stays empty. Empty means no room has accepted the body. Filled means somebody has committed air, filters, staff time, legal risk and lift mass. The receipt is useful because it separates a message being heard from a person being taken in.

## Scanner

Ceramic berth receipt. One corner heat-warped. Fields still readable: body mass, exposure band, pathogen hold, med owner, receiver berth, lift exception. Packet accepted. Person pending.
