# Quarantine Berth Custody Ledger

A quarantine berth is not a bed in the sentimental sense. It is a legal room with air, filters, waste handling, med locks, custody cameras, chain-of-evidence seals and a name on the liability line. When a survivor leaves a sealed ocean claim, the receiver does not accept a rescued person first. It accepts a biological object that may be injured, infected, contaminated, indebted, legally dangerous and still carrying evidence other parties want buried.

This is why the first clean answer from Black Keel can hurt. The relay may acknowledge a distress hash. Luyten may preserve packet custody. A broker may price samples before the player sees open sky. None of those receipts reserve a berth. A living body is not just heavier than a packet; it changes ownership. Someone must decide who breathes the same air, who treats the decompression injury, who keeps Deep Reach from classifying the survivor as claimant property, and who pays when the berth is locked for a month.

Old route workers called these ledgers mercy with a tariff column. That is unfair and accurate. The berth exists because frontier rescues killed stations when ships pretended contamination was a moral detail. It is also the place where companies learned to slow help until the language favored them. For the player, the ledger is readable pressure: if the berth line is blank, rescue language is theater; if the custodian and liability fields are filled, somebody has started spending real power on survival instead of paperwork.

## Field Note
