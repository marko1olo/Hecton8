# Pavel Sorn Procedure Hold

Pavel Sorn gives the HECTON-8 disaster its most ordinary human hinge: a local contract officer with enough authority to delay an evacuation and not enough courage to break the clause chain. His procedure hold is not a villain monologue or a secret murder order. It is a survival memo written inside Deep Reach law, where every life-safety action has to pass through certification, liability routing, Atlas continuity seals and custody language before anyone can call it rescue. The record matters because it keeps culpability human without making it simple. Sorn saw worker distress, knew the ascent queue was turning into a death sentence, and still chose the sentence that protected the office first: hold release until channel clears.

## Scanner
