# Antiseptic Locker Sweet Sting

The locker smells sweet before it burns the nose. Cheap solvent was stretched through clean work and ugly work alike.

## Scanner

Bottle seals vary by batch. One cap carries a bite mark where a crew member opened it through a respirator mask.

## Terminal

Usage rose after the coolant leak, then stayed high after the leak was marked closed. No hazard ticket explains the second rise.

## Audio

Glass taps against glass inside the cabinet. A loose latch keeps time with the pump room thud below the floor.

## Field Note

Antiseptic hides blood poorly in wet stations. Follow the overcleaned handles, not the dirty ones, when a room feels staged.
