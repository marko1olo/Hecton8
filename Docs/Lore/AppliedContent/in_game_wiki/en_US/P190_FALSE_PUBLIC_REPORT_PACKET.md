# False Public Report Packet

False Public Report Packet is the version of HECTON-8 that Deep Reach could safely show the worlds. It does not invent a fake ocean. The Great Tide was real, the relay delay was real, quarantine uncertainty was real, and that is why the report survives a casual reading. The lie lives in the omissions. It removes accepted risk margins, delayed evacuation authority, Atlas continuity weighting, insurance conversion and recovery custody pressure. The player should read it as the final corporate move: keep enough physics to sound sober, cut enough decisions to erase guilt.

## Scanner

The public report worked because it kept the real ocean and removed the human choices inside it.

## Terminal

PUBLIC PACKET: HECTON-8 loss attributed to exceptional tide cascade, relay delay and quarantine uncertainty. Omitted: accepted risk margins, evacuation release hold, Atlas continuity override, claim conversion and payload-first recovery request.
