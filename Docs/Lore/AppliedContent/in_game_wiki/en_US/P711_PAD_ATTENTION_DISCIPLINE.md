# Procedure AD-4, Attention Discipline

Procedure AD-4 lists the signs of gas narcosis and routes them through a conduct ladder: delayed response to instruction, repeated queries, inappropriate affect. Verbal warning, written warning, removal from the band with the depth supplement forfeit. Conduct is a supervisor matter, and it generates no incident number and no exposure record.

AD-4 covers wet work to 140 metres, where the water reads 1.4 MPa. Blend authority sits with the mix station. The panel two metres from this card carries five regulators and three are set on Blend 2. Blend 1 is helium-bearing and metered; the helium bank valve behind it is shut under a decommission tag dated eleven months before AD-4 was revised.

Nineteen carbon copies hang on the clip behind the card. Seventeen are stage one. Assessment: Blend 2 fittings are serviceable and the manifold holds charge.
