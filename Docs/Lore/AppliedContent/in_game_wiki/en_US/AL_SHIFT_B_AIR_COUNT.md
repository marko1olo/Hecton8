# Shift B Air Count, Dorm Nine

Dorm Nine held 101 kilopascals of total pressure while its oxygen fraction fell, and the wall panel called that nominal.

Total pressure is the sum of every gas in a room. Carbon dioxide off nineteen sleeping bodies replaced the oxygen close to one for one, so the sum held and the needle never moved. A Shift B clerk caught it on a pocket cell during a routine count, four hours before the morning relief would have cleared the room on the total.

The dorm sheet for that night carries eighteen signatures. The relief found nineteen bunks warm.

Assessment: no habitat panel in Sector 44 reads partial pressure. Carry your own oxygen cell and read a room before you unseal, not after.

## Scanner

Handheld dictation unit clipped to a gas ledger. Confidence 76 percent. Hazard: room reads nominal total pressure. Limit: no partial-pressure history stored on the unit.
