# Proof Weight PW-9, Crown Hoist Deck

Proof weight PW-9 is 240 kilograms of cast lead in a steel shell, issued to hoist rigger Emeka Lindqvist for daily proof loads at the crown, where the hoists stop.

All four edges carry his hoist-stop readings, struck with a 3 millimetre drift and hit twice each so they read through silt. Eleven years of them, one per shift start. The run goes 1,880, 1,880, 1,880, and by the last edge it reads 1,884. The drawings kept the crown datum at 1,880 throughout. Striking a rated weight voids its proof certification, which is why the readings sit on the lead instead of in the log, and Deep Reach carried the same movement as calibration drift.

Assessment: 240 kilograms against a 4,800 kilogram window. The claim-tender books lead by mass and reads nothing on the edges.

## Scanner

Rated proof weight, shell intact, edges struck with sequential four-digit stamps. Confidence 97 percent. Hazard: 240 kilograms unsecured on a hoist deck. Limit: stamp sequence appears in no manifest.

## Field Note

Recover as scrap, not as record - that disposition line does the whole job, because a melt has no edges. Eleven years of crown readings costs you five percent of the window, and the tender will pay you for the lead either way.
