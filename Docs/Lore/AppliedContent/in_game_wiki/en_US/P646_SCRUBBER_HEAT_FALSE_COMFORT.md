# Scrubber Heat False Comfort

A recovered scrubber log explains why warmth can be a bad sign. A failing bed may heat the room, soften alarm timing and make tired workers feel safer while CO2 climbs. HECTON-8 punishes comfort readings that are not tied to partial pressure, flow and return-corridor state.

## Scanner

SCRUBBER HEAT // Bed temperature rising without stable CO2 fall. Comfort warning: warm air may be masking bad exchange.

## Terminal

SCRUBBER BED STATUS // Heat above service band. Flow uncertain. Do not clear shelter state until partial pressure and return-corridor draw both stabilize.

## Audio

Worker recorder: room got warm and everyone stopped arguing. That was the first bad sign.

## Field Note

Warm is not safe. Safe is a number that stays boring.
