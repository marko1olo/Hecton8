# Vent Forge Cooling Strike

A recovered forge card turns vent heat into procedure. Pressure glass and ascent-qualified seal work need heat pulse, pressure hold, contamination seal and slow cooling. Strike the cooling too fast and the lattice fractures. Hold too long and corrosive bloom, false oxygen readings and migrating fauna turn a useful forge into a bad room with orange light.
