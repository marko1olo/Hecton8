# Shallow Predator Shadow Rule

The Shallow Predator Shadow Rule keeps the first predator from becoming a simple monster reveal. HECTON-8 should introduce danger through the ecology that was useful a minute earlier. Glass grazers stop ticking while mineral film remains. Lantern drift still glows, but its small noise tears open. Shell clamps pull tight on cable runs. Then a broad shadow crosses the bright route and the safe line stops feeling safe. The predator does not need a name at first. It needs consequences: a blocked channel, an oxygen-expensive detour, a tool task that suddenly sounds too loud, or a near-miss that proves the water has changed. Combat can happen later. The first contact should be readable pressure, not a face in the camera.

## Scanner

SHALLOW PREDATOR SHADOW // Missing grazer ticks, torn bloom-noise and a broad moving shade are first contact. Route pressure comes before combat contact.

## Terminal

CONTACT RULE: predator evidence should change route choice before forcing damage. Stage as absence, silence, silhouette and wake; direct attack only after the player ignores or misreads at least two cues.

## Audio

When the little mouths stop ticking, stop moving.

## Field Note

Do not give the predator a face first. Let the shelf go wrong: food remains, water brightens, small life leaves, then the shadow crosses the task.
