# Payroll Period Close, Sector 44

Atmosphere allocation in Sector 44 was calculated off the active payroll roll, so closing a badge stopped counting the body attached to it.

Scrubber sizing, bunk assignment and suit issue all keyed to one roster. A badge moved to asset reassignment left that roster while the worker stayed on shift: the plant stopped sizing air for them, the locker stopped issuing them a seal, and neither change was communicated to the worker. Thirty-four badges closed in a single period. None of the thirty-four appear on a departure manifest.

The form to reopen a badge took eleven days.

Assessment: the locker and door panels down here still test a badge against that roster. A recovered badge is a key only if it was open when the power went, so read the stamp before you carry it 95 metres for nothing.
