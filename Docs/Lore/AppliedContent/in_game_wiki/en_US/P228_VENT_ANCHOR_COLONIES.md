# Vent Anchor Colonies

Vent anchor colonies explain why the Marauder cannot solve every repair problem by staying shallow. The colonies grow where thermal vents push heat and sulfide chemistry into brine layers. Their ribbed bodies trap minerals, cool in plates and shelter small vent fauna along the edge of lethal water. That makes them valuable without turning them into magic ore. Shed ribs can feed pressure-ceramic filler, cold-sealant grit and chemical filters. Live hot ribs can kill a suit fast or crack a tool head with thermal shock. The correct read is patience: scan the heat bloom, wait for the cooled edge, cut only shed plates, and leave the active throat alone unless the objective is worth the risk.
