# Pressure Seal Clamp Ring Assembly

The pressure seal clamp ring is where repair work stops being simple survival. The same material that keeps water out of a safe room can trap the ascent capsule if it crosses the separation seam. A shallow patch only has to hold a compartment. A launch-rated seal has to hold pressure, absorb crush, then release cleanly under impulse.

HECTON-8 makes this ugly because Atlas-grown sealant is useful. It follows leaks, fills cracks and preserves habitat volume that would otherwise drown. That does not make it a flight seal. If the sealant map grows over the clamp ring seat or pressure-glass edge, the capsule may pass a static pressure test and still fail separation when the charge fires.

The assembly requires deep-rated metal, known crush history, a clean glass seat and a sealant map that ends before the launch boundary. The useful repair must be cut back from the useful escape. A Marauder who leaves the wrong patch in place can build a capsule that stays dry until it has to move.

## Scanner

CLAMP-RING SEAT // Impact scoring, shallow patch residue, sealant crossing separation seam. Compartment pressure may hold; launch impulse will tear the wrong layer.

## Terminal

SEAL CERT: shallow patch rated for compartment pressure only. Ascent seal requires deep-rated clamp ring, pressure-glass seat, shell-sealant map and verified Deep Reach crush profile. Reject sealant growth across separation seam.

## Audio

The patch holds the room. It will not let the capsule go.

## Field Note

A room patch can buy air. An ascent seal has to crush, release and leave the capsule skin clean enough for the next burn.
