# Pump Bay 12 Log, Maintenance Shift 4

Pump Six primes with its breaker racked out, and this tape is the only record of how often.

Four entries across nine days, each with the lockout tag hung and initialled. A contactor jarred closed by hand vibration accounts for the first two. The last two were logged with the bay bus isolated at both ends, and the recording keeps that contradiction rather than settling it.

Crews started knocking on the discharge casing to make it prime. Two hand injuries that month sit under the same work order number.

Assessment: the header can come up to pressure at 310 metres while every panel in the bay reads dead. Crack the test port before you trust a tag, including your own.
