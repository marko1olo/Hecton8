# Aegir Moon Ladder

Aegir's moon ladder is not a sky decoration. Ran is the star, Aegir is the giant, and HECTON-8 is one ocean moon inside a moving chain of moons, relays, storms, custody checks and insured mass windows.

Skarn can flood antennas with charged noise. Vela can shutter relays with ice dust. Kestrel can block a line of sight at the worst minute. HECTON can throw storm plume through a clean ascent corridor. Black Keel can receive your packet and still refuse lift if the window, quarantine handshake or mass class fails.
