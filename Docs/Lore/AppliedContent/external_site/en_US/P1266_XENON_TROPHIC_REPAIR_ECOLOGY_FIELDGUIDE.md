# Xenon Trophic Repair Ecology Fieldguide

Xenon-Omega is often described as the thing Deep Reach came to steal. That is true, but too small. On HECTON-8 the blue material is part of a vent-fed chain: heat, sulfide salts, pressure defects, fungal bloom, grazers, conductive biofilm and damaged Atlas routines all touch the same surfaces.

That makes ecology playable evidence. A blue seam may mark value, poison, current leakage, predator traffic or an old repair path. A tagged animal may be hunting, grazing or carrying stale sensor data through a maintenance loop. The player does not need a clean boundary between nature and machine. The useful question is what the process is doing now, and who profits if the sample survives.
