# Missing Return Mark Claim Conversion

Return marks are small, but they decide what a route is allowed to mean. A closed route can be audited. An open route can be delayed, transferred, billed, reattached to equipment or treated as unresolved mass. That ambiguity matters on HECTON-8 because the colony left many workers recorded as moving after their rooms stopped moving air.

The useful evidence sits between the route stamp and the later claim desk exception. If oxygen stopped, the suit tag stayed in the bay and the return field stayed blank, the missing mark is not a clerical blank. It is a retained opening. Someone could keep the worker inside a contract state while the body evidence moved somewhere else.

Deep Reach does not need a villain speech for that to hurt. A desk can follow rules and still choose the cheaper field. Open route. Pending recovery. Equipment mass unresolved. Salvage authority retained. Each field looks narrow. Together they keep a dead route from becoming a closed loss.

The mark should be read with route-stamp overlap, packet notary mask edge and transcript damage band. Those objects do not accuse by mood. They stack sequence: room exposure, route handling, missing closure, claim desk conversion.
