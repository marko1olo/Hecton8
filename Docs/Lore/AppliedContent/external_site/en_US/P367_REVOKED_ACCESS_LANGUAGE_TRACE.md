# Revoked Access Language Trace

The Revoked Access Language Trace is built around a simple HECTON-8 rule: a locked door can still give itself away. Deep Reach systems were designed to deny, classify and protect claims with careful language. When the old credential fails, that language remains.

The useful clues are not passwords. They are corporate phrases with physical consequences: maintenance reclassification, degraded counts, substitute checks and claim-preservation routes. A normal visitor sees denial. A former field systems worker sees a record of what the door was told to hide.

This keeps investigation grounded in labor and liability. The operator does not need a magic key or a villain's confession. A dead badge, a denial screen and the wrong maintenance terms are enough to start following the route that Deep Reach tried to bury under procedure.
