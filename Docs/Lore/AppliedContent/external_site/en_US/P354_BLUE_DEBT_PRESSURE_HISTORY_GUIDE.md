# Blue Debt Pressure History Guide

Blue debt is not a gemstone and not a magic ore. It is the field name for Xenon-Omega-bearing pressure-stable substrate: mineral structure that only becomes valuable after the ocean has squeezed, heated, cooled, and electrically scarred it in the right order. A bright sample can be worthless. A dull sealed lump can pay for a month of oxygen if its pressure history is clean and its signal drift stays quiet.

That is why every blue debt find immediately becomes legal material. Atlas wants the drift. Deep Reach wants the claim history. Salvage crews want a container that does not sing loud enough to wake drones. The resource is dangerous because it sits at the crossing of geology, computation, custody, and debt. It proves HECTON-8 was never just a drowned colony. It was a buried account book with pressure writing the numbers.
