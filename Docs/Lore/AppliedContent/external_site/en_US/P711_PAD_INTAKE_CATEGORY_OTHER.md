# Intake Form MC-2, Bed 4

Deep Reach clinical intake forms recovered from HECTON-8 use a fixed six-category injury list issued by the Keelmark Loss Desk for cross-site comparability. The list has no category for pressure injury, and recovered forms overwhelmingly record such presentations as category six, other, non-specific.

Category six is recorded but not aggregated into site injury rate, which was an input to tide-margin review. Recompression equipment recovered on the same decks carries hour meters in the low thousands.
