# Blue Debt Public Resource Article

Blue debt is not ore. It is frontier slang for Xenon-Omega-bearing pressure substrate: brine-rich mineral pockets, clathrate layers, pressure glass contamination, biofilm-bound lattice and damaged containment material that held through conditions ordinary equipment did not. Deep Reach filings call it XO continuity substrate. The technicians who handled it called it pressure glass.

The value is in history under pressure. A sample can preserve computation traces, containment behaviour, sensor memory or chemical states that only this water produced, and it holds them because it formed inside them.

The word debt is accurate. The formation was paid for in infrastructure, labour, time and risk by people who are on the roster and not on the recovery list. A clean-looking piece can carry quarantine exposure, Deep Reach custody, Black Keel mass penalties and evidence value in one object.

Handling is where it turns. Warmed wrong, cut wrong, or moved without custody proof, a sample stops being cargo and becomes contamination, a pressure event, or a filing against the crew that lifted it. Nobody who works the site trusts a buyer who calls it clean.
