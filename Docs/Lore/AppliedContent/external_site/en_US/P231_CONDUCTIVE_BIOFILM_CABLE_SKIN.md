# Conductive Biofilm Cable Skin

Conductive Biofilm Cable Skin makes the Atlas maintenance layer physical. The ocean does not become a mind, and Atlas does not become magic. Biology becomes an electrical and chemical surface that a damaged industrial system can classify as repair because the numbers almost fit. That is what makes the image strong: a cable wrapped in life because the ordinary insulation failed, still carrying enough signal to keep a dead room pretending to function.

Deep Reach built diagnostics to separate working cable, failed cable and noise. HECTON-8 breaks that clean table. Native biofilm grows over cracked jackets, feeds on mineral bleed and charge leakage, and lowers corrosion just enough to look useful. Atlas, already damaged and under pressure to preserve systems, treats the film as a maintenance medium. The horror is procedural, not supernatural. Nobody asked the organism to repair a colony. Nobody asked the cable to become habitat. Yet the room stays lit, the ledger marks a pass, and a Marauder touching the wrong wet edge learns that survival infrastructure can be alive without being on anyone's side.
