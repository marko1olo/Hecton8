# Comfort Channel Test, 620 Metres

Class-III and Class-IV pressure suits shipped with an optional guidance voice drawn from a holder-supplied sample rather than the stock synthesiser. Aegir salvage crews report the option enabled on recovered Sector 44 units, identifiable from a sample lot number stamped on the collar config plate.

Recovered audio from HECTON-8 includes holders using the channel at depths where inert gas effects are documented, and doing so with the recording correctly identified as a recording. Deep Reach classified the option as non-critical morale hardware, a category excluded from failure review, which is why the record contains no assessment of how it behaves at depth.
