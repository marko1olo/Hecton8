# Blue Debt

Blue Debt is not a treasure crystal in the reef. It is residue from industrial pressure systems: Xenon-Omega substrate, failed containment media, computation slag and sealed scrap that stayed stable because the ocean kept pressing on it. Raise it wrong and the sample changes state. Open it in shelter air and the shelter becomes part of the cleanup.

The value sits in the container as much as the material. A loose shard buys fuel and disappears into trade. A sealed casket carries pressure history, acoustic return and custody marks. That can buy better tools, trigger Black Keel liens, pull quarantine into the route, or prove Deep Reach worked a site after it claimed withdrawal. Blue Debt pays fast only when the operator ignores what the tag can prove.
