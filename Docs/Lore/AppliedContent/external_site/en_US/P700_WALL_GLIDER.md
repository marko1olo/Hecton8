# Wall Glider

Reports from HECTON-8 shelf salvage describe a surface-adherent grazer that travels exclusively on manufactured surfaces, including hull plate, conduit and cable runs, and that does not cross fractures in the material it is holding. Crews read the extent of its tracks as an indication of intact plate.

The animal deposits a conductive residue along its route, which carries low current where the underlying structure remains energised. Because the residue bridges hairline fractures electrically, automated continuity testing returns a sound result across plate that is mechanically failing. Deep Reach records for Annex P-63 accept the resulting coverage in lieu of scheduled structural monitoring, and report no pressure variance on surveyed track without describing the unsurveyed remainder.
