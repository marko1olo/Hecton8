# Pocket Ambusher

Aegir salvage crews describe a heavy-bodied ambush feeder that occupies cut openings in flooded industrial structure rather than natural cavities. Reported strike range exceeds visible body length, which makes visual estimation of a safe standoff unreliable.

Field practice on HECTON-8 is to read the silt collar around an opening rather than attempt to see the animal. Deep Reach records from Sector 44 classify the species as an incidental access control asset, a category that describes a closed corridor without describing why the corridor closed.
