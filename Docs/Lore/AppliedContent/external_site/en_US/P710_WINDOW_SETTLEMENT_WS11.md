# Window Settlement Worksheet WS-11

Return Action Queue schedule WS-11 governs how a tonne-window lien is settled against recovered mass. An aborted lift books expended consumables as a debit at full issue value with no offsetting credit, while recovered mass is credited at a multiplier set by the depth class it was taken from.

A worksheet recovered from a 1,240 metre camp carries the crew's own pencil working. Ascending without a lift would have left 3.0 tonnes outstanding against a remaining 2.1, while two custody-grade cases lifted from the 2,340 metre foundry floor at a 2.8 multiplier would have cleared the window with 0.98 tonnes spare. The consumables line on the sheet is completed for three sets.
