# Procedure AD-4, Attention Discipline

Deep Reach procedure documents recovered from HECTON-8 include AD-4, which lists the recognised indicators of gas narcosis and processes them through a three-stage conduct ladder ending in removal from the depth band and forfeit of the depth supplement.

The procedure classifies the presentation as conduct rather than a medical event, which keeps it inside supervisor discretion and outside incident reporting. Recovered mix stations in the same bays were commonly found configured to the nitrogen-bearing blend, with helium bank isolation valves closed under decommission tags.
