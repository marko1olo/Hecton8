# Pressure Failure Thresholds

Pressure Failure Thresholds define the readable edge between resource and hazard. They let HECTON-8 turn depth into player-facing grammar: visual stress, audio ticks, scanner warnings, salvage risk and crafting consequences all point to the same physical rule. A sample fails because the world is pressing on it, not because an inventory number silently expired.

## Scanner

A good sample becomes a hazard when it crosses the wrong pressure line. The ocean does not care that the label was close.

## Terminal

## Field Note

Failure thresholds must be readable before punishment: tick, drift, hairline, bloom, dead-sample fog. If the player can only learn after loss, the world is cheating.
