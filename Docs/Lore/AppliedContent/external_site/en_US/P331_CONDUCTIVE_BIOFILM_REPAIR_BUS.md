# Conductive Biofilm Repair Bus

The conductive biofilm repair bus begins with a practical accident. Old Deep Reach cable jackets split under pressure, copper salts bleed into seawater, and native microbial mats already adapted to mineral heat settle into the wound. Atlas reads the weak current as a service path. It does not need the ocean to speak; it only needs a film wet enough to carry a pulse.

For a Marauder, this is both gift and trap. A biofilm bridge can wake a sealed door, carry a scanner handshake, or let a pump answer after years of silence. The same bridge can also lie. The line may glow green while its load capacity is gone, and a clean cut can turn a survivable corridor into a flooded one. The right question is not whether the growth is natural or artificial. The right question is what Atlas has decided it can count as repaired.
