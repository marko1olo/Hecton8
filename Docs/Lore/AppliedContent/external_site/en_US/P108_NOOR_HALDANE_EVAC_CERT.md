# Noor Haldane Evacuation Certificate

Noor Haldane was Deep Reach evacuation certification counsel in 2147. Her signature appears on the holds that kept evacuation legally defensible while the physical route failed.

The holds are built from requirements that can each be defended: pressure-biological quarantine, carrier custody, external route review, downstream port acceptance, claimant chain integrity. None of the clauses is absurd, which is why the file still survives review. A system can be correct inside its own frame and kill by waiting.

What the certificates change is how a locked colony door reads. A sealed evacuation path is not only a failed machine. On the routes where a Haldane hold was filed, the door was a legal decision awaiting external review, and the review authority sat years of signal delay away. Oxygen consumption on those routes was logged separately. The two files were never reconciled.
