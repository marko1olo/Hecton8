# Glassworm, Juvenile

HECTON-8 salvage reporting describes a colonial tube-forming juvenile that accretes trace-class continuity substrate directly onto fractures in colony structure, with growth density tracking flow rate through the crack. Tube mouths orient consistently into the inflow, which allows the leak axis to be read from the outside of the bloom.

Growth of this kind conceals pressure leaks rather than removing them. Crews report glove and seal injuries at blooms that appeared inert, and sequential cracking of tubes when a load-bearing patch is cut. Deep Reach records from Sector 44 classify the organism as organic structural sealant, mark the associated pressure variance as mitigated, and record no adult reference for the species.
