# Carrier Window Audio Cue

Black Keel can sound close while remaining unavailable. The carrier-window cue separates packet receipt from recovery clearance: a pair of high chimes confirms that a message entered the queue, while the low closure tone keeps body, proof and salvage underwater until the window, seal, payload and legal line agree.

## Scanner

CARRIER WINDOW TONE // Two high chimes mean packet receipt only. Low third tone marks recovery lane closed; log the window before moving.

## Terminal

BLACK KEEL AUDIO STATE // Two high chimes accepted packet receipt. One low closure tone means recovery lane remains closed by ascent package, quarantine handshake, carrier geometry or tonne-window allocation. Audio acknowledgement does not allocate lift mass.

## Audio

Black Keel relay: Packet received. Recovery lane closed. Log the window. Do not ascend on tone.

## Field Note

Two bright notes are a receipt. The ugly note is the bill saying your body still does not fit the window.
