# Shore Skimmer

Aegir shelf crews report a fast surface-layer forager on HECTON-8 that holds to the upper eight metres and feeds in a continuous line along wind-concentrated swell. The line breaks around water with degraded surface oxygen, which makes the gaps in it legible at distance.

Field practice is to read the interruption rather than the flock, and to treat a gap in the line as unbreathable water rather than open water. Deep Reach records from Sector 44 classify the species as a surface condition indicator and note that the shelf buoy service it replaced was suspended in 2146.
