# Tonne-Window Body Ledger

The tonne-window body ledger is the place where Aegir stops sounding like a route and starts sounding like a scale. It does not ask whether a person deserves rescue. It asks what mass is being lifted, who accepts it, what it might contaminate, which proof travels with it, and whether the next carrier pass can decelerate without turning mercy into wreckage.

Black Keel can hear a Marauder because signal is cheap. It can reserve a tentative line because custody software is fast. It cannot lift a body by sympathy. A living contractor is water, bone, suit mass, pressure risk, quarantine exposure, liability history, oxygen addendum, sample contamination, and a name that may embarrass Deep Reach if delivered to the wrong receiver. The ledger is cruel because it is exact.

## Mass Is A Legal State

On paper the line begins as `4.8 tonne-window equivalent`, but the number is not a price tag alone. It is a claim on one narrow recovery opportunity. The route has to spend lift, thermal margin, shield tolerance, braking authority, receiver berth and quarantine volume. Every kilogram must arrive as something the receiver is allowed to open.

That is why a body can be alive and still not be accepted as body recovery. The living state proves urgency. It does not prove clean custody. If the suit carries blue-debt residue, if a recorder core is chained to the same ascent sleeve, if a corpse tag has priority witness value, or if Recovery Compliance has not named a quarantine berth, the ledger can hold the body as pending while it moves smaller truths first.

## Competing Lines

The ugly part is that the ledger is not choosing between a person and nothing. It is choosing between payload lines that can all matter. A sealed evidence core may expose Deep Reach liability. A pressure-glass sample may certify Xenon-Omega contamination. A tool crate may restore a future ascent path. A corpse tag may prove worker loss conversion. A living Marauder may carry all of it and still be the hardest thing to certify.

Black Keel's old firmware treats those lines as recoverable mass classes. Body. Proof. Sample. Salvage. Witness. Each class has different heat tolerance, quarantine rule, receiver risk and publicity cost. A proof hash can ride a dirty packet pass. A sample can sit in a sealed canister. A body needs a door that someone is willing to open.

## Why Proof Leaves First

This is not a moral defense of the system. It is how the trap becomes playable and believable. Proof can outrun rescue because proof can be copied, sealed, hashed, delayed and verified without breathing. A body cannot. Once a living body enters the ledger, every omitted clause becomes a possible lawsuit, quarantine order or insurance failure.

Deep Reach learned to hide inside that difference. It does not need to say "leave the diver." It can require payload class, route-notary witness, contamination statement and receiver assignment before biological recovery language. Every request sounds procedural. Underwater, each request spends air.

## What The World Shows

The body ledger should not appear only as text. It should be visible in objects. A bent ascent ring with a mass stencil. A suit tag rewritten from `operator` to `payload`. A Keelmark receipt where oxygen and proof share a column. A quarantine berth number left blank. A carrier tone that confirms packet receipt but refuses lift allocation. A salvage crate stamped cleaner than the hand that carried it.

Those objects teach the rule without a lecture: the route is not empty, and help is not absent. The machine is present. It is counting.

## How To Read The Ledger

Read every recovery promise as four questions. What mass is being lifted? What receiver will open it? What contamination follows it? What proof changes its priority?

If the answers disagree, do not spend ascent hardware just because Black Keel answered. Preserve receipts, keep proof sealed, separate samples from body line when possible, and listen for the low closure tone. In Aegir space, a rescue is not real when someone hears you. It becomes real when the ledger can no longer pretend your body is only cargo.
