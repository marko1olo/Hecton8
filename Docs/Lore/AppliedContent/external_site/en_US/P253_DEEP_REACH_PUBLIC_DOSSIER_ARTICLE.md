# Deep Reach Public Dossier

Deep Reach Extraterrestrial Development Combine was competent. It built pressure corridors, worker habitats, extraction machinery, route certification, insurance custody and autonomous maintenance at RAN-B:H8, because a remote ocean moon cannot be run on intent. That competence is what makes the recovered records damaging rather than merely sad.

No single decision drowned Sector 44. Risk tables entered workers as exposure. Continuity language ranked the project above the retreat. A relay economy years wide in each direction meant every accountability signature arrived stale, and the people signing knew the arrival time when they signed.

Publicly the combine is a failed development authority with valuable assets lost under pressure. The recovered paperwork is narrower and worse. The company learned to keep every document defensible while the physical system became less survivable. Evacuation became containment. Recovery became cargo. Safety language protected infrastructure first, in complete and grammatical sentences.

The shells are still filing. Aegir Continuity Holdings, the Atlas Continuity Office, the Keelmark Loss Desk and the Recovery Compliance Office all hold live positions on the site in 2190. None of them is the entity that signed in 2147.
