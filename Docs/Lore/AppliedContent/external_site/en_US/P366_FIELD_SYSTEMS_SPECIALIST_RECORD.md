# Field Systems Specialist Record

The Field Systems Specialist Record gives HECTON-8's central operator a useful past without making that past too clean. They were not command staff and not a secret architect of the disaster. They worked near the systems that were supposed to keep evacuation routes, pumps, seals and relay rooms honest.

That matters because the abandoned colony is full of procedure, not only wreckage. A route-safe stamp, a clamp rating or a maintenance phrase can be more incriminating than a dramatic recording. The operator knows how those records should look when people are being protected, and how they look when a company wants protection to become paperwork.

Revoked access makes the history sharper. The old badge does not grant control anymore, but it still leaves the operator able to recognize the dialect of failure. HECTON-8 turns that professional memory into motive: not guilt for causing the catastrophe, but responsibility after reading how preventable rooms were filed away.
