# Nearlight Drive Stack Cutaway

The Barnard Yards cutaway was made for contractors, which is why it tells the truth. It does not draw a heroic engine bell and call the problem solved. It starts with the route: who owns the beam array, who cleared the pellet lane, how much dust loss the shield can take, how many years the radiator margin can stay honest, and which receiver is allowed to brake human mass.

That is the important part. Near-light travel did not make distance small. It made distance scheduled, insured, metered, and punishable. A vessel can carry a fusion-electric throat and still be helpless without departure infrastructure. It can survive cruise and still die politically at the braking slot. In HECTON-8, this is why rescue is never just a ship turning around. The engine is the route, the heat, the custody chain, and the mass someone is willing to pay to stop.
