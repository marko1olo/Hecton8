# Tubular Coral

Aegir salvage crews on HECTON-8 report a colonial tube-forming organism that establishes along fractures in industrial plate rather than across sound surface, filling and pressure-sealing cracks in structure left unattended since 2147. Rooms have been recovered holding several megapascals on growth alone.

The same growth covers the stamped panel identification used for structural certification. Deep Reach repair records from Sector 44 close such rooms on exterior survey, a method that establishes a room is intact without establishing which room it is. Field practice is to treat any run crossing a welded seam as load-bearing.
