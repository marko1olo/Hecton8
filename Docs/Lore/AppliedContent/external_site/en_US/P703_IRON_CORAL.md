# Iron Coral

Salvage registers from HECTON-8 describe a mineralising colonial growth that establishes only on ferrous and alloy substrate, which makes standing crust a reliable surface indicator of buried structure. Crust colour is used in the field to estimate what lies beneath, including a blue discoloration associated with trace Xenon-Omega content.

Two practical costs are on record. The skeleton abrades suit glove seams, producing failures that pass a static seal check, and Deep Reach assay lots credit recovered crust at gross mass against the recovering party's tonnage without separating crust from substrate. Sector 44 records also close seam inspections marked overgrown.
