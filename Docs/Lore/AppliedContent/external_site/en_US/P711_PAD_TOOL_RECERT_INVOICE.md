# Recertification Invoice TC-3390

Contract Continuity Desk invoices recovered from HECTON-8 price the witnessed recertification of rated hand tools above the replacement cost of the same tool, with the nearest rated test bench located off-site. Sections routinely recorded these invoices as deferred at section request.

The invoices classify the affected items to the equipment register as non-safety, and record that failure of unlisted equipment is not a process event. Process continuity was the metric sections were assessed against.
