# Pressure Suit Repair Patch Ledger

A pristine pressure suit belongs in a brochure. HECTON-8 is not a brochure. The suits that matter are patched, rubbed raw at knees and cuffs, salted around seams and repaired with whatever strip could survive one more descent.

The best suit patch carries social information. Factory repair is even. Supervisor repair uses proper tape but no time. Worker repair uses wrong thread and knows exactly where the body bends. Medical repair seals fast and ugly. Black Keel repair strips identifiers first. A clean patch on a filthy suit can mean the person wearing it mattered to somebody with inventory access.

That is why suit patches belong in lockers, med bays, flooded changing rooms and abandoned tool benches. They make the colony personal without turning every body into a speech. The patch says: somebody was here, somebody knew the risk, and somebody decided the work still had to continue.
