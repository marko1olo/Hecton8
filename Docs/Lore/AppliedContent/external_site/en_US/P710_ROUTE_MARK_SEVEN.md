# Route Mark 7, Corrected Twice

Route marks in the HECTON-8 drowned factories are maintained in grease pencil over painted stock so a bearing can be corrected under pressure without removing the original figure. Standard practice strikes the superseded bearing and initials the strike.

Mark 7, recovered at 1,240 metres, carries three bearing and depth pairs. Two are struck and initialled by the same hand, and the standing figure, 188 at 1,255 metres, is written in a harder pencil. The bearing tracks toward the Sector 44 crown, where hoist coverage terminates at the 1,880 metre datum. Return Action Queue advisory 44-R-118 classifies the route as elective and crew selected.
