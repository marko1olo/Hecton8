# Rib Survey, Frames 22 to 29

A structures survey from Sector 44 documents speech transmitted between compartments with no shared atmosphere or opening. Welded pressure ribs conduct sound at roughly three times its speed in water and deliver it without usable bearing, so a voice raised in one bay is audible in another and appears to originate locally.

Colony crews used the effect to locate casualties and were repeatedly sent to unoccupied compartments. The survey also records one rib run picking up a deck on a separate run, with a negative dye check at the suspect frame. Rescue procedure was left unchanged.
