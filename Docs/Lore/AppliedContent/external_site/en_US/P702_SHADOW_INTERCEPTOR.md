# Shadow Interceptor

Aegir deep-transit crews report a predator that closes on a diver's projected position rather than tracking from behind, and that ceases to return sonar contact in the seconds before contact is made.

Current field practice on HECTON-8 treats loss of contact during an open crossing as the warning itself, and calls for a change of destination rather than an increase in speed. A 2147 Deep Reach transit loss involving three personnel is recorded with cause of loss given as route selection.
