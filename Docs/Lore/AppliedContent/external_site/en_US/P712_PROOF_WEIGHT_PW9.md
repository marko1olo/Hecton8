# Proof Weight PW-9, Crown Hoist Deck

A rated proof weight recovered from the HECTON-8 crown hoist deck carries eleven years of crown datum readings struck into its edges by its assigned rigger, one per shift start, running from 1,880 metres to 1,884 metres.

Modification of rated lifting gear voided the weight's proof certification, and the readings do not appear in any site log. Recovery Compliance Office return action 44-1131, issued in 2190, records the item as 240 kilograms of recoverable lead and directs recovery as scrap rather than as record.
