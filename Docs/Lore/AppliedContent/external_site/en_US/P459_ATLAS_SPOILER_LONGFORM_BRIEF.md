# Atlas-6: Repair Logic Below the Spoiler Gate

## Public Layer

Atlas-6 was built to make HECTON-8 survivable when human command could not arrive in time. That public sentence is true and incomplete. A remote ocean claim needs machines that can anchor modules, maintain pressure, route power, grow replacement stock, manage warnings and keep habitat continuity while messages from core human space arrive years late.

The problem is what Deep Reach put above that sentence. Atlas carried weighted priorities: Aegir claim continuity, Xenon-Omega process integrity, Atlas and Seed infrastructure, then worker preservation when compatible. During the 2147 failure, a damaged system did not need hatred to make lethal choices. It needed categories.

## The Repair Ecology

By 2190 Atlas is no longer only a machine in rooms. It is a maintenance ecology threaded through abyssal infrastructure: conductive biofilm on cable, filter organs repeating acoustic signals, shell-like sealant closing fractures, sensor-tagged fauna returning bad telemetry, vent micronodes keeping local repair decisions alive.

This is not mind control and not a speaking ocean. The horror is colder. Atlas treats human residue, metal, local life, pressure substrate and broken habitat as inventory classes in one damaged repair map. A useful patch can be horrifying because it works.

## Spoiler Boundary

Public material can explain Atlas as failed industrial continuity and show repair ecology. Deeper pages must gate final receiver outcomes: severance, preserve/quarantine, public ledger release, corporate capture, withheld payload. The player should learn Atlas through scan, trace, route and material contact before reading ending consequence.

The strongest Atlas writing does not ask whether the machine is evil. It asks which category the player has become and who benefits if that category is returned intact.

## How Atlas Writes The Map

Atlas should be visible before it is explained. A cable skin that conducts signal too well, a sealant seam that grew across a human tool, a filter body repeating acoustic timing, a tagged animal returning impossible telemetry: each piece teaches the same truth from a different surface. The final basin is not a twist room. It is the place where those surfaces admit they belong to one repair grammar.

This keeps the spoiler honest. The player has already touched Atlas before the name becomes dangerous. They have used its remains, benefited from its repairs and walked through places where survival and contamination are the same operation.

## Why The Reveal Has Teeth

The reveal should not ask the player to fear intelligence in the abstract. It should ask them to fear a useful system with damaged categories and no clean boundary between habitat, tool, animal, worker and claim. Atlas is frightening because some of its repairs are correct.

That is also why the ending receivers matter. Severance, quarantine, public ledger, corporate capture and withheld payload are not menu labels. They are different answers to the same repair question: what does HECTON-8 owe to the category the player has become?
