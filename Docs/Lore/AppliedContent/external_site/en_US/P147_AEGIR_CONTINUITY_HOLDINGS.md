# Aegir Continuity Holdings

Aegir Continuity Holdings makes continuity sound like care. In practice it means that a route lease can survive a worker, a damaged habitat can remain an asset, and a drowned work site can still produce options.

The shell matters because it lets Deep Reach wait. Public outrage cools, evidence ages, contracts roll over, and the holding company remains ready to receive whatever the next expedition can legally recover.
