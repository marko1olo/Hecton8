# Damped Insulation Blanket Field Article

Damped insulation blanket sits behind the visible metal of HECTON-8 pressure interiors. The quilted gray-beige fabric is an engineered layer, not domestic comfort. It reduces vibration, protects backing plates and records moisture before the wall admits a visible leak.

In play, the blanket can guide the reader toward powered equipment, hidden water paths, corroded cable trays or bad post-flood repairs. Dark stitch lines show repeated wet-dry cycles. Sagging quilt squares show retained water. Salt dust at seams shows condensation routes. Tears exposing a dark inner layer often mean the wall was opened fast and closed badly.

The material gives old colony interiors a human maintenance history. It lets the player read rooms by touch, sound and stain rather than by terminal exposition. A clean panel may lie; damp quilting usually tells the cheaper truth.
