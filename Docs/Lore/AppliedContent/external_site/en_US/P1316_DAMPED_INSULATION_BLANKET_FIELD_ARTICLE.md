# Damped Insulation Blanket Field Article

Damped insulation blanket sits behind the visible metal of a pressure module: quilted gray-beige technical fabric over the backing plate, installed to cut vibration, hold heat and keep a serviceable room quiet.

It records moisture before the wall shows any. Thread holes draw water inward, so stitch lines darken first. A sagging quilt square is retained water. Salt dust on one seam is a condensation route that dried and re-wet. A tear exposing the darker inner layer usually means the panel was opened fast and closed badly.

Habitat maintenance procedure HM-14 replaced blanket sections after the third wet-dry cycle. In the drowned modules the blankets are well past that count and the sections were resealed instead of replaced, which is why damp quilting and exposed backing plate now sit behind corridors that photograph dry. Where the blanket is salted next to a cable tray, the jacket seams beside it are corroded as well.
