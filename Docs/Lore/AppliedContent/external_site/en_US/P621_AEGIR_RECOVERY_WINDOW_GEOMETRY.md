# Aegir Recovery Window Geometry

Aegir recovery is a schedule before it is a rescue. Black Keel can hear a packet through a narrow relay pass and still refuse the body because the route has not cleared carrier phase, storm ceiling, Kestrel relay shadow, quarantine state, ascent package seal and accepted tonne-window mass. HECTON-8 makes that delay physical. The ocean holds the descent hardware below pressure, the sky breaks clean line-of-sight with weather and charged noise, and the carrier treats every kilogram as part of a claim ledger. The first answer from orbit is therefore not mercy. It is proof that one part of the machine aligned for a few minutes. Survival means making the rest of the machine agree before the next window closes.

## Scanner

WINDOW CLOCK // Black Keel can receive a packet this pass. Body recovery still needs ascent hardware, quarantine handshake, Kestrel shadow clearance, and an accepted tonne-window mass line.

## Terminal

KEELMARK RECOVERY NOTICE // Signal receipt is not lift approval. Align carrier phase, storm ceiling, relay shadow, quarantine clearance, ascent package state, and accepted payload mass before requesting recovery.

## Audio

Black Keel relay: packet received. Body not in this window. Keep the ascent package sealed, keep the proof tagged, and do not spend air celebrating a number.

## Field Note

If the carrier answers, do not spend the air celebrating. A reply is geometry plus accounting. Rescue starts when the window, seal, payload and body all fit the same line.
