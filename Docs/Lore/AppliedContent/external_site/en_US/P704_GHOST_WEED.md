# Ghost Weed

Aegir salvage crews report a pale, gas-vesicled plant that grows in flat mats across horizontal openings in flooded industrial structure, and that returns acoustic energy in a way consistent with plate steel. The name in use on HECTON-8 refers to the sonar return rather than to the organism, which is close to transparent under a working lamp.

Field practice at around 340 metres, roughly 3.4 megapascals, is to look for a second and later return beneath the apparent surface before loading it. Deep Reach survey records for Sector 44 carry mature mats forward as structural surface on floor plan 44-W-3, an entry that describes a flat return without describing what produced it. Cut blades retain vesicle gas for several hours and are used locally as lift.
