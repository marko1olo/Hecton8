# Evidence-First Charter Contract Seed

Evidence-First Charter changes the contract board before the descent. Material payout drops, notary capacity rises, proof lockers take priority and salvage stripping carries a penalty. The operator enters with a route that protects records better than parts.

The pressure appears in small choices. A hatch-state strip can block a panel sale. A wet accident packet can occupy the last dry slot. A pinger log may need a live relay that would otherwise feed a repair. The charter pays late because it asks the operator to keep inconvenient objects intact long enough for a receiver outside Black Keel to read them.
