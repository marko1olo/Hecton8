# Observation Bay 4 Watch Log

An observation-bay watch record from Sector 44 documents repeated output reduction on an exterior floodlight bank with no fault indication. The bank held automatic gain from a photocell measuring returned light, and reflection off a large body holding station in the beam was sufficient to drive output to roughly twenty percent.

The effect leaves the panel reading normal while the compartment loses illumination. Deep Reach classified the behaviour as lighting calibration drift, kept the viewport rated, and maintained a single-occupant watch posting on the bay.
