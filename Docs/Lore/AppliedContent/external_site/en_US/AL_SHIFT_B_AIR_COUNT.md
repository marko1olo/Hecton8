# Shift B Air Count, Dorm Nine

A dormitory atmosphere record from Sector 44 documents a room holding 101 kilopascals of total pressure while its oxygen fraction dropped through 17 percent, the shortfall taken up by exhaled carbon dioxide. Habitat panels of that generation summed total pressure and displayed no partial-pressure field, so the wall reading stayed nominal throughout.

The discrepancy was found on a hand-carried oxygen cell during a shift count. Deep Reach recorded the operator report as calibration drift and left the panel in service. Occupancy for the period is not entered on the sheet.
