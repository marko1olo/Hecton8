# Black Keel Nearlight Wake

Black Keel does not cross the Ran route for one Marauder. By 2190 it is already an Aegir-system claim tender: a working carrier caught inside the local economy of transfer orbits, relay shadows, Keelmark custody, storm ceilings and tonne-window accounting. Its engines matter because they explain the cruelty of the first answer from orbit. The ship can be near enough to hear a packet and still be physically, legally and thermally unable to lift a body.

## Wake Before Mercy

The old nearlight route is not a single engine bell. It is a chain of machines that spend years making velocity and then spend more years getting rid of it. Beam arrays, pellet lanes, fusion-electric throats, shield ice, magsails and aerobrake skins all leave a wake in the paperwork before they leave a wake in plasma. A carrier that has paid that price does not become a helicopter when it reaches a moon.

Around Aegir, Black Keel works at the slow end of that history. Its local burns are conservative because every correction consumes propellant, radiator margin, receiver time and claim authority. A careless transfer does not simply miss a dock. It can put a tender behind Kestrel's relay shadow, into Aegir charged-particle noise, above the wrong storm ceiling or outside the next accepted recovery lane. That is why the carrier sounds procedural instead of kind.

## The Tonne-Window

The tonne-window is not metaphor. It is the mass that can pass through a narrow recovery opportunity without breaking the schedule, overheating the package, contaminating custody or invalidating the insurance line. A living Marauder, a sealed evidence core, a pressure-glass sample, a tool case and a damaged ascent sleeve all compete for the same window. The carrier can calculate them. It cannot pretend they weigh nothing.

Black Keel's public authority comes through the Aegir Reclamation Pool. Keelmark Mutual counts the loss. Recovery Compliance can pressure the receiver clause. None of those offices need to be present in the sky for the system to feel their hands. Their rules are already in the packet queue, the ascent checklist and the mass line that decides whether the body, the proof or the cargo leaves first.

## Why A Signal Is Not Rescue

A data packet is small. It can ride a dirty relay pass through magnetic noise, storm scatter and Kestrel shadow if the timing is good enough. A body is not small. A body needs an ascent package, a pressure-safe seal, guidance timing, quarantine handshake, heat margin and an accepted mass allocation. A sample may need a different clearance than a person. Evidence may be allowed through a notary window that cargo cannot use.

That separation is why the first carrier chime is dangerous if misunderstood. Two high tones can mean Black Keel heard you. They do not mean the ocean has let go. The low closure tone is the useful cruelty: the ship is telling you which part of the machine is still refusing the lift.

## The Wake In The Water

From the surface shelf, this can feel obscene. The carrier is in the sky. A Marauder can hear it. The terminal can print a receipt. The ocean is bright enough to see Aegir and the moons. Still, the route is closed because the ascent ring is torn, the high-gain antenna is gone, the flotation heat-shield is not rated, and the window belongs to a ledger that has not accepted the payload.

HECTON-8 keeps hard sci-fi close to the hand. The big route economy becomes a flooded relay board, a bad clock in Shallow Annex P-63, an audio tone in a damaged speaker grille, a cold seal around an ascent sleeve and a Marauder deciding whether proof deserves mass before salvage. Black Keel is not absent. It is worse than absent: it is present enough to make the delay measurable.

## How To Read The Chime

The correct response to Black Keel is not relief. It is bookkeeping with shaking hands. Mark the time of the chime, preserve the packet receipt, compare the storm ceiling against the route clock, and keep the proof sealed until the mass line answers. In Aegir space, rescue does not begin when a ship hears you. It begins when geometry, seal, quarantine, payload and tonne-window all stop contradicting one another.
