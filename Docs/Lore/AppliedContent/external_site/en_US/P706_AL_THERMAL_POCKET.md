# Throat 12, Forty Metres Downstream

Vent fields between four and five thousand metres on HECTON-8 are crossed by a cold bottom current that displaces plumes laterally. Hot water is therefore found downstream of a throat at working depth rather than directly above it, in bands reported at forty to sixty metres across.

Suit thermal overlays resolve these bands with a lag of several seconds, which is significant at swim speed, and field practice is to cross upstream into the cold set. A Deep Reach field note from Sector 44 records an outer plate reading of 61 C and classifies the associated injury as equipment degradation.
