# Noble Gas Brine Pockets

Noble Gas Brine Pockets define the hard-science root of HECTON-8's blue economy. The valuable part is not a glowing rock and not a magic isotope. It is a sealed pressure history: noble gases caught in mineral brine, then locked inside clathrate traces, microfractures and defect lattices that only make sense if the sample is recovered intact. A careless crew can hear the pocket hiss and think it found money. Usually it has just converted the money into weather. Field extraction therefore uses pressure bells, slow thermal shielding and evidence logging before any refinery claim begins. The pocket's gas ratio matters, but so does how it got that ratio. A sample that cannot prove its pressure curve is low-grade feedstock even if the xenon count looks rich. This is the resource that teaches the player why Xenon-Omega is a process outcome instead of a collectible mineral. Deep Reach did not discover a miracle gem. It learned which sealed histories could be tortured into debt material. Atlas later classified those histories with damaged priorities. The pocket itself remains older and stranger than both of them: a bubble of geology still trying not to become weather.
