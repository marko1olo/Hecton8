# Crew Plate, Shelf Camp 186

Marauder crews operating under Aegir Reclamation Pool tenders are commonly bound by a single joint tonne-window rather than individual liens. Recovered crew plates print the window figure alongside the roster, and the standard clause records the obligation as joint and several.

A plate recovered from a 186 metre shelf camp lists four holders against a 6.2 tonne window under lien 6.2-0912. The associated Recovery Compliance Office record notes that survivorship does not divide the window, which leaves the full obligation with any surviving holder. The plate carries six slots; the remaining two were taped over rather than filled.
