# Suit Reserve Delta Mismatch

The wrist counter is an ugly little record: not a confession, not a final message, just pressure dropping under a worker's hand. That is why it belongs beside the oxygen ledger. It does not care whether a route was still open.

On Aegir, room support and suit reserve were supposed to move together. The room fed air; the suit buffered loss; the compressor wrote its handoff; the route board waited for a return mark. When those numbers split, the colony's paper map no longer matches the body path.

Deep Reach could use that gap. A suit with an unresolved reserve could stay tagged as recoverable equipment. A worker with an open route could stay billable. A claim hold could sit over both, delaying clean loss closure until the useful ownership language hardened.

The reserve delta does not solve the death. It points to the desk where breath became inventory. Read it with the oxygen ledger cutoff, missing return mark and route permission stamp overlap before trusting any active-worker status.
