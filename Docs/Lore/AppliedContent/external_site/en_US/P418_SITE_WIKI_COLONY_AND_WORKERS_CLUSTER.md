# Colony and Workers

The colony and worker lane keeps HECTON-8 from turning into a map of empty rooms. Deep Reach filed the catastrophe as storm, collapse, failed autonomy, Atlas rescue and missing data, but the surviving records keep people attached to work. A pump chief remains visible through a pressure board. A relay hand remains visible through witness trays. A brine-route crew remains visible through stamps, ration ledgers and access holds. A triage technician remains visible through repair tags that should have moved faster than the water. A vent-forge shift remains visible through rejects nobody had time to hide.

These records are not family hooks for the player. They are labor evidence. The Marauder does not arrive to avenge a relative; they arrive as a professional who can read why a door stayed open, why a pump was restarted late, why a quarantine route kept a worker in the wrong pressure band, and why an apparently small name tag can matter more than a polished loss summary. The colony cluster should lead readers through jobs first because HECTON-8 was held together by work before it failed through policy.
