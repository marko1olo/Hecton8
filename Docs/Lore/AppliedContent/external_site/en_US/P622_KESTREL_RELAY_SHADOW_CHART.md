# Kestrel Relay Shadow Chart

On HECTON-8, the sky is not background. Kestrel's passage across the Aegir lane throws a relay shadow through the same geometry Black Keel needs for packets, carrier timing and recovery math. The signal does not fade like a lamp being dimmed. It breaks into a comb: bright edges, noisy slots, stale returns and short bursts that arrive with enough checksum to prove someone listened, but not enough continuity to approve a lift. The ocean below makes the problem worse. Water eats power, storms bend surface timing, and Aegir's charged environment turns clean sky into a moving legal and physical hazard. A failed uplink is therefore not proof of abandonment. It may be the moon crossing the only piece of sky that could carry you.
