# Black Keel First Voice

The first voice of Black Keel should feel like a door opening only halfway. The audio is damaged, the text channel is clean, and the carrier does not waste a word on comfort. That absence matters. A friendly AI would soften the ocean. A dramatic villain would make the danger too human. Black Keel is more useful than both because it tells the truth in a format that still refuses safety. Signal acquired. Survival acknowledged. Recovery unresolved. The player learns that being heard is not the same as being saved, and that the only voice above the water is bound to ledgers the player has not seen yet.
