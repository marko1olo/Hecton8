# Silent Orbit Receipt Ledger

Black Keel is not a home ship waiting above HECTON-8. It is an Aegir-system claim tender built to hold orbit, price recovery, verify manifests and protect the Aegir Reclamation Pool from loss. Below the upper ocean, ordinary radio stops being useful. The water is dense with salts, metals and industrial dust; the player has to push data through low-frequency acoustic pulses that move slowly and carry little.

That constraint is central to HECTON-8. No FTL reply is coming from Sol. No ansible voice can talk the player out of a flooded module. Even local orbit is broken into windows by storms, moon geometry and Kestrel's relay shadow. Black Keel may confirm a packet because its ledgers require custody. It may still withhold extraction until ascent mass, quarantine status and payload value fit the contract.

The receipt is paper trail, not comfort. The carrier says received. The room stays dark. The contractor still has to breathe long enough to make the ledger worth opening again.
