# Deep Reach: Liability Under Real Water

## The Useful Half-Truth

Deep Reach's public report survives because it contains enough real water to sound honest. HECTON-8 did suffer a Great Tide. Cryosphere stress, resonance tides, storm stacking, heat discharge and ocean pressure did tear through the colony. The moon did not need a cartoon villain to flood rooms and crush modules.

That is why the liability chain matters. The lie is not "the tide happened." The lie is the missing priority stack around the tide: accepted sensor margins, evacuation capacity priced below need, Atlas continuity routed above worker extraction, quarantine language used as time, and missing people converted into claim categories after the fact.

## The Signatures

Iliya Varnek's margin acceptance turns warning into schedule exposure. Selene Arendt's Atlas waiver keeps worker safety inside a process-continuity frame. Noor Haldane's quarantine hold makes extraction wait for certification while rooms lose pressure. Marek Ibarra's ledger moves bodies into load and loss language. Vera Sato-Ren's 2190 return-action packet proves the company still asks for coordinates, XO proof and silence before it offers rescue wording.

None of these documents need to confess. A clean document is more dangerous when the flooded room supplies the missing cost. HECTON-8 noir works because every signature can be defended from a dry office and still become lethal below the waterline.

## What The Player Carries

A single memo is suspicion. A chain is evidence. The player finds dents, holds, timestamps, worker names, Atlas traces, packet seals and recovery clauses in different places, then decides what is worth carrying through Black Keel custody.

Deep Reach wants HECTON-8 back as material, access, proof control and Atlas continuity. The player can return cargo. The harder act is returning context.

## The Shape Of Guilt

Deep Reach should not be flattened into a single villain with a clean button to press. Its guilt is stronger because it is distributed. Varnek accepts the tide margin. Arendt moves Atlas weights above workers. Haldane keeps quarantine language clean. Ibarra converts names into loss categories. Sato-Ren tries to move a return action after the damage is already inside the system.

Each document is small enough to look ordinary. Together they show an institution that survived by making every lethal choice appear local, temporary and procedurally correct. The workers died inside that grammar.

## Why Proof Has Mass

The player cannot solve this with belief. Packets can be delayed, challenged, redacted or priced. Physical proof has to be carried: sensor margins, damaged evacuation hardware, Atlas trace, custody ledger, sample chain. Evidence is heavy because HECTON-8 makes truth share space with oxygen, tools and cargo.

That is the pressure at the center of the story. The player is not simply learning what happened. They are deciding how much survival can be spent to make what happened harder to erase.
