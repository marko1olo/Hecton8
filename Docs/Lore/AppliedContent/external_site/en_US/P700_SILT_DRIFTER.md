# Silt Drifter

Salvage reporting from HECTON-8 describes a broad benthic feeder that grazes shelf sediment and produces a persistent surface furrow. Crews use the shape of the furrow as an indicator of local flow: straight tracks over open sediment, curved tracks where water is drawn through a submerged intake.

Contact provokes a heavy sediment bloom that defeats both lamp and sonar, and field practice calls for reduced beam and navigation on bearing until clear. Deep Reach records classify the species under sediment management and record intake screen inspection for the affected depth range as deemed current.
