# Glass Grazer Schools

Glass Grazer Schools are HECTON-8's first lesson in animal behavior as instrumentation. The grazers are not pets, loot pods or harmless decoration. They are small transparent shell-feeders that make the shallow shelf readable by scraping mineral film into bright lanes. A living school tells the Marauder where pressure glass is growing, where a seam is too warm, where a current has just turned and where predators have not passed recently. The important moment is usually the absence: the film remains, the little shell-mouths stop ticking, and a clean route suddenly feels wrong. That silence ties directly into the shallow predator grammar, where danger arrives first as missing fauna, broken bloom-noise and a shadow across an otherwise beautiful reef. Good placement should let the player learn this with their body: swim toward the pale scrape line, hear the ticking sharpen against the shell, notice the clear zone around a warm patch of sealant, then watch the school fold away before the sonar knows why. Glass grazers make HECTON-8 feel alive because they are useful, fragile and honest. They do not explain the ocean. They expose what the ocean just did.

## Scanner

GLASS GRAZER SCHOOL // Transparent shell-feeders scrape mineral film from pressure glass and photic mat edges. Their ticking opens bright route scars; sudden silence is predator data.

## Terminal

FAUNA BASELINE: non-hostile grazer cluster. Track pressure-glass film, fresh mineral bloom, current disturbance, and predator displacement. Absence after active feeding is a route warning, not an empty spawn.

## Audio

Hear the ticking on the shell? That is lunch, map and warning in the same sound.

## Field Note

Do not chase the school. Read what it exposes: fresh film, old scrape, warm-sealant avoidance, and the empty lane the animals refuse to cross.
