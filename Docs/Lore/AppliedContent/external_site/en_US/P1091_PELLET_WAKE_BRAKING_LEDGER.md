# Pellet Wake Braking Ledger

HECTON-8 needs no magic wall to isolate the player. Distance is enough. The Aegir route was opened by pellet-beam probes: small autonomous scouts pushed by huge beam installations at Luyten Junction. A probe can be fired, lost and written off. A colony ship cannot.

Atlas-class freight is a different creature. It carries factories, cryo bays, drilling systems, sealed debt contracts and the people cheap enough to sign them. Its fusion-electric pulse engines spend years making velocity. Near arrival, the ship must spend more engineering discipline losing that velocity. Magsails deploy across absurd spans to brake against charged particles. Later passes scrape atmosphere, storm envelope and gas-giant edge. A minor error in the braking ledger can erase cargo, crew and claim.

Rescue in HECTON-8 feels cruel without breaking physics. Sol can receive a packet long after the decision has expired. Deep Reach can own a debt faster than it can move a hull. Black Keel can sit in local Aegir orbit and price a recovery window, but that is local salvage machinery, not a promise from home. Civilization reaches you first as paperwork, then as signal, and only much later as mass.
