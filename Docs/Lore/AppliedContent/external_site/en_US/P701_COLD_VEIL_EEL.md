# Cold Veil Eel

Crews working HECTON-8 pressure-base machinery at 1,900 metres report a cold-water eel with sheet morphology that holds station in scrubber discharge plumes. The species is routinely misidentified as torn damping blanket at lamp range; the reliable discriminator is edge behaviour, since the animal holds one margin rigid against flow that a free blanket follows.

Contact injuries are thermal rather than mechanical, with rapid suit heat loss and seal stiffening reported. Deep Reach records from Sector 44 carry the species as an unscheduled temperature instrument and log an embedded tag as reporting, on a bay whose cycle has been maintained without staffing since 2147.
