# Thermal Tubeworm

Thermal field survey on HECTON-8 reports dense tube-forming colonies restricted to active vent outflow between roughly 4,000 and 5,500 m. Colonies retract together seconds ahead of measurable changes in flow, and crews use that behaviour as a thermal pulse warning.

The recorded hazard is instrumentation. Vent chemistry interferes with low-cost electrochemical oxygen cells, driving indicated dissolved oxygen and suit reserve readings upward while conditions deteriorate, and the error is smooth enough that it does not present as a fault. Deep Reach sample lots from Sector 44 record recovery of embedded nodes from colony bases, with the host listed as not required.
