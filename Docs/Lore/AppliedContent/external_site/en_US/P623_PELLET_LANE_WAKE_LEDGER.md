# Pellet Lane Wake Ledger

Pellet lanes are not roads. They are promises made by infrastructure years before a ship arrives. A launch array, depot or route station throws streams of reaction pellets along a predicted path; a freighter catches the stream, feeds fusion pulses, rides behind shielding, coasts for years, then depends on braking architecture that may have changed owners before the ship reaches Aegir. That machinery built the route in layers: needleprobes first, seed machines after them, then heavy cargo, sleeper contracts, system carriers and local tenders like Black Keel. It also explains the cruelty of the present. A message can cross the route before useful mass can obey it. A rescue request may be fresh while the ship, propellant, brake slot, quarantine berth and survivor seat belong to schedules bought before the emergency existed. By 2190, drive plates read less like adventure and more like legal scar tissue. The Marauder can scan one pitted wake ledger and see who had enough money to throw metal through ten light years, and who was expected to pay for stopping it.
