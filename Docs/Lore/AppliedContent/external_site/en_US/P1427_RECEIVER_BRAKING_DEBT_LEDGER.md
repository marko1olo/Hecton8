# Receiver Braking Debt Ledger

HECTON-8's space lore works because the civilization is capable and still constrained. There is no faster-than-light rescue hidden behind the plot, and there is no primitive isolation either. Humanity can push mass through near-light routes, but every kilogram crosses on infrastructure somebody owns.

A braking debt ledger is the document that makes that visible. It records who owns the slowing path, who absorbs heat, who accepts quarantine, who receives the cargo, and who pays if the stop damages ship, station, cargo or body. A signal can be cheap because it does not ask a receiver to catch a person. Evidence can move before rescue because proof is not breath.

For the player, this turns distance into pressure instead of trivia. The question is not whether civilization exists. It does. The question is whether the player can make their body, evidence, samples and debt fit into a route that is already busy moving things that corporations cared about first.
