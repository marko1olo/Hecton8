# Marker Inventory, P-63 Back Wall

Colony crews on HECTON-8 marked their exits with a last-shift marker giving a shift code, an hour, and the method used to leave. Examples survive on doorframes in Sector 44, including the Shallow Annex P-63 area, in tape and in scratched paint.

Deep Reach recovery lists classify these markers as personal effects, non-recoverable, which excludes them from the safety record. Marauder crews working the sector transcribe them before disturbing a room, treating a stated hour as a tide window and a stated method as a route with a known outcome.
