# Receiver Handshake Schedule RH-4

Receiver handshake schedules issued by the Recovery Compliance Office time the Black Keel's automated claim-tender windows. Cargo tender windows run three times daily with tonnage credited at close; recovery assist is scheduled as a displacing window that consumes the next cargo tender and forfeits its credit.

The stated basis is finite receiver time and attribution of displacement cost. Forfeit is assessed per request rather than per tonne. Punch tallies on 2147-era tender boards record recovery assist requests in the dozens.
