# Pavel Sorn Procedure Hold

Pavel Sorn Procedure Hold is the bureaucratic horror article for HECTON-8. It shows how a real oceanic catastrophe became worse when Deep Reach procedure treated evacuation as a permission problem. Sorn is not written as a grand conspirator; he is more useful than that. He is the person who can read a flooding report, recognize the names attached to it, and still file the release under pending external certification because the contract language has trained him to fear the wrong consequence. For players, this entry frames the colony collapse as a chain of small lawful refusals: no instant rescue, no clean voice to blame on the line, only people and systems turning delay into death.

## Scanner

Pavel Sorn did not order a massacre. He did something easier to survive on paper: he kept the evacuation release in the pending tray.
