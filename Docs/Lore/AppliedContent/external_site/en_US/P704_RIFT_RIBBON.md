# Rift Ribbon

Survey crews working the thermal fissure lines at around 4,180 metres, roughly 41.8 megapascals, report a strap-form organism that colonises only the immediate margin of open fissures, within about twelve metres of a vertical gap. Because fissure walls return little acoustic energy and warm outflow refracts an outgoing ping upward, plotted floor across these margins is frequently continuous where the floor is not.

Practice on HECTON-8 is to treat the growth line as the boundary and the lean angle of the blades as an outflow reading, with blades flattened and streaming taken as warning of a rising thermal surge. Deep Reach records for Sector 44 classify the species as an uncontracted boundary indicator and record survey 44-T-7 as cancelled with instrument spend recovered.
