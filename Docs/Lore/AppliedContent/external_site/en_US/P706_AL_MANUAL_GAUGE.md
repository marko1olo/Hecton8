# Wrist Count Against The Board

Suit alarm boards report whether a reading falls inside a calibration band rather than reporting the reading. Where the band has been widened, the display remains nominal across an excursion, a condition Deep Reach records describe as calibration drift.

HECTON-8 salvage practice is to record a manually counted pulse at the glove seam alongside the printed telemetry and to keep both figures in the log. Deep Reach exception files from Sector 44 note holder annotations in ink as not admitted, with the telemetry record closed and no action taken.
