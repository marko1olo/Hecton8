# Pressure Ladder Acoustic Chart

HECTON-8 is built vertically. The ocean is a ladder of pressure and chemistry: photic shelf, cable reef, brine canyon, machine field and Atlas Basin. Each band changes what the player can see, hear, trust and repair. A deeper route means harsher seals, worse return timing, stranger buoyancy and evidence that arrives through damaged instruments.

Radio fails early because the mineral ocean eats electromagnetic signal. Sound remains useful, but the water column reshapes it. Dense brine can trap a pulse and carry it sideways. Vent heat can tear it apart. Cable reefs can bounce a signal until a corridor looks open. A sonar return is a claim, not proof, until pressure, line tension, motion and a second ping agree.
