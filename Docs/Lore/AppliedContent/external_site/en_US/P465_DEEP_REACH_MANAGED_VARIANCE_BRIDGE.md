# Deep Reach Managed Variance

## What the record proves

Deep Reach calls a flooded repair margin managed variance. The waterline, missing route stamp, warped P-63 collar, and black-box splinter carry more truth than the clean word nominal.

DEEP REACH EXTRATERRESTRIAL DEVELOPMENT COMBINE
RECOVERY COMPLIANCE OFFICE / LANGUAGE NORMALIZATION NOTE

Use managed variance when a measured condition remains reportable under a retained service class. Use nominal when the tolerance plate remains legally current. Use deferred replacement when material exchange is delayed by route certification, custody hold, or quarantine review.

Do not attach worker status, room waterline, route-stamp absence, or non-certified field repair to the primary variance line unless external review asks for it.

## How to read it

The notice says nominal tolerance. The room answers with salt, knee-water, a deformed collar, and a route stamp that never arrived.

When Deep Reach writes nominal, read the metal first. Paper survives audits; pressure leaves marks.

This is the first Deep Reach trick worth teaching: the lie does not need to sound evil. It sounds tidy. It is formatted, retained, exportable, and calm enough to cross a relay delay.

A scavenger who reads only the notice learns the wrong lesson. A scavenger who reads the notice beside the wet collar learns how HECTON-8 speaks back. The colony's floor keeps receipts in salt, scratched paint, and metal that bent before the memo did.
