# Ibarra Loss Conversion Ledger

## Accounting That Survived The Colony

The Ibarra ledger should feel colder than a missing-person list. A list mourns, even when it lies. A ledger converts. It receives damaged habitat modules, tool banks, sample custody, missing personnel and salvage rights, then places each fact into a category that can keep moving through contracts after HECTON-8 has gone silent.

This is the bridge between old Deep Reach liability and the player's present debt. If people become unresolved personnel load, the corporation can admit they are gone without naming a murder. If evidence becomes material custody, it can be bought, buried, routed or insured. If the claim remains continuous, ownership outlives the workers who made the place function.

The ledger gives the player a hard kind of power. Carrying it upward does not only reveal what happened. It carries something the market already knows how to price. The choice to sell, publish, redirect or bury evidence becomes ugly because the document was designed to make every choice legible to somebody's balance sheet.
