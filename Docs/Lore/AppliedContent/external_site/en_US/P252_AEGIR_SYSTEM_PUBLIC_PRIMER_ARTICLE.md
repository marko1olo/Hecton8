# Aegir System Public Primer

Aegir makes the sky practical. The system around HECTON-8 is a working arrangement of moons, windows, radiation weather, relay shadow, ice scatter and carrier transfer geometry, and every one of those sets a price.

The asymmetry runs through all of it. A message clears a narrow signal window in an hour when a recovery body remains impossible. A sample can be valuable and still miss quarantine timing by two days. The Black Keel can be in-system, holding a claim open, and still not be above a diver in any way that helps.

The effects are physical rather than theoretical. A signal clears and the lift does not. Storm noise corrupts a relay. A moon shadow turns a working uplink into a dead slot for the length of a window. A tide window decides whether a canyon route is survivable this shift or next.

The wider ladder is not decoration either. H1 Skarn through H9 Mute carry relay roles, cold claims, dead beacons, traffic scars and quarantine pressure. HECTON-8 is the drowned one. The rest of the ladder is why rescue is expensive, salvage is legal, and accountability arrives stale.
