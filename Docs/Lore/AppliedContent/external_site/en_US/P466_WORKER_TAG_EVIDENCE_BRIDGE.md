# Worker Tag Evidence Chain

## What the record proves

A worker tag is not proof by itself. It becomes hard to erase only when shift slate, route stamp, damage state, witness hash, and receiver path stay attached.

WORKER RECORD RECOVERY / LOCAL TRAY NOTE

A name without custody becomes inventory noise. Preserve the tag with its shift slate, route stamp, damage state, witness hash, receiver path, and room context. If Black Keel or Keelmark receives only the badge number, the worker can be converted into a pending cost line.

If the tray is contaminated, bag the tray. If the slate is corroded, record the corrosion. Do not polish the proof into a cleaner lie.

## How to read it

Loose badge: weak. Badge plus shift slate, route stamp, damage mark, witness hash, and non-claim receiver path: evidence.

Do not pocket the name and cut the room apart. Preserve the tray, the slate, and the ugly route around it.

HECTON-8 makes names fragile on purpose. Not because names are unimportant, but because a name alone is exactly what corporate paperwork knows how to digest.

The worker tag teaches a salvage habit: do not separate the human mark from the ugly evidence around it. The ugly part is what keeps the person from becoming a line item.
