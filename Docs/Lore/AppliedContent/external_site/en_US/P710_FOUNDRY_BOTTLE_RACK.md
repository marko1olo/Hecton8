# Bottle Rack, Foundry Floor

Closed-loop diving on HECTON-8 pairs a certified bottle or a receiver-fed transfer line with a CO2 scrubber bed. The bed removes carbon dioxide and does not generate oxygen, so bed count sets how many divers a rack can support regardless of stored volume.

Consumables reconciliation 44-C-0770 records media issued to the rack rather than to individual holders. The rack recovered from the 2,340 metre foundry floor holds four certified bottles, two vented and two charged at 480 minutes each, with a sealed media case tagged for two beds. The air technician's final pencil column on the tag is kept in minutes and reconciles against the tag total.
