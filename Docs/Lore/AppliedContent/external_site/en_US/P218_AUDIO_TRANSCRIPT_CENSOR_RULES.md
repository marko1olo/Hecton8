# Audio Transcript Damage Bands

A HECTON-8 voice log is not complete just because it has sound. The transcript has to show its wounds.

Damage bands are the layer that makes those wounds honest. They can mark static, packet loss, legal redaction, pressure distortion, bad speaker separation, failed machine translation or a later edit. Without that layer, every broken recording becomes either fake clarity or fake mystery. With it, the player can see the difference between what the world lost and what someone chose to remove.

The rule is strict because the game depends on evidence. Damage can hide names, serials and spoiler payloads. It can create suspicion. It can make a route feel dangerous before the player sees the door. But the remaining transcript must still carry the action: do not open the lock, return by the service ladder, count oxygen manually, ignore the green status lamp, leave the official line visible.

For localization and accessibility, the band is part of the content, not an afterthought. A translated transcript must preserve speaker role, timing pressure and readable warnings. Redaction is atmosphere only when the player can still act. Otherwise it is just a broken interface pretending to be lore.
