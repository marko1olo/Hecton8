# Domain Marks 2190

By 2190 one broken object can have several legal parents. A pressure latch recovered from HECTON-8 may be Sol-certified, repaired under Barnard Yards authority, routed through Luyten Packet Ladder custody, justified in Centauri charter language and seized under an Aegir claim shell. The marks did not make the colony safe. They made it documented.

The order of precedence is what matters in a dispute. Sol Core safety format is accepted everywhere and enforced nowhere at this distance. Barnard repair authority stays valid on dead-claim work. Luyten custody attaches to a packet regardless of what the packet proves. Aegir local claim law supersedes open salvage once a descent loss is recorded.

That is the shape of authority at a range where a reply takes years. Every domain can name the part. Only the local claim office can take it.
