# Two Accounts of the Crown Pin

Recovered HECTON-8 rigging paperwork occasionally preserves two field accounts of a single load test under one shift code. Where the accounts conflict, Deep Reach shell practice has been to hold both entries rather than schedule an inspection.

Item 44-S-118 concerns crown hoist pin 3 in Sector 44. One account records a test to 1.4 tonnes, held and serviceable; the second records an abort at 0.6 tonnes with shoulder deformation already present. Both carry shift code D-31 and the same date. The pin remains in the crown frame at the 1,880 metre datum, where hoist coverage for the sector terminates.
