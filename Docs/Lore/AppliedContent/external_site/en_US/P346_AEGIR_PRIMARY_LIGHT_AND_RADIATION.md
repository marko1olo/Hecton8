# Aegir Primary Light and Radiation

Aegir never reads as empty black. Its K-class primary gives the decks amber daylight, long shadows, and enough flux for exposed instruments to drift if nobody checks the belt count. The gas giant is the second clock: magnetosphere lanes harden, soften, and harden again as moons cross charge and shadow. Deep Reach sold those numbers as routine operating windows. Crews learned the practical version: a warm sky can still cook a cheap seal, mute a link, and price a rescue by the minute.
