# Sensor-Tagged Fauna

Sensor-Tagged Fauna prevents HECTON-8's biomechanical layer from becoming simple corruption fiction. The creatures are not magically converted into drones. They are animals and colonies that outlived monitoring hardware. The tag became socket, scar, growth ring, mineral blister or false eye because the ecosystem did what ecosystems do: it grew around damage.

This gives every signal ghost a history. A Marauder may hear a weak Deep Reach identifier from inside a shell grazer or see a biometal eye node blink from a body that ignores commands. The old corporation still leaves paperwork in flesh, but the flesh is not obedient. Atlas can exploit the signal, not own the animal. That keeps the horror sadder and stronger: industrial control tried to mark life, life survived, and the mark still reports to systems that no longer understand what they are hearing.
