# Cathedral Kelp

Deep-transit crews working the Aegir drop wall describe a single-holdfast growth form that carries stalk from roughly 1,240 metres, about 12.4 megapascals at the base, to within 380 metres of the surface, in stands several hundred metres across. Blade surfaces develop the same non-reflective film recorded on lamp housings and viewport frames, and return almost no light to a working lamp at any range.

Two consequences are reported consistently. Optical range inside a mature stand falls to a few metres. Acoustic range falls further, because gas-cored stalks scatter the outgoing ping and produce a return wall at about five metres with nothing readable beyond it. Crews inside a stand are correspondingly hard to locate from outside it, and recovery of an overdue diver from inside the mass has not been reported successful.

Deep Reach route records for Sector 44 classify the mature form as permanent navigation obstruction while retaining a transit line through one stand, with lamp and sonar performance inside the mass recorded as out of scope.
