# Lantern Drift Bloom

Lantern Drift Bloom is how HECTON-8 says bright is not safe. The bloom is not a decorative fog layer. It is a moving instrument panel made of living cells, current, vibration and appetite. In quiet water it sketches the shallow shelf better than a flashlight: eddies glow at their rim, cable loops appear as broken halos, pressure-glass shards throw thin blue cuts, and a safe channel can look like a river of stars. The trap is that the same light records disturbance. Start a cutter, cough a pump, ping sonar too often or kick a thruster in panic, and the bloom carries your position outward. Grazer ticks thin out. Filter-feeders turn. Something larger may follow the illuminated wake before the UI can name it. This is the article that keeps HECTON-8's beauty from becoming wallpaper. Good encounter design should let the player want the light, use the light, then fear what the light says about them. A bloom corridor can be the clearest way through a reef and still be the worst place to repair a noisy valve. It belongs beside glass grazers and predator shadows because all three teach the same rule: the ocean is readable, and it is also reading you.
