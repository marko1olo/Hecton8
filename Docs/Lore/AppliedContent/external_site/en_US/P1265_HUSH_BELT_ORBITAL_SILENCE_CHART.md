# Hush Belt Orbital Silence Chart

HECTON-8 is close enough for orbit to matter and far enough for rescue to fail. Black Keel can pass overhead while the Marauder below still has no usable channel. The Hush Belt names that contradiction: an overlap of Aegir magnetic weather, Kestrel occultation, conductive ocean chemistry and Deep Reach relay custody.

Radio dies first. Lasers need clean line of sight through storm, plume ice and glare. Acoustic telemetry survives, but it is slow, narrow and owned. Every useful packet climbs by buoy, buffer and receipt. The result is a cruel kind of realism: silence does not mean nobody is there. It means the system is working exactly as designed.
