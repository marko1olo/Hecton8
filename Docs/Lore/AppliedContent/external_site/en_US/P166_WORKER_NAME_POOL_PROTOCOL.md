# Worker Name Pool Record

The Worker Name Pool Record defines how HECTON-8 turns seeded evidence into human continuity. Each worker identity is built from authored names, domain-origin bands, pressure labor roles, badge fragments, route permissions and shift marks. The goal is not random flavor. The goal is readable responsibility: who could open a hatch, who serviced a pump, who was denied medical release, and whose trace a later Marauder is about to disturb.

The system keeps personal identity stable while allowing object placement and evidence pairings to vary. This supports replay without contradiction. A worker can surface through a locker, a job board, a route card or a manifest, but the article always protects the same principle: people are not procedurally disposable just because the room is seedable.
