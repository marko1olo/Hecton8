# Muster Two Queue Log

A muster-station record from Sector 44 documents printed evacuation route cards continuing to be issued after the routed corridor had flooded. The correction was passed by voice at the head of the queue because the station line to the print shop was out and the printing plate was never altered.

Roughly nine hundred cards remain in the box, all naming the same corridor. Deep Reach filed the matter under document control as a minor finding, with the station's issue tally left blank.
