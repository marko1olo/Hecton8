# Centauri Charter Legitimacy

## What the record proves

The Centauri Compact made old colony charters lawful. Deep Reach later wore that civic language like a clean suit over a drowned room.

CENTAURI COMPACT CHARTER MIRROR / PUBLIC LEGITIMACY EXPORT

Retain charter seal, audit seal, welfare clause, continuity clause, and contractor reference. Do not describe the document as forged. Its power comes from being real.

The colony charter did not drown the room by itself. It supplied a lawful doorway through which Deep Reach could move worker welfare, claim continuity, and Black Keel contracting into the same clean file.

## How to read it

Charter seal genuine. Audit seal genuine. Black Keel contract genuine. The problem is what the lawful words were used to protect.

A respectable seal can be real and still be dirty. Read who benefits when welfare becomes continuity.

This packet matters because it prevents a cheap villain shape. HECTON-8 is not built on fake law and cartoon corruption. It is built on real seals used past their moral limit.

The player should feel the discomfort of legitimacy: the paper is not fake, the audit is not invented, and that is exactly why the drowned room is harder to argue against.
