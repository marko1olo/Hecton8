# Ran Long Claim Transit

Ran Long Claim is the public name for a practical contradiction. Human civilization can reach Aegir. It can send probes, staged freight, packet custody and claim tenders into the system. It can put Deep Reach assets on HECTON-8 and keep Black Keel working above the moon. None of that means a stranded Marauder can be lifted when they ask.

The route is built in layers. Packets move first because information is light and legally useful. Fuel depots, receiver reserves and quarantine slots move slower. Heavy freight moves slower still, wrapped in contracts that decide who pays for braking, who owns the payload and what risk is allowed through a recovery window. A signal may cross the human sphere while a body remains trapped under tide weather and a four-tonne lien.

That is why the route plate matters. It does not say no one is listening. It says listening is the cheapest part of rescue. Black Keel can acknowledge a distress hash, open a file, reserve a tentative tonne-window and still leave the player underwater until mass, quarantine and claimant priority stop fighting each other. Ran Long Claim makes the frontier feel inhabited, expensive and indifferent rather than empty.
