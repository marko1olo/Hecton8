# Blood Kelp

Deep water survey on HECTON-8 records a red-pigmented anchored macroflora forming discrete stands on hard substrate between roughly 1,400 and 2,600 m. The pigment absorbs the blue-green band standard work lamps are weighted for, so a stand returns almost nothing to a lamp cone and resolves only at contact range, while amber emergency filters render it clearly.

Contact triggers photophore discharge across the stand, persisting on the order of ninety seconds. Salvage crews treat the resulting light as a disclosure of position rather than an aid. Deep Reach harvest records from Sector 44 list the growth as illumination feedstock and retain a non-hazard vegetation classification alongside four logged entanglement events.
