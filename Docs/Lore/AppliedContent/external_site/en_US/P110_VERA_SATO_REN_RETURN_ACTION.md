# Vera Sato-Ren Return Action

Vera Sato-Ren represents the Recovery Compliance Office in 2190, and she is the reason Deep Reach does not need to stand in the room to exert force. The office sends return-action packets.

A packet asks for coordinates, XO proof chain, Atlas access state and contractor silence within a custody window. The shape carries the pressure. Where the payload is recoverable, Deep Reach can offer rescue, legal cover or carrier access. Where evidence exposure grows, the same office can convert the contractor into a liability entry.

That keeps the faction grounded in procedure. Deep Reach in 2190 is dangerous because it is solvent, patient and still filing: recovery before rescue, custody before truth. The drowned colony is not only a grave in these papers. It is an active claim site, and every recovered record becomes proof, ransom, silence clause or bargaining weight depending on which desk receives it first. Sato-Ren's signature is the only one that appears on all four outcomes.
