# Lantern Sifter

Reports from the HECTON-8 cable reefs describe a colonial bioluminescent filter feeder that re-radiates incident sonar. Crews consistently record two returns from a single pulse, the second delayed by roughly two tenths of a second and displaced off the true bearing, with amplitude higher than range would predict.

Dissection reports a single dense mineral inclusion in each animal's gill basket, consistent with trace-class continuity substrate rather than biological mineralisation. Deep Reach records from Sector 44 carry the species as an inventory line for grown acoustic repeaters, with no service interval recorded on the grounds that the units replace themselves.
