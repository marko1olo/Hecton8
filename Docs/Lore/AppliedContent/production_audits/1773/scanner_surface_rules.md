# Scanner Surface Rules - 1773

Evidence class: STATIC_DOC.

## Bright Surface / Shallows Allowed

Use surface, coast, Aegir, moonlight, ocean skin, and photic-shelf wording as bright, readable, beautiful, and operational. Shallow danger comes from weather, oxygen, current, predator movement, tool noise, unstable wreckage, and false confidence, not from permanent gloom.

Allowed examples:

- `Bright mat oxygen-positive. Harvest loose edge only; sealed seams may be holding pressure.`
- `Glass grazer school active. Low immediate predator signal; if movement freezes, leave open water.`
- `Lantern drift improves visibility but may attract predator attention. Verify current before following.`
- `Aegir light clean enough for surface route scan; storm window may break relay timing.`
- `Ocean skin readable. Foam and current show capsule debris path.`

Forbidden surface/shallow framing:

- Surface is inherently dead, black, ugly, or permanently noir.
- Shallows are safe aquarium scenery with no pressure, oxygen, route, or salvage consequence.
- Darkness/fog hides weak art or weak route description.
- Scanner line says the moon is doomed, cursed, evil, or secretly understood before evidence exists.

## Deep / Dark Allowed

Use darkness for depth, caves, interiors, storms, eclipse windows, brine distortion, abyssal exposure, and pressure events. Even deep darkness must preserve route structure, silhouettes, instruments, and evidence.

Allowed examples:

- `Abyssal silt storm. Route marker intermittent; follow pinger return, not open glow.`
- `Deep cave: low light, high pressure, clean sonar gap. Retreat if fauna movement stops.`
- `Brine layer detected. False floor likely; descend along vane edge.`
- `Interior pressure loss. Hatch seal sweating; patch before cutting panel.`
- `Eclipse window: light loss temporary, relay delay rising.`

Forbidden deep framing:

- Empty black water with no route, signal, silhouette, or player action.
- Generic horror language replacing physical evidence.
- Omniscient Atlas/Deep Reach truth before scan stage supports it.
- Lore essay in scanner popup.

## Hardware Scaling Consequence

- Low: keep scanner copy shortest, strongest action words, and high-contrast UI fit.
- Middle: allow normal scanner plus one clear hazard/use hook.
- High: allow richer supporting codex/field note after scan, not longer scanner popups.
- Ultra: add optional archive/codex density around the same truth; scanner facts and gameplay instruction stay identical.
