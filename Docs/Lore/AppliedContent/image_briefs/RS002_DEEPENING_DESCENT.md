# RS002 Deepening Descent Image Brief

Status: production-facing art brief.
Use: in-game wiki cards, scanner thumbnails, external site article headers, localization-safe marketing crops.

## Shared Visual Rules

- NASA-punk industrial realism first: pressure hulls, utility paint, service labels, worn composites, exposed routing.
- Ocean readability first: clear silhouettes, believable water layers, particulate depth, pressure lighting.
- No fantasy magic glow. Blue debt can emit cold blue-white pressure artifacts, but it must feel contained, industrial, and hazardous.
- No clean horror monster framing. Atlas horror is repair logic with bad categories: practical, precise, and wrong.
- Crops must survive at 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, and narrow mobile card ratios.

## P006 Dead Claim Cache

Scene:
Mid-depth service canyon. A small private cache is wedged under a pipe saddle, marked with scratched Barnard-style route symbols and non-corporate salvage paint. Cut tethers drift nearby.

Required details:
- Hand-packed tool roll, pressure tape, cutter cells, corroded route slate.
- One old personal tag, not enough to identify a face.
- Black water gap behind it, implying the previous owner never returned.

Avoid:
- Treasure chest framing.
- Clean loot-box UI language.
- Heroic adventurer composition.

## P007 Brine Stair

Scene:
Layered ocean shelf where a submersible hangs above a dense brine boundary. Sonar pings bend visually through the layer; vents below cast slow heat distortion.

Required details:
- Clear density boundary like a second horizon under water.
- Ballast station or old Deep Reach measuring mast.
- Tiny scale marker so the route feels navigational, not abstract.

Avoid:
- Pure blue gradient.
- Space nebula look.
- Magical portal read.

## P008 Evacuation Hold

Scene:
Flooded emergency transit gate in a deep colony corridor. The gate is still locked. Status panels show route states, not gore.

Required details:
- Four priority lanes: evacuation, medical, substrate, Atlas continuity.
- Human-scale signs, pressure buckling, sealed medical cart.
- Evidence of delay: frozen queue markers, old suit clamps, locked manual wheel.

Avoid:
- Slasher scene.
- Bodies as the main image.
- Villain-logo overstatement.

## P009 Bottom Factory

Scene:
Vast factory-ship-temple on the seabed. Industrial ribs, living pressure membrane, cable roots, and Atlas conduits share one structure.

Required details:
- Factory scale: cranes, ducts, hull ribs, pump organs.
- Living membrane must be biological but not soft fantasy flesh.
- One readable route aperture for gameplay orientation.

Avoid:
- Gothic cathedral tropes without industrial function.
- Alien hive cliche.
- Smooth sci-fi cleanliness.

## P010 Payload Window

Scene:
Improvised ascent package in a launch floodgate, pointed upward into a dark water column. External storm/radiation/orbital timing is implied through UI marks and carrier geometry.

Required details:
- Limited payload rack with visible hard slots.
- Evidence containers competing with resource canisters.
- Black Keel window marker or distant carrier telemetry, not a visible rescue ship overhead.

Avoid:
- Triumphant rocket launch.
- Clean victory poster.
- Abstract ending-choice iconography.
