# RS008 - Escape / Endings / Atlas Question

Status: production-facing art brief.

Purpose: image/card references for the escape-chain, false exits, material payout, Deep Reach cleanup pressure, and final Atlas ethics.

Packets:
- P036_RETURN_VECTOR_WINDOW: orbital timing slate, Black Keel high over Aegir, storm gaps and relay geometry. No magic rescue UI.
- P037_COWARD_EXIT_CHAIN: repaired ascent cradle in bad weather, functional but emotionally empty. Must not look triumphant.
- P038_MATERIAL_PAYOUT_LEDGER: blue debt sample in custody case, salvage accounting, cold payout terminal. Wealth without truth.
- P039_DEEP_REACH_CLEANSE_ORDER: redacted proxy order, deniable contractor language, cleanup priority stack. Corporate pressure in present tense.
- P040_ATLAS_FINAL_ARGUMENT: factory-temple choice interface where ocean tissue, industrial iron and Atlas routing share one body.

Visual constraints:
- Escape is engineering, timing and debt, not heroic evacuation.
- False success must be materially real and morally thin.
- Deep Reach screens should feel procedural, not villain-theatrical.
- Atlas final imagery must ask a question; avoid boss-core clichés.
