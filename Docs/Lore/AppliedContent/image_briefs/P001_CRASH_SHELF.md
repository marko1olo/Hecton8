# P001 Crash Shelf - Image Briefs

## In-Game Wiki Image

Subject:
Damaged bathy-drop shell on a bright shallow alien shelf.

Composition:
Low camera near broken capsule hull, visible intact crush frame, cracked uplink panel, scattered heat-shield ceramic, colorful reef life beyond.

Mood:
Hard sci-fi survival with beauty in the water. Not dark fantasy, not horror-only.

Must Show:

- damaged industrial descent capsule;
- bright HECTON-8 shallow ecology;
- storm haze / Aegir light in sky or reflection;
- readable scale for human operator.

Must Avoid:

- magic glow;
- sleek luxury ship;
- clean tropical vacation look;
- generic crashed spaceship silhouette.

## Website Hero

Wide landscape.
Broken bathy-drop in foreground, alien shallow shelf and storm-lit horizon, Aegir presence implied by light/color. The image should sell the core contrast: beautiful ocean, hostile rescue state.

## Scanner Thumbnail

Close technical image of cracked ascent package and intact pressure frame. More diagrammatic, less cinematic.
