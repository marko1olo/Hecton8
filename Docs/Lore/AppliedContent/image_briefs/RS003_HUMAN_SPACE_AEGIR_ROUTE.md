# RS003 Human Space / Aegir Route Image Brief

Status: production-facing art brief.
Use: in-game wiki cards, scanner thumbnails, external site headers, route-map plates, localization-safe marketing crops.

## Shared Visual Rules

- Hard sci-fi infrastructure over sleek fantasy: labels, mass, heat shielding, service scars, routing marks.
- Human scale must remain visible through hands, clamps, stamps, signs, old tools, and worn panels.
- Distance should feel physical: delayed packets, big hardware, cold schedules, orbit windows.
- Avoid heroic starship freedom. Ships are contracts, cargo authority, timing, and debt.
- Crops must survive 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, and narrow mobile cards.

## P011 Domain Marks 2190

Scene:
Close-up cluster of HECTON-8 hardware with multiple domain marks: Sol safety plate, Barnard repair stamp, Luyten packet label, Aegir claim serial.

Required details:
- Worn but legible multi-domain tags.
- One repaired tool or panel showing Barnard field culture.
- Water damage and salt creep tying offworld history to local ruin.

Avoid:
- Political map infographic as primary image.
- Clean museum display.
- Flag-wall composition.

## P012 Relay Spine

Scene:
Flooded relay buffer with spooled packet cartridges and beam schedule display, still blinking through corrosion.

Required details:
- Delayed packet timestamps.
- Route custody seals.
- A warning that the system is operational but too slow for rescue.

Avoid:
- Magical communication beam.
- Instant hologram call.
- Broken-radio cliché as the only idea.

## P013 Nearlight Ship Classes

Scene:
Transit manifest wall or old route schematic showing silhouettes of needleprobe, seed ship, mass carrier, sleeper transport, system carrier, and transfer tug.

Required details:
- Scale contrast between small probes and heavy carriers.
- No FTL symbols.
- Launch/braking infrastructure visible as part of the route, not optional decoration.

Avoid:
- Sleek personal starfighter.
- Warp tunnel imagery.
- Starship glamour poster.

## P014 Aegir Route Window

Scene:
HECTON-8 surface or shallow storm break with Aegir visible, moon shadow crossing, route-window UI reflected on wet equipment.

Required details:
- Gas giant as a real sky presence.
- Window markers for comm, tide, radiation, carrier geometry.
- Weather and orbit interacting in the same image.

Avoid:
- Pure wallpaper planet shot.
- Abstract astrology diagram.
- Per-frame simulation implication; this is authored timing.

## P015 Black Keel Ledger

Scene:
Carrier-side ledger interface over a dark orbital service bay: claim clearance, debt custody, payload authority, hidden priority route.

Required details:
- Black Keel as distant infrastructure, not a cozy ship interior.
- Payload slots and claim ledger lines.
- One masked Deep Reach priority marker, subtle but readable.

Avoid:
- Villain command bridge.
- Friendly crew hub.
- Personal spaceship fantasy.
