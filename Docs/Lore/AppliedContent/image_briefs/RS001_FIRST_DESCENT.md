# RS001 First Descent - Image Briefs

Status: production-facing image direction.
Purpose: image prompts for in-game wiki cards, external site articles, scanner thumbnails, and future marketing fragments.

## Shared Visual Rules

- NASA-punk / deep sea noir.
- Real physical materials: pressure glass, scratched labels, bolts, seals, condensation, salt, damaged polymer, ceramic, cable.
- HECTON-8 shallows can be bright and beautiful.
- Horror should enter through function, not monster posing.
- No fantasy magic glow.
- No sleek luxury spaceships.
- No generic blue sci-fi UI as the main image.

## P001 Crash Shelf

In-Game Wiki:
Damaged bathy-drop shell on a bright alien shallow shelf. Intact crush frame visible, cracked uplink panel, scattered heat-shield ceramic, colorful reef life beyond.

Website Hero:
Wide shallow ocean crash scene with storm haze and Aegir light. The image should sell beauty plus hostile rescue state.

Scanner Thumbnail:
Technical close-up of cracked ascent package and intact pressure frame.

## P002 Black Keel Contact

In-Game Wiki:
Old relay mast rising from storm shallows, corroded but still aimed at sky. Broken status lights, Aegir glow through clouds, cable leading into water.

Website Hero:
Player-scale signal mast under immense sky, Aegir and moon geometry implied. No visible friendly ship. The sky should feel like machinery.

Scanner Thumbnail:
Salted relay connector, cracked antenna hinge, weak signal diode.

## P003 Barnard Mark

In-Game Wiki:
Close view of a worn pressure wrench with Barnard Yards stamp inside a flooded worker locker. Background should show ordinary colony life: name tag, meal tin, patched suit lining.

Website Hero:
Drowned worker room with mundane human objects, not gore. The emotional hook is recognition and absence.

Scanner Thumbnail:
Tool stamp, cheap alloy wear, cut pattern.

## P004 Blue Debt

In-Game Wiki:
Sealed pressure casket in industrial descent zone. Frost/pressure skin, warning labels, restrained blue-gray material visible through pressure glass.

Website Hero:
Deep industrial processing scar with pressure containers and cable growth. Resource should look valuable and dangerous, not magical.

Scanner Thumbnail:
Leaking casket seal, pressure gauge, warning stencil.

## P005 Repair Scar

In-Game Wiki:
Station wall patched with cable, coral-like growth, industrial resin, and unsettling biological material. The repair should look purposeful and functional.

Website Hero:
Wide view of corridor where machine and ocean life merge into a working repair surface. Horror through order and maintenance, not random gore.

Scanner Thumbnail:
Layered sealant cross-section: polymer, coral protein, processed tissue, cable strands.

## Negative Prompt Notes

Avoid:

- tentacle-monster composition;
- clean cyberpunk neon;
- skull piles;
- magic crystals;
- spaceships hovering over the ocean like fantasy rescue;
- generic underwater ruins without readable industrial function.
