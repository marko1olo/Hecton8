# RS007 - Depth Ecology / Factory-Temple

Status: production-facing art brief.

Purpose: image/card references for depth progression, ecological contrast, cable reefs, repair fauna, and bottom factory threshold.

Packets:
- P031_PHOTIC_SHELF_LIFE: bright shallow shelf, transparent grazers, living mats, readable shelter/capsule silhouette in the distance. Must not look cozy or aquarium-safe.
- P032_PRESSURE_LADDER_DEPTH_BANDS: pressure bulkhead / suit rating panel with depth bands, seals, scratched gauges and route math. No magical UI.
- P033_CABLE_REEF_SYMBIOSIS: mid-depth service trench where algae/root mats wrap power trunks and signal cables. Useful route cue plus drone risk.
- P034_ABYSSAL_REPAIR_FAUNA: altered fauna specimen with ceramic patch, cable scar, machine-clean wound edge. It must still read as animal under industrial repair.
- P035_FACTORY_TEMPLE_THRESHOLD: bottom truss gate with living pressure seals and industrial tissue. Feels like factory hull, shrine logic and body, not a boss arena.

Visual constraints:
- Shallows: color and life are allowed, but use them as contrast under pressure.
- Mid-depth: cable/flora merge must be readable as route and evidence.
- Abyss: horror is repair logic, not monster glamour.
- Bottom: industrial iron and living tissue share load-bearing structure.
