# HECTON-8 Field Atlas

Status: working world atlas.
Purpose: define player-facing depth zones, field identity, resources, hazards, and lore hooks for procedural placement.

This atlas is not a simulator spec. It is the world shape that generation, level design, scanner content, and website/wiki writing should preserve.

## Design Rule

Depth is not a number. Depth is a change in cost, color, sound, salvage, pressure, and truth.

Each zone should provide:

- a visual identity;
- a survival pressure;
- a salvage/resource reason to be there;
- a biological identity;
- at least one human trace;
- at least one false or incomplete interpretation;
- a deeper question.

## Z01 - Storm Shelf

Approx Depth:
0 to 80 m.

Visual Identity:
Bright water, storm light, broken surface foam, exposed reef, capsule debris, drifting plant mats, Aegir glow through weather.

Player Actions:
Seal the bathy-drop, recover first tools, scan safe life, build first shelter, repair weak uplink, learn currents.

Resources:
Heat-shield ceramic, battery slabs, emergency foam, shallow fibers, edible/oxygen biota, copper scraps, buoy parts.

Hazards:
Weather surge, low oxygen, electrical shorts, small predators, visibility loss, unstable wreckage.

Fauna/Flora:
Bladderfish clouds, Shore Skimmers, Kelp Raylets, Halo Sargassum, Tubular Coral.

Human Trace:
Bathy-drop wreck, old buoy, aquaculture frame, Deep Reach public marker.

Truth Hook:
The moon is alive and beautiful. The player is not trapped in a dead ruin.

False Read:
This looks like a normal salvage accident.

## Z02 - Drowned Shelf

Approx Depth:
80 to 300 m.

Visual Identity:
Blue-green gloom, flooded access tubes, aquaculture grids, low industrial silhouettes, colony edge modules, swaying cables.

Player Actions:
Enter first human rooms, restore partial power, cut doors, siphon oxygen, recover records, open service routes.

Resources:
Tools, seals, oxygen pockets, pipe metals, colony stores, first stable data cores.

Hazards:
Pressure transitions, sealed rooms, trapped gas, contaminated water pockets, timid but territorial fauna.

Fauna/Flora:
Wall Gliders, Silt Drifters, Ghost Weed, early Blood Kelp, filter mats on old pipe grids.

Human Trace:
Worker lockers, mess heaters, shift boards, pressure bunks, child marks if used sparingly.

Truth Hook:
The colony was people before it became evidence.

False Read:
Deep Reach records still look orderly enough to believe.

## Z03 - Service Canyons

Approx Depth:
300 to 800 m.

Visual Identity:
Steeper terrain, service trenches, pipeline shadows, tram wrecks, exposed rock ribs, early darkness between bright biological pockets.

Player Actions:
Follow power lines, repair route locks, find Marauder caches, start needing depth-specific upgrades, choose safer long route or risky shortcut.

Resources:
Cable, pressure valves, tram batteries, old route markers, salvage caches, conductive biota.

Hazards:
Current funnels, cable snags, ambush pockets, old machinery, poor sonar returns.

Fauna/Flora:
Cold Veil Eels, Pocket Ambushers, Iron Minnows, Rift Ribbon, cable-fed mats.

Human Trace:
Maintenance stations, tram authority terminals, dead claim buoys, first unofficial Marauder marks.

Truth Hook:
Other professionals came after the disaster. Some knew enough to hide caches. Some did not leave.

False Read:
The danger still looks like normal deep salvage.

## Z04 - Cable Forest

Approx Depth:
800 to 1500 m.

Visual Identity:
Dense cable growth, biota on lines, dim lamps, pipe organs of metal and kelp, maintenance drones at distance, station walls becoming reef.

Player Actions:
Navigate living cable, cut route knots, bait drones, harvest conductive material, detect first Atlas repair patterns.

Resources:
Conductive kelp, cable bundles, relay cores, insulation resin, small blue debt traces.

Hazards:
Drone response, false safe paths, electrical discharge, organisms using cable vibration, signal ghosts.

Fauna/Flora:
Cable Kelp, Static Thicket, Lantern Grass, Wall Gliders, Brine Siphoner juveniles.

Human Trace:
Relay junctions, pipe labels, power routing terminals, repair requests never closed.

Truth Hook:
Life is adapting to industry, and industry is starting to behave like life.

False Read:
Maybe the ecosystem simply colonized abandoned infrastructure.

## Z05 - Industrial Descent

Approx Depth:
1500 to 2800 m.

Visual Identity:
Mining scars, processor stacks, pressure caskets, broken intake towers, heavy machinery, dirty light, drone-maintained corridors.

Player Actions:
Recover blue debt samples, stabilize containers, unlock better depth tech, decide what to report to Black Keel, compare physical resource flow with corporate records.

Resources:
Blue debt, pressure glass, reactor ceramics, industrial catalysts, drone parts, sealed process logs.

Hazards:
Pressure container failure, drone nests, toxic process leaks, heavy predators drawn to heat and sound.

Fauna/Flora:
Brine Stalkers, Lantern Sifters, Iron Coral, Blood Kelp on pipes, contaminated filter organisms.

Human Trace:
Material tags, process locks, cargo rails, worker warning murals, sealed corporate labs.

Truth Hook:
HECTON-8 was valuable because Deep Reach was making and protecting something down here.

False Read:
Blue debt is just loot.

## Z06 - Brine Stairs

Approx Depth:
2800 to 3600 m.

Visual Identity:
Layered brine pools, density curtains, mineral terraces, blind drops, rippling boundaries that look like false floors.

Player Actions:
Use density layers to descend, hide, cool systems, route around pressure gates, recover preserved objects.

Resources:
Mineral salts, preserved records, rare pressure organisms, brine-stabilized samples.

Hazards:
Corrosion, wrong-density traps, sonar distortion, predators using layer boundaries, buoyancy failure.

Fauna/Flora:
Brine Siphoners, Glassworm adults, Lantern Sifters, mineral mat colonies.

Human Trace:
Collapsed descent routes, emergency ballast stations, missing evacuation pods.

Truth Hook:
The moon itself teaches descent. Better gear is not enough without reading the environment.

False Read:
The brine is just a natural obstacle.

## Z07 - Thermal Cathedral

Approx Depth:
3600 to 4500 m.

Visual Identity:
Vent towers, hot mineral bloom, black chimneys, living light, cathedral-like growths, machines half-buried in mineral skin.

Player Actions:
Harvest heat, recharge systems, craft pressure materials, enter altered labs, read Seed/Atlas precursor structures.

Resources:
Thermal mass, rare minerals, pressure lattice growth, high-energy containment parts, Atlas-compatible substrate.

Hazards:
Heat spikes, acid plumes, pressure shock, large fauna, drone-maintained vents, hallucination-like signal noise without making it magic.

Fauna/Flora:
Thermal Tubeworms, Cathedral Kelp, Iron Coral, Shadow Interceptors near heat shadows, altered filter bodies.

Human Trace:
Deep Reach thermal taps, Seed hull fragments, first factory-ship bones.

Truth Hook:
Natural geology and Deep Reach industry are fused here. The project was built into the moon's metabolism.

False Read:
Atlas is located "somewhere below" as a machine. In reality the machine is already around the player.

## Z08 - Deep Abyss Plain

Approx Depth:
4500 to 5000 m.

Visual Identity:
Low light, huge empty water, silt plains, broken silhouettes, pressure groans, route markers swallowed by sediment.

Player Actions:
Cross exposed dark, manage power and oxygen discipline, follow weak signals, recover evacuation truth, avoid or understand large predators.

Resources:
Deep salvage, evacuation black boxes, final route keys, old fuel/thermal stores, high-pressure samples.

Hazards:
Navigation loss, large predators, pressure fatigue, silt storms, signal misclassification, drone retrieval sweeps.

Fauna/Flora:
Shadow Interceptors, Brine Stalkers, Cold Veil Eels, deep filter mats, scarred animals with industrial inserts.

Human Trace:
Evacuation manifests, crushed transit routes, failed ascent equipment, sealed medical modules.

Truth Hook:
People did not simply drown. They were held, routed, delayed, and misclassified.

False Read:
The deep is empty.

## Z09 - Atlas Bottom Zone

Approx Depth:
5000 m and below local access shelves.

Visual Identity:
Factory-ship temple, industrial organs, processed seafloor, pressure arteries, drone rituals, tissue around hardware, metal grown into reef and reef grown into metal.

Player Actions:
Navigate living machinery, choose payload, expose or bury truth, interact with Atlas continuity logic, decide what leaves.

Resources:
Atlas-compatible substrate, final evidence, carrier-control leverage, living samples, destroyed human records, bottom-zone tech.

Hazards:
Pressure, signal dominance, drone coordination, misclassification, route collapse, moral payload traps.

Fauna/Flora:
Not normal species lists. Life appears as function: sealers, conductors, filters, carriers, skins, roots, mouths, patch material.

Human Trace:
Deep Reach hardware, colony remains, Atlas directive fragments, final evacuation records, fused factory-ship sections.

Truth Hook:
The bottom is not the base where the answer is stored. The bottom is the answer.

False Read:
The final problem is to destroy a hostile AI.

## Procedural Use

Generation should preserve zone identity even when geometry changes:

- storm shelf must stay bright and violent;
- drowned shelf must carry human texture;
- service canyons must teach route/salvage logic;
- cable forest must blur natural adaptation and repair;
- industrial descent must make blue debt valuable and suspicious;
- brine stairs must turn geology into traversal;
- thermal cathedral must make beauty and industry inseparable;
- abyss plain must create exposure and truth pressure;
- bottom zone must feel like factory, ship, temple, organ, and crime scene at once.

## Multilingual / Runtime Note

Zone names, scanner strings, POI labels, and codex bodies should split into localized text units. Runtime should consume baked zone IDs, POI tags, unlock flags, and LocID hashes.
