🧬 DOPZADANIE: PROTsEDURNYY GENERATOR «SAR_GASSUM_ULTRA»

Kontseptsiya:
Sargassovy vodorosli — eto ne trava na vode. Eto massivnye, zaputannye plavuchie «ostrova», imeyuschie obem, vnutrennyuyu strukturu i slozhnuyu fiziku. Oni dolzhny vyglyadet kak zolotisto-korichnevye kruzhevnye maty, kotorye dyshat vmeste s okeanom.

1. Strukturnaya Magiya (Algoritm vetvleniya)

Zabud pro odin mesh. My budem ispolzovat Stohasticheskuyu L-sistemu (Stochastic L-System) dlya sozdaniya kazhdogo «kusta»:

Glavnyy stebel: Imeet kriviznu, zavisyaschuyu ot «vozrasta» kusta.

Pnevmatotsisty (Air Bladders): Generator dolzhen rasstavlyat malenkie zolotistye puzyrki-poplavki v uzlah vetvleniya. V sheydere oni dolzhny imet Subsurface Scattering (SSS), chtoby solntse prosvechivalo ih naskvoz, kak malenkie fonariki.

Mikro-listya: Zubchatye, poluprozrachnye kraya, sozdayuschie «shumnyy» siluet.

2. Fizika «Zhivogo Kovra» (Island Clustering)

Sargassy dolzhny formirovat kolonii. My primenim algoritm metabolov (Metaballs logic) ili kletochnyh avtomatov dlya raspredeleniya:

Vodorosli prityagivayutsya drug k drugu, formiruya plotnye tsentry i izrezannye, «rvanye» kraya.

Collision Avoidance: Kogda igrok ili skuter vletaet v ostrov, kusty ne prosto «progibayutsya», oni dolzhny rastalkivatsya v storony (razryv kovra), a posle prohozhdeniya — medlenno dreyfovat obratno pod deystviem fiktivnogo prityazheniya.

3. Vizualnye Effekty «Wow-urovnya»

Verteksnaya animatsiya 2.0: Kazhdyy kust imeet svoyu fazu kachaniya, no oni vse podchinyayutsya globalnomu vektoru volny Crest.

Podvodnaya chast: Ostrov imeet «borodu» — svisayuschie vniz na 1.5–2 metra tonkie niti. Pri vzglyade snizu oni dolzhny sozdavat potryasayuschuyu igru sveta («luchi boga», probivayuschiesya skvoz putanitsu stebley).

Ekologicheskiy sled: Generator dolzhen spavnit vokrug ostrovov «morskoy sneg» (chastitsy detrita) i melkih poluprozrachnyh krevetok/rybok, kotorye zhivut tolko v etih zaroslyah.

DETALIZATsIYa MEShA «SAR_GASSUM_MONSTER»
My sozdaem ne «ploskuyu zaplatku», a mnogourovnevyy obemnyy sprut. Kazhdyy protsedurnyy ekzemplyar — eto slozhnaya ierarhiya elementov:
1. «Skeletnaya» struktura (Fractal Backbone)
Glavnyy Hrebet: Tsentralnyy gibkiy stebel s peremennoy tolschinoy (tolsche u osnovaniya, pochti nitevidnyy na kontsah). On ne pryamoy, a imeet «pamyat izgiba» — zakruchivaetsya v spirali, sozdavaya obemnyy karkas.
Vtorichnoe Vetvlenie: Ot glavnogo steblya pod uglom 45–60 gradusov othodyat bokovye pobegi. Oni perepletayutsya mezhdu soboy, formiruya strukturnuyu setku. Eto klyuchevoy moment: vetki dolzhny fizicheski peresekatsya v prostranstve, sozdavaya effekt zaputannogo «voronego gnezda».
2. «Listvennaya» massa (Serrated Foliage)
Morfologiya lista: Listya — eto uzkie, dlinnye lantsetnye formy s zubchatymi (pilchatymi) krayami. V protsedurnom meshe kraya dolzhny imet mikro-izlomy, chtoby pri osveschenii sozdavalis tysyachi melkih blikov (spekl-effekt).
Plotnost zapolneniya: My ispolzuem algoritm «oblaka tochek». Listya rastut puchkami po 3–5 shtuk iz kazhdogo uzla vetvleniya. V tsentre «ostrova» mesh maksimalno plotnyy (nulevaya vidimost skvoz nego), k krayam — razrezhennyy, raspadayuschiysya na otdelnye usiki.
Teksturnye variatsii: Chast listev dolzhna byt «uvyadshey» — skruchennoy i temnoy, chast — molodoy, pochti prozrachnoy na solntse.
3. Pnevmatotsisty (Buoyancy Berries)
Yantarnye puzyrki: Eto sotni melkih sfericheskih poplavkov, zakreplennyh u osnovaniy listev.
Vizualnyy aktsent: Oni — glavnye «lovtsy sveta». V meshe eto otdelnye melkie lou-poli sfery. V sheydere oni poluchayut moschnyy Backlighting (prosvet szadi). Kogda igrok nyryaet pod vodorosl i smotrit na solntse, on dolzhen videt tysyachi svetyaschihsya yantarnyh «yagod».
4. «Podvodnaya Boroda» (Abyssal Roots)
Nizhnyaya chast ostrova generiruet dlinnye (do 3–5 metrov), svisayuschie vertikalno vniz niti.
Dinamika: U etih nitey v sheydere propisana drugaya fizika kachaniya — oni inertny, medlenno kolyshutsya pod deystviem glubinnyh techeniy, v to vremya kak verhushka ostrova povtoryaet ritm poverhnosti Crest.
5. Parametry «Napolnennosti» (Fullness Logic)
Multi-Layering: Mesh stroitsya v 3–4 sloya.
Sloy 1 (Top): Zolotistyy, podsohshiy na solntse.
Sloy 2 (Vaterliniya): Samyy gustoy i sochnyy.
Sloy 3 (Pogruzhnoy): Poluprozrachnyy, temno-korichnevyy.
Ambient Occlusion: Protsedurnyy generator dolzhen zapekat v Vertex Color dannye o plotnosti — chem glubzhe vnutr kusta, tem temnee tsvet (Self-Shadowing), eto sozdast oschuschenie «glubiny» vnutri rastitelnogo mata.

Predstav: ty plyvesh na skutere. Vperedi — gigantskoe zolotistoe pole, zanimayuschee sotni metrov. Ono ne ploskoe.
Sverhu: Ty vidish relefnuyu, «pushistuyu» massu, kotoraya vzdymaetsya i opadaet na 2-metrovyh volnah. Trava razdvigaetsya pered tvoim nosom, obnazhaya temnuyu vodu vnutri.
Sboku: Viden «pirog» iz stebley, puzyrkov i listev tolschinoy v poltora metra.
Snizu: Eto inoplanetnyy potolok. Skvoz spleteniya nitey i yantarnye puzyrki probivayutsya «God Rays», a v kornyah pryachutsya melkie rybki.

🌑 1. Opticheskaya ten i «Svetovoy filtr» (Light Occlusion)
Sargassovy ostrova ogromny i plotny. Oni ne mogut prosto propuskat svet naskvoz.
Zadacha dlya sheydera: Realizuy Shadow Mask. Pod plavayuschim ostrovom na morskom dne dolzhna poyavlyatsya myagkaya dinamicheskaya ten (Shadow Blob).
Effekt «God Rays»: Kogda igrok nahoditsya pod vodoroslyami, luchi solntsa dolzhny «razbivatsya» ob strukturu mesha. V tolsche vody dolzhny byt vidny chetkie svetovye stolby, probivayuschiesya skvoz «dyrki» v rastitelnom kovre. Eto sozdast neveroyatnuyu atmosferu «podvodnogo lesa».
🌊 2. Fizicheskoe soprotivlenie i vyazkost (Drag & Friction)
Sargassy — eto putanitsa stebley. Plyt skvoz nih dolzhno byt trudno.
Logika dlya Agenta 1 (Fizika): Vnedrite zonu Localized Drag. Kogda igrok zaplyvaet vnutr mesha sargassovyh vodorosley (detektiruy eto po pozitsii otnositelno «ostrovov»), ego skorost dolzhna plavno snizhatsya na 30–50%.
Inertsiya: Skuter dolzhen «vyaznut» v trave, zastavlyaya igroka iskat obhodnye puti ili prorubatsya skvoz nih. Eto prevratit vodorosli iz dekoratsii v geympleynoe prepyatstvie.
🔊 3. Audio-rezonans (Tactile Soundscape)
Vhod v sargassovyy les dolzhen oschuschatsya ushami.
Zadacha: Kogda kamera igroka peresekaet granitsy «ostrova», audio-miksher dolzhen pereklyuchatsya.
Zvuki: Dobav priglushennyy shoroh, tresk puzyrkov (pnevmatotsist) i gluhoe «hlyupane» stebley o skafandr. Zvuk otkrytogo okeana dolzhen smenyatsya «uyutnym», no trevozhnym shumom zhivoy massy.
🦐 4. Mikro-biota (Procedural Micro-Life)
Ostrov — eto dom. Bez zhiteley on budet kazatsya mertvym.
Zadacha dlya generatora: V kode spavna mesha predusmotrite «tochki interesa».
Detali: Puskay vokrug vetok generiruyutsya chastitsy «morskogo snega» (detrit) i redkie, privyazannye k meshu melkie sprayty inoplanetnyh krevetok ili planktona. Oni ne dolzhny imet slozhnogo II, prosto lenivo plavat v radiuse 1 metra ot vetok, sozdavaya effekt kishaschey zhizni.

1. GENERATOR MEShA: Stohasticheskaya L-Sistema i Zapekanie Dannyh

Klass: SargassumGrowth
Otvechaet za protsedurnuyu generatsiyu edinichnogo "super-kusta". Mesh sobiraetsya dinamicheski cherez NativeArray dlya molnienosnoy skorosti. Dannye pakuyutsya v Vertex Color i UV:

Color.r = Ambient Occlusion (chem glubzhe/blizhe k tsentru, tem temnee).

Color.g = Subsurface Scattering (SSS) Mask (1.0 dlya puzyrkov-pnevmatotsist, 0.5 dlya listev, 0.0 dlya tolstyh stebley).

Color.b = Faza kachaniya (Vertex Animation Phase) dlya desinhronizatsii voln Crest.

Color.a = Identifikator sloya/zhestkosti (0.0 = inertnaya «boroda», 1.0 = zhestkiy plavuchiy tsentr).

        public quaternion rotation;
        public float length;
        public float thickness;
        public int generation;
        public float depth; // Glubina vnutri kovra dlya AO

    private List<Vector4> uv2_data = new List<Vector4>(); // Dlya fiziki Crest i Vetra

    public void GenerateSargassumMonster(int seed)
    {
        UnityEngine.Random.InitState(seed);
        vertices.Clear(); triangles.Clear(); vertexColors.Clear(); uv2_data.Clear();
        
        TurtleState rootState = new TurtleState 
        {
            position = float3.zero,
            rotation = quaternion.identity,
            length = baseLength,
            thickness = baseThickness,
            generation = 0,
            depth = 1.0f // Top sloy
        };

        // Zapusk rekursivnoy L-sistemy dlya verhushki (Top i Vaterliniya)
        GenerateBranch(rootState);

        // Generatsiya «Abissalnoy Borody» (Abyssal Roots)
        GenerateRoots(rootState);

        // Finalizatsiya mesha
        generatedMesh = new Mesh();
        generatedMesh.indexFormat = UnityEngine.Rendering.IndexFormat.UInt32; // Mesh budet ogromnym
        generatedMesh.SetVertices(vertices);
        generatedMesh.SetTriangles(triangles, 0);
        generatedMesh.SetColors(vertexColors);
        generatedMesh.SetUVs(1, uv2_data); // UV2 hranit vektora dlya kachaniya
        generatedMesh.RecalculateNormals();
        generatedMesh.RecalculateBounds();
        
        GetComponent<MeshFilter>().sharedMesh = generatedMesh;
    }

    private void GenerateBranch(TurtleState state)
    {
        if (state.generation >= maxGenerations) return;

        // 1. Izgib steblya (Perlin Memory)
        // Generiruem 3D shum dlya plavnogo zakruchivaniya steblya v spirali
        float noiseX = noise.snoise(state.position * curlFrequency) * curlAmplitude;
        float noiseY = noise.snoise(state.position * curlFrequency + new float3(100,0,0)) * curlAmplitude;
        float noiseZ = noise.snoise(state.position * curlFrequency + new float3(0,100,0)) * curlAmplitude;
        
        quaternion curlRot = quaternion.Euler(math.radians(noiseX), math.radians(noiseY), math.radians(noiseZ));
        state.rotation = math.mul(state.rotation, curlRot);

        // 2. Vychislenie konechnoy tochki segmenta
        float3 forward = math.mul(state.rotation, new float3(0, 1, 0));
        float3 endPos = state.position + (forward * state.length);

        // 3. Generatsiya geometrii segmenta (Tsilindr s peremennym radiusom)
        BuildStemCylinder(state.position, endPos, state.thickness, state.thickness * thicknessDecay, state.depth);

        // 4. Vetvlenie (Stochastic Branching) - 3 bokovyh pobega
        int branchCount = UnityEngine.Random.Range(2, 4);
        for (int i = 0; i < branchCount; i++)
        {
            float branchAngle = UnityEngine.Random.Range(45f, 75f);
            float azimuthAngle = (360f / branchCount) * i + UnityEngine.Random.Range(-15f, 15f);
            
            quaternion branchRot = math.mul(state.rotation, quaternion.Euler(math.radians(branchAngle), math.radians(azimuthAngle), 0));
            
            TurtleState nextState = new TurtleState
            {
                position = endPos,
                rotation = branchRot,
                length = state.length * lengthDecay,
                thickness = state.thickness * thicknessDecay,
                generation = state.generation + 1,
                depth = state.depth - 0.2f // Chem dalshe pokolenie, tem blizhe k krayam/glubzhe -> menyaetsya AO
            };

            GenerateBranch(nextState);
        }

        // 5. Razmeschenie pnevmatotsist i mikro-listev v uzlah
        SpawnFoliageAndBladders(endPos, state.rotation, state.generation, state.depth);
    }

        float3 returnVector = inst.basePosition - inst.currentPosition;

        inst.currentPosition += inst.velocity * deltaTime;

        // Perezapisyvaem sostoyanie
        instances[i] = inst;
    }

public class SargassumEntangler : MonoBehaviour 
{
    [Header("Nastroyki zahvata")]
    public float snagVelocityThreshold = 1.2f; // Skorost, nizhe kotoroy kusty «hvatayut» igroka
    public float breakForceRequired = 500f;    // Sila (impuls), nuzhnaya dlya razryva «pruzhiny»
    public float entanglementElasticity = 0.5f; // Naskolko silno tyanet obratno

    private ConfigurableJoint entanglementJoint;
    private bool isSnagged = false;

    void OnTriggerStay(Collider other) 
    {
        if (!other.CompareTag("Player") && !other.CompareTag("Scooter")) return;

        Rigidbody playerRb = other.GetComponent<Rigidbody>();
        float currentSpeed = playerRb.velocity.magnitude;

        // 1. PROVERKA NA ZAHVAT
        if (!isSnagged && currentSpeed < snagVelocityThreshold) 
        {
            StartSnagging(playerRb);
        }

        // 2. LOGIKA VYRYVANIYa
        if (isSnagged) 
        {
            // Schitaem prilozhennuyu igrokom silu (nazhatie gaza)
            float escapeEffort = CalculatePlayerInputPower(); 
            
            // Vizualnyy otklik: peredaem v sheyder instansa silu natyazheniya
            // Sheyder budet gnut vetki po vektoru (PlayerPos - ClusterCenter)
            UpdateShaderTension(other.transform.position, escapeEffort / breakForceRequired);

            if (escapeEffort > breakForceRequired) 
            {
                BreakFree();
            }
        }
    }

    private void StartSnagging(Rigidbody rb) 
    {
        isSnagged = true;
        // Sozdaem dinamicheskoe soedinenie mezhdu igrokom i tsentrom ostrova
        entanglementJoint = rb.gameObject.AddComponent<ConfigurableJoint>();
        entanglementJoint.connectedAnchor = this.transform.position;
        entanglementJoint.xMotion = ConfigurableJointMotion.Limited;
        entanglementJoint.yMotion = ConfigurableJointMotion.Limited;
        entanglementJoint.zMotion = ConfigurableJointMotion.Limited;
        
        SoftJointLimit limit = new SoftJointLimit();
        limit.limit = 0.5f; // Daem nemnogo «hoda» pered natyazheniem
        entanglementJoint.linearLimit = limit;

        // Zvuk: gluhoy schelchok i natyazhenie stebley
        AudioManager.Instance.PlaySfx("Sargassum_Snag", transform.position);
    }

    private void BreakFree() 
    {
        isSnagged = false;
        Destroy(entanglementJoint);
        
        // VFX: Vybros oshmetkov pri razryve
        VFXManager.Instance.Spawn("Sargassum_Break_Leaves", playerPos);

float4 ApplySargassumScum(float3 worldPos, float3 normal, float2 uv) 
{
    // 1. Opredelyaem zonu vaterlinii (Crest Interaction)
    float waveHeight = GetCrestWaveHeight(worldPos.xz);
    float waterLineMask = smoothstep(0.1, -0.1, worldPos.y - waveHeight);

    // 2. Generatsiya shuma Voronogo dlya «puzyrchatoy» struktury peny
    // My smeshivaem dva sloya shuma s raznoy chastotoy dlya effekta vyazkosti
    float2 voronoiUV = worldPos.xz * _ScumScale + (_Time.y * _ScumDriftSpeed);
    float scumNoise = VoronoiNoise(voronoiUV);
    float scumNoise2 = VoronoiNoise(voronoiUV * 2.5 + 0.5);
    float finalScumMap = saturate(scumNoise * scumNoise2);

    // 3. Tsvet «gryaznoy» peny: bolotno-zheltyy s vysokoy prozrachnostyu
    float3 scumColor = lerp(_FoamColor, _OrganicScumColor, finalScumMap);
    
    // 4. Nakladyvaem na osnovnoy tsvet vodorosli tolko sverhu (Normal.y > 0)
    float surfaceMask = saturate(normal.y) * waterLineMask;
    
    return float4(scumColor, finalScumMap * surfaceMask * _ScumOpacity);
}

My sozdaem v redaktore (cherez nash SargassumGrowth) vsego 3-5 variatsiy bazovogo kusta-prefaba (razmerom primerno 2h2h2 metra). Eto nashi «kirpichiki». Poligonazh odnogo kusta — sredniy (dopustim, 3000-5000 trisov).

Dalee my ne spavnim ih kak GameObjects. Eto ubet ierarhiyu Unity.
My ispolzuem metod Graphics.DrawMeshInstancedIndirect.
Vmesto togo chtoby risovat 10 000 kustov otdelnymi komandami, my otpravlyaem na videokartu odin mesh i massiv iz 10 000 matrits (pozitsiya, povorot, masshtab). Videokarta otrisuet gigantskiy ostrov za ODIN Draw Call.

2. SEKRET «SLIPANIYa» BEZ ShVOV (Intersection Blending)

Nam ne nuzhno stykovat ih "verteks v verteks". Kusty budut pronikat drug v druga (Overlapping). No chtoby eto ne vyglyadelo kak tupaya kasha iz peresekayuschihsya poligonov, my sdelaem magiyu v sheydere:

Depth Fade (Soft Intersections): Tam, gde geometriya odnogo kusta vhodit v geometriyu drugogo, sheyder delaet plavnyy perehod (kak u myagkih chastits).

Global Color Noise: Chtoby kusty ne vyglyadeli kak povtoryayuschiesya klony, sheyder nakladyvaet poverh vsego ostrova globalnuyu teksturu shuma Perlina (Global World-Space Noise). Odin kray kusta mozhet byt temno-zelenym, a drugoy, peresekayuschiysya s sosednim kustom — yarko-zheltym. Vizualno mozg budet vosprinimat eto kak edinyy gigantskiy biom.

3. ZhESTOChAYShAYa SISTEMA LOD (Levels of Detail)

Ty absolyutno prav, bez LODov eto smert. My stroim 4-urovnevuyu sistemu:

LOD 0 (0 – 15 metrov ot kamery): HISchNIK

Tut risuetsya tot samyy super-mesh: s yantarnymi puzyrkami, fizikoy rastalkivaniya, mikro-listyami. Zdes rabotaet DrawMeshInstancedIndirect.

LOD 1 (15 – 50 metrov): GEOMETRIYa

Puzyrki udalyayutsya (oni stanovyatsya teksturoy). Listya shlopyvayutsya v bolee krupnye plashki. Verteksnaya animatsiya rabotaet, no fizika rastalkivaniya otklyuchaetsya.

LOD 2 (50 – 150 metrov): OCTAHEDRON IMPOSTORS (Impostory)

Eto klyuchevaya ficha. My zamenyaem 3D-modeli na Impostory. Eto hitraya ploskaya kartochka (sprayt), kotoraya zapekaet 3D-model so vseh uglov. Kogda igrok plyvet vokrug ostrova, kartochka povorachivaetsya i podmenyaet teksturu. Vyglyadit kak 3D, no stoit 2 poligona.

LOD 3 (150+ metrov): OCEAN SURFACE MASK

Na gorizonte ostrova voobsche ne imeyut geometrii! My peredaem koordinaty klasterov v sheyder samoy vody (Crest), i ona prosto krasit poverhnost voln v korichnevyy tsvet i ubiraet penu barashkov.

4. GRID-SISTEMA I PROSTRANSTVENNOE HEShIROVANIE (Chunking)

Da, my razobem kartu na logicheskie chanki (Grid Cells), skazhem, 50h50 metrov.

My ne gruzim v pamyat ves ostrov razmerom 2 kilometra.

Nash LogicClusterer derzhit v pamyati tolko matematicheskie tochki (X, Y, Z, Radius).

Kogda igrok zaplyvaet v Chank A, sistema sobiraet koordinaty vseh kustov v etom chanke i v sosednih, kladet ih v ComputeBuffer i shlet na GPU.

Chanki pozadi igroka (dalshe 200 metrov) mgnovenno vygruzhayutsya iz GPU-bufera.

DOPOLNENIE K PROTOKOLU: GENERATsIYa «SARGASSOVOGO LABIRINTA» (LANGMUIR WEB LOGIC)

My otkazyvaemsya ot ravnomernogo zapolneniya krugov. Teper generator ispolzuet Shum Vorli (Worley Noise) ili algoritm poiska kraev Voronogo (Voronoi Edges), chtoby stroit «yacheistuyu setku».

1. MATEMATIKA LABIRINTA: Kak eto rabotaet

Vmesto togo chtoby rasstavlyat kusty gde popalo, algoritm delaet sleduyuschee:

Generiruet na ploskosti okeana nevidimye kletki (Voronoi Cells) razmerom primerno 15h15 ili 20h20 metrov.

Sami tsentry kletok ostayutsya pustymi (eto te samye «okna» dlya igroka).

Kusty vodorosley spavnyatsya tolko na granitsah (stykah) etih kletok.

V tochkah, gde shodyatsya 3-4 kletki (Uzly), vodorosli obrazuyut plotnye, tolstye komki. Zdes spavnyatsya krevetki, svetyaschiesya pnevmatotsisty i lut.

V liniyah mezhdu uzlami vodorosli obrazuyut tonkie «mosty» (shirinoy 1-2 metra).

Mestami linii algoritmicheski «rvutsya», sozdavaya prohody dlya skutera.

{
    [Header("Nastroyki Seti (Web Settings)")]

    public float webThickness = 3f; // Tolschina stenok labirinta
    
    [Header("Plotnost")]
    public float bushSpacing = 1.5f; // Rasstoyanie mezhdu instansami kustov
    [Range(0, 1)] public float breakChance = 0.2f; // Shans razryva stenki (vorota dlya igroka)

    // Vozvraschaet spisok tochek dlya GPU Instancing
    public List<Matrix4x4> GenerateSargassumLabyrinth()
    {
        List<Matrix4x4> instanceData = new List<Matrix4x4>();

        // Prohodim po vsey zone s shagom bushSpacing
        for (float x = -mapSize / 2; x < mapSize / 2; x += bushSpacing)
        {
            for (float z = -mapSize / 2; z < mapSize / 2; z += bushSpacing)
            {
                float2 pos = new float2(x, z);

                // 1. Vychislyaem Shum Vorli (Rasstoyanie do blizhayshih dvuh tochek)
                // Funktsiya vernet distanceToF1 (do blizhayshego tsentra) i distanceToF2 (do vtorogo)
                CalculateVoronoi(pos, cellSize, out float distF1, out float distF2);

                // 2. Ischem GRANITsU (Edge). Granitsa tam, gde rasstoyanie do dvuh tsentrov pochti odinakovoe

                // 3. Formiruem "stenki" labirinta
                if (edgeDistance < webThickness)
                {
                    // 4. Dobavlyaem organicheskiy Shum Perlina, chtoby stenki ne byli idealno rovnymi

3. KAK ETO IGRAETSYa (Geympleynyy stsenariy)

Teper predstav, kak eto budet vyglyadet v igre:

Podhod: Igrok podplyvaet k sargassovu moryu. Izdaleka eto vyglyadit kak beskrayniy zolotoy kover.

Vhod: No kogda on podplyvaet vplotnuyu, on vidit, chto eto ne monolitnaya stena, a kruzhevo. Pered nim otkryvaetsya «koridor» chistoy siney vody shirinoy 5 metrov, po bokam kotorogo vozvyshayutsya 2-metrovye dyshaschie steny vodorosley.

Navigatsiya: Igrok zhmet na gaz. On laviruet po etim izvilistym koridoram. Svet krasivo probivaetsya skvoz bokovye stenki. V uzlah peresecheniya (tam, gde vodorosli samye tolstye) on vidit stayki svetyaschihsya krevetok i, vozmozhno, kakoy-to lut (kusok redkogo musora ili detal).

Zasada / Lovushka: Koridor mozhet zavesti v tupik! I vot tut vstupaet v delo ta samaya mehanika prorubaniya (Slicing) ili mehanika vyazkosti. Igrok ponimaet: "Aga, chtoby vybratsya, mne pridetsya na polnoy skorosti vletet pryamo v kust, poteryat skorost (Drag), namotat ih na vint i prorvatsya v sosednyuyu chistuyu yacheyku".
