1. Religiya AUP (Absolute Universe Position)
Eto glavnaya «skvoznaya» vesch. V mandatah po fizike, vokselyam, II i skatteru vezde figuriruet AUP.
Common Pattern: My nikogda ne doveryaem transform.position. Ono — lish vremennoe predstavlenie dannyh dlya Unity.
Sut: Lyubaya koordinata v igre — eto int64x3 (sektor) + float3 (smeschenie).
Zachem: Eto pozvolyaet tebe odnomu stroit mir razmerom v tysyachi kilometrov. Esli agent pishet Vector3 pos bez privyazki k OriginShift, on sovershaet greh. Vse sistemy «obschayutsya» na yazyke AUP.
2. Pattern «Double-Buffer & Fence»
Pochti v kazhdom mandate (ot II do Rendera) propisan printsip dvoynoy buferizatsii dannyh.
Common Pattern: bufferA (Read-only dlya vseh sistem) i bufferB (Write-only dlya tekuschey dzhoby). V kontse kadra oni menyayutsya mestami.
Sut: Eto isklyuchaet «sostoyanie gonki» (Data Race). II prinimaet resheniya, glyadya na «vcherashnie» (predyduschiy kadr) pozitsii fiziki, poka fizika schitaet «zavtrashnie».
Zachem: Eto baza dlya Burst i Job System. Bez etogo igra na i3 prevratitsya v mesivo iz oshibok dostupa k pamyati.
3. Bitovaya Diktatura (Bitmask over Objects)
V mandatah po logistike (energoseti), II i inventaryu zaprescheny slozhnye obekty sostoyaniy.
Common Pattern: Ispolzovanie uint ili byte flagov dlya hraneniya sostoyaniy.
Sut:
V energoseti: bit 0 = active, bit 1 = ruptured.
V II: bit 0 = hunting, bit 1 = fleeing.
Zachem: Eto pozvolyaet protsessoru obrabatyvat tysyachi suschnostey za odin prohod (SIMD). Agenty postoyanno pytayutsya zasunut tuda Enums ili, upasi bog, Booleans — bey ih za eto. Nam nuzhna plotnost dannyh.
4. Vizualnyy Dizering i «Noir-stabilizatsiya»
Eto obschaya cherta vseh mandatov po grafike (REND-) i interfeysam (UI-).
Common Pattern: Zapret na prozrachnost cherez Alpha-Blending. Tolko Dithered Transparency s ispolzovaniem sinego shuma (Blue Noise).
Sut: Prozrachnost «ubivaet» MX350. My ispolzuem dizering (shahmatku iz pikseley), kotoruyu potom «razmyvaet» tuman i TAA.
Zachem: Eto sozdaet tot samyy Deep Sea Noir. Kartinka kazhetsya plotnoy i obemnoy, hotya na samom dele my ekonomim 70% resursov videokarty.
5. Printsip «Sensornoy Eholokatsii»
Fizika i Zvuk v nashih mandatah — eto pochti odno i to zhe.
Common Pattern: Kazhdoe fizicheskoe sobytie obyazano porozhdat signal v PhysicsEventBus.
Sut: Zvukovoy dvizhok ne «ugadyvaet», kogda proigrat zvuk. On podpisyvaetsya na tenzornye izmeneniya podlodki. II hischnikov ne «vidit» skvoz steny, on delaet RaycastCommand.ScheduleBatch, ispolzuya tot zhe sloy okklyuzii, chto i render.
Zachem: Eto sozdaet Realism-by-Data. Igrok chuvstvuet, chto mir chestnyy. Esli on slyshit skrezhet — znachit, fizicheskiy dvizhok realno zafiksiroval izgib mesha pod davleniem.
1. Kontseptsiya «Snapshot & Resolve» (Dvoynaya buferizatsiya dannyh)
Eto krasnaya nit cherez vse mandaty (AI, Physics, Logistics).
Common Thing: Sistema nikogda ne rabotaet s «zhivymi» dannymi v realnom vremeni. Snachala delaetsya Snapshot (snimok sostoyaniya v NativeArray), zatem Burst-dzhoba provodit Resolve (vychislenie), i tolko potom rezultat zapisyvaetsya v Back Buffer.
Zachem eto tebe: Ty mozhesh izmenyat chto ugodno v lyuboy sisteme, ne boyas «sostoyaniya gonki» (race conditions). Poka fizika schitaet novoe polozhenie podlodki, II smotrit na stabilnyy snimok iz predyduschego kadra. Eto klyuch k determinizmu.
2. Diktatura Bitmask (Bitovaya upakovka sostoyaniy)
Zabud pro bool isBroken, bool isActive. Mandaty po Logistike, II i Skatteru trebuyut upakovki v uint ili byte.
Common Thing: Sostoyanie obekta — eto nabor bit.
bit 0 — zhiv, bit 1 — v zone vidimosti, bit 2 — povrezhden.
Zachem eto tebe: Eto pozvolyaet delat mgnovennye filtratsii cherez bitovye maski ((flags & MASK) != 0). Eto ekonomit kesh protsessora i3 i pozvolyaet tebe obschityvat 10,000 ryb tam, gde obychnaya igra tormozila by na 100.
3. Zakon «Matematicheskoy Konservatsii»
Eto to, chto delaet HECTON-8 realistichnym. Mandaty po Energosetyam (LOGI) i Zhidkostyam (PHYS-Fluid) trebuyut soblyudeniya fizicheskih zakonov v kode.
Common Thing: Nikakih «magicheskih» chisel. Esli voda vtekaet v otsek, ona beretsya iz vneshney sredy. Esli energiya techet po provodam, ona zatuhaet po zakonu Oma.
Zachem eto tebe: Tebe ne nuzhno vruchnuyu nastraivat balans. Esli ty pravilno realizoval Jacobi Relaxation dlya uzlov seti, balans «vyrovnyaetsya» sam po zakonam fiziki. Matematika rabotaet na tebya.
4. Printsip «ALU over Bandwidth» (Vychisleniya vazhnee pamyati)
Eto kritichno dlya MX350. Videokarta medlenno chitaet tekstury, no bystro schitaet tsifry.
Common Thing: V mandatah REND-Noir i REND-URP propisano: ispolzovat protsedurnyy shum (ALU), a ne tyanut tekstury. Dizering (Blue Noise), kaustika, iskazheniya — vse eto formuly, a ne kartinki.
Zachem eto tebe: Bild budet vesit menshe, a kartinka budet chetche. My ne zabivaem 2GB VRAM musorom, my zastavlyaem sheydery «risovat» matematikoy.
5. Atomarnost «Floating Origin» (AUP-tsentrichnost)
Ves proekt stoit na Absolute Universe Position.
Common Thing: Ni odin skript ne imeet prava hranit Vector3 pozitsii dolshe odnogo kadra bez ucheta GlobalFloatingOrigin.Offset.
Zachem eto tebe: Eto izbavlyaet tebya ot samogo strashnogo baga ogromnyh mirov — «drozhaniya» (jitter) modeley na rasstoyanii 5 km ot tsentra. Tvoy mir mozhet byt beskonechnym, i on budet stoyat kak vkopannyy.
6. Interfeysnaya Izolyatsiya (SRP — Single Responsibility)
Sistemy ne znayut drug o druge napryamuyu.
Common Thing: Kommunikatsiya idet cherez EventBus ili GlobalRegistry. Fizika ne vyzyvaet zvuk. Fizika kidaet sobytie ImpactEvent, a zvukovaya sistema ego «slyshit».
Zachem eto tebe: Poskolku ty odin, ty budesh chasto menyat sistemy. Blagodarya etoy izolyatsii, ty mozhesh polnostyu vykinut staruyu sistemu zvuka i vstavit novuyu, ne menyaya ni odnoy strochki v kode fiziki.

1. Smert «Aktera», Vlast «Bufera» (DOD Sovereignty)
V standartnom Unity «Akter» (GameObject) — eto tsentr mira. V HECTON-8 «Akter» — eto musor.
Common Thing: Vse, chto imeet sostoyanie, zhivet v NativeArray ili NativeStream.
Sut: Pozitsii, golod hischnikov, zaryad batarei, potok toka v kabele — eto prosto indeksy v massivah dannyh.
Zachem tebe eto: Kogda ty odin, ty ne mozhesh debazhit 1000 raznyh obektov. Tebe prosche debazhit odin massiv. Esli dannye v bufere chistye — vsya igra rabotaet. Eto pozvolyaet Burst-kompilyatoru vyzhimat iz i3 mosch servernogo protsessora, potomu chto dannye lezhat rovno, a ne razbrosany po kuche.
2. PCIe-Distsiplina (Hardware over Software)
My optimiziruem ne «kod», my optimiziruem shinu peredachi dannyh mezhdu CPU i GPU. Eto kritichno dlya MX350.
Common Thing: Povsemestnyy perehod s ComputeBuffer.SetData na GraphicsBuffer.LockBufferForWrite.
Sut: My ne prosim videokartu «prinyat dannye», kogda nam udobno. My napryamuyu pishem v oblast pamyati, kotoruyu videokarta vidit.
Zachem tebe eto: Eto ubiraet «mikro-frizy». Bolshinstvo igr na Unity tormozyat ne iz-za grafiki, a iz-za togo, chto protsessor zhdet, poka videokarta podtverdit priem dannyh. HECTON-8 prosto let dannye v pamyat potokom.
3. Matematika «Absolyutnogo Nulya» (Deterministic Precision)
V okeane net «primerno». Esli matematika drozhit — immersiya dohnet.
Common Thing: Ispolzovanie AUP (Absolute Universe Position) i 3D Triple Product.
Sut: My emuliruem 64-bitnuyu tochnost tam, gde dvizhok daet tolko 32-bitnuyu. Smeschenie Floating Origin — eto ne prosto «teleport igroka», eto atomarnaya peresborka vseh matrits.
Zachem tebe eto: Bez etogo na rasstoyanii 10 km ot spavna tvoi vokseli nachnut «plyasat», a podlodka — provalivatsya skvoz dno. Eti mandaty garantiruyut, chto mir budet stoyat kak vkopannyy, dazhe esli ty uplyvesh na kray sveta.
4. Pattern «Dvoynogo Zaslona» (Double-Buffer & Fence)
Eto edinstvennyy sposob zastavit Job System rabotat bez krashey.
Common Thing: FrontBuffer dlya chteniya, BackBuffer dlya zapisi + JobHandle zavisimosti.
Sut: Kazhdaya sistema (II, Fizika, Render) potreblyaet dannye «proshlogo kadra» i gotovit dannye «buduschego». Oni nikogda ne peresekayutsya v odnom momente vremeni.
Zachem tebe eto: Eto daet tebe besplatnuyu mnogopotochnost. Poka render risuet kadr, fizika uzhe schitaet sleduyuschiy, i oni ne meshayut drug drugu. Dlya odinochki eto znachit, chto ty mozhesh naraschivat slozhnost sistem, ne boyas, chto oni «sozhrut» drug druga.
5. Temporalnaya Segregatsiya (The Cadence of Reality)
Ne vse v mire dolzhno proishodit odnovremenno.
Common Thing: Razdelenie na FastTick (60Hz), SlowTick (10Hz), ColdTick (1Hz) i FrostTick (raz v paru sekund).
Sut: Striming chankov — eto ColdTick. Poisk puti II — SlowTick. Lokomotsiya igroka — FastTick.
Zachem tebe eto: Eto tvoy byudzhet vremeni. Esli ty zapihnesh vse v Update, MX350 sgorit. Raspredelyaya zadachi po raznym «pulsam», my sozdaem illyuziyu zhivogo, slozhnogo mira pri minimalnyh zatratah.

1. Smert «Umnyh Obektov» (DOD Sovereignty)
Vo vseh mandatah (ot II do Energosetey) propisano: nikakoy logiki vnutri obektov.
Pattern: Obekt (GameObject) — eto pustaya vizualnaya obolochka. Vse dannye lezhat v ploskih NativeArray.
Dlya tebya: Eto znachit, chto ty mozhesh izmenit logiku raboty vseh 5000 ryb v odnom meste (v odnoy Job), ne pereklikivaya 5000 prefabov. Esli agent pytaetsya zasunut sostoyanie v MonoBehaviour, on pytaetsya sdelat tebya rabom ruchnogo truda. Gnat v sheyu.
2. Matematika «Syroy Pamyati» (Pointer over Property)
Mandaty po seyvam, vokselyam i renderu trebuyut pryamogo dostupa k pamyati.
Pattern: My uhodim ot Vector3, Transform.position i byte[]. My perehodim na (byte*)ptr + offset i UnsafeUtility.MemCpy.
Zachem: Na MX350 i slabom i3 marshaling dannyh (kopirovanie iz C# v C++) — eto glavnyy ubiytsa FPS. My rabotaem v «obschey pamyati», isklyuchaya posrednikov. Eto daet AAA-skorost na ofisnom zheleze.
3. Temporalnaya Ierarhiya (The Pulse of Reality)
V proekte vnedrena zhestkaya segregatsiya chastot.
Pattern: FastTick (60Gts — lokomotsiya), SlowTick (10Gts — sensorika), ColdTick (1Gts — II-direktor/spavn), FrostTick (raz v 5 sek — striming/pamyat).
Dlya tebya: Eto tvoy byudzhet. Esli igra lagaet, ty prosto perenosish sistemu iz Fast v Slow. Eto rychag, kotoryy pozvolyaet tebe odnomu balansirovat nagruzku na ves proekt za 5 minut.
4. Religiya «Bit-Packing & Masking»
Mandaty po logistike, II i inventaryu trebuyut ispolzovat bity vmesto klassov.
Pattern: Vmesto bool isBreached, bool isPowered, bool isUnderRepair — odna peremennaya uint32 flags.
Sut: Proverka sostoyaniya tseloy energoseti dlya protsessora prevraschaetsya v odnu operatsiyu AND. Eto to, chto pozvolyaet delat «glubokuyu» simulyatsiyu (Kirchhoff laws, Fluid Incursion) bez prosadok.
