🚨 KRITIChESKIE OBSERY (Bagi, Padeniya, Memory Leaks)

1. Access Violation / Use-After-Free v upravlenii potokom (Garantirovannyy Crash)
V StopAudioProducerThread() ty delaesh producerThread.Join(250). Esli potok zavisaet (naprimer, Burst JIT-kompilyator v pervyy raz sobiraet metod, chto legko zanimaet >300 ms na slabyh CPU), Join vozvraschaet false. Tvoy kod ignoriruet etot taym-aut, idet dalshe i vyzyvaet DisposeBuffers(), osvobozhdaya _hullScratch i drugie NativeArray.
Cherez dolyu sekundy fonovyy potok prosypaetsya i pytaetsya pisat zvuk v uzhe unichtozhennuyu nekontroliruemuyu pamyat.
Reshenie: Ubrat taym-aut Join(). Esli ty planiruesh unichtozhit unmanaged pamyat, ty OBYaZAN dozhdatsya polnoy smerti potoka. Ispolzuy Join() bez argumentov ili CancellationToken.

2. State Machine Bug: Gluhoy batiskaf
Posmotri na etot shedevr v UpdateThrusterTargets:

Esli igrok saditsya v transport/ekzokostyum, ego locomotionMode menyaetsya na Transport/Exosuit. Sledovatelno, isSwimMode = false. Iz-za etogo ternarnogo operatora tyaga transporta (transportBoost) prinuditelno obnulyaetsya. Ty sam sebe zablokiroval audio dvizhka vo vremya upravleniya lyuboy tehnikoy.

3. Thread Safety & Ring Buffer Audit (Audio Crackling)
Poskolku fayl NativeAudioFrameRingBuffer.cs skryt, ya obyavlyayu strogiy arhitekturnyy ultimatum. Esli v nem dopuschena hot odna iz sleduyuschih oshibok, zvuk budet treschat (Pops/Clicks) ili potok zavisnet:

Otsutstvie Memory Barrier: Esli vnutri TryWrite() prodyuser zapisyvaet dannye v massiv, a zatem obnovlyaet indeks writeCursor BEZ Thread.MemoryBarrier() ili Interlocked, potrebitel v OnAudioFilterRead uvidit obnovlennyy kursor, no prochitaet starye dannye iz kesha L1/L2 protsessora (False Sharing / Stale Data).

Keshirovanie Volatile: Vnutri AudioProducerLoop proveryayutsya svoystva _sampleRingBuffer.BufferedFrames i WritableFrames. Esli pod kapotom eti polya ne pomecheny kak volatile (ili ne chitayutsya cherez Volatile.Read), kompilyator C# zakeshiruet ih v registr, i potok zavisnet v beskonechnom Thread.Sleep(1).

4. Data Race v Sonar Trigger (Torn Read)
V HandleSonarPingSent ty pishesh 4 volatile inta, a potom atomarno menyaesh _pendingSonarStartFrame. V TryConsumePendingSonarTrigger vorker chitaet freym, i esli on svezhiy, chitaet inty.

Matematika:
Vnutri Jobs ty uzhe ispolzuesh saturatsiyu: math.tanh(mixed * Drive) * MasterGain.
Maksimalnaya amplituda signalov:

Sonar: 1.0 * sonarMasterGain (0.85)

Vot obektivnyy tehnicheskiy razbor tretego eshelona problem (uroven yadra i planirovschika):

1. Paradoks «Sinhronnogo Burst» (Scheduling Overhead)

Gde: hullJob.Run(frameCount); vnutri ProduceAudioBlock.

V chem problema:
Ty ispolzuesh IJobParallelFor, no vyzyvaesh ego cherez .Run(). V Unity metod .Run() vypolnyaet dzhobu strogo posledovatelno (v odin potok) na tom potoke, kotoryy ego vyzval. Poskolku tvoy potok _audioProducerThread — eto storonniy System.Threading.Thread, Unity ne mozhet zadeystvovat svoi Worker Threads dlya parallelizatsii etogo tsikla.

Obektivnaya realnost:
Ty platish «nalog» na infrastrukturu Job System (kopirovanie parametrov dzhoby, proverka bezopasnosti), no ne poluchaesh profita ot mnogopotochnosti Burst.

Bolee togo: Esli Burst ne smozhet skompilirovat dzhobu (naprimer, iz-za konflikta tipov), on pereydet na JIT (C#), i tvoy kastomnyy potok nachnet tormozit, vyzyvaya «zaikanie» zvuka, tak kak taymingi audio-vorkleta krayne zhestkie.

Kak nado: Esli ty v otdelnom potoke — pishi chistyy Burst-kod bez obertok Jobs, libo ispolzuy Schedule() i JobHandle.Complete() vnutri glavnogo audio-treda Unity, chtoby ispolzovat vse yadra protsessora.

2. Latentnaya katastrofa (The Latency Trap)

Gde: workerTargetLeadFrames = 16384.

V chem problema:
Ty pytaeshsya derzhat bufer v 16 384 freyma. Pri chastote 48 kGts eto ~341 millisekunda zaderzhki.
Dlya «kritichnogo» audio (udary korpusa, otklik dvigatelya na gaz) zaderzhka v 0.3 sekundy — eto proval. Igrok nazhimaet na gaz, a zvuk «razgonyaetsya» tolko cherez tret sekundy. Eto polnostyu razrushaet taktilnoe oschuschenie ot upravleniya (Game Feel).

Obektivnaya realnost:
Esli ty umenshish etot parametr do bezopasnyh 1024–2048, tvoy tekuschiy potok _audioProducerThread mozhet ne uspet prosnutsya vovremya iz-za Thread.Sleep(1), tak kak kvantovanie vremeni v Windows/Linux dlya obychnyh potokov chasto sostavlyaet 1–15 ms.

3. Nakopitelnyy dreyf fazy (Floating Point Drift)

Gde: double time = sampleFrame * InvSampleRate;

V chem problema:
Razrabotchik ispolzoval double, chto horosho, no on schitaet time kak absolyutnoe znachenie ot nachala sessii.

Cherez 2 chasa igry sampleFrame stanet ogromnym chislom (~345 millionov pri 48kGts).

Pri umnozhenii ogromnogo tselogo na maluyu drob (InvSampleRate) tochnost mantissy nachinaet padat.

V math.sin(TwoPi * FractionalCycles(time, frequency)) drobnaya chast (FractionalCycles) nachnet «drozhat» (jitter).

Rezultat: Zvuk sintezatorov (osobenno vysokochastotnyh) nachnet «plavat» ili «hripet» posle dlitelnoy igrovoy sessii. Eto klassicheskiy bag dolgozhivuschih simulyatorov.

4. Musor v Ring Buffer (The Silence Poisoning)

Gde: ProduceAudioBlock i logika zapisi.

V chem problema:
Chto proishodit, esli ProduceAudioBlock ne uspel vypolnitsya? V OnAudioFilterRead ty beresh dannye iz _sampleRingBuffer. Esli dannyh tam net (Underflow), bufer, skoree vsego, vernet starye znacheniya, kotorye lezhali v pamyati, ili prosto ne dopishet massiv data.

Rezultat: Ty uslyshish «petlyu» (loop) poslednego korotkogo fragmenta zvuka dlinoy v razmer bufera. Eto zvuchit kak rezkoe zhuzhzhanie (Audio Feedback Scream). V kode net yavnogo zanuleniya (Array.Clear) vyhodnogo massiva v sluchae nehvatki dannyh v koltsevom bufere.

5. Obektivnaya problema s Reverb Filter (State Reset)

Gde: _listenerReverbFilter.reverbPreset = AudioReverbPreset.User; v kazhdom UpdateCaveReverb.

V chem problema:
V Unity (FMOD pod kapotom) svoystvo reverbPreset — eto tyazhelyy setter. Dazhe esli ty prisvaivaesh User, kogda tam uzhe User, dvizhok mozhet proizvodit vnutrenniy sbros (Reset) DSP-tsepochki. Eto mozhet vyzyvat edva zametnye «mikro-kliki» v obschem mikse kazhdye neskolko kadrov.

Obektivnaya realnost:
Tvoy kod ne proveryaet if (_listenerReverbFilter.reverbPreset != AudioReverbPreset.User). On prosto "lupit" v setter kazhdyy kadr.

Itogovyy verdikt (Level 3):

Kod vyglyadit «umnym», no on ne uchityvaet vremya.

On ne uchityvaet vremya zhizni sessii (dreyf fazy).

On ne uchityvaet vremya otklika (zaderzhka v 340ms).

On ne uchityvaet vremya pereklyucheniya konteksta OS (risk starvation pri malom bufere).

Obektivno: Etot komponent nuzhno perepisat, vykinuv System.Threading.Thread i zameniv ego na standartnuyu Job System Unity s ispolzovaniem AudioSettings.dspTime v kachestve istochnika sinhronizatsii, a ne samopalnogo schetchika freymov.

Eto chetvertyy etap audita. Esli predyduschie punkty kasalis «arhitekturnyh pozharov», to teper my perehodim k sistemnym anomaliyam i oshibkam fizicheskogo modelirovaniya. Eti veschi vyplyvayut na etape polishinga, kogda zvuk kazhetsya «deshevym» ili igra vedet sebya nestabilno na raznyh konfiguratsiyah zheleza.

Vot obektivnye defekty urovnya Lead System Architect:

1. Narushenie kontrakta Raycast i ierarhii (Reverb Fail)

Sut: Reverberatsiya budet vsegda zvuchat «kak v korobke», dazhe esli igrok v otkrytom okeane.
Gde: Metod ResolveProbeDistanceMeters i proverka hitTransform.IsChildOf.

V chem problema:

Logicheskaya oshibka: Kod proveryaet, yavlyaetsya li obekt, v kotoryy popal luch, «rebenkom» igroka. No v 99% sluchaev v Unity igroki/kamery yavlyayutsya detmi transporta (submariny), ili transport i igrok — siblingi v korne stseny.

Proizvoditelnost: IsChildOf — eto rekursivnyy prohod po derevu transform. Vyzyvat ego v tsikle vnutri SlowTick dlya 8 popadaniy — eto lishnyaya nagruzka na Main Thread.

Rezultat: Esli igrok vnutri submariny, luchi radara budut bit vo vnutrennyuyu obshivku submariny. Proverka IsChildOf ne srabotaet (tak kak submarina — roditel, a ne rebenok). Radar reshit, chto vy v peschere radiusom 2 metra, i vrubit caveDecayTime.

Obektivnoe reshenie: Ispolzovat QueryTriggerInteraction.Ignore i LayerMask, isklyuchayuschiy sloy igroka i ego transporta, vmesto togo chtoby proveryat ierarhiyu posle popadaniya.

2. Gonka pri smene audio-konfiguratsii (Race Condition)

Gde: HandleAudioConfigurationChanged -> RefreshAudioConfiguration.

V chem problema:
Kogda polzovatel menyaet ustroystvo vyvoda (naprimer, vtykaet naushniki), Unity vyzyvaet OnAudioConfigurationChanged. Tvoy kod delaet StopAudioProducerThread() i DisposeBuffers().
No: Potok OnAudioFilterRead (Audio Thread) upravlyaetsya yadrom Unity i ne ostanavlivaetsya mgnovenno.

Main Thread vyzyvaet DisposeBuffers().

Audio Thread v etot zhe moment vyzyvaet OnAudioFilterRead.

Proishodit obraschenie k _sampleRingBuffer, kotoryy uzhe null ili disposed.
Rezultat: Vylet (Crash) pri smene naushnikov ili izmenenii nastroek Windows Sound.

Obektivnoe reshenie: V OnAudioFilterRead dolzhna byt atomarnaya proverka flaga gotovnosti bufera (Volatile.Read), a v DisposeBuffers — snachala sbros etogo flaga, a potom ozhidanie (SpinWait) ili bezopasnoe udalenie.

4. False Sharing (Degradatsiya kesha protsessora)

Gde: Polya klassa v zagolovke.

V chem problema:
U tebya peremennye _audioHullStressValue, _targetHullStressValue i drugie volatile polya lezhat v pamyati ryadom drug s drugom.

Main Thread postoyanno pishet v _target....

Audio Producer Thread postoyanno chitaet iz nih i pishet v _audio....
V sovremennyh CPU (osobenno na mobilnyh chipah ili Ryzen) dannye keshiruyutsya strokami po 64 bayta. Kogda odin potok pishet v peremennuyu, vsya stroka kesha v drugom yadre CPU pomechaetsya kak «gryaznaya» (Invalidated).

Rezultat: Protsessor tratit takty na sinhronizatsiyu keshey mezhdu yadrami (Cache Line Bouncing). Eto ne veshaet igru, no povyshaet zaderzhki (latency) audio-potoka i «otzhiraet» 1-2% proizvoditelnosti Main Thread na rovnom meste.

5. Risk perepolneniya (Ring Buffer Overflow)

Gde: ProduceAudioBlock i workerTargetLeadFrames.

V chem problema:
Ty pytaeshsya derzhat «operezhenie» (lead) v 16 384 freyma.
Esli ringBufferCapacityFrames po umolchaniyu (65 536), a Burst-skript vnezapno srabotaet bystree (naprimer, na moschnom PK), a Thread.Sleep(1) okazhetsya slishkom dolgim, ty mozhesh popast v situatsiyu, gde ProduceAudioBlock pytaetsya zapisat bolshe, chem ostalos mesta.
V kode _sampleRingBuffer.TryWrite net logiki «chastichnoy zapisi». Esli mesta net — blok prosto vybrasyvaetsya.
Rezultat: Schelchok (drop) zvuka, kotoryy budet povtoryatsya kazhdye neskolko sekund na moschnyh protsessorah.

6. Otsutstvie initsializatsii (Cold Start Click)

Gde: Pervyy zapusk ProduceAudioBlock.

V chem problema:
Vse peremennye _lowPassInputHistory i _lowPassOutputHistory obnulyayutsya v ClearLowPassState.
Kogda zapuskaetsya pervyy ApplyGlobalAbyssalLowPass, filtr nachinaet rabotu s nulevymi znacheniyami. Esli pervyy zhe sempl ot sintezatora budet imet amplitudu (naprimer, stress srazu 1.0), filtr poluchit rezkiy skachok napryazheniya.
Rezultat: Gromkiy schelchok (DC Offset Pop) v kolonkah pri samom pervom poyavlenii igroka v mire.

Itogovyy tehnicheskiy audit:

Kod stradaet ot «proklyatiya mikro-optimizatsiy»: razrabotchik potratil mnogo sil na Burst i RingBuffer, no propustil fundamentalnye problemy Unity (zhiznennyy tsikl potokov, fizika reykastov) i DSP (obrabotka signalov, faza, doppler).

Chto nuzhno ispravit pryamo seychas:

Dobavit Safety Check v OnAudioFilterRead, chtoby on ne chital iz disposed bufera.

Zamenit IsChildOf na proverku po LayerMask v sonare.

Dobavit plavnyy Fade-in na master-vyhode pri starte sinteza.

Ispolzovat akkumulyator fazy v sintezatorah vmesto time * frequency.
