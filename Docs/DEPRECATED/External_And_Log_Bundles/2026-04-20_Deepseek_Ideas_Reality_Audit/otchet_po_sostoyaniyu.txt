1. GLOBALNYE TsELI (ChTO MY HOTELI SDELAT)

My stroim AAA-survayval na dvizhke Unity 6 pod zhelezo i3-10gen / MX350.

  - Arhitektura: 100% DOD (Data-Oriented Design), Zero-GC v goryachih tsiklah,
    otkaz ot Update() v polzu SystemDispatcher.
  - Pamyat: Ispolzovanie NativeArray, ruchnoe upravlenie allokatsiyami,
    Memory-Mapped Files (MMF) dlya mgnovennyh seyvov.
  - Grafika: URP Forward+, BatchRendererGroup (BRG) dlya flory, SDF-teni ot
    fonarika (bez VRAM), Blue Noise dizering (vmesto prozrachnosti).
  - Fizika & II: Foveated Simulation (LOD dlya logiki), 3D DDA Raymarching dlya
    obhoda prepyatstviy, PD-kontrollery dlya tyazhelogo fizicheskogo
    vzaimodeystviya (ruki).

2. GLOBALNYE PREPYaTSTVIYa (ChTO NAM MEShALO)

  - Sabotazh Agentov (Early Exits): Vmesto 2 chasov raboty oni vypolnyali 10–20
    minutnye "sprinty", lataya pervyy popavshiysya bag i vozvraschaya PENDING
    VERIFICATION.
  - API Limity & Obryvy soketov: LLM-sessii padali, teryaya kontekst (Error
    reading command from socket).
  - Mertvye petli v Editore (Editor Deadlocks): Oshibki arhitektury proshlogo, gde
    tyazhelye vyzovy (generatsiya, SetParent, izmenenie RectTransform) byli zasunuty
    v OnValidate() ili [ExecuteAlways]. Eto veshalo Unity namertvo (te samye 12%
    CPU).
  - Crash kompilyatora Burst (ILPP): Razdutye struktury i krivye ssylki krashili
    C++ translyator Unity (Unity.ILPP.Trigger.exe).

3. POLNYY SPISOK PROBLEM IZ PROShLYH LOGOV (THE HISTORICAL DEBT)

Do tekuschego momenta my vyzhigali sleduyuschiy musor:

1.  ILPP Stack Overrun: Krash kompilyatora Burst iz-za ogromnyh value-type
    struktur (GrabState, ContextualPhysicalIkApplyJob).
2.  12% CPU Freeze (Play Mode & Reload): Zavisaniya iz-za while(true) v
    logistike, WFC, i sinhronnyh JobHandle.Complete() na nulevom kadre.
3.  Black Screen & Transparent Water: Otval podvodnogo post-protsessinga. Sboy
    Depth Priming, MSAA=2 na PC-rendererah (chto lomalo _CameraDepthTexture).
4.  SargassumMicroFaunaBoids Kernel Crash: Zapros kernelIndex (5), kogda sheyder
    imel menshe yader.
5.  AbyssalThermalManager Buffer Crash: ArgumentOutOfRangeException iz-za
    nesovpadeniya count i stride (32 vs 40 bayt) pri vyzove
    LockBufferForWrite.
6.  SargassumCollapseChunk OnValidate Crash: Vyzov AddComponent i SendMessage v
    rezhime redaktora.
7.  LoreDatabaseManager Hash Mismatch: 50+ oshibok. Stroki heshirovalis
    algoritmom UTF-16, a sveryalis s odnobaytovym FNV-1a.
8.  FBX Import Spam: Tysyachi logov MaterialLocation.External is obsolete i
    otsutstvuyuschie tangensy.
9.  Audio DSP Crash: Otsutstvie nativnoy DLL plagina Hecton Sensory Kernel, chto
    veshalo AudioMixer.
10. UI Matrix Collapse: HUD szhimalsya v tochku iz-za krivogo umnozheniya localMatrix
    bez ucheta pivotov.

IV. TRENAZhERNYY ZAL: PREPARATsIYa POSLEDNEGO LOGA (APRIL 25)

Eto samyy glubokiy analiz «Monstr-Loga», razdelennyy po podsistemam:

1. Sektor: Kompilyatsiya i ILPP (Smert na vzlete)

  - Problema: ILPP-Configuration ne generiruet vyvod. Tandra trebuet
    dopolnitelnyh progonov.
  - Detali: Vidno, chto Hecton8.Core.dll prohodit post-protsessing Burst (EILPP)
    za 460ms. Eto slishkom dolgo dlya takogo obema.
  - Sled: zzzUnity.Burst.CodeGen.BurstILPostProcessor — osnovnoy potrebitel
    vremeni. Kto-to peregruzil dzhoby ogromnymi strukturami.

2. Sektor: Animatsiya i Rigi (Smert v Awake)

  - Problema: Object.GetInstanceID() is obsolete.
  - Fayl: SargassumMicroFaunaBoids.cs(3190,37).
  - Detali: Agent ispolzuet ustarevshiy API vnutri goryachego tsikla initsializatsii.
    Nuzhno perehodit na GetEntityId.

3. Sektor: Narrativ i Lor (Hash Collision)

  - Problema: Spec hash mismatch (desyatki strok).
  - Fayl: LoreDatabaseManager.cs:105.
  - Detali: Konstruktor LoreSeed vychislyaet hesh v rantayme, kotoryy ne sovpadaet
    s tem, chto zahardkozheno v LoreDatabaseManager.cs:157. Rezultat: Lor ne
    podgruzhaetsya, UI pust, tekst ne otobrazhaetsya.

4. Sektor: UI i Layout (SendMessage Hell)

  - Problema: SendMessage cannot be called during Awake... (BiosBackdrop:
    OnRectTransformDimensionsChange).
  - Fayl: SuitHUDV4CanvasOverlay.cs:2992.
  - Detali: Metod EnsureContentRoot v HectonUIScaler menyaet sizeDelta u
    RectTransform. Eto triggerit tsepochku obnovleniy v Unity, kotoraya pytaetsya
    poslat soobschenie OnRectTransformDimensionsChange. No tak kak eto vyzvano
    iz OnValidate, Unity blokiruet potok. Eto glavnaya prichina 12% friza.

5. Sektor: Okruzhenie i Lor-hosty (Broken References)

  - Problema: Runtime lore recovery host not found: --- WORLD
    ---/Resource_FieldSources.
  - Fayl: HectonLoreSystemsRoot.cs:391.
  - Detali: Kod pytaetsya dinamicheski nayti roditelskie obekty na stsene dlya
    rasstanovki predmetov. Obektov net. Sistema vosstanovleniya (Recovery)
    rabotaet vholostuyu i spamit vorningi.

6. Sektor: Grafika i Sheydery (Syntax Death)

  - Problema: expecting TVAL_ID or TVAL_VARREF at line 87.
  - Detali: V odnom iz sheyderov dopuschena sintaksicheskaya oshibka (lishniy simvol
    ili propuschennaya skobka). Iz-za etogo sheyder ne kompiliruetsya, i obekt
    renderitsya rozovym ili chernym.

7. Sektor: Import Assetov (Workflow Noise)

  - Problema: MaterialLocation.External is obsolete.
  - Detali: Sotni FBX modeley iz ScifiFacility nastroeny na vneshniy poisk
    materialov. Eto legasi-put Unity. Nuzhno pereklyuchit v In Prefab.
