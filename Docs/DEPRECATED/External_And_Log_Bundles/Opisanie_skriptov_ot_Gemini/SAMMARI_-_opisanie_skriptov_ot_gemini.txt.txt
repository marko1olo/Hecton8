TEHNIChESKIY DOKUMENT: HECTON-8 — POLNYY RAZBOR KODOVOY BAZY
RAZDEL 1: CORE & MEMORY MANAGEMENT (Yadro i upravlenie pamyatyu)
ITickable.cs / ISlowTickable.cs
Chto eto: Interfeysy-kontrakty. Lyuboy klass, kotoryy hochet poluchat obnovleniya kazhdyy kadr, obyazan realizovat ITickable (metod Tick(float dt)). Dlya redkih obnovleniy (2 raza v sekundu) — ISlowTickable (metod SlowTick(float dt)).

Zachem nuzhno: Polnaya zamena MonoBehaviour.Update(). Unity vyzyvaet Update cherez sloy C++ -> C# marshallinga, chto stoit poryadka 500 nanosekund na obekt. Pryamoy vyzov interfeysa cherez tsikl for stoit okolo 5 nanosekund. Pri tysyachah aktivnyh obektov raznitsa kritichna.

Kak rabotaet: Klassy registriruyutsya v GameTickManager pri starte i otpisyvayutsya pri unichtozhenii. Nikakogo Reflection, nikakih nakladnyh rashodov Unity.

GameTickManager.cs
Chto eto: Tsentralnyy dispetcher vseh igrovyh obnovleniy. Edinstvennyy MonoBehaviour v sisteme, u kotorogo est Update().

Vnutrennee ustroystvo:

Ispolzuet vlozhennyy klass TickList<T> — buferizovannuyu kollektsiyu s metodami BeginIteration() i EndIteration().
Pri dobavlenii ili udalenii obektov vo vremya iteratsii izmeneniya skladyvayutsya vo vremennye bufery _toAdd i _toRemove. Primenyayutsya tolko posle zaversheniya tekuschego prohoda.
Eto isklyuchaet InvalidOperationException pri unichtozhenii obekta vnutri ego sobstvennogo Tick.
Proizvoditelnost: Obhod kollektsii cherez for (int i = 0; i < _tickList.Count; i++) vmesto foreach isklyuchaet vydelenie obekta-perechislitelya (Enumerator) v kuche.

SlowTick: Vyzyvaetsya cherez vnutrenniy taymer (~0.5 sek). Ispolzuetsya dlya sistem II, zhizneobespecheniya i biomnogo analiza — tam, gde tochnost do kadra ne nuzhna, no regulyarnost vazhna.

ObjectPoolManager.cs / IPoolable.cs
Chto eto: Globalnyy pul obektov. Zamenyaet Instantiate i Destroy dlya vseh igrovyh suschnostey.

Ustroystvo:

Hranit Dictionary<int, Queue<GameObject>>, gde klyuch — stabilnyy ID prefaba iz PrefabRegistry.
Pri zaprose obekta: beret iz Queue, vyzyvaet SetActive(true), vyzyvaet IPoolable.OnSpawn().
Pri vozvrate: vyzyvaet IPoolable.OnDespawn(), SetActive(false), menyaet roditelya na konteyner pula, kladet v ochered.
Warmup (Progrev): Na starte stseny SceneBootstrap vyzyvaet Warmup(prefab, count). Sozdaet count kopiy obekta zaranee, chtoby pervyy spavn ne vyzval zaderzhku. Progrev vypolnyaetsya portsiyami cherez await Awaitable.NextFrameAsync(), ne blokiruya ekran zagruzki.

DespawnTimer: Vlozhennyy klass, realizuyuschiy ITickable. Registriruetsya v GameTickManager tolko kogda zapuschen. V rezhime prostoya ne potreblyaet resursov. Ispolzuetsya dlya effektov, snaryadov i fayerov.

PrefabRegistry.cs / RuntimeInstanceId.cs
Chto eto: Sistema stabilnyh identifikatorov dlya vseh instansov igrovyh obektov.

Problema kotoruyu reshaet: GameObject.GetInstanceID() v Unity 6 ne garantiruet stabilnost mezhdu sessiyami i ne prigoden dlya ispolzovaniya v mnogopotochnyh Job-ah (dostup k Unity API iz potoka zapreschen).

Reshenie:

RuntimeInstanceId — struktura (struct) iz 64 bit: starshie 32 bita — ID prefaba iz reestra, mladshie 32 — poryadkovyy nomer instansa (Sequence).
ID generiruetsya determinirovanno na osnove hesha mirovyh koordinat obekta.
Ispolzuetsya v sisteme sohraneniy dlya identifikatsii konkretnyh zaspavnennyh obektov.
Peredaetsya v Job-y bez ogranicheniy, tak kak yavlyaetsya chistoy strukturoy dannyh.
RaycastBatchHelper.cs / QueryCacheContext.cs
Chto eto: Sistema paketnoy obrabotki fizicheskih zaprosov (Raycasts).

Problema: Kazhdyy instrument, II-agent i kamera nezavisimo puskayut luchi v Physics. Pri sotnyah obektov eto tysyachi sinhronnyh vyzovov na Main Thread, kazhdyy iz kotoryh blokiruet vypolnenie.

Reshenie:

Obekty registriruyut zaprosy (RaycastRequest) v RaycastBatchHelper vmesto pryamogo vyzova Physics.Raycast.
V kontse kadra vse zaprosy pakuyutsya v NativeArray<RaycastCommand> i otpravlyayutsya v RaycastCommand.ScheduleBatch — asinhronnyy Job na svobodnyh yadrah CPU.
Rezultaty (NativeArray<RaycastHit>) dostupny v sleduyuschem kadre.
QueryCacheContext: Keshiruet rezultaty luchey v predelah odnogo kadra. Esli dva raznyh skripta puskayut luch v odnom napravlenii iz odnoy tochki v odnom kadre — fizika schitaetsya tolko odin raz.

HectonFloatingOrigin.cs
Chto eto: Zaschita ot poteri tochnosti chisel s plavayuschey zapyatoy (float) v ogromnyh mirah.

Problema: float imeet tochnost okolo 7 znachaschih tsifr. Na rasstoyanii 10 000 metrov ot nulya obekty nachinayut drozhat (Floating Point Jitter) iz-za poteri tochnosti v drobnoy chasti koordinat.

Reshenie: Skript otslezhivaet pozitsiyu igroka. Kogda ona prevyshaet porog (1000 metrov ot mirovogo nulya), vse kornevye obekty stseny sdvigayutsya obratno k nulyu na velichinu smescheniya igroka. Igrok vozvraschaetsya v nachalo koordinat, mir peremeschaetsya vmeste s nim. Vizualno nichego ne menyaetsya.

Integratsiya: Uvedomlyaet vse sistemy (fiziku, kamery, Job-y) o smeschenii cherez sobytie OnOriginShifted, chtoby te skorrektirovali svoi vnutrennie koordinaty.

PerformanceMonitor.cs / RuntimeDiagnosticsTrace.cs
Chto eto: Vstroennaya sistema profilirovaniya, rabotayuschaya v finalnom bilde.

Mehanika:

PerformanceMonitor ispolzuet ProfilerRecorder dlya chteniya nativnyh metrik Unity bez nakladnyh rashodov: vremya kadra na CPU, allokatsii GC za kadr, kolichestvo batchey i SetPass vyzovov.
Dannye usrednyayutsya po skolzyaschemu oknu (poslednie N kadrov).
RuntimeDiagnosticsTrace: Pri obnaruzhenii anomalii (prosadka FPS, GC Spike) pishet detalizirovannyy log v fayl sessii. Zapis vypolnyaetsya cherez StreamWriter na fonovom potoke, chtoby ne dobavlyat zaderzhku na Main Thread.

StringBuilderPool.cs
Chto eto: Pul obektov StringBuilder dlya formirovaniya strok bez allokatsiy.

Ustroystvo: Stack<StringBuilder>. Pri zaprose — beret iz steka ili sozdaet novyy. Pri vozvrate — vyzyvaet sb.Clear() i kladet obratno.

Gde ispolzuetsya: V kazhdom UI-skripte, kotoryy obnovlyaet tekst (HUD, paneli PDA, overlei stroitelstva). Isklyuchaet sozdanie promezhutochnyh strok pri konkatenatsii.

ZeroGCStringCache.cs
Chto eto: Kesh dlya operatsiy nad strokami, kotorye obychno sozdayut musor.

Konkretnaya zadacha: Keshiruet rezultat string.ToUpperInvariant(). Normalnyy vyzov etogo metoda sozdaet novuyu stroku v kuche kazhdyy raz.

Ustroystvo: Massiv na 16 slotov s prostoy hesh-funktsiey (& 0xF). Hesh schitaetsya ot ishodnoy stroki, rezultat kladetsya v sootvetstvuyuschiy slot.

Ogranichenie: Pri bolee chem 16 raznyh vhodnyh strokah nachinayutsya kollizii i perezapis kesha. Effektiven tolko dlya nebolshogo fiksirovannogo slovarya strok (nazvaniya instrumentov, biomov, komandy HUD).

EntityChangeDetector.cs
Chto eto: Sistema detektirovaniya izmeneniy sostoyaniya suschnostey na osnove bitovyh masok.

Problema kotoruyu reshaet: Klassicheskiy podhod — kazhduyu sistemu proveryaet kazhduyu peremennuyu kazhdyy kadr (polling). Eto rastochitelno.

Reshenie:

Kazhdaya sistema, izmenyayuschaya sostoyanie (zdorove, pozitsiya, inventar), vyzyvaet MarkDirty(EntityChangeFlag.Health).
EntityChangeFlag — eto [Flags] enum (bitovaya maska).
V kontse kadra FlushChanges() proveryaet masku i rassylaet sobytiya tolko po tem kanalam, kotorye byli pomecheny kak gryaznye.
Podpischiki poluchayut uvedomlenie i pererisovyvayut tolko nuzhnye chasti UI ili pereschityvayut tolko nuzhnye dannye.
RAZDEL 2: PHYSICS & FLUID DYNAMICS (Fizika i voda)
HectonFluidEngine.cs
Chto eto: Tsentralnaya sistema rascheta vodnoy fiziki dlya vseh obektov v stsene odnovremenno.

Payplayn:

Sobiraet dannye vseh zaregistrirovannyh BuoyancyObject v NativeArray<BuoyancyParams> (pozitsiya, skorost, plotnost, obem).
Peredaet massiv v BuoyancyJob (Burst Compiled, IJobParallelFor).
Job parallelno na vseh yadrah CPU vychislyaet: silu Arhimeda, vyazkoe soprotivlenie (Drag), vliyanie globalnyh techeniy.
Rezultaty (NativeArray<BuoyancyResult>) primenyayutsya obratno cherez Rigidbody.AddForce() na Main Thread.
LOD fiziki: Obekty za predelami zadannogo radiusa ot igroka obschityvayutsya rezhe ili polnostyu zamorazhivayutsya (Rigidbody.isKinematic = true).

Suhie zony: Parametr isInAir v BuoyancyParams vystavlyaetsya modulyami bazy. Esli obekt vnutri bazy — Job propuskaet raschet vodnyh sil dlya nego.

BuoyancyObject.cs / BuoyancyProfile.cs
Chto eto: Komponent, veshaemyy na lyuboy fizicheskiy obekt, kotoryy dolzhen plavat.

BuoyancyProfile: ScriptableObject s parametrami: plotnost materiala, obem tela, vysota tsentra vytesneniya vody. Razdelenie dannyh i logiki.

Ground Check: Obekt periodicheski puskaet luch vniz dlya opredeleniya kontakta s dnom. Proverka ne vypolnyaetsya kazhdyy kadr — obekty raspredeleny po vremeni (staggered), chtoby ne bylo pika nagruzki.

Integratsiya s bazoy: Slushaet sobytie ot BaseModule.OnDryZoneEntered/Exited i peredaet flag v HectonFluidEngine dlya isklyucheniya iz raschetov.

CurrentManager.cs / CurrentVolume.cs
Chto eto: Sistema podvodnyh techeniy.

CurrentManager: Generiruet globalnyy fonovyy vektor techeniya cherez trehmernyy Simplex Noise s animirovannym smescheniem fazy. Techenie medlenno menyaetsya so vremenem, sozdavaya organicheskoe povedenie vody.

CurrentVolume: Komponent na triggere-kollaydere. Dobavlyaet lokalnyy vektor techeniya (naprimer, potok iz ventilyatsionnoy truby bazy ili geyzer). Registriruetsya v CurrentManager. Pri raschete finalnoy sily techeniya dlya obekta summiruetsya globalnyy vektor i vse aktivnye lokalnye.

RAZDEL 3: PLAYER & SURVIVAL (Igrok i vyzhivanie)
HectonPlayerMovement.cs
Chto eto: Rigidbody-kontroller igroka s podderzhkoy pyati rezhimov peredvizheniya.

Rezhimy (Locomotion Mode):

DryGroundWalk — dvizhenie po sushe. Standartnaya gravitatsiya, prilipanie k sklonam.
DryInteriorWalk — dvizhenie vnutri bazy. Snizhennaya gravitatsiya, otsutstvie vodnoy fiziki.
ShallowWadeWalk — dvizhenie vbrod. Skorost snizhaetsya proportsionalno glubine pogruzheniya.
SurfaceSwim — plavanie na poverhnosti. Pozitsiya Y fiksiruetsya otnositelno vysoty voln (integratsiya s Crest Ocean).
UnderwaterSwim — polnoe podvodnoe plavanie. Trehmernoe dvizhenie, vodnoe soprotivlenie ot HectonFluidEngine.
Pereklyuchenie rezhimov: Opredelyaetsya cherez seriyu SphereCast i Raycast proverok glubiny i nalichiya poverhnosti vody nad golovoy.

CameraJuiceProcessor.cs
Chto eto: Protsessor protsedurnyh effektov kamery. Realizovan kak chistyy struct bez zavisimostey ot Unity API.

Effekty:

Head Bobbing: Sinusoida kachaniya golovy, chastota i amplituda zavisyat ot rezhima dvizheniya i skorosti.
Turn Lag Sway: Pri povorote kamery pod vodoy dobavlyaetsya inertsionnyy naklon — imitatsiya soprotivleniya vody shlemu.
Collision Shake: Pri udare o poverhnost — korotkaya tryaska na osnove 3D Perlin Noise s zatuhaniem.
Breath Rhythm: Ritmichnoe mikrosmeschenie kamery, sinhronizirovannoe s animatsiey vydoha puzyrey pod vodoy.
FOV Compression: Ugol obzora plavno szhimaetsya s uvelicheniem glubiny. Sozdaet oschuschenie klaustrofobii.
Arhitektura: Rabotaet tolko s chistymi chislami (float, Vector3). Rezultat (CameraJuiceResult) peredaetsya naruzhu i primenyaetsya k transformu kamery. Nikakih zavisimostey ot MonoBehaviour.

HectonSurvivalSystem.cs / SurvivalStats.cs
Chto eto: Yadro mehaniki vyzhivaniya. Obrabatyvaet vse zhiznennye pokazateli igroka.

Parametry:

O2 (Kislorod): Rashoduetsya pod vodoy. Pri nule nachinaetsya uron HP.
Energiya kostyuma: Tratitsya na fonar, instrumenty, aktivnye moduli skafandra.
HP (Tselostnost): Zdorove igroka.
Davlenie: Rasschityvaetsya iz tekuschey glubiny. Pri prevyshenii SafeDepth skafandr poluchaet uron tselostnosti.
Temperatura: Chitaetsya iz lokalnogo bioma (cherez HectonHazardManager). Kriticheskaya temperatura razryazhaet batareyu bystree.
Radiatsiya: Nakaplivaetsya v zonah izlucheniya. Uhudshaet vse parametry pri nakoplenii.
Golod i Zhazhda: Medlennaya degradatsiya. Pri kriticheskom urovne snizhayut skorost vosstanovleniya O2.
SurvivalStats: ScriptableObject s bazovymi znacheniyami parametrov. RuntimeSurvivalStats — rantaym obertka, pozvolyayuschaya modulyam skafandra (SuitUpgradeData) dinamicheski izmenyat bazovye harakteristiki bez izmeneniya ishodnogo asseta.

HectonPlayerHealth.cs
Chto eto: Otdelnyy komponent upravleniya zdorovem igroka.

I-frames: Posle polucheniya urona aktiviruetsya period neuyazvimosti (Invincibility Frames). Isklyuchaet situatsiyu, kogda odin udar monstra zaschityvaetsya neskolko raz za odin kadr iz-za neskolkih kollayderov.

Integratsiya: Poluchaet uron ot HectonSurvivalSystem (davlenie, temperatura) i ot EnvironmentalHazard (ozhogi, toksiny, pryamye udary fauny).

RAZDEL 4: TOOLS & EQUIPMENT (Instrumenty)
PlayerTool.cs
Chto eto: Abstraktnyy bazovyy klass dlya vseh instrumentov igroka.

Kontrakt:

OnEquip() / OnUnequip() — animatsiya dostaniya i ubiraniya.
UsePrimary(bool held) / UseSecondary(bool held) — osnovnoe i vtorichnoe deystvie.
Integratsiya s ToolDurabilitySystem — kazhdyy vyzov UsePrimary umenshaet prochnost.
Integratsiya s IBatteryTool — rashod energii iz batareynogo slota instrumenta.
PlayerToolManager.cs
Chto eto: Upravlenie slotami bystrogo dostupa (1-4) i vizualnoy smenoy instrumentov.

State Machine smeny:

Idle — instrument v ruke.
Lowering — ruka plavno opuskaetsya vniz (Mathf.Lerp).
Swap — staryy instrument vozvraschaetsya v pul, novyy beretsya iz pula.
Raising — ruka podnimaetsya obratno s novym instrumentom.
Puling modeley: Vizualnaya 3D-model instrumenta v ruke upravlyaetsya cherez ObjectPoolManager. Pri smene instrumenta — despavn staroy modeli, spavn novoy.

LaserCutter.cs
Chto eto: Lazernyy rezak. Ispolzuetsya dlya dobychi rudy i dekonstruktsii moduley bazy.

Mehanika nagreva:

Pri uderzhanii LKM nakaplivaetsya parametr heat (0.0 — 1.0).
Pri heat > 0.7 uron po rude uvelichivaetsya na 15%.
Pri heat >= 1.0 — Overheat Lockout: instrument blokiruetsya na lockoutDuration sekund, izdaet zvuk peregreva.
Pri otpuskanii LKM teplo rasseivaetsya so skorostyu coolingRate.
Rezhim dekonstruktsii (PKM): Pri napravlenii na postroennyy modul bazy (BaseModule) — srezaet HP modulya. Pri razrushenii vozvraschaet resursy v inventar (protsent ot stoimosti postroyki).

ScannerTool.cs
Chto eto: Sonar-skaner. Analiziruet okruzhenie.

Tri rezhima:

Expedition — poisk tochek interesa, vhodov v peschery, signalov.
Resources — poisk resursnyh uzlov (HarvestableOutcrop).
Structures — poisk fragmentov chertezhey (ScannableFragment) i moduley bazy.
Mehanika: Pri aktivatsii spavnit ScannerPulseDrawer (vizualnoe rasshiryayuscheesya koltso cherez Shapes). Parallelno vyzyvaet Physics.OverlapSphereNonAlloc s keshirovannym massivom Collider[]. Naydennye obekty filtruyutsya po rezhimu i otpravlyayutsya v ScanEvents.

PropulsionTool.cs
Chto eto: Gravipushka. Tolkaet ili prityagivaet fizicheskie obekty.

Push (LKM): Rigidbody.AddForce(direction * pushForce, ForceMode.Impulse). Sila masshtabiruetsya obratno proportsionalno masse — tyazhelye obekty otletayut menshe.

Pull/Hold (PKM): Uderzhanie obekta na distantsii holdDistance. Realizovano cherez Spring-Damper matematiku:

Vychislyaetsya raznitsa mezhdu tekuschey pozitsiey obekta i tselevoy.
Prikladyvaetsya korrektiruyuschaya sila s koeffitsientom zhestkosti (stiffness) i dempfirovaniem (damping).
Obekt ne dergaetsya, a plavno stremitsya k tselevoy tochke.
Ogranichenie po masse: Obekty s massoy vyshe maxPullMass ne mogut byt prityanuty. Vizualno — instrument gudit i pokazyvaet oshibku na displee.

HarpoonLauncherTool.cs
Chto eto: Garpun s trosom dlya fiksatsii suschestv i transportirovki gruzov.

Mehanika:

Vystrelivaet snaryad (iz pula) s fizicheskim Rigidbody.
Pri popadanii v tsel (suschestvo ili obekt) snaryad fiksiruetsya i sozdaet virtualnyy tros (LineRenderer).
Igrok mozhet Reel (podmatyvat tros) korotkimi impulsami sily, prityagivaya tsel.
Esli tsel — FaunaBrain, suschestvo teryaet vozmozhnost uyti za radius trosa.
Pri masse tseli vyshe maxReelMass — podmotka nevozmozhna, tros uderzhivaet, no ne tyanet.
StunPistolTool.cs
Chto eto: Elektroshoker dlya neytralizatsii fauny.

Mehanika:

Pri popadanii lucha v suschestvo ischet komponent FaunaBrain.
Esli nayden — na suschestvo veshaetsya vremennyy komponent StunTargetRuntime (realizuet ITickable).
StunTargetRuntime vystavlyaet FaunaBrain.enabled = false i otschityvaet stunDuration.
Po istechenii taymera komponent snimaetsya, FaunaBrain vklyuchaetsya, komponent uhodit v pul.
Otsutstvie korutin: Vsya logika taymera cherez ITickable, bez StartCoroutine.

Otsenka sostoyaniya tseli pered vystrelom: Proveryaet tekuschee sostoyanie (FaunaState) suschestva. Spyaschee suschestvo oglushaetsya mgnovenno i bez zvuka. Agressivnoe — s vizualnym razryadom i povyshennym rashodom zaryada.

BuilderTool.cs / PlayerBuilder.cs
Chto eto: Sistema stroitelstva moduley bazy. Razdelena na dva klassa po zonam otvetstvennosti.

PlayerBuilder (Logika):

Ischet aktivnye tochki stykovki (ModuleSocket) cherez Physics.OverlapSphereNonAlloc.
Pri nahozhdenii soketa — spavnit «prizrak» (Ghost Prefab) modulya s prozrachnym materialom.
Prizrak fiksiruetsya k soketu s gisterezisom: prilipanie na 2.0m, otryv na 2.5m.
Pri podtverzhdenii postroyki: snimaet resursy iz inventarya, despavnit prizrak, spavnit realnyy modul.
BuilderTool (Vizual):

Realizuet «NASA-punk inertsiyu»: model instrumenta s zaderzhkoy sleduet za kameroy (Lerp s bolshim lagom).
Vstroennyy ekran na instrumente cherez MaterialPropertyBlock otobrazhaet nazvanie i stoimost tekuschego vybrannogo modulya.
RepairTool.cs
Chto eto: Remontnyy instrument dlya vosstanovleniya moduley bazy.

Mehanika: Pri uderzhanii LKM na povrezhdennom BaseModule vosstanavlivaet ego HP so skorostyu repairRate. Rashoduet energiyu skafandra i rashodnyy material (gel, metall). Esli modul zatoplen — instrument rabotaet medlennee.

FlashlightTool.cs / PlayerFlashlight.cs
Chto eto: Fonarik. Razdelen na logiku (FlashlightTool) i realizatsiyu sveta (PlayerFlashlight).

Rezhimy:

Flood — shirokiy rasseyannyy luch. Blizhnyaya vidimost.
Focus — uzkiy dalnoboynyy luch. Privlekaet bolshe vnimaniya II.
Peregrev i mertsanie: Esli batareya padaet nizhe 15% ili fonar peregret — PlayerFlashlight zapuskaet protseduru mertsaniya cherez Mathf.PerlinNoise(Time.time * flickerFrequency). Intensivnost umnozhaetsya na shum, sozdavaya organicheskoe mertsanie.

Integratsiya s II: LightDetectionSystem chitaet tekuschiy rezhim fonarya (Flood/Focus) i uroven nagreva (HeatLevel) dlya rascheta zametnosti igroka.

Volumetric Light Beam: Integratsiya s plaginom VLB. Intensivnost i nalichie «vzvesi» v luche menyayutsya v zavisimosti ot glubiny i bioma.

MantaScooter.cs
Chto eto: Podvodnyy dvizhitel (analog Seaglide iz Subnautica). Nasleduetsya ot PlayerTool, realizuet IBatteryTool.

Mehanika:

Pri uderzhanii LKM prikladyvaet silu v napravlenii vzglyada kamery.
Skorost ogranichena maxSpeed. Dopolnitelno ogranichena glubinoy (davlenie vody).
Rashoduet zaryad batarei proportsionalno prikladyvaemoy sile, ne postoyanno.
Vstroennyy displey na modeli obnovlyaet zaryad cherez MaterialPropertyBlock.
ToolDurabilitySystem.cs
Chto eto: Singlton. Otslezhivaet prochnost vseh instrumentov v igre.

Mehanika: Hranit Dictionary<RuntimeInstanceId, float> prochnostey. Instrumenty obraschayutsya k nemu pri kazhdom ispolzovanii. Pri dostizhenii nulya — instrument perehodit v sostoyanie Broken, UsePrimary blokiruetsya do remonta.

Sohranenie: Realizuet ISaveable. Sostoyanie prochnostey sohranyaetsya v SaveData.

ToolLoadoutPreset.cs
Chto eto: ScriptableObject s presetom iz 4 instrumentov dlya bystrogo snaryazheniya.

Primenenie: Interfeys PDA predlagaet presety na osnove konteksta (na chto smotrit igrok cherez FieldTargetSemantics). Odin klik — polnaya smena ekipirovki.

RAZDEL 5: BASE BUILDING & POWER GRID (Stroitelstvo i energetika)
BaseModule.cs
Chto eto: Logicheskiy i fizicheskiy komponent postroennogo modulya bazy (komnata, koridor, shlyuz).

Parametry:

HP — tekuschee zdorove. Pri nule — zatoplenie.
isFlooded — status zatopleniya. Pri true — vklyuchaet ParticleSystem proboiny i zvuk vody.
DryZoneCollider — kollayder-trigger vnutri modulya. Pri vhode v nego u obektov otklyuchaetsya vodnaya fizika (BuoyancyObject.isInAir = true) i menyaetsya akusticheskiy profil.
Integratsiya s sistemoy urona: Realizuet ICuttable. LaserCutter pri dekonstruktsii snimaet HP cherez etot interfeys.

Integratsiya s setyu pitaniya: Pri spavne registriruetsya v PowerGridManager kak PowerNode.

ModuleSocket.cs / HectonSocketHelper.cs

PowerGrid.cs / PowerNode.cs / PowerGridManager.cs
Chto eto: Simulyatsiya seti elektropitaniya bazy.

PowerNode: Komponent na kazhdom module. Hranit generiruemuyu ili potreblyaemuyu moschnost (v vattah) i prioritet potrebitelya.

PowerGrid: Logicheskaya set iz svyazannyh uzlov. Hranit spisok vseh PowerNode.

Algoritm balansirovki:

Summiruet vsyu generatsiyu i vse potreblenie v seti.
Esli defitsit — sortiruet potrebiteley po prioritetu (ot vysokogo k nizkomu).
Otklyuchaet naimenee vazhnyh potrebiteley (Dekor = 100, Svet = 50, Zhizneobespechenie = 0) poka ne dostignut balans.
Algoritm razdeleniya seti (BFS): Pri unichtozhenii modulya PowerGridManager zapuskaet BFS (poisk v shirinu) ot vseh ostavshihsya uzlov. Esli obhod ne ohvatyvaet vse uzly — set razdelilas. Sozdaetsya novyy PowerGrid dlya izolirovannoy chasti.

Algoritm sliyaniya: Pri postroyke novogo modulya, soedinyayuschego dve seti, menshaya pogloschaetsya bolshey.

RAZDEL 6: WORLD GENERATION & GEOLOGY (Generatsiya mira)
HectonVoxelEngine.cs
Chto eto: Asinhronnyy generator vokselnyh meshey dlya pescher i razlomov.

Payplayn generatsii (posledovatelnost Job-ov):

VoxelDensityJob (Burst, Parallel): Vychislyaet 3D pole plotnosti (SDF). Podderzhivaet primitivy: sfery, ellipsoidy, kapsuly, Box. Primenyaet Domain Warping (iskrivlenie prostranstva shumom). Delaet ploskoe dno cherez math.max(density, -y + floorLevel).

VoxelMCExtractJob (Burst): Po tablitsam Marching Cubes generiruet treugolniki iz polya plotnosti.

VoxelWeldJob (Burst): Skleivaet dubliruyuschiesya vershiny cherez NativeParallelHashMap<float3, int>. Umenshaet razmer mesha.

VoxelNormalJob (Burst, Parallel): Vychislyaet normali vershin cherez gradient plotnosti (analiticheski, ne usrednyaya treugolniki). Daet bolee gladkiy rezultat.

VoxelColorJob (Burst, Parallel): Krasit vershiny v zavisimosti ot glubiny, uklona poverhnosti i bioma.

VoxelSpawnPointJob (Burst): Nahodit ploskie uchastki pola cherez prostranstvennyy hesh (SpatialHash) dlya determinirovannogo razmescheniya resursov.

Asinhronnost: Ves payplayn vypolnyaetsya cherez JobHandle.CombineDependencies. Finalnaya sborka mesha — cherez Mesh.ApplyAndDisposeWritableMeshData (Unity 2021+).

CaveGraphGenerator.cs
Chto eto: Matematicheskiy generator topologii pescher (skelet pered vokselnym zapolneniem).

Algoritm:

Zapuskaet Random Walk (sluchaynoe bluzhdanie) ot tsentralnoy tochki obema.
S veroyatnostyu branchChance sozdaet otvetvlenie tunnelya.
Ischet tochki vyhoda na poverhnost terreyna (CaveEntrance).
Ot kazhdogo vhoda «prorubaet» pryamoy tunnel k blizhayshemu uzlu grafa.
Zony vokrug vhodov pomechayutsya kak EntranceExempt — oni isklyuchayutsya iz algoritma «zapechatyvaniya kraev» (Edge Sealing) vokseley.

RAZDEL 8: AI, FAUNA & DIRECTORS (II, fauna, rezhissura)
HectonBoidController.cs
Chto eto: GPU-simulyatsiya rybih stay.

Arhitektura Ping-Pong buferov:

Dannye ryb (BoidData — 32 bayta: pozitsiya, skorost, ID stai) hranyatsya v dvuh ComputeBuffer (A i B).
Na chetnyh kadrah Compute Shader chitaet iz A, pishet v B. Na nechetnyh — naoborot.
CPU nikogda ne zhdet GPU — chtenie i zapis proishodyat v raznye bufery.
Povedenie stai (Compute Shader): Kazhdaya ryba chitaet dannye blizhayshih sosedey (cherez prostranstvennyy hesh v GPU-pamyati) i primenyaet tri sily: Separation (izbeganie stolknoveniy), Alignment (vyravnivanie skorostey), Cohesion (styagivanie k tsentru stai).

Render: Graphics.RenderMeshIndirect — odin Draw Call dlya 5000+ ryb.

Ogranichenie zony: Os Y zony stai ogranichena sverhu waterSurfaceY — ryby ne vyletayut v vozduh.

FaunaDirector.cs
Chto eto: Upravlenie spavnom i despavnom suschestv vokrug igroka.

Koltso spavna: Suschestva spavnyatsya v koltse ot spawnRadiusMin (50m) do spawnRadiusMax (150m). Slishkom blizko — II mog by poyavitsya pryamo pered igrokom. Slishkom daleko — igrok nikogda ne vstretit.

Limity: globalFaunaLimit (obschiy limit) i biomeFaunaLimit[] (limit na biom). Hranyatsya kak prostye schetchiki int[]. Bez LINQ Count() — tolko inkrement/dekrement.

Despavn: Suschestva za cullRadius (200m) vozvraschayutsya v pul. Pri despavne proigryvaetsya perehodnaya animatsiya ischeznoveniya (fade out), esli suschestvo v pole zreniya.

HectonDirectorAI.cs
Chto eto: Rezhisser napryazheniya. Upravlyaet tempom straha i opasnosti.

TensionScore (0-100):

O2 < 30% → +25 ochkov.
HP < 40% → +20 ochkov.
Hischnik v radiuse 30m → +15 ochkov za kazhdogo (limit +40).
Glubina > 500m bez bazy → +10 ochkov.
Fazy:

BuildUp (0-60): Redkie pugayuschie zvuki. Sluchaynye glitchi HUD. Dalekiy siluet leviafana.
Peak (60-100): Spavn ordy hischnikov (ForceSpawnHorde). Polomka fonarya (prinuditelno snizhaet zaryad batarei). Destabilizatsiya kompasa.
Relax (posle Peak): Hischniki poluchayut komandu otstupit (FaunaState.Retreat). Spavn redkogo luta ryadom s igrokom. Avtosohranenie.
Raschet: Vypolnyaetsya v SlowTick (2 raza v sekundu). Ispolzuet Physics.OverlapSphereNonAlloc s keshirovannym Collider[].

RAZDEL 9: HAZARDS & SURVIVAL (Opasnosti sredy)

## RAZDEL 20: AUDIO SYSTEMS (Audiosistemy)

### SpatialAudioManager.cs
Chto eto: Globalnyy Zero-GC pul AudioSource.

Chto eto: Upravlenie akusticheskim profilem pri perehode mezhdu sredami.

Mehanika: Chitaet flag IsInAir iz HectonSurvivalSystem. Pri perehode okean→baza pereklyuchaet AudioMixerSnapshot cherez TransitionTo(snapshot, transitionTime). Snimaet Low-Pass filtr (gluhoy zvuk vody), vklyuchaet reverberatsiyu malenkoy metallicheskoy komnaty (IR-reverb).

### PlayerFootstepAudio.cs
Chto eto: Sistema zvukov shagov.

- Raycast vniz. Popadanie v Terrain — zaprashivaet indeks bioma u MapMagicBridge (pesok, kamen, il, gryaz) → sootvetstvuyuschiy zvukovoy profil.
- Popadanie v GameObject — chitaet Tag (Metal, Grate, Composite, Glass) → sootvetstvuyuschiy profil.

Adaptatsiya gromkosti: volume *= Mathf.Clamp01(rigidbody.linearVelocity.magnitude / referenceSpeed). Na medlennom shagu tiho, na bege gromko.

### PlayerThrusterAudio.cs

### HectonMusicDirector.cs (detali)

Raschet Tension: SlowTick vychislyaet TensionScore analogichno HectonDirectorAI no nezavisimo — dlya muzyki nuzhna sobstvennaya otsenka bez pryamoy zavisimosti ot II-direktora.

## RAZDEL 24: PLAYER SUBSYSTEMS (Podsistemy igroka)

### LightDetectionSystem.cs
Chto eto: Raschet vizualnoy zametnosti igroka dlya II.

Formula: visibilityScore = baseScore * distanceFalloff * angleFactor * lightModifier * heatModifier.

- distanceFalloff: 1.0 / (distance * distance) — obratnye kvadraty.
- angleFactor: Dot Product mezhdu napravleniem vzglyada suschestva i vektorom k igroku.
- lightModifier: Flood rezhim = 1.2, Focus = 1.5, vyklyuchen = 0.3.
- heatModifier: heat > 0.7 → lightModifier *= 1.15 (peregretyy fonar svetit yarche v IK-diapazone).

Rezultat 0.0–1.0 peredaetsya v FaunaSensorSuite kak mnozhitel dalnosti obnaruzheniya.

### NoiseSystem.cs
Chto eto: Raschet akusticheskoy zametnosti igroka.

Formula: noiseLevel = velocityMagnitude * noiseCurve.Evaluate(depth) * toolNoiseFactor.

- velocityMagnitude: Skorost Rigidbody igroka.
- noiseCurve: AnimationCurve — na bolshoy glubine zvuk rasprostranyaetsya dalshe.
- toolNoiseFactor: Lazernyy rezak = 2.0, skaner = 0.5, pustye ruki = 1.0.

### FieldTargetDescriptor.cs / FieldTargetSemantics.cs
Chto eto: Semanticheskaya razmetka obektov mira.

FieldTargetDescriptor: Komponent na obekte. Hranit FieldTargetRole (enum) i dopolnitelnye metadannye (massa, status zatopleniya, tip bioma).

FieldTargetSemantics: Rule-based sistema. Prinimaet FieldTargetDescriptor + tekuschiy instrument igroka → generiruet SemanticAssessment (zagolovok, kratkoe opisanie, rekomendatsiya).

Primer pravila: Esli role == CargoHeavy I activeTool == PropulsionTool I rigidbodyMass > 60 → rekomendatsiya "Ispolzuyte korotkie impulsy. Massa prevyshaet optimalnyy porog".

Ispolzuetsya v: EnvironmentalAnalyzerTool (detalnoe skanirovanie), InteractionUI (kontekstnye podskazki), FieldLoadoutAdvisor (rekomendatsii presetov snaryazheniya).

### RuntimeSurvivalStats.cs
Chto eto: Rantaym obertka nad bazovymi SurvivalStats.

Mehanika: Hranit bazovye znacheniya iz SurvivalStats ScriptableObject. Apgreydy (SuitUpgradeData) vyzyvayut ApplyUpgrade() i RemoveUpgrade() — izmenyayut tekuschie znacheniya multiplikatorami. Bazovyy ScriptableObject ne izmenyaetsya. Pri zagruzke igry — primenyayutsya vse ustanovlennye apgreydy poverh bazy.

### InventoryGrid.cs / PlayerInventory.cs
Chto eto: Inventarnaya sistema s setkoy (Tetris-mehanika).

InventoryGrid: Chistyy C# klass. Dvumernyy massiv ItemData[,] _grid. TryAddItem — lineynyy poisk pervogo svobodnogo pryamougolnika sootvetstvuyuschego razmera. Pri razmeschenii — zapolnyaet vse zanyatye yacheyki ssylkoy na predmet. RemoveItem — obnulyaet yacheyki v pryamougolnike predmeta. Nikakih Unity zavisimostey.

PlayerInventory: MonoBehaviour obertka. Otslezhivaet summarnyy ves (vliyaet na skorost peredvizheniya cherez HectonPlayerMovement). Realizuet ISaveable — serializuet setku kak spisok zapisey (ItemId, PositionX, PositionY).
