`Your channel fits because [actual observed content pattern], and HECTON-8 maps to that through [asset-backed hook], without asking you to sell [forbidden/weak angle].`

- "Your channel fits because you cover systems-heavy survival first-looks, and HECTON-8 maps to that through pressure, oxygen, salvage, and base recovery, without asking you to sell a vague lore teaser."

- "Your channel fits because you test whether survival mechanics actually hold up, and HECTON-8 maps to that through route risk, scarcity recovery, and pressure warnings, without claiming realism we have not measured."
