## Hard Truth

Price is positioning. A cheap price can increase impulse buys but can also signal "small janky survival clone". A high price can signal ambition but will punish weak content, weak performance, and thin demo loops.

HECTON-8 must not price against dreams. It must price against the public build players can actually buy.

## Commercial Boundaries

Forbidden:

- fake "big discount" by inflating base price;
- promising future scope to justify current price;
- using unsupported multiplayer modes as value proposition;
- "zero-stutter" as commercial claim without receipts;
- Early Access copy that sells the final fantasy instead of current build;
- launching with unclear refund-risk content length.

Required:

- price proposal tied to build scope;
- regional pricing check in Steamworks;
- discount calendar;
- EA questionnaire if EA is chosen;
- review-risk note before launch.

## Price Bands

These are planning bands, not final price.

| USD band | When it fits | Risk |
|---:|---|---|
| 9.99-12.99 | Very small EA/demo-like initial content, honest experimental scope. | Can look cheap/low-confidence. |
| 14.99-19.99 | Strong indie survival EA with several hours of loop, base systems, good visuals. | Must avoid thin-content reviews. |
| 24.99-29.99 | Polished survival game with convincing content depth, trailer, demo, Steam reviews confidence. | Dangerous without content/performance proof. |
| 34.99+ | Only if AA production value and content depth are obvious. | Not currently justified. |

Default planning recommendation today: do not decide. Build the demo, measure feedback, then choose between 14.99-19.99 or a lower EA entry price if content is thin.

## Price Decision Inputs

Score 0-2 each before selecting price.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| First-hour polish | broken/unclear | workable | strong |
| Content depth | <2h meaningful loop | 2-5h | 5h+ with progression |
| Base systems | absent | partial | strong visible value |
| Vehicle/machinery hook | absent | teaser | playable/proven |
| Performance proof | absent | limited | stable target hardware proof |
| Steam demo response | absent/weak | mixed | positive useful feedback |
| Wishlist conversion | unknown/weak | acceptable | strong for niche |
| Review risk | high | medium | controlled |

If total is under 10/16, avoid premium pricing.

## Early Access Decision

Early Access is valid only if:

- current build is fun without future promises;
- roadmap is honest and small enough to believe;
- save compatibility policy exists;
- update cadence is realistic;
- known missing features are stated plainly;
- price reflects current content;
- Steam EA questions are answered conservatively.

EA is invalid if:

- the build relies on "it will be good later";
- core loop is not repeatable;
- first screenshots oversell systems;
- no support/community response process exists;
- performance is unstable on target minimum hardware.

## Early Access Copy Rules

Use:

- "current build includes...";
- "planned focus areas...";
- "we will update this page if scope changes";
- "single-player-first";
- "performance targets will be shown with hardware/build context when measured."

Do not use:

- "will include multiplayer";
- "massive future ocean";
- "zero stutter";
- "fully simulated";
- "AAA";
- "launches soon" if date is not locked.

## Discount Strategy

Steam discount rules and cooldowns can change. Recheck docs before scheduling.

Planning policy:

| Moment | Discount | Why |
|---|---:|---|
| Launch/Early Access launch | 0-10% | Small launch discount can reward wishlists without training deep discount behavior. |
| First major update | 10-15% | Only if update has real content. |
| Seasonal sale early | 10-20% | After reviews stabilize. |
| Deep discount | Avoid early | Deep discount teaches low value and can pull wrong audience. |

Do not discount to cover weak reviews. Fix the build or messaging first.
