# Compliance And Disclosure Playbook

Status: compliance guardrail / pre-outreach
Public stance: single-player-first / no co-op promise
Runtime impact: none

## Objective

Keep creator outreach, keys, paid placements, and platform posts honest. The low-budget strategy cannot afford trust damage from undisclosed sponsorship, fake reviews, key scams, or false claims.

## Disclosure Rules

If a creator receives anything of value, disclosure must be clear:

- free key;
- demo access;
- paid placement;
- travel/event support;
- affiliate link;
- sponsored video/stream.

Plain wording is enough:

- "Demo/key provided by the developer."
- "Sponsored by HECTON-8."
- "Paid promotion."

Do not ask creators to hide the relationship.

## Key Distribution Rules

- Use verified public business contact routes.
- Log every key.
- Do not send keys to random DMs.
- Do not send keys to suspicious Gmail-only impersonators.
- Prefer Steam Curator Connect for curators.
- Revoke or rotate keys if scam signs appear.

## Paid Creator Terms

Every paid placement needs written terms:

- deliverable;
- date/window;
- platform;
- disclosure language;
- whether VOD remains public;
- whether the creator controls opinion;
- what assets are provided;
- payment amount and schedule;
- cancellation terms.

Do not demand positive coverage.

## Forbidden Claims

Never pay for or publish:

- fake reviews;
- fake wishlists;
- fake comments;
- undisclosed sponsorship;
- "zero stutter" without proof;
- "runs on low-end PCs" without proof;
- co-op implication;
- "Subnautica killer" ad copy;
- competitor asset use;
- "realistic full ocean simulation" without proof.

## Platform Notes

Recheck official sources before launch:

- FTC endorsement guidance;
- YouTube paid promotion/disclosure rules;
- TikTok branded content rules;
- Steam keys and review rules;
- Steam Curator Connect;
- regional ad/disclosure requirements if running paid placements outside English markets.

## Outreach Footer

Use in key/demo emails:

```text
If you cover the build, please disclose that the demo/key was provided by the developer. We do not require positive coverage and we do not ask you to hide criticism.
```

## Scam Checklist

Before sending a key:

- [ ] contact route matches creator-owned public page;
- [ ] domain/social handle matches official profile;
- [ ] recent uploads exist;
- [ ] request is specific to the game;
- [ ] no pressure for bulk keys;
- [ ] no reseller language;
- [ ] no mismatched channel stats;
- [ ] key logged.
