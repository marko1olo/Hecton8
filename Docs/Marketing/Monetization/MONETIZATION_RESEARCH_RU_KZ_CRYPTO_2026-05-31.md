## Brutal Verdict

1. Do not monetize the game build now. At G0 the project has no public proof pack, no measured funnel, no demo, and no store conversion surface. Selling now would convert attention into support debt.
2. Steam remains the primary eventual revenue path. Not because Steam is perfect, but because PC game buyers, wishlists, demos, reviews, festivals, and creator traffic all converge there.
3. A Kazakhstan card helps as a payment instrument. It does not automatically solve developer identity, tax residency, platform KYC, beneficiary-name matching, USD/SWIFT payout, or sanctions review.
4. Crypto is not a clean game-sales route for a Russia-resident developer. It creates legal, tax, refund, consumer-support, and platform-trust risk. Do not make "buy Submerge with crypto" a public route.
5. The correct near-term monetization is infrastructure and proof: official custody, legal/bank route test, waitlist-ready landing page, and a proof pack that sells pressure decisions instead of ocean-sim claims.

## Russia / Kazakhstan / Crypto Reality

### Russia

Russian residence creates platform and payment friction. PayPal and Payoneer routes are unreliable or unavailable for Russia-based users. Steam pays by bank transfer, and the practical issue is whether a bank/payment profile is accepted and continues to work under current restrictions.

Operational rule: do not spend money on store marketing until a real payout route has been tested against platform onboarding, beneficiary-name matching, tax forms, and bank receiving ability.

### Kazakhstan

A Kazakhstan card is useful for paying platform fees, services, ads, subscriptions, and contractors if the card works. It does not make you a Kazakhstan developer.

To use Kazakhstan as a serious developer payout route, the human must verify:

- legal right to open and use the account;
- whether the platform accepts the profile and bank;
- tax residency and reporting;
- USD/SWIFT receipt conditions;
- name match between platform payee and bank beneficiary;
- whether a business entity or sole-proprietor status is required.

Do not use VPN or mismatched country data to pass KYC. That is an account termination path, not a business model.

### Crypto

Crypto can exist as private treasury or limited donation infrastructure only after legal and tax review. It should not be the public checkout for HECTON-8, should not be tied to keys, and should not be framed as a way around banking.

Reasons:

- Russian digital-currency law creates risk around accepting digital currency as payment for goods, works, or services.
- Platforms dislike payment routes that bypass consumer protection, refunds, VAT/sales tax, and sanctions controls.
- Game buyers expect refunds, support, receipts, and stable regional pricing.
- Crypto buyers do not solve Steam wishlist/review/festival discovery.

## Price Boundary

Do not set final price now. Existing pricing policy remains authoritative.

Working range only:

- thin demo / weak content proof: do not sell;
- credible Early Access loop with several hours and support plan: likely 14.99-19.99 USD candidate range;
- strong content density, strong proof, clean build, strong asset identity: higher can be argued later;
- anything above that without demo proof is fantasy.

Discounts should not train buyers to wait. No bundle fire sale until the game has real review and wishlist data.
