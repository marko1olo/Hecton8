# Capsule, Trailer, And Thumbnail Briefs

Status: creative production brief / pre-asset
Public stance: single-player-first / no co-op promise
Runtime impact: none

## Creative Objective

Make HECTON-8 readable in one glance:

- underwater survival;
- pressure;
- machinery;
- black water;
- hostile depth;
- not clean sci-fi;
- not bright reef wonder;
- not co-op.

## Steam Capsule Brief

### Must Communicate

1. This is underwater.
2. This is survival under pressure.
3. Machines matter.
4. The world is industrial and dangerous.
5. HECTON-8 has a distinct silhouette.

### Visual Ingredients

Use 2-3, not all:

- pressure-rated habitat silhouette;
- floodlight cone through silt;
- heavy submersible/tool silhouette;
- black-water void;
- scratched glass/gauge overlay;
- Seed Ship anomaly shape in distance;
- orange emergency light against cold green/black water;
- worn metal/rust/salt.

### Avoid

- generic diver swimming in blue water;
- cute alien fish;
- tropical reef;
- monster face as whole composition;
- clean white sci-fi lab;
- unreadable black rectangle;
- tiny UI detail;
- "from fans of Subnautica" text;
- co-op/player group imagery.

### Three Capsule Concepts

| Concept | Composition | Risk | Test question |
|---|---|---|---|
| Floodlight Against Structure | Habitat/sub silhouette bottom-left, floodlight hits impossible Seed Ship structure in black water. | Could be too abstract. | Does it read as underwater survival, not just sci-fi art? |
| Base Pressure Failure | Interior/exterior split: pressure hatch, warning light, water/silt pressing outside. | Could look too horror-only. | Does base-as-machine read instantly? |
| Salvage Descent | Player/tool/vehicle descends toward industrial wreck with gauges/floodlights. | Could look derivative if too blue. | Does it show a player verb and distinct machinery? |

## Trailer Brief

Target: 60 seconds reveal; cut into 20s and 6s versions.

### Structure

| Time | Shot | Purpose |
|---:|---|---|
| 0-4 | Machine/floodlight/pressure warning | Immediate identity, no logo first. |
| 4-10 | Descent into black water | Establish depth and isolation. |
| 10-18 | Salvage/tool interaction | Show player verb. |
| 18-28 | Habitat/base system | Show base as survival machine. |
| 28-38 | Route risk/threat/instrument | Build tension through readable system. |
| 38-48 | Consequence: leak, power, hull, oxygen | Show failure and recovery. |
| 48-55 | Seed Ship/anomaly tease | Myth hook, not lore dump. |
| 55-60 | Title + Steam CTA | Clean CTA only if page exists. |

### Audio Notes

Use:

- low machinery;
- hull creak;
- muffled alarm;
- sonar ping;
- breath restraint;
- water pressure groan;
- one silence break before reveal.

Avoid:

- generic epic trailer percussion;
- loud jump-scare sting every 5 seconds;
- clean heroic sci-fi music;
- overexplaining VO.

## YouTube Thumbnail Brief

Goal: readable at small size and honest to the build.

### Good Thumbnail Text

- BELOW THE LIGHT
- PRESSURE WARNING
- THE BASE IS FAILING
- BLACK WATER
- SIGNAL FOUND
- HULL STRESS
- THE OCEAN WON
- DO NOT DESCEND

### Bad Thumbnail Text

- SUBNAUTICA KILLER
- BEST SURVIVAL GAME EVER
- CO-OP OCEAN
- REALISTIC OCEAN SIM
- ZERO STUTTER
- THIS CHANGES EVERYTHING

## Creator Thumbnail Angles

| Creator segment | Thumbnail image |
|---|---|
| Direct underwater survival | Floodlight + deep structure + pressure UI hint. |
| Survival route risk | Salvage object, oxygen warning, route back visible but unsafe. |
| Engineering/base systems | Pump/hatch/seal system under warning light. |
| Abyss horror pressure | Sonar/floodlight partial silhouette, no full monster reveal. |
| Regional first-look | Strong logo + one clear gameplay image, localized title if reviewed. |

## Screenshot Export Rules

- Capture in final target aspect ratios.
- Keep UI only if it helps explain the system.
- Do not crop out the player verb.
- Do not hide unreadable darkness with post-caption.
- Do not use editor-only cinematic camera unless clearly representative.
- Capture low/mid/high settings only if performance proof is being discussed.

## Creative Review Score

Score every candidate from 1-5:

| Criterion | Question |
|---|---|
| Genre read | Does it say underwater survival? |
| Distinctiveness | Does it avoid bright reef / generic diver look? |
| Player verb | Can the viewer infer what the player does? |
| Pressure | Does depth feel dangerous or costly? |
| Machinery | Does technology look functional? |
| Thumbnail read | Does it survive small size? |
| Honesty | Is it representative of real gameplay? |

Kill any asset below 3 in genre read, thumbnail read, or honesty.
