## Weekly Agent Routine

Every week before screenshots:

1. Verify 25 raw leads.
2. Add 10 personalized pitch notes.
3. Review 10 competitor/community posts for pain points.
4. Draft 5 post hooks tied to planned visuals.
5. Update CRM status.
6. Update source ledger.
7. Kill weak directions explicitly.

Every week after screenshots:

1. Score each screenshot by cold-read clarity.
2. Score each clip by retention hook.
3. Track Steam page visits/wishlists.
4. Send one small creator batch, not mass spam.
5. Summarize feedback into product issues.
6. Revise copy only from evidence.
7. Stop any spend that fails conversion.
