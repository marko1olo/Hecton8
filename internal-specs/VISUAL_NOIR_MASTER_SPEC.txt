======================================================================
1. CHEAP REALISM DOCTRINE
======================================================================

1.1 Primary visual theorem

- Darkness is the default state.
- Light is scarce, directional, and locally expensive.
- Water is the dominant medium, not an empty backdrop.
- The image must read through extinction, scatter, wet specular breakup, low-luminance color separation, and selective emissive punctuation.
- Premium perception comes from coherent light transport, scale cues, stratified medium density, and robust material hierarchy.
- Premium perception does not come from stacking bloom, chromatic aberration, or global fog until the frame becomes milk.

1.2 Cheap realism rules

- Rule A: Always pay first for depth cueing.
  Depth cueing order:
  1. extinction
  2. medium particulate parallax
  3. local contrast shaping
  4. wetness/specular breakup
  5. emissive hierarchy

- Rule B: Pay second for contact fidelity.
  Contact fidelity order:
  1. caustics where physically plausible
  2. wet masks and occlusion
  3. trim/specular edge breakup
  4. silt accumulation and streak directionality

- Rule C: Pay third for motion in the medium.
  Medium motion order:
  1. marine snow
  2. shallow shafts
  3. vent bubbles
  4. exhale bubbles
  5. refractive shimmer

- Rule D: Never hide a weak material response under stronger post.
- Rule E: Never use post-processing to fake geometry ownership failures.
- Rule F: Any effect that destroys silhouette readability in the 5 m to 25 m band is rejected.

1.3 Baseline visual promise by tier

MX350 tier:
- 1080p output via reduced internal scale if needed
- strong biome separation
- strong fog layering
- readable shallow caustics
- readable flashlight shaft
- marine snow density near camera
- wet hull/rock response
- HUD trauma without full-screen sludge

RTX 4090 tier:
- retains same DNA
- more stable shadow density
- higher pattern variety
- longer LOD residency
- richer multi-layer shafts
- denser mid-field particulate
- higher-frequency wet breakup
- additional secondary emissive and beam events

1.4 Visual failures that invalidate the look

- Pure black geometry with no low-end luminance floor outside absolute cave override
- Uniform cyan fog across all depth bands
- Constant-strength caustics at deep depth
- Global godrays in dark abyss biome
- Rock surfaces that read as dry chalk instead of pressure-wet stone
- Metal that reads as clean sci-fi aluminum instead of salt-worn industrial hull
- Bubbles that rise with identical phase and no compression or wobble suppression
- Marine snow that moves like random snowflakes instead of current-driven suspended matter
- HUD glitch that feels like a damage overlay instead of stressed instrumentation drift

2. RENDER PIPELINE CONTRACT
======================================================================

2.1 Color pipeline

- Internal shading space: linear
- Preferred HDR scene target: `R11G11B10_UFLOAT`
- Tonemap: ACES fit
- Output gamma: 2.2
- Dither injection: after tonemap, before output encode
- UI composite: after world dither, separate clean pass

ACES fit:

`ACES(x) = saturate((x * (2.51 * x + 0.03)) / (x * (2.43 * x + 0.59) + 0.14))`

Per-channel apply:

`color_aces.rgb = ACES(color_hdr.rgb)`

2.2 Render target discipline

- World color target: `R11G11B10_UFLOAT`
- Water distortion optional target on Medium+: `R16G16_SFLOAT`
- Depth target: standard URP depth
- Blue noise texture: `R8_UNORM`, 64x64, repeat, point sample, no mips
- LUT targets:
  - color grading LUT: 32x32x32
  - biome fog LUT: 256x32
  - stress HUD LUT: 128x16

2.3 Material policy

- No runtime material clones for Crest
- No uncontrolled per-instance material variants
- First-party wetness and trauma response through `MaterialPropertyBlock` or global constant buffer only
- Shared trim, grime, wetness, and wear atlases per material family

2.4 Lighting policy

- Darkness floor exists for open water and exterior seafloor
- Cave absolute darkness override is allowed only inside authored cave/dark-volume logic
- Additional lights remain capped by project quality settings
- Point-light count is not the primary aesthetic tool
- Emissive hierarchy is primary for hazard punctuation and fauna punctuation
- Flashlight is privileged; environment lights are budgeted second

2.5 Transparency policy

- Transparent layers allowed only for:
  - visor glass
  - beam shells
  - selected bubbles
  - limited wet sheens
  - selected particles
- Transparent layers must obey near-camera density clamps
- Transparent layers must obey depth fade and distance cull
- Transparent layers must never become the main source of image structure

3. BIOME OPTICAL MODEL
======================================================================

3.1 Optical variables

Definitions:

- `d` = traveled distance in meters
- `z` = player/world depth below reference surface, meters
- `beta_abs_rgb` = absorption coefficient per channel
- `beta_sca_rgb` = scattering coefficient per channel
- `beta_ext_rgb = beta_abs_rgb + beta_sca_rgb`
- `T_rgb = exp(-beta_ext_rgb * d)` transmittance
- `sigma_t = average(beta_ext_rgb)` scalar extinction used for low-cost fog gates
- `sigma_s = average(beta_sca_rgb)` scalar scattering used for haze injection
- `g_hg` = Henyey-Greenstein anisotropy

Full channel transmittance:

`T_rgb(d) = exp(-beta_ext_rgb * d)`

Low-cost scalar transmittance:

`T_scalar(d) = exp(-sigma_t * d)`

Single-scatter approximation:

`L_scatter = L_in * (1 - T_rgb(d)) * scatter_tint_rgb * phase_hg`

Henyey-Greenstein phase:

`phase_hg = (1 - g_hg * g_hg) / (4 * PI * pow(1 + g_hg * g_hg - 2 * g_hg * cosTheta, 1.5))`

MX350 cheap phase approximation:

`phase_cheap = lerp(0.0796, 0.45, saturate((cosTheta * 0.5 + 0.5) * ForwardScatterBias))`

3.2 Biome depth bands

- Shallow band: `0 m` to `40 m`
- Midnight band: `40 m` to `250 m`
- Abyss band: `250 m` to `1500 m`
- Interior dark-volume override: independent of band, multiplies ambient and haze terms
- Thermal vent local override: additive particulate and amber/green emissive response, not a full biome replacement

3.3 Channel absorption coefficients

Shallow coefficients:

- `beta_abs_shallow = float3(0.170, 0.082, 0.036)`
- `beta_sca_shallow = float3(0.055, 0.070, 0.095)`
- `beta_ext_shallow = float3(0.225, 0.152, 0.131)`
- `sigma_t_shallow = 0.169`
- `sigma_s_shallow = 0.073`
- `g_hg_shallow = 0.74`

Midnight coefficients:

- `beta_abs_midnight = float3(0.260, 0.118, 0.050)`
- `beta_sca_midnight = float3(0.065, 0.060, 0.052)`
- `beta_ext_midnight = float3(0.325, 0.178, 0.102)`
- `sigma_t_midnight = 0.202`
- `sigma_s_midnight = 0.059`
- `g_hg_midnight = 0.67`

Abyss coefficients:

- `beta_abs_abyss = float3(0.410, 0.180, 0.062)`
- `beta_sca_abyss = float3(0.040, 0.036, 0.032)`
- `beta_ext_abyss = float3(0.450, 0.216, 0.094)`
- `sigma_t_abyss = 0.253`
- `sigma_s_abyss = 0.036`
- `g_hg_abyss = 0.54`

3.4 Derived visibility metrics

Distance for 50 percent transmittance by channel:

Formula:

`d_50 = ln(2) / beta_ext`

Shallow:
- `d50_R = 3.08 m`
- `d50_G = 4.56 m`
- `d50_B = 5.29 m`

Midnight:
- `d50_R = 2.13 m`
- `d50_G = 3.89 m`
- `d50_B = 6.80 m`

Abyss:
- `d50_R = 1.54 m`
- `d50_G = 3.21 m`
- `d50_B = 7.37 m`

Distance for 10 percent transmittance by channel:

Formula:

`d_10 = ln(10) / beta_ext`

Shallow:
- `d10_R = 10.23 m`
- `d10_G = 15.15 m`
- `d10_B = 17.58 m`

Midnight:
- `d10_R = 7.09 m`
- `d10_G = 12.94 m`
- `d10_B = 22.58 m`

Abyss:
- `d10_R = 5.12 m`
- `d10_G = 10.66 m`
- `d10_B = 24.50 m`

Interpretation contract:

- Red disappears first in every biome.
- Blue must not remain neon-clean in abyss; low scatter and high ambient suppression keep it cold, not bright.
- Green remains dominant in shallow and vent-adjacent bands.
- Blue dominance in abyss is a transmittance result, not an excuse for saturated cyan fog.

3.5 Depth interpolation

Band interpolation must be continuous. Use weighted blend by normalized depth:

`w_shallow = saturate(1 - smoothstep(25, 55, z))`
`w_midnight = saturate(smoothstep(25, 55, z) * (1 - smoothstep(220, 320, z)))`
`w_abyss = saturate(smoothstep(220, 320, z))`

Blended coefficients:

`beta_abs = beta_abs_shallow * w_shallow + beta_abs_midnight * w_midnight + beta_abs_abyss * w_abyss`
`beta_sca = beta_sca_shallow * w_shallow + beta_sca_midnight * w_midnight + beta_sca_abyss * w_abyss`
`beta_ext = beta_abs + beta_sca`
`g_hg = g_hg_shallow * w_shallow + g_hg_midnight * w_midnight + g_hg_abyss * w_abyss`

3.6 Weather and turbidity modulation

Weather/turbidity factor `tau` in `[0..1]`, where:
- `0.0` = clear
- `1.0` = storm / disturbed silt / low visibility

Scattering modulation:

`beta_sca_mod = beta_sca * lerp(1.0, 1.75, tau)`

Absorption modulation:

`beta_abs_mod = beta_abs * lerp(1.0, 1.15, tau)`

Anisotropy modulation:

`g_hg_mod = lerp(g_hg, g_hg + 0.08, tau)`

Result:
- high turbidity increases forward haze
- high turbidity reduces contrast
- high turbidity shortens usable caustic depth

======================================================================
4. EXACT FOG AND SCATTERING FORMULAS BY BIOME
======================================================================

4.1 Shared fog foundation

Depth values:

- `depth_lin = LinearEyeDepth(raw_depth, _ZBufferParams)`
- `world_y_norm = saturate((worldPos.y - FogFloorY) / FogDepthRange)`
- `floor_boost = 1.0 + (1.0 - world_y_norm) * AbyssalDensityBoost`

Base extinction scalar:

`sigma_t = dot(beta_ext_mod, float3(0.3333, 0.3333, 0.3333))`

Base exponential fog:

`fog_base = 1.0 - exp(-depth_lin * sigma_t * floor_boost * FogDensityMul)`

Noir contrast remap:

`fog_noir = pow(saturate(fog_base), NoirPower)`

Color-channel extinction:

`fog_channel.rgb = 1.0 - exp(-depth_lin * beta_ext_mod.rgb * floor_boost * FogDensityMul)`

Final fog blend:

`scene_rgb = lerp(scene_rgb, fogColor_rgb, fog_noir)`

4.2 Shallow biome fog

Shallow usage:
- player depth below `40 m`
- sunlight contribution still available
- caustics allowed
- shallow beam allowed
- marine snow density moderate

Shallow constants:

- `FogDensityMul = 0.90`
- `NoirPower = 1.75`
- `AbyssalDensityBoost = 1.40`
- `HeightFogBias = 0.22`
- `SunScatterGain = 0.85`
- `CausticDepthGate = 24 m clear water, 12 m turbid water`

Shallow fog formula:

`depth_term = depth_lin * sigma_t * floor_boost * 0.90`
`height_term = saturate((FogCeilY - worldPos.y) * 0.02) * 0.22`
`fog_shallow = 1 - exp(-(depth_term + height_term))`
`fog_shallow = pow(fog_shallow, 1.75)`

Shallow fog color:

`fogColor_shallow = lerp(float3(0.020, 0.032, 0.040), float3(0.036, 0.058, 0.070), SunVisibility)`

Shallow in-scatter:

`scatter_shallow = (1 - exp(-depth_lin * sigma_s_shallow * 1.2)) * phase_cheap * SunScatterGain`

Shallow final:

`scene_rgb = scene_rgb * exp(-beta_ext_shallow * depth_lin) + fogColor_shallow * fog_shallow + SunColor * scatter_shallow`

Shallow use rules:
- keep blue under control with fogColor cap
- use green-cyan push only near upward-facing and lit paths
- do not brighten underside cavities with shallow scatter

4.3 Midnight biome fog

Midnight usage:
- player depth `40 m` to `250 m`
- surface influence weak
- directional shafts rare
- caustics usually disabled
- flashlight and emissive hierarchy dominate

Midnight constants:

- `FogDensityMul = 1.05`
- `NoirPower = 2.35`
- `AbyssalDensityBoost = 2.10`
- `HeightFogBias = 0.30`
- `SunScatterGain = 0.18`
- `LocalLightScatterGain = 0.42`

Midnight fog formula:

`depth_term = depth_lin * sigma_t_midnight * floor_boost * 1.05`
`height_term = saturate((FogCeilY - worldPos.y) * 0.015) * 0.30`
`fog_midnight = 1 - exp(-(depth_term + height_term))`
`fog_midnight = pow(fog_midnight, 2.35)`

Midnight fog color:

`fogColor_midnight = lerp(float3(0.008, 0.012, 0.020), float3(0.014, 0.022, 0.032), LocalLightFactor)`

Midnight local scatter from flashlight or proxy lights:

`scatter_midnight = (1 - exp(-depth_lin * sigma_s_midnight * 0.85)) * phase_cheap * LocalLightScatterGain * LightVisibility`

Midnight final:

`scene_rgb = scene_rgb * exp(-beta_ext_midnight * depth_lin) + fogColor_midnight * fog_midnight + LightColor * scatter_midnight`

Midnight use rules:
- increase contrast between lit cone and surrounding void
- preserve cold-green gradients around metallic assets
- do not let fog become flat navy wash

4.4 Abyss biome fog

Abyss usage:
- player depth beyond `250 m`
- sunlight effectively absent
- local emissive and flashlight dominate
- low scatter, high extinction, narrow highlight windows
- ambient floor is minimal

Abyss constants:

- `FogDensityMul = 1.22`
- `NoirPower = 3.10`
- `AbyssalDensityBoost = 2.80`
- `HeightFogBias = 0.38`
- `SunScatterGain = 0.00`
- `LocalLightScatterGain = 0.26`
- `BiolumFogPulse = 0.08 max`

Abyss fog formula:

`depth_term = depth_lin * sigma_t_abyss * floor_boost * 1.22`
`height_term = saturate((FogCeilY - worldPos.y) * 0.010) * 0.38`
`fog_abyss = 1 - exp(-(depth_term + height_term))`
`fog_abyss = pow(fog_abyss, 3.10)`

Abyss fog color:

`fogColor_abyss = float3(0.003, 0.006, 0.011) + BiolumPulseTint * BiolumFogPulse`

Biolum pulse tint:

`BiolumPulseTint = float3(0.000, 0.050, 0.085) * saturate(BiolumFieldEnergy)`

Abyss local scatter:

`scatter_abyss = (1 - exp(-depth_lin * sigma_s_abyss * 0.60)) * phase_cheap * 0.26 * LightVisibility`

Abyss final:

`scene_rgb = scene_rgb * exp(-beta_ext_abyss * depth_lin) + fogColor_abyss * fog_abyss + LightColor * scatter_abyss`

Abyss use rules:
- keep background compression aggressive
- maintain low-end separation on silhouettes
- use emissive only as punctuation, not world fill
- abyss must feel pressure-heavy, not bright blue

4.5 Interior dark-volume override

Dark-volume scalar `dv` in `[0..1]`, where:
- `0.0` = exterior
- `1.0` = sealed cave / module void / occluded crack

Override:

`ambient_floor = lerp(ambient_floor, 0.0, dv)`
`fogColor = lerp(fogColor, fogColor * 0.55, dv)`
`scatter_gain = lerp(scatter_gain, scatter_gain * 0.35, dv)`
`NoirPower = lerp(NoirPower, NoirPower + 0.75, dv)`

Use rule:
- do not zero fog entirely in interiors; keep some medium density
- do zero sky/surface contribution in authored cave dark volumes

4.6 Thermocline crossfade

Thermocline window:
- begin `32 m`
- center `55 m`
- end `90 m`

Thermocline factor:

`thermo = smoothstep(32, 55, z) * (1 - smoothstep(55, 90, z)) * 4`
`thermo = saturate(thermo)`

Thermocline additions:

`fogColor += float3(0.004, 0.006, 0.008) * thermo`
`roughness_bias += 0.06 * thermo`
`scatter_gain += 0.10 * thermo`

Purpose:
- slight desaturation band
- stronger softness across depth crossing
- readable biome boundary without visible line

======================================================================
5. FOUR-SAMPLE GODRAY / LIGHT SHAFT MODEL
======================================================================

5.1 Target

Simulate localized shafts using only four depth samples plus blue-noise dither. This is for shallow sun pockets, flashlight shaft thickening, rupture beams in ruins, and limited hazard spot shafts. This is not global volumetric fog marching.

5.2 Ray setup

Inputs:
- `P0 = camera position`
- `P1 = sample endpoint along light direction or beam cone`
- `ray_dir = normalize(P1 - P0)`
- `ray_len = length(P1 - P0)`
- `step = ray_len * 0.25`

Blue-noise dither:

`bn = BlueNoise(screenUV + FrameJitter).r`
`jitter = (bn - 0.5) * step * 0.75`

Sample distances:

- `s0 = 0.125 * ray_len + jitter`
- `s1 = 0.375 * ray_len + jitter`
- `s2 = 0.625 * ray_len + jitter`
- `s3 = 0.875 * ray_len + jitter`

Sample positions:

`Xn = P0 + ray_dir * sn`

5.3 Density evaluation

At each sample:

`depthFactor_n = saturate(1 - Linear01DepthAtWorld(Xn))`
`biomeDensity_n = SampleBiomeDensity(Xn)`
`shadow_n = SampleLightOcclusion(Xn)`
`phase_n = phase_cheap(cosTheta_n)`
`shaft_n = biomeDensity_n * shadow_n * phase_n`

Weighting:

- `w0 = 0.32`
- `w1 = 0.28`
- `w2 = 0.24`
- `w3 = 0.16`

Accumulation:

`shaft_accum = shaft_0*w0 + shaft_1*w1 + shaft_2*w2 + shaft_3*w3`

5.4 Depth rejection

Reject samples inside geometry using depth compare:

`sampleSceneDepth = SceneDepthAtScreen(Project(Xn))`
`sampleRayDepth = EyeDepth(Xn)`
`visible_n = step(sampleRayDepth, sampleSceneDepth + DepthBias)`

Depth bias:

`DepthBias = 0.04 + 0.02 * sn`

Visibility apply:

`shaft_n *= visible_n`

5.5 Shaft color

Shallow sun shaft color:

`shaftColor_shallow = lerp(float3(0.120, 0.160, 0.180), float3(0.260, 0.330, 0.320), SunWarmth)`

Flashlight shaft color:

`shaftColor_flashlight = float3(0.125, 0.155, 0.180)`

Ruin breach shaft color:

`shaftColor_breach = float3(0.090, 0.120, 0.145)`

Final shaft term:

`shaft_rgb = shaftColor * shaft_accum * ShaftIntensity * saturate(1 - depth_lin / ShaftFadeDepth)`

5.6 Dither stabilization

Frame phase:

`frame_phase = FrameIndex & 3`
`FrameJitter = float2(frame_phase * 0.17, frame_phase * 0.31)`

Temporal clamp:

`shaft_rgb = clamp(shaft_rgb, prev_shaft * 0.7, prev_shaft * 1.4 + 0.05)`

5.7 Tier rules

MX350:
- 4 samples only
- no history buffer
- no bilateral blur
- only shallow local shafts and flashlight shaft

GTX/RTX:
- optional 4-sample base + 4-sample secondary on selected hero shots
- optional light-space blur
- optional secondary distributed scatter mask

5.8 Reject conditions

- reject if biome is abyss and sun shaft source
- reject if `depth > CausticMaxDepth + 4`
- reject if `SunVisibility < 0.15`
- reject if local render scale drops below `0.80` on MX350 budget path

======================================================================
6. CAUSTICS AND FLOOR LIGHT BREAKUP
======================================================================

6.1 Authority

- Crest owns underwater caustic asset path
- Use assigned asset material only
- No runtime cloned Crest materials
- Depth gating and intensity gating can be driven by owner logic through approved property writes

6.2 World-space UV

`uv0 = worldPos.xz / CausticWorldScale + Time * FlowDirA * SpeedA`
`uv1 = worldPos.xz / (CausticWorldScale * 0.73) + Time * FlowDirB * SpeedB + float2(0.37, 0.13)`

Constants:

- `CausticWorldScale = 6.0 clear shallow, 8.5 turbid shallow`
- `SpeedA = 0.028`
- `SpeedB = 0.041`
- `FlowDirA = normalize(float2(0.84, 0.24))`
- `FlowDirB = normalize(float2(-0.42, 0.91))`

6.3 Pattern math

`c0 = texCaustic(uv0).r`
`c1 = texCaustic(uv1).r`
`c = pow(saturate(c0 * c1), 3.1)`

Shadow modulation:

`c *= shadowTerm`

Depth gate:

`c *= saturate(1 - depthBelowSurface / CausticDepthGate)`

Slope gate:

`c *= saturate(dot(normalWS, float3(0,1,0)) * 0.8 + 0.2)`

Final:

`caustic_rgb = c * CausticStrength * CausticColor`

6.4 Caustic color

`CausticColor = lerp(float3(0.06, 0.10, 0.11), float3(0.16, 0.22, 0.20), SunWarmth)`

6.5 Reject conditions

- no caustics in midnight beyond isolated breach situations
- no caustics in abyss
- no caustics on cave ceilings
- no caustics in heavy storm turbid factor above `0.75`

======================================================================
7. SHADER FOUNDATION
======================================================================

7.1 Shared PBR inputs

Standard packed inputs:

- BaseColor
- NormalTS
- Metallic
- Roughness
- Occlusion
- Wear
- Wetness
- EmissiveMask
- Height or cavity if available

Shared conversions:

`perceptualRoughness = saturate(roughness)`
`alphaG = perceptualRoughness * perceptualRoughness`
`N = normalize(normalWS)`
`V = normalize(viewDirWS)`
`L = normalize(lightDirWS)`
`H = normalize(V + L)`

7.2 Wetness modulation standard

Wetness is not only roughness reduction. Wetness changes:
- roughness
- dark diffuse energy
- fresnel strength
- micro-normal flattening
- drip streak orientation

Wetness standard:

`wet = saturate(wetMask * WetnessGlobal)`
`roughness = lerp(roughness, max(0.02, roughness * 0.35), wet)`
`diffuseDarken = lerp(1.0, 0.84, wet)`
`fresnelBoost = lerp(1.0, 1.35, wet)`
`normalBlend = lerp(1.0, 0.65, wet)`

7.3 Noir cavity boost

Micro-cavity reinforcement:

`cavity = saturate(1 - ao)`
`localNoir = pow(cavity, 1.8) * LocalNoirStrength`
`albedo *= 1 - localNoir * 0.08`
`roughness = saturate(roughness + localNoir * 0.06)`

7.4 Distance simplification

At distance:
- reduce normal intensity
- reduce wet normal secondary sample
- reduce biofilm and wear contrast
- preserve silhouette and broad roughness zones

Formula:

8. SHADER MASTER RECIPE: VOXEL ROCK

8.2 Triplanar basis

Constants:

- `TriplanarSharpness = 4.0 shallow rock`

`uvX = worldPos.zy * TileScale`
`uvY = worldPos.xz * TileScale`

8.3 Sample stack

`wetZ = WetMask(uvZ * 0.08 + float2(Time * 0.01, 0))`

`hMacro = HeightMacro(...)`

Constants:

- `SiltBias = 0.10`

Purpose:
- silt collects in low regions
- rock protrusions remain exposed

8.5 Wetness modulation for rock

- depth-based constant wetness
- upward seep zones

- local current splash if applicable

10. SHADER MASTER RECIPE: DITHERED ALPHA AND BLUE NOISE
======================================================================

10.1 Use cases

- LOD crossfade on flora and thin debris

- fog-transition gating for distant shafts

10.2 Threshold formula

Blue-noise sample:

`bn = texBlueNoise(pointRepeat, screenUV * BlueNoiseScale + FrameOffset).r`

Threshold:

`threshold = bn`

Alpha test:

`pass = step(threshold, alphaCoverage)`

For smoother mid-range transition:

`alphaCoverage = saturate((alpha - CutoffLow) / max(CutoffHigh - CutoffLow, 1e-4))`

Constants:

- `CutoffLow = 0.32`

10.3 Shadow-band reduction

When used in shadow caster or shadow receiver path:

`shadowAlpha = saturate(alphaCoverage * ShadowCoverageGain + ShadowCoverageBias)`

- `ShadowCoverageGain = 0.92`
- `ShadowCoverageBias = 0.04`

10.4 Banding elimination in darks

Final dark dither injection:

`ditherOffset = (bn - 0.5) * (1.0 / 255.0) * DitherStrength`

Recommended strengths:
- `0.75` for world final pass
- `0.40` for visor distortion chain
- `0.00` for clean UI compositing

10.5 Frame rotation

`FrameOffset = float2((FrameIndex & 3) * 0.25, ((FrameIndex + 1) & 3) * 0.25)`

10.6 Bayer fallback

If blue noise unavailable on lowest path:

`bayer4x4 = (Bayer[x & 3][y & 3] + 0.5) / 16.0`
`pass = step(bayer4x4, alphaCoverage)`

Use only as lowest fallback. Blue noise is preferred.

13. VFX MASTER RECIPE: BUBBLES
======================================================================

13.1 Bubble classes

- Class A: exhale bubbles
- Class B: vent seep bubbles
- Class C: pressure leak bubbles
- Class D: ambient shallow disturbance bubbles

13.2 Canonical particle state

Per bubble:
- position
- velocity
- radius
- normalized life
- wobble phase
- refractive strength
- atlas tile
- class flags

13.3 Buoyancy physics

Volume:

`V = (4/3) * PI * r^3`

Buoyant force:

`F_b = V * g * (rho_water - rho_air)`

Bubble mass approximation:

`m_b = max(rho_air * V, 1e-5)`

Buoyant acceleration:

`a_b = F_b / m_b`

Simplified:

`a_b = g * ((rho_water - rho_air) / max(rho_air, 1.0))`

Because this explodes for pure physical bubble mass, clamp to authored scale:

`a_b_clamped = lerp(1.8, 4.6, saturate((r - 0.004) / 0.030))`

Velocity update:

`vel.y += a_b_clamped * dt`

Terminal rise speed:

`v_term = 0.28 + r * 8.5`
`vel.y = min(vel.y, v_term)`

13.4 Pressure compression

Pressure:

`P = 101325 + rho_water * g * depth`

Compressed radius:

`r_compressed = r_surface * pow(101325 / P, 1.0 / 3.0)`

Cheap form:

`r_compressed = r_surface * sqrt(101325 / P)`

Use:
- exhale bubbles: full cheap compression
- vent bubbles: partial compression
- ambient shallow bubbles: compression ignored

13.5 Wobble frequency

Base:

`freq = 1.6 + (0.03 - r) * 55.0`

Clamped:

`freq = clamp(freq, 1.8, 5.2)`

Wobble magnitude:

`amp = r * 0.22`

Lateral perturbation:

`offset.x = sin(t * freq + phase + pos.y * 1.4) * amp`
`offset.z = cos(t * freq * 0.79 + phase + pos.y * 1.1) * amp`

Velocity apply:

`vel.xz += offset.xz * dt * 10.0`

13.6 Current suppression

`currentStrength = length(flowSample)`
`wobbleSuppression = 1 - saturate(currentStrength / 2.5)`
`vel.xz *= lerp(1.0, wobbleSuppression, 0.65)`

13.7 Refraction math

Bubble fragment normal:

`N_bubble = normalize(float3(uv*2 - 1, sqrt(saturate(1 - dot(uv*2-1, uv*2-1)))))`

Refraction offset:

`eta = eta_air / eta_water`
`R = refract(-V, N_bubble, eta)`
`uvOffset = R.xy * BubbleRefractionStrength`

Strength:
- exhale `0.010`
- vent `0.014`
- leak `0.012`

Rim light:

`rim = pow(1 - saturate(dot(V, N_bubble)), 4.0)`
`rimColor = LightColor * rim * RimGain`

13.8 Bubble color

Base transmission:

`bubble_rgb = scene_rgb(uv + uvOffset)`

Scattering tint:

`bubble_rgb = lerp(bubble_rgb, bubble_rgb * float3(0.90, 0.98, 1.04), 0.18)`

13.9 Spawn logic

Exhale:
- burst count `6..18`
- radius `0.004..0.018`
- lifetime `0.7..1.5 s`
- initial vertical velocity `0.08..0.22`

Vent:
- continuous
- radius `0.006..0.024`
- lifetime `1.2..2.8 s`
- spawn jitter radius `0.05`

Leak:
- pulses by damage severity
- radius `0.003..0.015`
- lifetime `0.5..1.4 s`

13.10 Cull rules

- cull beyond `18 m`
- cull inside visor near plane under `0.08 m`
- reduce count by 50 percent when render scale below `0.85`
- cap overlapping bubble transparency layers to 2 in same pixel bucket on MX350

`vel += (flow - vel) * (0.18 + DensityBias * 0.08) * dt`

14.7 Boid-like micro-clustering

This is not full animal boids. It is micro-cluster suspended matter.

Neighbor radius:

- `r_align = 0.55`
- `r_cohesion = 0.85`
- `r_separation = 0.25`

Weights:

- `w_align = 0.22`
- `w_cohesion = 0.14`
- `w_separation = 0.34`
- `w_flow = 0.30`

Grid:
- `cellSize = 0.75`
- `maxPerCell = 32`
- `groupSize = 64`

Alignment:

`alignForce = normalize(avgNeighborVel) * w_align`

Cohesion:

`cohesionForce = normalize(avgNeighborPos - pos) * w_cohesion`

Separation:

`separationForce += normalize(pos - nPos) * saturate(1 - dist / r_separation) * w_separation`

Final acceleration:

`accel = alignForce + cohesionForce + separationForce + normalize(flow) * w_flow * length(flow)`

Purpose:
- avoid visual randomness
- create subtle streamers and clumps
- make shaft intersections richer

14.8 Density stratification

Density by altitude above seafloor:

`bottomProx = saturate(1 - distToFloor / 4.0)`
`stratDensity = lerp(0.4, 1.0, bottomProx)`

Density by depth band:

`depthDensity = lerp(0.7, 1.3, saturate(depth / 250.0))`

Final alpha:

`alpha *= stratDensity * depthDensity`

14.9 Collision / accumulation

Screen-space collision on Medium+:

`if SceneDepth < ParticleDepth + SizeBias then collided`

On collision:

`vel *= 0.35`
`vel.y = max(vel.y, -0.004)`
`life -= dt * 0.18`

Reason:
- slow slide and settle
- avoid immediate death pop

14.11 Lighting

======================================================================
17. UI GLITCH AND TRAUMA LOGIC
======================================================================

17.1 Input variable

`PlayerStress` in `[0..1]`

Sources feeding stress:
- oxygen panic
- hull pressure warning
- near-threat proximity
- impact or damage
- electrical interference
- blackout or low-power mode

Weighted composition:

`PlayerStress = saturate(0.28*O2Stress + 0.22*PressureStress + 0.20*ThreatStress + 0.16*DamageStress + 0.14*PowerStress)`

17.2 Temporal smoothing

Rise time:

`StressFast = lerp(prevStress, PlayerStress, 1 - exp(-dt * 5.5))`

Fall time:

`StressSlow = lerp(prevStress, PlayerStress, 1 - exp(-dt * 1.4))`

Selection:

`StressSmoothed = PlayerStress > prevStress ? StressFast : StressSlow`

17.3 Distortion stage order

1. resolve stress scalar
2. compute sync-shift lane offsets
3. compute chromatic fringe
4. compute scanline instability
5. compute UV wobble
6. compute dropout mask
7. compute ghost image blend
8. composite warning color drift
9. apply final blue-noise dither

17.4 Chromatic aberration

Base strength:

`CA = lerp(0.0000, 0.0038, StressSmoothed)`

Damage boost:

`CA += DamageStress * 0.0022`

Directional bias:

`CA_dir = normalize(float2(sin(Time*1.7), cos(Time*1.1)))`

Sample offsets:

`uvR = uv + CA_dir * CA`
`uvG = uv`
`uvB = uv - CA_dir * CA`

17.5 Vertical sync-shift

Goal:
- emulate HUD scan transport instability
- not VHS meme distortion

Stripe frequency:

`stripeFreq = lerp(6.0, 18.0, StressSmoothed)`

Lane mask:

`lane = floor(uv.y * stripeFreq)`

Shift noise:

`laneNoise = hashSin(float2(lane, floor(Time * 12.0)))`

Shift amplitude:

`shiftAmp = StressSmoothed * 0.012`

Apply:

`uv.x += (laneNoise - 0.5) * shiftAmp * step(0.65, laneNoise)`

17.6 UV wobble

Low-frequency wobble:

`wobble.x = sin(Time * 1.9 + uv.y * 9.0) * 0.0015 * StressSmoothed`
`wobble.y = cos(Time * 1.3 + uv.x * 11.0) * 0.0010 * StressSmoothed`

Damage impulse:

`wobble += ImpactImpulse * float2(hashSin(float2(Time, 1.2)), hashSin(float2(Time, 2.3))) * 0.006`

17.7 Ghost image

Ghost offset:

`ghostOffset = float2(0.0025, -0.0015) * StressSmoothed`

Ghost alpha:

`ghostAlpha = saturate((StressSmoothed - 0.35) / 0.65) * 0.22`

Apply:

`hud_rgb = lerp(hud_rgb, hud_rgb_at(uv + ghostOffset), ghostAlpha)`

17.8 Dropout logic

Dropout noise:

`drop = hashSin(float2(floor(uv.y * 128.0), floor(Time * 20.0)))`

Dropout threshold:

`dropT = lerp(1.0, 0.82, StressSmoothed)`

Mask:

`dropMask = step(dropT, drop)`

Apply:

`hud_rgb *= lerp(1.0, 0.25, dropMask * StressSmoothed)`

17.9 Scanline instability

Scanline phase:

`scan = sin(uv.y * 720.0 + Time * lerp(2.0, 10.0, StressSmoothed))`

Scanline strength:

`scanAmp = lerp(0.00, 0.06, StressSmoothed)`

Apply:

`hud_rgb *= 1 - ((scan * 0.5 + 0.5) * scanAmp)`

17.10 Warning palette drift

When stress rises:

`warnLerp = saturate((StressSmoothed - 0.45) / 0.55)`
`hud_rgb = lerp(hud_rgb, lerp(float3_from_hex(6FD2E4), float3_from_hex(D08A2F), warnLerp), warnLerp * 0.45)`

At critical:

`crit = saturate((StressSmoothed - 0.82) / 0.18)`
`hud_rgb = lerp(hud_rgb, float3_from_hex(BB5A2A), crit * 0.55)`

17.11 Text jitter

Text elements only:

`textJitter = (hashSin(float2(ElementId, floor(Time * 24.0))) - 0.5) * 2.0`
`textPos.x += textJitter * StressSmoothed * 0.8 px`
`textPos.y += textJitter * StressSmoothed * 0.3 px`

17.12 Opacity and legibility guard

Legibility floor:

`uiAlpha = max(uiAlpha, 0.65)`

Critical indicators exempt:
- oxygen meter
- pressure warnings
- interaction prompt
- hard objective

Exempt elements receive:

`criticalUiAlpha = max(criticalUiAlpha, 0.82)`
`criticalCA = CA * 0.30`
`criticalDropMask = 0`

17.13 Distortion budget tiers

MX350:
- one pass
- 3 texture reads world UI base + 2 offset samples
- no blur
- no bloom on HUD

RTX:
- optional additional soft ghost or phosphor persistence pass

22. APPENDIX B: FORMULA SUMMARY
======================================================================

Transmittance:

`T_rgb = exp(-beta_ext_rgb * d)`

Fog base:

`fog = 1 - exp(-depth_lin * sigma_t * floor_boost * FogDensityMul)`

Noir remap:

`fog_noir = pow(fog, NoirPower)`

HG phase:

`phase_hg = (1 - g^2) / (4*PI*pow(1 + g^2 - 2*g*cosTheta, 1.5))`

Godray 4-sample:

`shaft = sum(sample_n * weight_n), n=0..3`

Triplanar weights:

`w = pow(abs(N), Sharpness); w /= dot(w,1)`

Wetness roughness:

`roughness = lerp(roughnessDry, roughnessDry * WetGain, wet)`

Bubble compression:

`r = r_surface * sqrt(P_surface / P_depth)`

Bubble wobble:

`offset.x = sin(t*f + phase + y*k1) * amp`
`offset.z = cos(t*f*0.79 + phase + y*k2) * amp`

HUD CA:

`uvR = uv + dir * CA`
`uvB = uv - dir * CA`

HUD sync shift:

`uv.x += (laneNoise - 0.5) * shiftAmp * step(0.65, laneNoise)`

- saturation `-18`
- post exposure `-0.12`

White Balance:
- temperature `-14`
- tint `-4`

Channel Mixer or SMH:
- reduce red mids slightly
- preserve blue-green in highlights only

25.7 Emergency / low-power post

When power or HUD stress high:
- bloom intensity reduce by 40 percent
- vignette increase by 15 percent
- exposure drops by `-0.10`
- CA moves to HUD/visor, not world

Reason:
- preserve readability of hazard cues
- keep world stable while instrumentation destabilizes

25.8 Forbidden post combinations

- strong bloom + strong fog + strong CA at once
- full-scene grain in clean shallow onboarding
- lens distortion + CA + motion blur in same gameplay moment on MX350
- bright lifted shadows in abyss
- saturated teal shadows in all biomes

25.9 Tone-curve target by biome

Shallow:
- moderate toe
- mild shoulder
- preserve highlight water energy

Midnight:
- deeper toe
- more midtone compression
- preserve narrow highlight cone

Abyss:
- strong toe
- minimal shoulder lift
- protect biolum hot pixels from bloom blowout

26. EMISSIVE, BIOLUM, AND ELECTRICAL LIGHT DNA
======================================================================

26.1 General rule

Emissive exists to describe:
- system status
- power hierarchy
- hazard intensity
- biological response
- spatial lure

Emissive does not exist to:
- brighten dead corners
- replace bounce
- make dark scenes easier by brute force

26.2 Emissive brightness bands

Band A: dormant indicator
- `1.0` relative unit
- tiny LEDs, passive console glow

Band B: active function
- `2.5` relative unit
- powered seams, navigation marks

Band C: alert or hazard
- `5.0` relative unit
- alarms, stressed power feeds, electrical arcs

Band D: biolum focal point
- `4.0` relative unit
- fauna lure organs, pulse nodes

Band E: rare critical burst
- `8.0` relative unit
- should be time-limited and sparse

26.3 Electrical flicker logic

Do not use random binary flicker. Use stateful degraded waveform.

Wave:

`flicker = 0.78 + 0.12*sin(t*11.0 + phase) + 0.06*sin(t*23.0 + phase*0.7)`

Failure spike:

`failure = step(0.985, hashSin(float2(floor(t*7.0), phase)))`

Final:

`emissiveGain = flicker * (1 - failure*0.65)`

26.4 Biolum pulse families

Family 1: soft inhalation pulse
- `freqA = 0.42`
- `freqB = 0.87`
- `amp = 0.18`

Family 2: alert stutter pulse
- `freqA = 1.40`
- `freqB = 2.20`
- `amp = 0.28`

Family 3: abyss lure pulse
- `freqA = 0.16`
- `freqB = 0.33`
- `amp = 0.36`

Formula:

`pulse = base + ampA*sin(t*TAU*freqA + phase) + ampB*sin(t*TAU*freqB + phase*0.6)`

Clamp:

`pulse = saturate(pulse)`

26.5 Biolum color behavior

Biolum colors allowed:
- teal-cyan
- mint-cyan
- cold blue
- rare sick amber-green for hazard organisms

Biolum colors forbidden as dominant fauna family:
- neon purple as default
- candy pink
- clean white flood

26.6 Emissive fog interaction

Emissive should feed local fog scatter only within limited radius.

Radius:

`r_scatter = lerp(1.5, 4.5, emissiveGain / 8.0)`

Scatter term:

`emissiveScatter = emissiveColor * (1 - exp(-sigma_s * localDistance)) * 0.22`

26.7 Electrical arc punctuation

Arc lifetime:
- `0.04..0.18 s`

Arc color:
- core `#BFE7F7`
- fringe `#76BDD8`

Arc bloom threshold handling:
- allow threshold punch only during event
- no persistent arc halo

26.8 Signage emissive

Signage luminance:
- use Band A or Band B only
- hazard red or amber only where gameplay supports it
- signage never casts world-scale readability

27. FLORA AND ORGANIC SHADING SPEC
======================================================================

27.1 Goal

Organic assets must read as living or once-living submerged matter with:
- broad flow response
- translucency only where justified
- wet edge sheen
- varied thickness
- stratified silhouette quality

27.2 Global flow first

Per-flora motion order:

1. global current sway
2. low-frequency stem bend
3. optional frond ripple near camera
4. contact reaction only where noticeable

Global sway formula:

`sway = sin(dot(worldPos.xz, FlowDir) * FlowFrequency + Time*FlowSpeed + phase) * FlowAmp`

Defaults:
- `FlowFrequency = 0.18`
- `FlowSpeed = 0.75`
- `FlowAmp = 0.08 for kelp stems`
- `FlowAmp = 0.03 for short flora`

27.3 Thickness-based translucency

Translucency mask from thickness or authored channel:

`trans = ThicknessMask * TransStrength`

Lighting:

`transLight = saturate(dot(-L, N)) * trans * TransColor`

Defaults:
- kelp green `#6AA388`
- pale algae `#8CB098`
- toxic variant `#90A46A`

27.4 Wet edge response

Edges facing light and viewer receive subtle water sheen.

`edge = pow(1 - saturate(dot(N, V)), 3.5)`
`wetEdge = edge * Wetness * 0.22`
`specular += wetEdge`

27.5 Dithered alpha for flora

Near:
- full alpha clip with blue-noise threshold

Mid:
- stronger dither crossfade

Far:
- simplified card or clustered silhouette

Coverage rule:

`alphaCoverage = saturate(alphaMask * DensityFactor - Cutoff)`

27.6 Color bands

Shallow flora:
- warmer green
- occasional yellow-green tips

Midnight flora:
- cooler olive-black
- lower saturation

Abyss flora:
- almost black with minor blue-green rim
- occasional biolum tips only if species authored for it

27.7 Avoided failures

- plastic bright green
- translucent glow in full darkness
- uniform motion phase
- identical frond width and color
- low-poly silhouette collapse without dither

27.8 LOD transition rules

LOD0:
- branch variation
- full translucency
- flow + local ripple

LOD1:
- keep silhouette
- remove local ripple

LOD2:
- card cluster
- dither transition

Cull:
- based on authored family size and biome density

`grime = GrimeMask.r`

`albedo *= 1 - streakDark`
`roughness += grime * 0.08`

- base saturation max `0.68`

Forbidden:
- large screen-facing quad halos

- 22 percent near
- 50 percent mid
- 28 percent far

- 15 percent near

- strong shaft
- strong emissive cluster

31.4 Readability ladder

Player must first read:
1. path or threat direction
2. nearest interactable silhouette
3. major biome cue
4. secondary motion in medium
5. micro storytelling detail

31.5 Camera move interaction

Fast move:
- reduce HUD distortion amplitude by 10 percent
- reduce marine snow alpha by 15 percent
- keep shaft and cone core stable

Slow move:
- allow higher particle visibility
- allow more subtle shimmer
- wet reflections become more noticeable

37. APPENDIX H: LIGHTING GRAMMAR
======================================================================

37.1 Light categories

- Category A: solar contribution
- Category B: player flashlight
- Category C: fixed industrial practicals

- Category E: biolum sources

37.2 Solar contribution

Only valid in shallow and select breach shafts.

Properties:
- broad
- low-frequency
- soft-edged
- caustic-coupled

Invalid solar behavior:
- hard spotlight beams in open deep water
- lighting far abyssal floor
- filling interiors through solid mass

37.3 Player flashlight

Flashlight is the main authored readability tool below shallow safe water.

Cone grammar:
- center cone carries shape and surface truth
- mid cone carries particles and local fog

Center cone intensity target:
- `1.00`

Mid cone intensity target:
- `0.35`

Outer cone intensity target:
- `0.08`

37.4 Industrial practicals

Practical lights must feel sparse and motivated.

Valid positions:
- corridor corners
- door frames
- maintenance panels
- ruined mast heads
- vent monitors

Invalid positions:
- evenly spaced ambient uplights
- decorative fill lights
- soft beauty lights with no source

37.5 Hazard lights

Hazard lights use amber or red-amber family, but must not saturate the scene.

Rules:
- keep radius local
- allow light flicker or pulse only if narrative/systemic
- do not allow multiple hazard lights to flatten into orange wash

37.6 Biolum lighting

Biolum sources:
- pulse out of phase
- illuminate only local medium
- should barely influence hard-surface albedo except at close range

Biolum radius guidance:
- small fauna `0.5..1.5 m`
- cluster node `1.5..3.0 m`

37.7 Key / fill / rim logic by biome

Shallow:
- key = sun/flashlight
- fill = water body scatter
- rim = caustic or reflective wet edge

Midnight:
- key = flashlight or local practical
- fill = local fog only
- rim = occasional wet edge or emissive spill

- key = flashlight or rare biolum

- rim = highly selective

37.8 Light-to-material pairing

Rock under flashlight:
- broad rough highlight
- mineral variation
- limited edge fresnel

Hull under flashlight:
- sharper functional spec
- seam catchlights
- readable wear breaks

Flora under flashlight:
- thin translucency
- damp edge sheen
- low-intensity subsurface response

37.9 Light-to-particle pairing

Marine snow:
- strong forward scatter
- weak side scatter
- almost no back fill

Bubbles:
- rim first
- transmission second

Vent plume:
- warm fog scatter local

37.10 Light budget by shot

Per shot dominant light sources:
- one primary
- one supporting
- optional tertiary accent

If more than three meaningful sources fight for dominance, the shot is structurally weak.

41. APPENDIX L: QUICK-REFERENCE MATRICES
======================================================================

41.1 Biome quick-reference matrix

Shallow:
- dominant read: moving light through medium
- support read: wet geology and safe-path visibility
- fog color bias: green-cyan restrained
- emissive policy: minimal, functional only
- particle density: moderate
- shaft policy: local and justified
- caustic policy: yes, depth-gated

Midnight:
- dominant read: flashlight cone and silhouette falloff
- support read: fabricated wear or rock wetness
- fog color bias: slate-blue with low green carry
- emissive policy: sparse and status-driven
- particle density: moderate-high in cone
- shaft policy: flashlight or breach only
- caustic policy: generally no

Abyss:
- dominant read: void pressure and narrow readability corridor
- support read: sparse biolum or threat punctuation
- fog color bias: blue-black and cold teal at very low value
- emissive policy: highly sparse, localized, meaningful
- particle density: moderate, lower alpha than midnight
- shaft policy: no solar shafts, only rare local events
- caustic policy: no

41.2 Material quick-reference matrix

Voxel rock:
- wetness high
- metallic low
- roughness broad
- macro breakup critical
- triplanar mandatory on irregular world masses

Hull metal:
- wear readable
- edge polish selective
- leak sheen local
- warning color muted by grime
- PBR packing fixed as `R Metallic G AO B Roughness A Wear`

Visor:
- refraction subtle until event
- scratches visible at grazing
- runoff directional
- stress distortion layered after world, before final UI widgets

Flora:
- global sway first
- translucency limited to thin zones
- silhouette quality more important than micro texture

41.3 HUD quick-reference matrix

Stress `0.0..0.2`
- no dropout
- no ghosting
- CA negligible
- warning palette off

Stress `0.2..0.5`
- light sync-shift
- light CA
- warning palette enters amber edge cases only

Stress `0.5..0.8`
- visible lane drift
- ghost image appears
- scanline instability active
- critical widgets protected

Stress `0.8..1.0`
- strong but controlled trauma
- dropout non-critical only
- amber-red warning drift
- readability floor enforced

41.4 Sign-off gates

The shot or biome implementation is not acceptable unless all items below are true:

- The frame reads correctly with post reduced by 50 percent.
- The material hierarchy survives in grayscale.
- The frame still reads on Tier 0 without global volumetric cheats.
- The player can distinguish shallow, midnight, and abyss from a still image.
- Hazard amber and biolum cyan do not collapse into the same perceived role.
- Wetness is readable on rock and hull without mirror gloss.
- Marine snow enhances scale instead of becoming weather noise.
- HUD trauma escalates by system state, not random timer noise.
- Flashlight cone, particulate density, fog compression, and emissive punctuation remain ordered under motion, pause, stress, and fallback tiers.

End of spec.
